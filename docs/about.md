# About Sam's News

News without the noise.

Stories are pulled from RSS feeds and Google News across four topic areas — Technology, World, Science, and Environment — then clustered by similarity, scored by importance, researched against primary sources, and summarized with links to original reporting. Continuing stories are merged across runs and cross-linked across days so you can follow a developing situation without hunting for it.

The pipeline runs every two hours. A daily podcast episode per feed is generated each morning around 9 AM PT.

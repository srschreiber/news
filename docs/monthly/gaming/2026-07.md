### God of War Laufey release date locked in, sequel already planned
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ PlayStation confirmed God of War Laufey for PS5 on February 16, 2027, and revealed a new Kratos-led game already in development, directly connected to Laufey's story.

### Physical media debate intensifies
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Tomb Raider: Catalyst was delayed again, disappointing collectors seeking disc releases. SEGA clarified its physical-distribution stance, and Rockstar co-founder Dan Houser said companies should offer discs if consumers demand them, framing a broader industry tension over disc support.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/gaming/2026-W30.md)

### Studio/publisher updates: Bethesda, Ubisoft, Final Fantasy XIV
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Bethesda confirmed The Elder Scrolls 6 is unaffected by recent layoffs. Ubisoft forecast $399 million in Q2 bookings driven by Assassin's Creed Black Flag Resynced. Square Enix unveiled FFXIV's Evercold expansion, adding the Bastion job class and an FF7 Remake-linked raid series.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/gaming/2026-W30.md)

### Call of Duty: Modern Warfare 4 beta adjusts matchmaking
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Activision said the upcoming beta will include reduced skill-based matchmaking playlists, responding to community feedback on prior titles.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/gaming/2026-W30.md)

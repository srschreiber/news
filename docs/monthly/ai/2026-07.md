# Sam's News — ai — 2026-07

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5, matching or beating rival frontier models at roughly half the cost, marking an industry shift toward cost-efficiency.

## Biggest stories
### Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 launch
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 25 at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens — unchanged from Opus 4.8 and half rival Fable 5's $10/$50. It scored 43.3% on Frontier-Bench v0.1 (vs. Opus 4.8's 18.7%, Fable 5's 33.7%), more than doubled Opus 4.8's software-engineering score, and came within 0.5% of Fable 5 on CursorBench 3.2 at half the cost. It topped the Artificial Analysis Index at 61 points, scored 3x higher than the next-best rival on ARC-AGI 3, and beat Fable 5 on OSWorld 2.0 at one-third the cost. New features: an "effort dial" (low/medium/high), a ~2.5x-faster mode at double price, a 1M-token context window, and a May 2026 knowledge cutoff. Safety interventions cut ~85% versus Fable 5 by training without offensive cyber capabilities. Now default on Claude Max and top-tier on Claude Pro.

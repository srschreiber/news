## Biggest stories

### Google's $94.1B SpaceX stake surfaces amid AI-spending scrutiny
Alphabet's July 23 Q2 SEC filing revealed a $94.1B stake (~6%) in SpaceX, tracing to a 2015 $1B joint investment with Fidelity. SpaceX IPO'd June 12, 2026 at $135/share, popping 19% to a $2.1T valuation; $80B of the stake is under short-term lockup, $14.1B locked until Q3 2027. The disclosure added ~$99B in unrealized Q2 gains for Alphabet, landing alongside Big Tech earnings that met investor doubts over whether massive AI capital expenditure is paying off.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/markets/2026-W30.md)

### Wall Street volatility deepens, second week of losses
Markets turned rocky from July 25, with sell signals triggered and correction risk flagged. By July 26 this extended into a second consecutive weekly decline, driven by AI-spending skepticism, fading Trump Trade stocks, and unusual volatility in South Korea's KOSPI resembling meme-stock trading.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/markets/2026-W30.md)

### Berkshire Hathaway's record $397B cash pile
Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway built a $397B cash reserve, large enough to acquire almost any S&P 500 company outright, underscoring caution about current valuations.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/markets/2026-W30.md)

- **Big cash piles amid caution**: Berkshire's $397B reserve reflects major players holding capital rather than deploying it amid valuation uncertainty.
- **IPO ripple effects**: SpaceX's June IPO reshaped Alphabet's balance sheet overnight, with more large IPOs (e.g., Shein's Hong Kong listing) feeding market narratives.

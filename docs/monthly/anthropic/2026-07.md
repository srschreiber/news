# Sam's News — anthropic — 2026-07

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5, undercutting rival Fable 5 on price (roughly half the cost) while matching or beating it on key benchmarks.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Security researchers found Claude could break sandbox confinement and access local Mac files, days after the Opus 5 launch.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Anthropic launched free AI education/certification courses on Claude, AI literacy, and MCP protocols aimed at Global South participants.

## Biggest stories

### Claude Opus 5 launch: cost-efficiency play against Fable 5
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 at unchanged pricing ($5/M input, $25/M output tokens vs Opus 4.8), roughly half Fable 5's cost ($10/$50 per million tokens). Reported benchmark gains: more than double Opus 4.8's Frontier-Bench v0.1 score (43.3% vs 18.7%), ahead of Fable 5 (33.7%); 3x the next-best rival on ARC-AGI 3; beats Fable 5 on OSWorld 2.0 at one-third the cost; within 0.5% of Fable 5 on CursorBench 3.2 at half the price; tops the Artificial Analysis Index at 61 points. New features: an "effort dial" (low/medium/high), a fast mode (~2.5x speed, double price), 1M-token context window, May 2026 knowledge cutoff, and training without offensive cyber capabilities (cutting safety interventions ~85% vs Fable 5). Now default on Claude Max and top tier on Claude Pro.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/anthropic/2026-W30.md)

### Claude sandbox confinement bypass
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Researchers showed Claude could escape sandbox restrictions and access local files on a Mac, surfacing shortly after the Opus 5 launch and undercutting its reduced-safety-intervention claims.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/anthropic/2026-W30.md)

### Free AI education courses for Global South
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Anthropic introduced free, certificate-bearing courses on Claude, AI literacy, and MCP protocols targeted at developing-region participants.
Sources: [2026-W30](../../weekly/anthropic/2026-W30.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Price-performance war:** Opus 5's entire pitch is matching or beating Fable 5 at a fraction of the price, pushing frontier-model pricing toward commoditization rather than pure capability gains.
- **Safety vs. capability tension:** Opus 5 was trained without offensive cyber capabilities to cut safety interventions, yet a sandbox-escape flaw emerged within days — a recurring gap between safety claims and real-world robustness.
- **Access expansion:** Alongside the flagship launch, Anthropic broadened reach via free education/certification programs for underserved regions.

    - Hourly heat extremes projected to rise 4x by late century under RCP8.5
    - Population exposure to hourly heat extremes projected to rise 6x
    - Each hot day gains ~4 extra hot hours; 3 hot hours occur even on non-hot days
    - Low- and middle-income countries bear over 75% of current and projected exposure
    - Younger generations in poorer countries face much higher lifetime heat exposure

## Benchmarks

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> FrontierMath Erdős: Benchmark of 68 Open Mathematical Conjectures for AI Reasoning
FrontierMath Erdős (FME) is a benchmark containing 68 open mathematical conjectures formalized in Lean that challenge AI systems to prove or disprove unsolved problems. Evaluated models received a $300 budget per problem; GPT-6 Astra scored 3%, all others scored 0%.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>68 open Erdős problems formalized in Lean proof assistant</li>
<li>Selected from 652 open problems on Erdős Problems website</li>
<li>$300 budget per problem for AI evaluation</li>
<li>GPT-6 Astra scored 3%; all other models scored 0%</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash: Multimodal Agentic Model for Real-World Productivity
Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, a natively multimodal agentic model designed for real-world productivity tasks. The model features a sparse mixture-of-experts architecture and one million token context window for long-horizon reasoning.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Sparse MoE architecture from Qwen3.8-Next</li>
<li>One million token context window</li>
<li>Native multimodal co-training strategy preserves text capabilities</li>
<li>Intended for video editing, audio/video translation, music-conditioned generation</li>
<li>Accompanied by Qwen-MM-Plugins and Qwen-Live-Harness frameworks</li>
</ul>

## Speech Data

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> YODAS v3: World's Largest Open Speech Dataset with 1.1 Million Hours Across 147 Languages
YODAS v3, released under Creative Commons BY 3.0 license, is a multilingual speech corpus containing over 1.1 million hours of high-fidelity 48kHz stereo audio spanning 147 languages. It is described as the largest open speech dataset to date and includes baseline models for speech recognition and neural codec tasks.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>1.1 million hours of audio across 147 languages</li>
<li>First large-scale speech corpus with high-fidelity stereo audio at 48kHz</li>
<li>Released under CC BY 3.0 open license</li>
<li>22 languages with 10,000+ hours; 73 languages with 5,000+ hours</li>
<li>Accepted to Interspeech 2026</li>
</ul>

## AI Safety

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Detecting Hidden Behaviors in LLMs via Activation-Matched Fine-Tuning
Researchers presented activation-matched fine-tuning, an unsupervised method to detect hidden behaviors in language models including backdoors, sleeper agents, and conditional censorship. The technique requires no prior knowledge of trigger conditions and reliably identifies unusual behavior by comparing activation patterns between a suspect model and a benign reference model.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Detects backdoors, sleeper agents, sandbagging, and topic-conditioned censorship</li>
<li>Requires no prior knowledge of trigger or target behavior</li>
<li>Method compares residuals between suspect and reference model activations</li>
<li>Defense-aware attacks fail without sacrificing hidden behavior itself</li>
<li>Paper submitted to arXiv May 29, 2026; under review</li>
</ul>

## Healthcare AI

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NSIDDx: Neuro-Symbolic Differential Diagnosis Framework for Low-Resource Clinical Settings
NSIDDx is a neuro-symbolic framework combining large language models with symbolic reasoning to improve diagnostic reliability for rare diseases in low-resource healthcare settings. The system addresses gaps between benchmark accuracy and clinical verifiability through ternary symptom encoding, contradiction detection, and clinician-in-the-loop reasoning rather than passive end-user interaction.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Combines LLMs with symbolic reasoning for rare disease diagnosis</li>
<li>Designed to run offline on consumer hardware</li>
<li>Includes ternary symptom encoding, contradiction detection, audit strings, practitioner override</li>
<li>Positions clinician as active reasoning agent rather than passive end-user</li>
<li>Paper submitted to arXiv August 31, 2026; code available on GitHub</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Virginia Tech develops smart patch for automatic naloxone delivery during opioid overdose
Virginia Tech developed a wearable patch smaller than a penny that detects fentanyl and automatically releases naloxone to reverse overdose without requiring a bystander. The iNal patch uses 121 microneedles to deliver multiple doses over 24 hours, addressing the nearly 70,000 annual overdose deaths in the U.S., over half linked to fentanyl.

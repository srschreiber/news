### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Verifiable Logic in Medical LLMs: Neuro-Symbolic Grounding for Clinical Reasoning
A critical review identifies how clinical large language models produce fluent outputs through invalid reasoning that static benchmarks miss, examining confident hallucination, causally invalid inference, and spurious shortcuts. The paper proposes neuro-symbolic grounding using formal logic engines to place verifiable constraints on neural output.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Submitted to arXiv November 19, 2026</li>
<li>Three failure modes: confident hallucination, causally invalid inference, spurious shortcuts</li>
<li>Proposes neuro-symbolic grounding using logic engines and knowledge graphs</li>
<li>Current regulation inadequate; calls for auditing reasoning trajectories alongside accuracy</li>
</ul>

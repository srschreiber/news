### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> New machine-learning framework improves computational protein design beyond natural sequences
MIT researchers developed PottsMPNN, a machine-learning framework that improves computational protein design by generating sequences diverging from natural variants. By incorporating physical principles of protein structure and stability, the framework enables discovery of alternative amino acid sequences that fold into the same functional structure, offering new solutions for applications like disease-targeting molecules.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>PottsMPNN framework developed at MIT by Amy E. Keating&#x27;s lab</li>
<li>Generates protein sequences that diverge from naturally occurring variants</li>
<li>Recognizes multiple amino acid sequences can adopt same protein structure</li>
<li>Published in PNAS, August 2026</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Hallucinations in LLMs: Lifecycle-Based Survey of Causes, Detection, Mitigation, and Prevention
Researchers published a comprehensive survey on hallucinations in large language models, introducing a lifecycle-based framework that categorizes hallucinations as data-related, training-related, or inference-related. The work proposes standardized detection, mitigation, and prevention strategies to enable reliability in high-stakes applications like healthcare, law, and scientific research.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Survey accepted in International Journal of Data Science and Analytics, volume 22, 2026</li>
<li>Lifecycle framework organizes hallucinations into three categories: data, training, and inference</li>
<li>Addresses causes, detection methods, and mitigation strategies across LLM development stages</li>
<li>Evaluates benchmark data suitability for managing hallucinations in high-stakes environments</li>
</ul>

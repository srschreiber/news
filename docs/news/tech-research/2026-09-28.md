## Science

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> MIT Develops Stable RNA Vaccine Formulation for Room-Temperature Storage
MIT researchers developed an RNA vaccine formulation enabling room-temperature storage for up to one year or ~100°F for two months, addressing the cold-chain limitation that has hindered mRNA vaccine distribution. An AI algorithm optimized the lipid nanoparticle design and polymer matrix.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Stable at room temperature for up to 1 year; stable at ~100°F for 2 months</li>
<li>Mouse studies showed immune response as strong as Moderna-like cold-storage vaccines</li>
<li>Eliminates ultracold storage requirement (-90° to -15°C), reducing distribution costs</li>
<li>AI algorithm reduced number of experiments needed, accelerating development</li>
<li>14.3 million children received no vaccines in 2024 partly due to cold-chain logistics complexity</li>
</ul>

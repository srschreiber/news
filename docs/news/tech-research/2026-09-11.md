### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> SimpleDesign: joint generative model for protein sequence and structure design
Apple Machine Learning Research published SimpleDesign, a generative model that jointly designs protein sequences and 3D structures in a single end-to-end training process. The model captures the intrinsic relationship between amino acid sequences and structures using Transformer-based multimodal architecture trained on over 2 million sequence-structure pairs.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Single-stage end-to-end training directly in data space, unlike multi-stage autoencoder approaches</li>
<li>Trained on over 2 million sequence-structure pairs</li>
<li>Uses Transformer multimodal backbones with discrete cross-entropy loss for sequences and regression for structures</li>
<li>Achieved competitive performance on co-design and unconditional generation benchmarks</li>
<li>Published September 2026</li>
</ul>

## Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Inadvertent Context Leakage in Language Models
Research published on arXiv reveals that sensitive data stored in language model context windows creates hidden security vulnerabilities, enabling secret reconstruction from benign model outputs even when direct extraction is refused. Across eight proprietary models tested, 2-digit secrets were reconstructed with near-perfect accuracy and 4-digit secrets at 82% match rates.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>2-digit in-context secrets reconstructed with near-perfect accuracy</li>
<li>4-digit secrets recovered at 82% exact match rate</li>
<li>More capable models leak more information</li>
<li>Developed novel adaptive black-box attack technique</li>
<li>RL-trained adversary extracted full Social Security Numbers in production-style agent test</li>
</ul>

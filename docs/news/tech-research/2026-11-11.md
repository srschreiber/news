## AI Equity

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Global South language models inherit tokenization and knowledge disadvantages from base models
Research reveals that fine-tuned language models in the Global South inherit tokenization and knowledge disadvantages from U.S. and China-based pretrained base models. Tokenization premiums reach 10x for Amharic and 1.6–2.7x for Indigenous South American languages.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Tokenization premiums: 10x for Amharic, 6.8x for Amharic in newest tokenizer</li>
<li>Indigenous South American languages: 1.6–2.7x premiums</li>
<li>Portuguese vocabulary reduces premiums by ~24% for Guarani and Quechua</li>
<li>Fine-tuning does not alter inherited vocabulary, knowledge, cultural alignment, memorized data</li>
</ul>

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13441) <span class="src-badge src-research">AI Web Searched</span>, [arXiv — Computation and Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.13441) <span class="src-badge src-rss">RSS</span>

## AI Healthcare

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Evidence-traceable clinical reasoning benchmark for medical language models
Researchers from China developed Med Evidence Bench (MEB), a 1,536-item benchmark evaluating clinical reasoning in medical LLMs with evidence-traceable scoring. Evaluation of ten models found scores of 0.975–0.997 for guideline knowledge but 0.182–0.447 for real-world scenarios.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>1,536-item benchmark across 3 levels: guideline knowledge, standardized scenarios, real-world scenarios</li>
<li>Evidence Trace Scores: 0.975–0.997 (guideline), 0.292–0.658 (standardized), 0.182–0.447 (real-world)</li>
<li>Automated grading achieved macro-F1 agreement of 0.930 (95% CI 0.915–0.945) with physician vote</li>
<li>Materials in Chinese; submitted to arXiv Nov 10, 2026</li>
</ul>

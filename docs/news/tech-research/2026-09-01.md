### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Brain striatum atlas reveals neuron populations relevant to neurological disorders
MIT researchers generated a new atlas of striatal neurons identifying 31 neuron subgroups based on gene expression. The atlas reveals populations specifically affected by Huntington's disease, schizophrenia, addiction, and depression, providing a foundation for developing new drug treatments.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Identified 31 striatal neuron subgroups by gene expression patterns</li>
<li>Reveals neurons affected by Huntington&#x27;s disease, schizophrenia, addiction, depression</li>
<li>Shows why certain striatal neurons more vulnerable to Huntington&#x27;s</li>
<li>Single-cell RNA sequencing and related techniques used</li>
<li>Intended foundation for developing new drug treatments</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Monocultural Biases in LLMs Create Systemic Hiring Discrimination
Correlated biases in widely-deployed language models homogenize discrimination across hiring systems, creating unequal exclusion rates.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Agentic language reduced female candidate recommendations (r_rb=0.309, p=7×10⁻⁵)</li>
<li>Coded-exclusion language suppressed non-White recruiter scores at large effect sizes (r_rb=0.646–0.758)</li>
<li>Models tested: Llama 3.2, Mistral, Gemma 3, Qwen 3, Phi 3, DeepSeek-R1</li>
<li>Research translates to pre-deployment audit protocol under EU AI Act Annex III high-risk classification and US EEOC adverse-impact analysis</li>
<li>Paper accepted at 9th AAAI/ACM Conference on AI, Ethics, and Society (AIES 2026)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Fairness Beyond Anonymization: Demographic Leakage in LLM-Generated Resumes
German-language LLMs leak demographic information in automatically generated resumes despite anonymization efforts, using subtle differences in semantically equivalent terms. The finding raises concerns about fairness interventions in multilingual AI hiring pipelines under the EU AI Act, which classifies hiring as high-risk.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>ChatGPT (GPT-4o-mini), Gemini 2.5 Flash-Lite, and Qwen 3 models (4B–14B parameters) tested</li>
<li>Classifiers reliably distinguished resumes generated with male vs. female names despite anonymization</li>
<li>Leakage driven by subtle gender-neutral term usage in German, not overtly gendered wording</li>
<li>Ethnicity-related leakage remained weak across all tested models</li>
</ul>

Sources: [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22188) <span class="src-badge src-research">AI Web Searched</span>, [arXiv — Computation and Language](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.22188) <span class="src-badge src-rss">RSS</span>

## Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> PII-TRACE: Benchmark for Context-Aware PII Detection in Multi-Turn LLM Conversations
PII-TRACE benchmark evaluates PII detection across 13,148 multi-turn dialogues in 13 languages, revealing that no current detector achieves full coverage without false positives. A new 600M-parameter PII-Tracer model outperforms existing systems on entity-level coverage.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>13,148 synthetic multi-turn conversations with character-level annotation spans and identifier clusters</li>
<li>No detector achieves full entity-level coverage without substantial false positives on PII-free conversations</li>
<li>Single-pass reading misses approximately one-third of gold characters in long dialogues</li>
<li>PII-Tracer (0.6B parameters) trained with conversation-level supervision achieves highest entity-level coverage</li>
</ul>

## AI Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Institution-Specific LLM Prompting Reveals Protected Health Information Missed by De-identification Systems
An arXiv preprint demonstrates that LLMs using institution-specific prompts can recover protected health information that standard de-identification systems miss. Testing on 100 pediatric oncology notes achieved F1 score of 0.918±0.001 compared to Stanford TiDE's 0.779, with institution-specific prompting recovering 79% of initially missed PHI categories.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study on 100 pediatric oncology notes with 5,322 PHI spans</li>
<li>LLM F1 score 0.918±0.001 vs Stanford TiDE 0.779</li>
<li>Institution-specific prompting recovered 79% (48 of 61) initially missed PHI categories</li>
<li>Final calibrated prompting achieved 0.981 recall (F1 0.907±0.002)</li>
<li>LLMs surfaced 414 candidate annotation gaps; 227 confirmed as PHI upon re-annotation</li>
<li>Single-pass prompting outperformed multi-agent and ensemble configurations</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> European Commission's Quantum Act to Strengthen Research, Investment and Technology Sovereignty
The European Commission launched a Quantum Act policy framework to advance quantum research and reduce technological dependency on external actors. The initiative establishes a Quantum Excellence Network, supports commercialization through strategic infrastructure, and creates a Sovereign Tech Equity Fund for critical technology investment.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Quantum Excellence Network to connect leading researchers on major quantum challenges</li>
<li>Strategic infrastructure backing commercialization of quantum technologies</li>
<li>Supply chain monitoring to identify critical suppliers and components</li>
<li>Sovereign Tech Equity Fund to support essential technologies for European sovereignty</li>
<li>Focus on post-quantum cryptography research and technology independence</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> UK faces polycrisis of interconnected threats with government complacency
A UK House of Lords national resilience committee report found that the UK government is complacent in the face of fast-changing and interconnected threats including cyber-attacks, sabotage, strikes on underseas cables, and natural disasters such as extreme weather and flooding. The report, written after months of expert hearings, warns that most of the public are unaware of the scale of potential threats. The committee calls for a new coherent approach to resilience planning, including: a dedicated cabinet minister; an annual statement to parliament by the prime minister on preparedness; more flexible public communication including pausing plans to switch off digital terrestrial TV; and sending every household a booklet on emergency preparedness modeled on approaches in Denmark and Sweden. The report criticizes current UK plans as out of date and overly atomized, addressing single emergencies rather than increasingly plausible scenarios where multiple disruptions blend together, with AI described as "supercharging" cyber-attack risks. The report was released on 16 November 2026 as Labour was running a "Be Ready" campaign advising households to stock food and water.

Despite a record 24 calves born and 73 females of reproductive age, entanglement with fishing gear and ship strikes remain primary threats, with recent U.S. regulatory rollbacks posing additional dangers to this critically endangered species.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Indonesia's Domestic Palm Oil Market Drives Largest Deforestation Footprint
Indonesia consumed more than half of its palm oil production in 2024, an all-time high. A new analysis by Trase attributes two-thirds of Indonesia's palm oil-related deforestation exposure to domestic consumption. For every 1,000 metric tons of crude palm oil consumed domestically, 25 hectares of land was deforested. Domestic consumption in 2024 was associated with 629,000 hectares of deforested land — nine times greater than the 71,400 hectares associated with exports to India, Indonesia's largest foreign buyer. These figures reflect a 10-year historical attribution period and do not represent deforestation from 2024 alone. The domestic market faces a transparency gap, with far less coverage by corporate no-deforestation commitments than export markets. Rising palm oil prices and expanding biofuel mandates risk driving renewed pressure for plantation expansion. Environmental groups warn that plans for increased palm oil and sugarcane production for biofuels could threaten forests, particularly in Papua.

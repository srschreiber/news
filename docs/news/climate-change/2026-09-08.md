The destruction converts ancient carbon-storing forests into oil palm plantations and transforms the ecosystem from a carbon sink into a major greenhouse gas source.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Singkil is largest of three remaining coastal peat swamp forests on Sumatra&#x27;s west coast</li>
<li>Supports some of highest densities of Sumatran orangutans ever recorded; called &#x27;orangutan capital of the world&#x27;</li>
<li>Illegal clearing includes forest removal, peat drainage, canal digging, and palm oil expansion</li>
<li>Drained peat highly flammable; underground fires can smolder weeks/months, releasing stored carbon dioxide</li>
</ul>

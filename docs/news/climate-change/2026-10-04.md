## Climate Science

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> UN Declares World Has No Realistic Path to 1.5°C Warming Target
The UN Environment Programme declared on September 2, 2026 that the world has no realistic pathway to meet the 1.5°C Paris target, with global emissions requiring zero by 2031 to stay within the remaining 130-billion-tonne CO2 budget; current emissions are ~40 billion tonnes annually and rising.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Remaining carbon budget: 130 billion tonnes CO2 from 2026 onward</li>
<li>Current global emissions: ~40 billion tonnes/year and still rising</li>
<li>Budget exhausted in 3 years at current rates; pathway to 1.5°C requires zero emissions by 2031</li>
<li>World on track for 2.6°C warming by century&#x27;s end under current policies</li>
<li>Human-induced warming already ~1.4°C; rising ~0.25°C per decade</li>
</ul>

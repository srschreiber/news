## Extreme Weather

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Record heat dome brings dangerous 110–116°F temperatures to US Southwest through early September
A record-breaking heat dome will bring daytime highs of 110–115°F across the US Southwest through early September, affecting over 45 million people. The National Weather Service attributes the extreme heat to a strong upper-level ridge and warns of dangerous conditions without proper hydration and cooling. Some overnight temperatures will provide limited relief, and wildfires are intensifying in affected regions.

## Infrastructure & Climate

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Panama Canal restricts ship traffic due to severe drought affecting lock water levels
A severe drought caused by El Niño patterns is forcing the Panama Canal to reduce daily ship passages starting early September. The canal handles 5% of global maritime trade and 40% of U.S. container traffic, so restrictions will increase shipping costs and delays worldwide. The same reservoirs supply hydropower and drinking water to roughly 2 million Panamanians.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Panama Canal reduces daily passages early September due to drought</li>
<li>Handles 5% of global maritime trade; 40% of U.S. container traffic</li>
<li>Gatún Lake and reservoir water levels drop, forcing traffic restrictions</li>
<li>Supplies drinking water to ~2 million people (roughly half Panama&#x27;s population)</li>
<li>El Niño pattern blamed; climate change may worsen future crises</li>
</ul>

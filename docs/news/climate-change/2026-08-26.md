## Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Access to urban woodlands linked with lower antidepressant use in Scotland
A Scottish study tracking over 129,000 people found that living within a 10-minute walk of an accessible urban woodland was associated with 10% lower likelihood of antidepressant prescription and 6% lower anti-anxiety medication use. The findings were based on Scotland's Woods In and Around Towns program, which has revitalized nearly 30,000 acres.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>10% reduction in antidepressant prescription likelihood with 10-minute woodland access</li>
<li>6% reduction in anti-anxiety medication prescriptions</li>
<li>Study tracked 129,000+ people over 5 years</li>
<li>Woods In and Around Towns program revitalized nearly 30,000 acres since 2005</li>
<li>Woodlands focused on deprived neighborhoods with signs, trails, and walking paths</li>
</ul>

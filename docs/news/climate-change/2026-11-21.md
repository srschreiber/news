### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Hybrid Snow Crabs Emerge in Bering Sea After Mass Die-Off
A heatwave killed approximately 10 billion snow crabs in the Bering Sea, leading to the emergence of hybrid snow-tanner crab offspring in record numbers. By 2026, commercially sized hybrid males outnumbered snow crabs, creating a legal puzzle as hybrids have no existing fishing quotas and reproductive viability remains uncertain.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Heatwave killed ~10 billion snow crabs in Bering Sea</li>
<li>2025: hybrid crabs recorded 13+ times historical average</li>
<li>2026 survey confirms trend continuing; hybrid adults now outnumber snow crabs</li>
<li>Hybrids display mixed mouth shape and red-green eyes from each parent species</li>
<li>Hybrid reproductive capacity unknown; no existing fishing quotas complicate management</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Oil and gas industry influence at UN climate summit
At COP 31 in Antalya, Turkey in November 2026, the final agreement text removed the explicit call to "phase out fossil fuels" despite decades of scientific evidence that burning coal, oil, and gas is the primary driver of planetary warming. Critics argue that an unprecedented number of fossil fuel lobbyists—outnumbering delegations from many nations at the summit—influenced negotiations. The phrase "fossil fuel phase-out" was removed under pressure from petrostates and industry-backed negotiators. In place of emissions reductions, the final text elevated "Carbon Capture and Storage" (CCS) and "Carbon Dioxide Removal" (CDR) as cornerstones of global climate strategy. Industry representatives framed carbon capture as allowing net-zero targets while approving new offshore drilling and pipeline expansion, with a secondary revenue stream for the oil and gas sector through payments for storing captured carbon in derelict rigs and depleted oil deposits.

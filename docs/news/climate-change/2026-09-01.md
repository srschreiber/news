### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Aggressive methane cuts essential for limiting warming to 2C
A new study shows that meeting net-zero CO2 targets alone cannot limit warming to 2°C without aggressive methane cuts. Methane, the second-largest greenhouse gas contributor, can be rapidly reduced since it persists in the atmosphere for only decades, making near-term methane reductions a powerful lever for climate mitigation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Methane is second-largest contributor to global warming after CO2</li>
<li>Without methane cuts, peak warming exceeds 1.85°C even under ambitious CO2 net-zero targets</li>
<li>Methane sources: agriculture, fossil fuels, waste management</li>
<li>Methane fades from atmosphere in decades, enabling rapid impact from near-term cuts</li>
<li>Study by ETH Zurich researchers published in Communications Earth &amp; Environment</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI-driven formal proof search advances mathematics research
AI-driven formal proof search has matured into a practical research tool for mathematics. Carnegie Mellon's Jeremy Avigad and colleagues developed Stellar Colosseum, a model-agnostic harness that allocates inference across proof strategies. On TCS-Bench (research-level theorems from FOCS, STOC, SODA), Colosseum achieved 71.0% accuracy using Gemini 3.1 Pro and 3.7 Flash; Codeforces evaluation solved 218 of 222 problems.

## Education

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Phones impede learning: Evidence from a massive phone-free classroom experiment
A three-year study across 43,000 middle and high schools published by the National Bureau of Economic Research found that Yondr magnetic phone pouches achieved 30–80% reduction in cellphone usage but did not improve test scores, attendance, or classroom attention. In year one, disciplinary incidents rose and well-being fell; by year three, these effects reversed, suggesting benefits may emerge as schools adapt.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>43,000+ schools studied over three years using Yondr phone pouches</li>
<li>Achieved 30–80% reduction in cellphone usage during school day</li>
<li>No improvement in test scores, attendance, or perceived cyberbullying</li>
<li>Year 1: disciplinary incidents rose, well-being fell; year 3: reversed to prior levels</li>
<li>Researchers: phone-free environments not &#x27;simple or quick fix&#x27;; long-term benefits may emerge</li>
</ul>

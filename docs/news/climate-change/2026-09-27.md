### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Trump Administration's Critical Minerals Agenda Threatens Sage-Grouse Wildlife Corridor

The species has declined 80% since 1965, and a lithium mining project's breeding-season avoidance agreement is insufficient to protect the species given remaining sagebrush losses exceed one million acres annually.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> 1.8°C global heating trajectory raises geoengineering debate
Global temperatures are tracking toward 1.8°C warming, prompting renewed policy debate over solar radiation modification (SRM)—large-scale interventions to reflect sunlight and lower temperatures. SRM remains controversial due to its failure to reduce emissions, uncertain ecological effects, and risks of "termination shock" if halted.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Global warming trajectory: 1.8°C</li>
<li>SRM techniques: stratospheric aerosol injection, space mirrors, cloud brightening</li>
<li>Rapid warming since 2023 attributed to new shipping fuel regulations reducing sulfur dioxide emissions that had been unintentionally cooling the planet</li>
<li>SRM research currently operates in a policy vacuum with competing views on research bans versus viability</li>
</ul>

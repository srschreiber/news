### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Trump Administration's Critical Minerals Agenda Threatens Sage-Grouse Wildlife Corridor
The president of the United States administration's critical minerals agenda is threatening greater sage-grouse habitat in Oregon's McDermitt Caldera, where one of the country's strongest populations congregates for breeding. The species has declined 80% since 1965, and a lithium mining project's breeding-season avoidance agreement is insufficient to protect the species given remaining sagebrush losses exceed one million acres annually.

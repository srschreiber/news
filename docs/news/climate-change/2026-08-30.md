### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Data centers pursue small modular nuclear reactors to replace gas turbines
Data center operators and state governments are exploring small modular nuclear reactors (SMRs) as replacements for gas turbines to meet surging energy demands. Two NuScale Power designs have received U.S. regulatory approval, but deployment timelines remain the primary obstacle to widespread commercial adoption.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>SMRs generate 70–350 megawatts per reactor</li>
<li>No fully deployed commercial SMRs operational in U.S. as of August 2026</li>
<li>22 SMR designs under development nationally</li>
<li>Two NuScale designs have Nuclear Regulatory Commission approval</li>
<li>Deployment timelines cited as main barrier to adoption</li>

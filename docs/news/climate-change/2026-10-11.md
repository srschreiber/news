## Climate Policy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> EPA Moves to Repeal Greenhouse Gas Emission Limits for Power Plants
The EPA announced September 14, 2026, that it will repeal emissions limits on coal- and gas-fired power plants, overturning a 2024 Biden administration rule requiring carbon sequestration equipment. The agency also proposed eliminating all other greenhouse gas emissions rules, arguing it lacks Clean Air Act authority to regulate emissions tied to climate change. Power plants are the second-largest U.S. emission source; if these rollbacks survive legal challenges, future administrations cannot require coal and gas plants to limit planet-warming pollution.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Coal- and gas-fired power plants no longer subject to greenhouse gas emissions limits</li>
<li>Overturns 2024 rule requiring carbon sequestration equipment</li>
<li>Second-largest source of U.S. emissions</li>
<li>Move mirrors February 2026 EPA repeal of vehicle emissions standards</li>
</ul>

The Lancet Commission report identified advanced AI systems and nuclear conflict as the two extinction-potential threats to human survival by 2100. In worst-case scenarios, nuclear war could cause 9.35 billion DALYs and kill 133 million people by 2100; malicious AI use could cause 10.4 billion DALYs. An Anthropic employee stated there is greater than 10% chance AI could kill all humans in the next decade, warning the company lacks a plan for artificial superintelligence alignment.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> The Race for Lithium Is Becoming a Race for Water
The lithium extraction boom in California's Imperial Valley is intensifying competition for water resources in an already arid region. Lithium mining is attracting investors to Calipatria, near the Salton Sea, one of the nation's poorest areas facing deep economic disparities. The expansion creates additional environmental pressures on vulnerable communities, including dust pollution affecting nearby schools.

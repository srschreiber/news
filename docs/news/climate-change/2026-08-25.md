### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Study: Largest Carbon Polluters Responsible for Half of Western U.S. Water Loss to Climate Change
A Nature study attributes approximately half of the American West's 2014–2024 water loss—including a one-third reduction in snowpack and 13% streamflow decrease—to climate change from emissions by 122 fossil fuel companies and cement producers. The water loss equals the full capacity of Lake Mead, the nation's largest reservoir.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Climate change caused roughly 50% of American West water loss from 2014–2024</li>
<li>Snowpack decreased over one-third; streamflows fell approximately 13%</li>
<li>Water loss attributed to 122 carbon-major fossil fuel and cement companies</li>
<li>Drained water volume equivalent to full capacity of Lake Mead reservoir</li>
<li>First-of-its-kind attribution study linking specific major polluters to regional water crisis</li>
</ul>

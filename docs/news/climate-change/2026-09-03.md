### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Trump administration Roadless Rule repeal threatens drinking water for 25 million Americans
The president of the United States administration proposed fully rescinding the 2001 Roadless Rule, which protects 45 million acres of national forest land from road construction and commercial logging. Approximately 25 million Americans draw drinking water from watersheds fed by these roadless areas. A 30-day public comment period closes September 21, 2026. Critics note that roads increase stream sediment loads and that most U.S. wildfires ignite near roads rather than in roadless areas.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Repeal affects 45 million acres of protected national forest land</li>
<li>25 million Americans depend on drinking water from roadless watersheds</li>
<li>Forest roads contribute up to 90% of sediment loads in streams</li>
<li>Ignition density roughly four times higher near roads than in roadless areas</li>
<li>Most Tribes consulted oppose the repeal</li>
</ul>

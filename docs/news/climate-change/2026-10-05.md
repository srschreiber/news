### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Indonesia Orangutan Population Threatened by El Niño-Fueled Fires
Rainforest fires in Indonesia are burning through areas containing 13 of 20 largest remaining Bornean orangutan populations, representing more than half the critically endangered species globally. As of September 22, 100 wildlife were rescued including 53 orangutans; rescued animals showed severe burns. The IUCN warns that without urgent action, thousands could be killed or displaced, with El Niño conditions intensifying fire spread.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Fires threaten 13 of 20 largest Bornean orangutan populations (&gt;50% of species)</li>
<li>100 animals rescued as of September 22, including 53 orangutans</li>
<li>Severe injuries: one ~4-year-old female (Novita) burned on all four hands/feet</li>
<li>Adult male found in oil palm plantation died from smoke exposure</li>
<li>1997-98 El Niño fires: estimated one-third of global population killed</li>
<li>Current fires fueled by strong El Niño conditions</li>
<li>Females with young particularly at risk (slow movement, established ranges)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Turkey's COP31 Host Landfills Emit Some of World's Worst Methane Plumes
Turkey, hosting COP31, operates landfills producing some of the world's largest methane emissions. The Silivri landfill near Istanbul emits 7.4 tonnes of methane per hour—equivalent to 1.5 million gas-guzzling SUVs—with satellite imagery capturing the plume visible from space. Multiple super-emitting landfills stretch across the country from İzmir to Antalya.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Silivri landfill (near Istanbul): 7.4 tonnes methane per hour (among world&#x27;s worst)</li>
<li>Warming impact equivalent to 1.5 million SUVs</li>
<li>Methane plume visible from satellite imagery</li>
<li>Multiple super-emitting landfills across Turkey (İzmir, Ankara, Antalya)</li>
<li>Residents near Silivri dump report persistent odors, windows closed even in heat</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Australia Records Second-Hottest September as El Niño and Climate Change Converge
Australia recorded its second-hottest September on record in 2026, with average temperatures 2.71°C above the 1961–1990 baseline. Minimum night-time temperatures were the hottest for any September since 1910. Alpine areas experienced record heat; snow in Kosciuszko National Park fell to zero in September for the first time since monitoring began in 1954, with scientists attributing warming to climate change and strengthening El Niño.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Second-hottest September on record; 2.71°C above 1961-1990 baseline</li>
<li>Hottest September minimum temperatures since 1910 (2.52°C above average)</li>
<li>Kosciuszko National Park snow depth zero in September for first time since 1954 monitoring began</li>
<li>Every state and territory top-5 warmest Septembers (except NT ranked 6th)</li>
<li>No September below-average temperatures since 2004</li>
<li>Global heating warmed continent &gt;1.5°C since 1910 records began</li>
<li>Warming attributed to climate change plus strengthening El Niño (&#x27;seasonal creep&#x27;)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ethiopia's fifty billion seedlings reforestation initiative
Ethiopia is implementing a fifty billion seedling reforestation initiative to restore severely degraded forests after nearly a century of deforestation. Land degradation costs the nation an estimated $4.3 billion annually, while over 12% of the population faces food insecurity linked to environmental decline.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Fifty billion seedling reforestation target</li>
<li>47+ billion seedlings planted in 7 years prior to October 2026</li>
<li>Land degradation costs ~$4.3 billion annually in lost harvests and farmer income</li>
<li>15.8 million Ethiopians faced food insecurity in 2024 (&gt;12% of population)</li>
<li>100+ million Ethiopians depend on agriculture; project aims to reduce climate vulnerability</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Carbon Pollution Drives 'Massive Expansion' of European Marine Heatwaves
World Weather Attribution research shows anthropogenic carbon pollution has driven a massive expansion of marine heatwaves in European waters during summer 2026, with some regions recording temperatures 6°C above normal. Climate breakdown accounted for approximately two-thirds of observed excess heat, making record conditions virtually impossible in a preindustrial climate.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Marine heatwaves in European waters summer 2026</li>
<li>Some regions recorded 6°C above normal temperatures</li>
<li>Carbon pollution responsible for ~2-thirds of excess heat</li>
<li>Mediterranean warmed ~2°C, Bay of Biscay/Iberian ~1.4°C, Ireland/Channel ~1.3°C</li>
<li>Hottest fortnights virtually impossible in preindustrial climate (3 of 4 regions)</li>
<li>Celtic region heat frequency: once per century historically</li>
<li>Human-caused climate change attributed 2.0–3.1°C warming to Mediterranean</li>
<li>1.3–1.5°C warming in Bay of Biscay, Celtic regions</li>
<li>Climate change made some events ~30 times more likely</li>
<li>80% of western Mediterranean experienced marine heatwaves in 2026 vs. 40% predicted without human warming</li>
<li>Sea surface temperatures &gt;6°C above normal in hottest two weeks</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> London car pollution cuts accelerated lung growth in children
London's Ultra Low Emission Zone (ULEZ) accelerated lung growth in children and improved respiratory health, with nitrogen dioxide pollution falling more than twice as fast in the emission-controlled zone compared to a control city. Lung function impairment in children dropped from 14% to 9%, and roadside pollution fell 27% while pollution-linked deaths declined 40%.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Lung growth acceleration within 5 years of 2019 launch</li>
<li>NO₂ pollution fell 27%, deaths linked to toxic air fell 40%</li>
<li>Lung function impairment: 14% → 9% in London children</li>
<li>Daily fee ~$17 for older, polluting vehicles</li>
<li>Expanded to all of Greater London by 2023</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> New Zealand Opens Marine Reserves with Groundbreaking Māori Co-Management Model
New Zealand established five marine protected areas in the far southeast using an innovative co-management model partnering with Kāi Tahu tribe and the Department of Conservation through shared decision-making. The network, named Te Au Roa o Te Rakihouia, marks the first such Indigenous co-management model in New Zealand and protects seabed habitats, canyons, reefs, and kelp forests.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Five MPAs effective July 1, 2026; sixth expected soon; all no-take zones banning extractive activities</li>
<li>First co-management model pairing regional Māori tribe (Kāi Tahu) with Department of Conservation</li>
<li>Joint marine ranger team; shared decision-making authority</li>
<li>Network protects seabed habitats, offshore canyons, rocky reefs, tidal lagoons, kelp forests</li>
<li>Follows 10+ years of grassroots and governmental work; contrasts with failed 2015 Kermadec Ocean Sanctuary attempt</li>
</ul>

## Climate & Policy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> UK Food Supply Vulnerability: Heavy Reliance on Heat-Stressed Regions
The UK faces food security risks due to dependence on imports from climate-vulnerable regions. The UK imports 40% of total food supply, including 80% of fruit and 47% of vegetables—higher fruit reliance than France, Germany, or Spain—from regions increasingly threatened by climate-driven production shocks.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>UK imports 40% of total food supply, 80% of fruit, 47% of vegetables</li>
<li>Highest fruit import reliance in Europe vs France, Germany, Spain</li>
<li>Heavy dependence on Mediterranean, North Africa, South/Central America</li>
<li>UK experienced 5 heatwaves in 2026 plus drought and wildfires</li>
<li>IPCC forecasts lower yields and heat stress in import-source regions without resilience improvements</li>
</ul>

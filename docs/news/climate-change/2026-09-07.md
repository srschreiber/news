### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> French winemakers forecast 30-year low harvest after severe drought and heat
France's hottest summer on record and severe drought have slashed wine production to a 30-year low of just under 34 million hectolitres. The harvest represents a 6% drop from 2025 and 17% below the recent five-year average, with regional losses ranging from one-third in Burgundy-Beaujolais to nearly half in Champagne. Wildfires pose an additional threat of smoke taint affecting wine quality.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Heatwaves and wildfires undermine air quality improvements globally, UN warns
The World Meteorological Organization warned that extreme weather events driven by climate change are reversing air quality improvements globally. Rising heatwaves and wildfires create feedback loops: wildfire smoke and ground-level ozone from heat surge pollution, which then accelerates climate breakdown. Exposure to wildfire smoke increases asthma attacks, COPD hospitalizations, and respiratory medication use.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Wildfire smoke linked to increased asthma attacks and COPD hospitalizations</li>
<li>Rising heatwaves cause surges in ground-level ozone production</li>
<li>Climate-driven extreme weather creates pollution feedback loop worsening climate breakdown</li>
<li>Wildfire smoke carries high concentrations of fine particulate matter causing oxidative stress</li>
</ul>

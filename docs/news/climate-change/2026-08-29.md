### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Nepal flood crisis deepens as debris dams threaten additional inundation
Nepal faces a cascading disaster as debris from catastrophic Wednesday flooding has blocked rivers, creating unstable natural dams at risk of catastrophic failure that could trigger additional waves of inundation. The initial floods destroyed hydropower plants, farms, and infrastructure across multiple valleys, with 538 bodies recovered and nearly 1,000 people missing as of Friday.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>538 bodies recovered, nearly 1,000 people missing</li>
<li>Debris traveled downstream more than 60 miles</li>
<li>Initial flood destroyed hydropower plants, farms, solar facilities, roads, forests</li>
<li>Debris-blocked rivers create fragile natural dams at risk of failure</li>
<li>Pattern consistent with global warming, scientists say</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> The president of the United States administration expedites environmental review for Trans-Alaska Pipeline reauthorization
The president of the United States administration is expediting environmental review of the 800-mile Trans-Alaska Pipeline to secure 30 years of continued authorization. The Bureau of Land Management has compressed the public comment period to 15 days, bypassing standard 30–60-day procedures, as part of the administration's push to unlock Alaska energy resources.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>800-mile pipeline reauthorized for 30 years</li>
<li>Public comment period shortened to 15 days vs. standard 30–60 days</li>

<li>Tied to administration&#x27;s plan to develop National Petroleum Reserve and Arctic National Wildlife Refuge</li>

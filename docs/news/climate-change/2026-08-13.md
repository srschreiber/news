### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI use by oil and gas companies far outweighs climate benefits of AI data centers
A peer-reviewed Nature study by former Microsoft researchers Will and Holly Alpine found that AI tools accelerating oil and gas exploration increase global emissions 0.47–1.8 gigatonnes CO₂ annually—1.2–4.8% of 2024 energy-related emissions and up to 13 times the emissions from operating AI data centers. AI has made fossil fuel production faster, cheaper, and more profitable, expanding output that otherwise would not occur.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Enabled emissions: 0.47–1.8 gigatonnes CO₂ annually from AI-accelerated oil and gas</li>
<li>Represents 1.2–4.8% of global energy-related emissions (2024)</li>
<li>Up to 13 times greater than AI data center emissions (IEA 2025 estimate)</li>
<li>Published in Nature by Will and Holly Alpine</li>
<li>AI expands fossil fuel production beyond what would naturally occur</li>
</ul>

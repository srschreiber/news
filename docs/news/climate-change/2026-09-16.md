### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> UK Climate Change Committee warns Heathrow expansion could push aviation to 80% of emissions by 2050
The UK's Climate Change Committee warns aviation could account for 80% of the country's carbon dioxide emissions by 2050 if current trends continue. Heathrow expansion would add 2.4 megatonnes of CO2 by 2050, representing approximately 5% of all UK emissions. The CCC calls for revised aviation policy by 2027 based on a 'polluter pays' principle.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Aviation emissions projected to reach 80% of UK&#x27;s total CO2 by 2050 without intervention</li>
<li>Heathrow expansion would add 2.4 MtCO2 by 2050, or 4.5 MtCO2 by 2054</li>
<li>Without expansion, aviation emissions projected at 38 MtCO2 in 2050</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> China's electric grid is world's largest emitter of potent greenhouse gas sulfur hexafluoride
China's electric grid is the world's largest emitter of sulfur hexafluoride (SF6), a potent synthetic greenhouse gas used as an electrical insulator. Despite leading global renewable energy expansion, Chinese utilities release vast quantities of SF6 through end-of-life equipment venting rather than recycling.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>SF6 is 24,300 times more effective than CO2 at warming planet per pound</li>
<li>Persists in atmosphere for 1,000 years</li>
<li>Used as insulator in high-voltage equipment like circuit breakers</li>
<li>Emissions primarily from end-of-life equipment venting</li>
<li>Recycling could significantly reduce emissions at little cost</li>
</ul>

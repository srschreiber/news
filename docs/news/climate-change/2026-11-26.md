### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Brazil's Lula moves to formally recognize Indigenous Kapôt Nhĩnore land
Brazil's President Lula announced an ordinance recognizing permanent possession rights of the Mẽbêngôkre (Kayapó) and Yudjá (Juruna) peoples over the 362,000-hectare Kapôt Nhĩnore territory. Full recognition requires border demarcation, presidential decree, and registration; the territory faces pressure from 259 registered farms, ranches, and soy operations.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>362,000 hectares (895,000 acres) in Pará and Mato Grosso states</li>
<li>Mẽbêngôkre and Yudjá fighting for rights since 2004; announced November 19, 2026</li>
<li>83% retains original forest; deforestation surged from 4,178 ha (2000) to 58,000 ha (2026)</li>
<li>259 registered farms/ranches overlap 95.6% of territory; 8 sell to JBS, 14 grow soy</li>
</ul>

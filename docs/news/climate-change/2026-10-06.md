### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Indigenous knowledge and science restore kelp forests off Canada's coast
A partnership in Clayoquot Sound has planted more than 4,200 square meters of new kelp habitat as part of the largest kelp restoration project on North America's West Coast. The initiative combines scientific methods with Indigenous knowledge and sea otter reintroduction to restore British Columbia's marine ecosystems.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>4,200+ square meters of kelp habitat planted (45,200 sq ft)</li>

<li>Project cost ~$250,000 CAD, funded by provincial government and private organizations</li>
<li>Kelp forests globally lose ~11,542 sq km annually</li>
<li>Sea otter reintroduction controls sea urchin populations, enabling kelp recovery</li>
</ul>

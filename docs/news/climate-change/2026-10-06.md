<li>287,000 Indigenous citizens eligible to vote (84% increase from 2024 election)</li>
<li>Amazon deforestation fell to lowest level in over a decade since August 2025 under Lula</li>
<li>High-deforestation municipalities contain Indigenous territories facing invasions by land grabbers, loggers, illegal miners</li>

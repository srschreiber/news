### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Water Levels in World's Rivers and Lakes Plummeted During Two Hottest Years on Record
Extreme global heat in 2023–2024 triggered unprecedented declines in surface water worldwide. River flows fell 5.7% below normal while lakes and reservoirs dropped 9.5%, marking the largest synchronized decline since 1991. South America was hit hardest, with river flows down 17.8% and reservoir water down 16.5%.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Global river flows fell 5.7% below normal in 2023–2024</li>
<li>Lakes and reservoirs dropped 9.5% combined</li>
<li>South America: river flows down 17.8%, reservoirs down 16.5%</li>
<li>Study tracked 150 major river basins and 1.2 million lakes/reservoirs since 1991</li>
<li>Largest synchronized decline in surface water since at least 1991</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Australia faces elevated bushfire and heatwave risk this summer
Australian fire authorities warned of elevated bushfire risk across southern and central Australia for the 2026–27 summer due to high fuel loads, dry soil, and hot weather forecasts. Last summer saw over 440,000 hectares burn in Victoria alone, exceeding the 2009 Black Saturday fires.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Most of Victoria, eastern Tasmania, South Australia, Western Australia, NSW, NT face heightened risk</li>
<li>High fuel loads, low soil moisture, hot and dry conditions driving risk</li>
<li>Victoria burned 440,000+ hectares in 2025–26 (larger than 2009 Black Saturday fires)</li>

<li>Heatwaves and extreme temperatures expected nationwide</li>
</ul>

## Conservation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Community forest conservation initiative in Democratic Republic of Congo
The Democratic Republic of Congo awarded community forestry land titles covering 7.1 million hectares—an area the size of Ireland—to protect biodiversity while recognizing customary rights. The initiative has grown from eight titles in 2017 to over 350 by 2026, though implementation faces profitability and coordination challenges.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>7.1 million hectares (17.5 million acres) awarded in community forestry titles</li>
<li>Titles grew from 8 in 2017 to 350+ by 2026, with 236+ having legal status</li>
<li>Community income primarily from cacao, coffee cultivation, and sustainable fishing</li>
<li>Challenges: lack of conservation profitability, poor coordination, insufficient service synergy</li>
<li>Without economic incentives, some communities resort to illegal forest exploitation</li>
</ul>

## Energy Policy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Senate passes bill to accelerate clean energy permitting, raising fossil fuel concerns
The U.S. Senate passed the Bipartisan American Affordability and Jobs Act on November 19 with a 62–33 vote, accelerating energy infrastructure permitting by weakening environmental reviews. Environmental groups and 120+ scientists opposed it, warning that technology-neutral fast-tracking benefits fossil fuel projects alongside renewables.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Senate passed BAAJA on November 19 with 62–33 vote including Democratic support</li>
<li>Reduces NEPA review requirements, shortens assessment timeframes and legal challenge windows</li>
<li>Proponents claim 1.5 gigaton emissions reduction through 2040</li>
<li>Weakens protections for endangered species and ocean/wildlife habitats</li>
<li>Enables faster permitting for oil, gas, mining on public and tribal lands</li>
</ul>

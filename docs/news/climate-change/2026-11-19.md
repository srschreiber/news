### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Central Amazon Region Experiences Alarming Temperature Increase
Central-northern Amazonia experienced alarming temperature increases despite being outside deforestation zones, with extreme temperatures rising 0.75°C per decade over 43 years—totaling 3.2°C (5.8°F). Causes remain unclear but may involve ocean current changes and El Niño; impacts included animal mortality during 2023 fire season and record low water levels in 2024.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Extreme temperatures in central-northern region rose 0.75°C per decade</li>
<li>Total increase of 3.2°C (5.8°F) over 43 years (1981–2023)</li>
<li>10% of Amazon biome experienced extreme temperatures</li>
<li>Rio Negro water level hit lowest point since 1902 during 2024 drought</li>
<li>Causes unclear; global phenomena such as ocean currents and El Niño suspected</li>
</ul>

Half of shark catches at an Indonesian port comprise threatened or critically endangered species, with the five most-caught species all classified as vulnerable or worse. ### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Lake Victoria faces ecological collapse from contamination and overfishing
Lake Victoria faces ecological collapse from overfishing and pollution, with Kenya's fish catch plummeting 25% from 2021 to 2025 and processing plants declining from 21 to six. The crisis affects 45 million people across three nations; climate-driven record water levels in 2020 and 2024 displaced hundreds of thousands, with El Niño expected to worsen flooding into 2027.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Kenya fish catch fell from 94,349 metric tons (2021) to 70,231 (2025)—25% decline</li>
<li>Uganda processing plants dropped from 21 to 6</li>
<li>Affects over 45 million people across Kenya, Uganda, and Tanzania</li>
<li>El Niño expected to persist into 2027, increasing flood risk</li>
<li>Over 100 civil society groups from 35 countries proposed UNESCO recognition (July 2026)</li>
</ul>

## Developmental Biology

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> DNA Typewriter records complete cell lineage history from mouse zygote to late organogenesis
Researchers used DNA Typewriter, a lineage-tracing technology, to record the complete developmental history of a mouse embryo from fertilized egg through late organogenesis. The system traced approximately 1.3 million cells over nearly two weeks of development, marking the first successful complete cellular lineage mapping in a mammal.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Traced ~1.3 million cells over nearly 2 weeks of development</li>
<li>10 embryos examined from 100 fertilized eggs; one most informative</li>
<li>First complete cellular lineage mapping in mammal</li>
<li>Results published in Science and Cell journals</li>
<li>Only simpler organisms like C. elegans previously achieved this</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Nepal's Tiger Population Surges to 429 With New National Parks Driving Recovery
Nepal's wild tiger population has surged to 429 individuals as of August 2026, up from 355 in 2022—a 16-fold increase since 2010. Growth concentrates in newer national parks (Banke, Parsa, Shuklaphanta) driven by habitat conservation and corridor efforts, while older parks like Bardiya saw declines despite rising prey density, raising concerns about tiger-human and human-leopard conflict.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Extreme Heat Poses Greater Risk to Adults Over 60 Than Previously Understood
Stanford-led research in The Lancet Public Health shows older adults face dangerous heat stress at just 1.5°C above preindustrial levels, far lower than previous estimates. Adults over 60 sweat less, have less efficient cardiovascular cooling, and face mounting risk as the 60+ population doubles to 2.1 billion by 2050.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Adults 60+ at heat stress risk at 1.5°C above preindustrial baseline</li>
<li>Previous estimates applied younger-age thresholds to all populations</li>
<li>Older adults sweat less and have reduced blood vessel flexibility</li>
<li>Global 60+ population projected to double to 2.1 billion by 2050</li>
</ul>

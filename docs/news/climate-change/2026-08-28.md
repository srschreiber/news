### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Costa Rica Mandates Wildlife Crossings for Roads to Protect Fauna
Costa Rica unanimously approved a mandate requiring wildlife crossings on new and existing roads that interrupt animal migration corridors. The regulation allows citizens to request crossings on existing roads with scientific evidence; at least 59 species including monkeys, sloths, and crabs have documented benefits from such infrastructure.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Approved August 2026 with unanimous vote</li>

<li>At least 59 documented species using wildlife bridges</li>

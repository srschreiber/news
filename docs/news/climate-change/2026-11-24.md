### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> COP31 becomes climate diplomacy's 'darkest moment'
COP31 in Antalya, Turkey, concluded on November 23, 2026, in diplomatic deadlock after negotiations ran nearly 50 hours over. More than 90 nations rejected the draft cover decision for omitting 'fossil fuels' and failing to address climate finance. It was the first COP failure over a cover decision since COP15 in 2009.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Planet set to permanently exceed 1.5°C warming limit by 2030
Human-caused global warming reached 1.37°C above pre-industrial levels in 2025 and is rising at 0.27°C per decade—the fastest rate on record. At current emission rates, the world will permanently exceed the Paris Agreement's 1.5°C warming limit around 2030, in approximately four years.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Current warming: 1.37°C above pre-industrial (2025)</li>
<li>Warming rate: 0.27°C per decade (fastest on record)</li>
<li>Will permanently breach 1.5°C limit around 2030</li>
<li>Carbon budget for 50% chance at 1.5°C: 130 billion tonnes CO2, exhausted in ~3 years</li>
<li>2024 emissions: 56.8 billion tonnes CO2-equivalent; 73% from fossil fuels</li>
<li>Atmospheric CO2: 425.6 ppm in 2025, over 50% above pre-industrial levels</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> South Africa's Constitutional Court blocks Shell oil exploration off Wild Coast
South Africa's Constitutional Court ruled on August 14 to cancel Shell and Impact Africa's fossil fuel exploration rights off the Wild Coast in the Eastern Cape. The court found authorities failed to meaningfully consult affected communities about impacts to their cultural rights and livelihoods, and cited the 2025 International Court of Justice climate opinion requiring heightened diligence in decisions affecting emissions and future generations.

The International Energy Agency reports global methane emissions show no sign of decline and identifies a significant implementation gap in current policies. Only one-third of 100+ identified methane policies across 32 jurisdictions are mandatory, with two-thirds showing little implementation evidence.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Methane has 80 times warming effect of CO₂ over 20-year period</li>
<li>Over 100 methane policies identified across 32 jurisdictions; &lt;1/3 are mandatory</li>
<li>Two-thirds of methane policies show little implementation sign or sanctions for non-compliance</li>
<li>India and Indonesia together account for 12%+ of global methane emissions but lack policies</li>
<li>Japan leader in methane policy; reduced emissions ~40% between 1990 and 2022</li>
</ul>

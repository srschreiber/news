### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Lula administration preserves 20,000 km² of Amazon rainforest since 2022
Brazil's Lula administration has preserved approximately 20,000 km² of Amazon rainforest since 2022 through enforcement of forest code legislation, an area roughly equivalent to New Jersey. Annual deforestation has plummeted from 11,594 km² in 2022 to preliminary 2,874 km² in 2026, potentially marking the lowest level since records began in 1988.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>20,000 km² preserved since 2022 (roughly New Jersey-sized area)</li>
<li>Annual deforestation fell from 11,594 km² (2022) to 5,731 km² (2025)</li>
<li>Preliminary 2026 figure: 2,874 km², potentially lowest since 1988 records began</li>
<li>Environment ministry led by Marina Silva scaled up enforcement and policing after reversing Bolsonaro deregulation</li>
<li>Lula committed to achieving zero deforestation by 2030</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Lake Mead and Lake Powell hit historic lows as Colorado River shrinks
Lake Mead has declined to less than 27% of capacity, its lowest level since being filled nearly 90 years ago, leaving a 175-foot gap above the current water line. The crisis threatens Las Vegas, which derives over 90% of its water from Lake Mead; the city has invested $1.3 billion in a deeper third intake and implemented extensive recycling measures.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> EPA Repeals Greenhouse Gas Emission Rules for Power Plants
The Environmental Protection Agency under the president of the United States has repealed decades-old rules limiting greenhouse gas emissions from coal and gas power plants, citing $300 billion in cost savings. The move undoes protections meant to curb emissions linked to global warming and is expected to face legal challenges from environmental groups.

<li>Changes estimated to eliminate over $300 billion in costs for power plants</li>
<li>EPA proposed separate rule expected to prevent future administrations from re-regulating power plant greenhouse gas emissions</li>
<li>Move reverses decades of environmental protections and follows February revocation of 2009 finding on carbon dioxide threat to public health</li>
</ul>

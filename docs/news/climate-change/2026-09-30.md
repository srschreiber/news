The 2026 Atlantic hurricane season could end with zero hurricanes for the first time in over 100 years, as Super El Niño conditions suppressed storm formation through westerly winds, sinking air, and dry conditions despite record-warm ocean temperatures.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>No Atlantic hurricanes recorded August–September 2026</li>
<li>May conclude with zero hurricanes first time since 1914 (or 1907)</li>
<li>Super El Niño driving westerly winds that prevent hurricane formation</li>
<li>Atlantic sea surface temperatures second-warmest on record</li>
<li>Sinking air and dry conditions suppressed development</li>
<li>Reliable satellite data only available since 1966</li>

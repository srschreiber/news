A peer-reviewed study found that AI-driven productivity gains in fossil fuel extraction generate more emissions than AI applications in renewables offset, undercutting AI's climate benefits.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Net emissions rose 0.47-1.8 gigatonnes CO2/year across 64 scenarios, ~1-5% of sector emissions</li>
<li>First study to quantify AI&#x27;s climate impact across the full power sector</li>
<li>Renewable AI productivity gains would need to outpace fossil fuel gains by at least 4x to break even</li>
<li>AI projected to create nearly £370 billion in value for fossil fuel companies 2026-2030</li>
<li>Identifies &#x27;enabled emissions&#x27; from productivity gains as a previously unquantified driver</li>
</ul>

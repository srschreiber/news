### <span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> Sydney suburb Marsden Park exemplifies climate adaptation failures in urban planning
A Sydney Morning Herald investigation documented how Marsden Park, a rapidly growing western Sydney suburb developed since 2013, exhibits poor climate adaptation design—dark roofs, black asphalt roads, minimal tree coverage, and small yards—that amplifies urban heat island effects despite being in an area already experiencing extreme heat and flood risk.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Marsden Park population reached 15,000 by 2021 census and is growing rapidly</li>
<li>Western Sydney has recorded the hottest temperatures on Earth during summer heatwaves</li>
<li>Design choices include dark roofs, black roads, minimal greenery, and houses built to boundary lines</li>
<li>NSW Treasury projections exist for 2°C global warming impacts on the state</li>
<li>NSW Planning Minister Paul Scully acknowledged climate adaptation as urgent parallel task to emissions reduction</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-2" title="Importance 2/5" aria-label="Importance 2 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i><i></i></span> Turkmenistan launches UNDP-supported methane emissions reduction training for oil and gas sector
Turkmenistan held a three-day training programme on March 25, 2026, organized by the UN Development Programme to build technical capacity for methane emissions reduction in its oil and gas sector, advancing the country's Global Methane Pledge commitment to cut methane emissions by 30% by 2030.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Training conducted March 25, 2026 in Ashgabat for sectoral ministries and agencies</li>
<li>Turkmenistan committed to 30% methane emissions reduction by 2030 under Global Methane Pledge (joined December 2023)</li>
<li>Methane has warming impact 80+ times that of CO2 over relevant timescales</li>
<li>Programme supports updated Nationally Determined Contribution (NDC 3.0) under Paris Agreement</li>
<li>Focus on measurement, reporting, verification (MRV) systems and alignment with global best practices</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Scientists Deploy Sunflower Sea Stars to Restore California's Kelp Forests
Scientists are reintroducing captive-bred sunflower sea stars (critically endangered with up to 24 arms) into California's kelp forests, where approximately 95% of ecosystems have been decimated over the past decade. Off Monterey, researchers place the creatures in open-door cages to naturally resume their ecosystem role and help restore carbon-sequestering forests.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>95% of Pacific coast kelp forests decimated over past decade</li>
<li>Sunflower sea stars can have up to 24 arms; species critically endangered</li>
<li>Kelp grows up to 2 feet per day, reaching heights taller than 10-story buildings</li>
<li>Kelp forests sequester approximately 200 million tons of CO2 annually worldwide</li>
<li>Restoration effort is first phase of reintroduction project near Monterey</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Indonesia Reverses Course on Massive Deforestation Project for Agriculture
Indonesia announced it is reversing plans to convert 10 million acres of tropical rainforest in New Guinea and Borneo into rice and sugarcane plantations, citing achievement of rice self-sufficiency three years ahead of schedule. However, a Forest Ministry spokesperson cautioned that implementation remains uncertain and government commitment to stopping activities in both regions cannot yet be confirmed.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Indonesia planned to convert 7 million acres in New Guinea and 3 million acres in Borneo</li>
<li>Rice self-sufficiency achieved three years ahead of schedule</li>
<li>Government stated future production will use degraded land instead of forest</li>
<li>Forest Ministry spokesperson acknowledged implementation uncertainty and cannot confirm timeline for halting activities</li>
<li>Indonesia&#x27;s forest loss hit eight-year high last year amid conversion project</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Coastal erosion claims 183 homes along England's east coast in 16 years
A Guardian investigation reveals coastal erosion and landslides destroyed at least 183 homes along England's east coast over 16 years, with councils demolishing vulnerable properties at a total cost of £1.1 million. East Riding of Yorkshire accounts for 66 demolished homes; Great Yarmouth for 64 (41 since December 2025). Scientists confirm climate crisis is accelerating shore retreat.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>183 homes demolished across nine councils over 16 years</li>
<li>East Riding of Yorkshire demolished 66 homes (highest)</li>
<li>Great Yarmouth demolished 64 homes; 41 demolitions occurred since December 2025</li>
<li>North Norfolk proactively demolished 21 homes</li>
<li>Demolition costs £1.1 million total; Environment Agency contributed ~£482,000</li>
<li>Hemsby experienced 50% of predicted 30-year erosion in single winter; 10+ metres lost in 12 months</li>
</ul>

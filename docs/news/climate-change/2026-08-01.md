Climate change is driving demographic shifts in Florida as flood risk increasingly factors into homeownership decisions and affordability. High-flood-risk counties lost 63,357 net residents from mid-2024 to mid-2025, while low-risk counties gained 70,000, marking the largest such movement since 2018 as insurance premiums and maintenance costs surge.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>High-flood-risk US counties lost 63,357 net residents from mid-2024 to mid-2025</li>

<li>Florida paid 125,090 flood insurance claims from 2020–2025; Louisiana paid 20,774 claims</li>
<li>Rising insurance premiums and maintenance costs are now directly linked to affordability in flood-prone regions</li>
</ul>

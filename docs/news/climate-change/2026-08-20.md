### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Global methane emissions remain on upward trajectory; International Energy Agency signals policy failure
The International Energy Agency reported that global methane emissions show no sign of declining, revealing widespread policy implementation failures. Methane is 80 times more potent than carbon dioxide over 20 years, yet over two-thirds of identified methane policies lack meaningful enforcement.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Methane 80x more warming than CO₂ over 20 years</li>
<li>Over 100 methane policies identified across 32 jurisdictions, fewer than one-third mandatory</li>
<li>Four countries (India, Indonesia, Thailand, Tanzania) lack identifiable methane policies</li>
<li>India and Indonesia together account for 12%+ of global emissions</li>
<li>Two-thirds of policies show little implementation evidence</li>
<li>Japan&#x27;s 1998 law with mandatory disclosure cut methane emissions ~40% by 2022</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Trump administration advances policy to open national forests to development
The president of the United States administration proposed rescinding the 25-year-old Roadless Rule to permit development on nearly 45 million acres of national forest land. The USDA framed the change as improving forest management and reducing wildfire risk, though leading forest scientists found wildfire ignitions lowest in roadless areas and 85% of wildfires are human-caused. The proposal removes protections for wildlife habitat and draws support from states like Montana.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Roadless Rule protects nearly 45 million acres nationally; 6 million in Montana, 10 million in Greater Yellowstone</li>
<li>Proposed rescission announced August 19, 2026 by Secretary Brooke Rollins</li>
<li>30+ forest scientists found wildfire ignition density lowest in roadless and wilderness areas</li>
<li>Nearly 85% of wildfires human-caused</li>
<li>Loss of habitat protections for elk, mule deer, trout, and grizzly bears</li>
</ul>

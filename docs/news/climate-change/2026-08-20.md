### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Trump administration advances policy to open national forests to development
The president of the United States administration proposed rescinding the 25-year-old Roadless Rule to permit development on nearly 45 million acres of national forest land. The USDA framed the change as improving forest management and reducing wildfire risk, though leading forest scientists found wildfire ignitions lowest in roadless areas and 85% of wildfires are human-caused. The proposal removes protections for wildlife habitat and draws support from states like Montana.

<li>Proposed rescission announced August 19, 2026 by Secretary Brooke Rollins</li>
<li>30+ forest scientists found wildfire ignition density lowest in roadless and wilderness areas</li>
<li>Nearly 85% of wildfires human-caused</li>
<li>Loss of habitat protections for elk, mule deer, trout, and grizzly bears</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> EPA to Repeal Greenhouse Gas Endangerment Finding
The EPA is repealing the 2009 endangerment finding that established greenhouse gases threaten public health and welfare, eliminating the legal foundation for federal climate regulations. The president of the United States and EPA Administrator Lee Zeldin will formalize the rescission on Thursday, February 10, 2026. The repeal removes EPA authority to set emissions standards for vehicles and power plants and require emissions reporting from fossil fuel companies.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Endangerment finding established under Clean Air Act in 2009 being rescinded</li>
<li>Rescission formalized Thursday, February 10, 2026</li>
<li>White House estimates $1.3 trillion in regulatory cost savings</li>
<li>EPA authority removed for car/truck emissions standards and fossil fuel emissions reporting</li>
<li>Environmental groups preparing legal challenges; full rule text not yet released</li>
</ul>

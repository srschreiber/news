### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Methane Emissions Acceleration Requires Rapid Mitigation Strategy
Scientists and the UN Environment Programme deployed the Methane Alert and Response System (MARS) using AI to identify major methane emissions and rapidly notify governments and companies. Since becoming operational in 2024, MARS has contributed to 40+ mitigation actions, identifying 1.2 million tonnes of methane emissions and delivering climate benefits equivalent to removing 24 million gasoline-powered cars annually.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>MARS operational since 2024; contributed to 40+ methane mitigation actions worldwide</li>
<li>AI-assisted workflows identified 80–85% of confirmed methane detections before expert review</li>
<li>Mitigation efforts: 1.2 million tonnes of methane, climate benefit of ~24 million cars&#x27; annual emissions removed</li>
<li>MARS processes 12–15× more satellite data (30+ satellites) than humans while maintaining rigor</li>
<li>IPCC calls for 33% global methane reduction by 2030 to avoid climate tipping points</li>
</ul>

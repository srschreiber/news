Global mangrove forest cover increased by 477 square kilometers between 1985 and 2025, representing a 0.3% net gain over four decades and reversing sustained historical losses. Data from the Global Mangrove Watch analyzed 108 countries using satellite imagery and machine learning.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Net gain of 477 km² (184 sq mi) mangrove forest 1985–2025; 0.3% increase over 40 years</li>
<li>India led expansion with 1,180 km²; Pakistan, Australia, Philippines, Senegal also gained</li>
<li>Indonesia recorded largest losses at 2,043 km²; Myanmar 1,458 km², Malaysia 390 km²</li>
<li>Drivers of loss: agriculture, aquaculture, coastal development, pollution</li>
<li>Gains attributed to community reforestation, legal protections, corporate accountability improvements</li>

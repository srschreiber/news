## Climate Science

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> UN report: world lacks realistic pathway to meet 1.5°C warming limit
A September 2, 2026 UN Environment Programme report concluded the world has no realistic pathway to meet the 1.5°C warming limit set by the Paris Agreement. Current emissions of 40 billion tonnes yearly will exhaust the remaining 130-billion-tonne carbon budget in roughly three years.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Remaining CO2 budget from 2026 onward: 130 billion tonnes</li>
<li>Current emissions: ~40 billion tonnes per year</li>
<li>Exceeding 1.5°C now characterized as &quot;unavoidable&quot; by IPCC lead author</li>
<li>Human-induced warming: nearly 1.4°C, rising ~0.25°C per decade</li>
<li>World headed for 2.6°C warming by century&#x27;s end under current policies</li>
<li>Three-in-four probability 2026–2030 mean will breach 1.5°C target</li>
</ul>

## Climate Policy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Trump administration moves to eliminate greenhouse gas emissions rule for power plants
The Environmental Protection Agency announced repeal of Biden-era carbon dioxide emissions limits for coal and natural gas power plants, eliminating rules expected to prevent thousands of premature deaths annually. Power plants are the second-largest source of U.S. greenhouse gas emissions behind transportation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>EPA rescinding all remaining greenhouse gas emissions standards for power plants</li>
<li>Biden-era rules expected to prevent thousands of premature deaths annually</li>
<li>Power plants: second-largest U.S. source of CO2 emissions</li>
<li>Rollback disproportionately burdens Black communities near power plants</li>
<li>Described as largest deregulatory action in U.S. power sector history</li>
</ul>

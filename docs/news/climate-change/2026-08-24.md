### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> World's Oceans Reach Record Heat Temperature in August
The world's oceans reached an all-time record average surface temperature of 21.1°C on August 24, 2026, an unprecedented August peak driven by long-term human-caused warming and an intensifying El Niño. The warming is accelerating marine heatwave frequency and exacerbating sea level rise and ecosystem impacts.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Record average ocean surface temperature: 21.1°C (August 24, 2026)</li>
<li>August peak unprecedented; typically peaks March–April after austral summer</li>
<li>Measurement excludes polar regions</li>
<li>Marine heatwave days: more than triple frequency since early 1990s</li>
<li>90%+ of heat trapped by atmospheric CO₂ enters oceans</li>
</ul>

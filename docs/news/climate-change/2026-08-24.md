Thirty-seven states and local governments, including New York City and Los Angeles, sued in federal appeals court to challenge the president of the United States' reversal of the 2009 endangerment finding—the legal foundation for federal climate regulations. The lawsuit argues the repeal violated the Clean Air Act and removed foundational protections for federal climate policy.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>President called the reversal a victory against the &#x27;Green New Scam&#x27; last month</li>
<li>EPA claimed finding does not provide authority to set vehicle emission standards for climate purposes</li>

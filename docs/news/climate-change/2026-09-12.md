The U.S. Forest Service formally proposed rescinding the 2001 Roadless Rule on August 18, 2026, directly threatening Michigan's Norwich Plains and other wilderness areas. ### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Large-Scale Invasion Threatens Brazil's Ituna/Itatá Indigenous Land With Fire and Illegal Clearing
Over 500 invaders have occupied Brazil's Ituna/Itatá Indigenous Land in recent weeks, setting fires and cutting over 30 kilometers of illegal roads while threatening the isolated Igarapé Ipiaçava people. A federal court ordered deployment of security forces within 48 hours on August 26; as of September 11 they had not arrived, and prosecutors requested a 50,000-real daily fine against the government.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Formal repeal of federal Roadless Rule threatens Midwest wilderness conservation
The U.S. Forest Service formally proposed rescinding the 2001 Roadless Rule on August 18, 2026, directly threatening Michigan's Norwich Plains and other wilderness areas. The 59-million-acre protection currently shields endangered eastern gray wolves and American martens, along with beaver ponds and trout streams in the Upper Peninsula.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Large-Scale Invasion Threatens Brazil's Ituna/Itatá Indigenous Land With Fire and Illegal Clearing
Over 500 invaders have occupied Brazil's Ituna/Itatá Indigenous Land in recent weeks, setting fires and cutting over 30 kilometers of illegal roads while threatening the isolated Igarapé Ipiaçava people. A federal court ordered deployment of security forces within 48 hours on August 26; as of September 11 they had not arrived, and prosecutors requested a 50,000-real daily fine against the government.

<li>500+ invaders occupied federally protected Indigenous land in Pará state</li>
<li>Over 30 km (18.6 miles) of forest trails and clandestine roads cleared with heavy machinery</li>
<li>Overlapping state APA Bacajaí protection zone weaponized by land-grabbers for cattle ranching</li>

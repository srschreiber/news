### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Global methane emissions show no sign of declining
The International Energy Agency reports global methane emissions show no sign of declining, with an implementation gap in existing policies. Of 100+ methane policies identified across 37 jurisdictions, fewer than one-third are mandatory, and over two-thirds show little sign of implementation with limited enforcement mechanisms.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>1 tonne methane causes 80× more warming than 1 tonne CO2 over 20 years</li>

<li>Fewer than one-third of 100+ identified policies are mandatory; two-thirds show limited implementation</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump administration prepares to dismantle Biden's climate agenda
The president of the United States is moving to eliminate Biden's 2024 carbon emissions rule for power plants through EPA repeal this week. The rule had required electric utilities to retire coal plants or install carbon capture technology; its elimination removes all federal regulations on electricity sector emissions. EPA Chief Lee Zeldin cited uncertain climate science and potential consumer savings, though experts note coal phase-out is the single largest lever for slowing global warming.

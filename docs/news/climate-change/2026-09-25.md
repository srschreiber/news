### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> First new cat species in over 100 years discovered in Bolivia's cloud forests
Scientists identified the tilcayo (Leopardus tilcayo), a tiger cat species from Bolivia's Yungas cloud forests weighing 1.4 kilograms. This is the first new living cat species described in over 100 years—the previous discovery was in 1923. Genetic analysis shows the tilcayo diverged from other tiger cats roughly 1.4 million years ago, and research revealed that the previously single species Leopardus tigrinus actually comprises at least four distinct species.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First new cat species in 100+ years</li>
<li>Tilcayo weighs 1.4 kg (3 pounds), smaller than house cat</li>
<li>Diverged from other tiger cats ~1.4 million years ago</li>
<li>Leopardus tigrinus reclassified as at least four distinct species</li>
<li>Discovery came from rescued pet named Tigrino brought to sanctuary in 2017</li>
</ul>

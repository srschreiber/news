### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Worst Bluetongue Outbreak on Record Hits British Farms
British livestock farms experienced the worst bluetongue outbreak on record in 2026-27, with 2,201 cases recorded by early October—exceeding the previous three years combined. The viral disease, serotype 3, is transmitted by biting midges and spread north to Aberdeenshire; climate change is cited as a key factor.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>2,201 bluetongue cases by early October 2026, including 1,103 in September</li>
<li>Cases exceed combined total of previous three seasons</li>
<li>Serotype 3 (BTV-3) originated in Africa, first detected in England in 2023</li>
<li>Spread as far north as Aberdeenshire; affects cattle with fever, ulcers, lameness</li>
<li>Vaccine shortage due to high demand and supply constraints; climate change cited as transmission factor</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Satellite data reveals massive scale of routine ocean oil pollution
Satellite analysis by Skytruth revealed that oil slicks from ships and offshore platforms cover nearly 1.6 million square kilometers of ocean annually—an area larger than Mongolia. Vessels account for 81% of slick area, primarily cargo ships and tankers, with most discharges illegal under international maritime pollution treaty.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>1.6 million square km of ocean annually covered by oil slicks (618,000 sq mi)—larger than Mongolia</li>
<li>Vessels responsible for 81% of slick area; offshore platforms 18%</li>
<li>Most discharges violate MARPOL treaty (max 15 ppm oil in bilge water)</li>
<li>823 verified slicks in Indonesian waters, 214 in Malaysia</li>
<li>Study analyzed 2023-2025 satellite imagery and vessel tracking data</li>
</ul>

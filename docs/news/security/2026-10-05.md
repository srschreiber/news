### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Denmark's CPR Database Breach Exposes 8.8 Million Citizens' Personal Data
Denmark's Central Person Register (CPR) suffered a major breach on October 5 exposing personal data of approximately 8.8 million people—roughly 80% of the 11 million records. Attackers exploited a domestic company's legitimate access credentials to conduct large-scale automated searches identifying valid CPR numbers, compromising names, addresses, and 10-digit identifiers used for healthcare, banking, and government services.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>8.8 million people affected (~80% of 11M total CPR records)</li>
<li>Breach occurred in September; detected October 3</li>
<li>Data exposed: names, addresses, CPR numbers (10-digit identifiers like SSN)</li>
<li>Attackers exploited legitimate access credentials of Danish company</li>
<li>Large-scale automated searches to identify valid CPR numbers</li>
<li>Most significant CPR breach since 2015 (5M+ records on unencrypted CDs)</li>
<li>Extended security hotline: 8am-midnight</li>
<li>No suspects identified as of October 5</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Citrix NetScaler Zero-Day Exploited in Targeted SAML Attacks
Citrix released patches for CVE-2026-88779, a high-severity memory overflow vulnerability (CVSS 8.7) in NetScaler ADC and NetScaler Gateway being actively exploited in targeted zero-day attacks. Exploitation requires SAML service provider or identity provider configuration and can cause denial-of-service; CISA ordered federal agencies to patch by October 7.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2026-88779: memory overflow vulnerability, CVSS score 8.7/10</li>
<li>Active exploitation in targeted zero-day attacks (discovered by Bishop Fox, watchTowr)</li>
<li>Requires SAML SP or IdP configuration to exploit</li>
<li>Affected versions: 14.1 prior to 14.1-73.41; 13.1 prior to 13.1-64.28</li>
<li>Patches released; CISA deadline for federal agencies: October 7, 2026</li>
<li>No customer data integrity impact identified; DoS risk under specific conditions</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Rejetto HFS servers targeted for critical RCE vulnerability
Hackers are actively scanning for CVE-2026-61500, a critical weak signing key vulnerability in Rejetto HFS that enables session forgery, account takeover, and remote code execution.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2026-61500: critical vulnerability, CVSS 9.3, affects all versions before 3.2.1</li>
<li>Weak RNG (xorshift128+) outputs mathematically reversible by attackers</li>
<li>Enables session cookie forgery and remote code execution via server_code</li>
<li>Discovered June 2026 using Anthropic&#x27;s Mythos AI for mathematical analysis</li>
<li>Rejetto released patch version 3.2.1 on July 13, 2026</li>
<li>Active exploitation in wild as of October 2, 2026 from China Telecom IPs</li>
<li>Targeting canaries in Japan and US</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Dell System Update vulnerability allows root privilege escalation
Dell disclosed six critical vulnerabilities in its Container Storage Modules affecting authentication and privilege management, with CVSS scores ranging from 9.6 to 10.0. The flaws enable unauthenticated attackers to bypass security entirely, obtain administrator credentials, and escalate to root-level access across storage infrastructure and Kubernetes clusters.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Two CVSS 10.0 vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-63688, CVE-2026-63692)</li>
<li>CVE-2026-63688: missing authentication in gRPC server, affects all five Dell storage product families</li>
<li>CVE-2026-67269: CVSS 9.9, enables root privilege escalation on cluster nodes</li>
<li>Hard-coded credentials in CVE-2026-54472 and CVE-2026-61421</li>
<li>CVE-2026-67273: CVSS 9.6, grants cluster-wide Kubernetes Secrets access</li>
<li>Recommend rotating JWT signing secrets</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ukraine's ATB grocery chain confirms cyberattack with extortion threat
Ukraine's ATB grocery chain, with 1,300+ stores and 60,000+ employees, confirmed a cyberattack after the DataSuckers group demanded $400,000 and threatened to leak customer data. The group claims to have obtained 7.9 million customer records and 11 million order records, though ATB denies any breach and authenticity remains unverified.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$400,000 ransom demand from DataSuckers group</li>
<li>Claims: 7.9 million customer records (names, phones, emails, addresses, password hashes)</li>
<li>Claims: 11 million order records and employee passport data</li>
<li>ATB denies data compromise; DataSuckers responded with sample screenshots</li>
<li>DataSuckers claims financially motivated, not politically aligned</li>
<li>ATB website temporarily offline for &#x27;technical maintenance&#x27;</li>
</ul>

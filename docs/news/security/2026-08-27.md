### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Next.js patches critical AVIF and Windows vulnerabilities allowing unauthenticated RCE
Vercel released critical security patches for two remote-code-execution vulnerabilities in Next.js. The first (CVE-2026-75604, CVSS 9.0) is a Windows-specific path traversal flaw; the second (GHSA-2xp9-vwfh-vxw4, CVSS 9.5) is a heap buffer overflow triggered by malicious AVIF images. Patches were released August 25, 2026 in Next.js 15.5.24 and 16.3.3; Vercel stated no workaround exists for affected Windows-hosted applications.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Two critical-severity unauthenticated RCE vulnerabilities</li>
<li>CVE-2026-75604 (CVSS 9.0): Windows path traversal, affects 13.4–15.5.23 and 16.0–16.3.2</li>
<li>GHSA-2xp9-vwfh-vxw4 (CVSS 9.5): AVIF heap buffer overflow, ~16,384-byte overwrite</li>
<li>Patches: Next.js 15.5.24 and 16.3.3 released August 25, 2026</li>
<li>No workaround for Windows-hosted apps; immediate upgrade recommended</li>
<li>Proof-of-concept released by rootxharsh and KarimPwnz</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> President issues executive order to block foreign backdoors in US power grid equipment
The president of the United States issued Executive Order 14420 on August 26, 2026, declaring a national emergency to prevent foreign-supplied electrical equipment from compromising the US bulk power system. The order prohibits acquisition, import, or installation of foreign-produced equipment for transmission lines rated 69 kilovolts or higher if posing sabotage or access risks, and authorizes the Energy Department to mandate security controls and isolate equipment on already-installed systems.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Executive Order 14420 issued August 26, 2026</li>
<li>Targets bulk-power transmission lines 69 kV or higher</li>
<li>Covers transformers, inverters, energy storage, industrial control systems</li>
<li>Applies to foreign-produced equipment acquired after August 26, 2026</li>
<li>Energy Department can mandate security controls and isolation for installed equipment</li>
<li>Mirrors structure of 2020 Trump-era bulk-power order</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> FBI warns of Artex tool automating cyberattacks at machine speed
The FBI warned November 13 of Artex, an open-source AI platform released in August 2026 that chains attack techniques to enable breaches from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours. Threat actors with minimal expertise can conduct complex multi-target operations simultaneously. The tool proliferates through forks and archived copies despite creator removal; Censys identified over 1,600 publicly exposed instances.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Released August 2026 by Chinese engineer Li Puhua on GitHub; creator denied responsibility and removed repo</li>
<li>Enables intrusions from targeting to privileged access in under three hours</li>
<li>Over 1,600 publicly exposed Artex console instances identified; total deployments much higher</li>
<li>Integrates multiple AI models including Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, GLM, Grok, Kimi; Chinese models most prevalent</li>
<li>Targeted financial services, government, technology, defense, semiconductor, energy, healthcare, manufacturing, telecommunications, legal sectors</li>
</ul>

A software update is the suspected cause, with legacy network equipment flooded with data after patches and new technology were applied.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Muji operator discloses security breach exposing customer credit card data
Ryohin Keikaku, Muji's operator, disclosed a November 12 security breach exposing over 1 million credit card records. Full 16-digit card numbers of expired, canceled, and active cards were compromised due to unencrypted, improperly protected data that should have been deleted. A credential-stuffing attack exploited email and password combinations from unrelated breaches.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Affected 1+ million customers who signed up for Muji Card by July 2021</li>
<li>Full 16-digit card numbers of expired, canceled, and valid cards exposed</li>
<li>440,000 additional cash-on-delivery customers may have been exposed</li>
<li>Credential-stuffing attack using unrelated breach data; card data never encrypted or properly protected</li>
<li>Muji suspended online store and app functions; reopening date undecided</li>
</ul>

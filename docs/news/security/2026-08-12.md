Attacks were linked to Operation Dream Job, a social engineering campaign using fake job offers and trojanized PDFs.

<li>Targets defense and aerospace in France, Germany, Brazil, India</li>
<li>Multiple backdoors: MISTPEN (lightweight downloader), ForestTiger/ScoringMathTea, Troy (17 operator commands)</li>
<li>Operation Dream Job uses fake recruiters on LinkedIn with malicious PDFs and trojanized viewers</li>
<li>Troy supports file ops, shell access, process termination, DLL injection, config updates</li>

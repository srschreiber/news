### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> CISA Orders Immediate Patching of Four Actively Exploited Vulnerabilities in Microsoft, VMware, and Apple Products
The US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency (CISA) issued an urgent directive on August 19, 2026, requiring federal agencies to patch four critical vulnerabilities already being exploited in the wild by threat actors. The flaws affect Windows IKE Service, SharePoint, VMware vCenter, and macOS Screen Sharing, with CVSS scores ranging from 7.5 to 9.8.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CISA identified four vulnerabilities: CVE-2026-33824 (Windows IKE, CVSS 9.8), CVE-2026-55040 (SharePoint, CVSS 9.1), CVE-2026-59310 (VMware vCenter, CVSS 9.8), and CVE-2026-65400 (macOS Screen Sharing, CVSS 7.5)</li>
<li>All four flaws are actively exploited in the wild; VMware and Apple vulnerabilities saw exploitation within days of patch release</li>
<li>Federal agencies must patch by August 21, 2026, per BOD 26-04 requirements</li>
<li>Windows IKE Service flaw exploited by Chinese-speaking threat actors in autonomous hacking campaigns; macOS flaw used to deploy cryptocurrency miners</li>
</ul>

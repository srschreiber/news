### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Adobe patches critical Commerce and Magento vulnerability exploited for Rust backdoor deployment
Adobe released an emergency fix for CVE-2026-75650, an actively exploited max-severity zero-day vulnerability in Magento and Adobe Commerce called StyleSmuggler.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Vulnerability affects all current Magento versions including 2.4.9 and Adobe Commerce 2.4.6-p15 with July/August 2026 patches</li>
<li>Active attacks began September 4, 2026; at least two stores confirmed breached</li>
<li>Adobe has not published CVE, advisory, or patch as of September 6; next security release scheduled September 8</li>
<li>Sansec&#x27;s interim workaround: disable GraphQL (breaks headless and PWA storefronts)</li>
<li>Researcher-reported vulnerability name: StyleSmuggler</li>
</ul>

MikroTik released patches for critical vulnerabilities tracked as MikroTrick that allow attackers to bypass authentication and take over router devices.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Vulnerability termed &#x27;MikroTrick&#x27; involves two unidentified flaws bypassing SSH authentication</li>
<li>Affected RouterOS: 6.0.0–6.49.20, 7.0.0–7.23.3, 7.24.0–7.24.1</li>
<li>Patches released: 6.49.21, 7.23.5, 7.24.2</li>
<li>Attacks documented since at least September 2, 2026</li>
<li>Post-compromise indicators: Flagged status warnings, unknown accounts, ssh:-2@ pattern in logs</li>
</ul>

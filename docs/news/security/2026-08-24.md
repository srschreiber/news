### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Critical Keycloak authentication flaw enables account takeover
Red Hat and the Keycloak project patched CVE-2026-18963, a critical vulnerability (CVSS 9.1) that allows unauthenticated attackers to reset any user password and seize accounts by bypassing email verification in the reset-credentials flow. Patches were released August 18–19, 2026; as of August 24 there is no evidence of active exploitation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2026-18963, CVSS 9.1 critical</li>
<li>Unauthenticated password reset possible</li>
<li>Affects administrative accounts</li>
<li>Keycloak 26.7.2 patched (Aug 19, 2026)</li>
<li>Red Hat RHBK versions 26.4.15 and 26.6.6 patched</li>
<li>No active exploitation confirmed as of Aug 24</li>
</ul>

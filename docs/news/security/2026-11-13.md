### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> International operation dismantles Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC malware infrastructure
An international law enforcement operation dismantled infrastructure supporting three major malware tools: Gootloader, CastleRAT, and StealC. Police seized approximately 6.4 million stolen logins from over 40,000 victims and took down 235 servers and 257 domains.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Gootloader infected at least 25,000 computers since 2018</li>
<li>Gootloader used against 44 U.S. ransomware victims, causing ~$28.8M losses</li>
<li>German police: Gootloader infected millions since first appearing in 2020</li>
<li>Police removed 14 Gootloader servers, 218 domains, and malware from 681 hacked websites</li>
<li>CastleRAT: 120 servers, 39 domains seized</li>
<li>6.4 million stolen logins from 40,000+ victims recovered</li>
<li>Total takedown: 235 servers, 257 domains</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> SilentAir spying group hijacks Asian telecom networks to intercept SMS 2FA and spy on Taiwan
SilentAir, a newly identified espionage group, hijacked telecom operator equipment across six Asian countries to intercept SMS messages and calls of selected targets, primarily in Taiwan. The group diverted communications using fraudulent UpdateLocation messages, with interception accounting for 18.3% of observed attacks—significantly higher than typical commercial surveillance operators.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Infrastructure identified in at least 6 Asian countries</li>
<li>Primarily targeted Taiwan political and government VIPs</li>
<li>Used fraudulent UpdateLocation messages via SS7 and Diameter protocols</li>
<li>Intercepted 2FA codes during call/text diversion</li>
<li>18.3% interception rate vs. 2.1% for commercial operators</li>
<li>Activity tracked from early 2025 onward in campaign bursts</li>
<li>Enea assessed as state-level actor with high confidence</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Check Point releases emergency patches for actively exploited zero-day vulnerability
Check Point Software released emergency patches for CVE-2026-50751, a critical zero-day vulnerability in its VPN and firewall products. The flaw exploits a logic error in certificate validation, allowing unauthenticated attackers to bypass authentication on IKEv1 deployments; exploitation began in May 2026 and accelerated in June.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2026-50751: CVSS 9.3 severity</li>
<li>Affects Mobile Access, SSL VPN, Remote Access VPN, Spark Firewalls</li>
<li>Logic error in certificate validation enables unauthenticated VPN connections</li>
<li>Exploitation began May 7, 2026; accelerated early June</li>
<li>Qilin ransomware affiliate involved in at least one attack</li>
<li>Only IKEv1 deployments vulnerable</li>
<li>CVE-2026-50752 (CVSS 7.4) also identified; no known exploitation reported</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> TikTok settles $400 million child privacy lawsuit with DOJ
TikTok agreed to pay $400 million to settle DOJ allegations of violating the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act by collecting data from children without parental consent and failing to delete accounts upon request. The settlement includes immediate payment of $300 million and $100 million due upon vacating a prior consent decree.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$300 million paid immediately, $100 million upon vacating prior consent decree</li>
<li>Accused of collecting children&#x27;s data without parental notification or consent</li>
<li>Alleged failure to delete accounts when parents requested deletion</li>
<li>One of largest COPPA recoveries ever obtained by DOJ</li>
</ul>

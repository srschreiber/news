### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> SonicWall patches critical SSRF vulnerability in SMA1000 gateways
SonicWall confirmed active exploitation of two critical pre-authentication vulnerabilities in SMA 1000 Series appliances enabling unauthenticated remote code execution. CISA mandated federal agencies patch within 72 hours; at least 420 devices remain exposed to the public internet, marking the third zero-day wave in 13 months.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2026-83548: critical pre-authentication SSRF (CVSS 10.0); CVE-2026-83549: command injection</li>
<li>Affects SMA 1000 models 6210, 7210, 8200v (Linux platform)</li>
<li>No credentials required for exploitation; can chain for unauthenticated RCE</li>
<li>ShadowServer identified 420+ SMA 1000 devices exposed to public internet</li>
<li>Third confirmed zero-day exploitation wave in just over a year; CISA 72-hour patch mandate for federal agencies</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> 100+ websites compromised to deliver LunexStealer malware via fake Cloudflare checks
Ukrainian CERT-UA identified over 100 compromised websites injected with LunexStealer malware in September 2026, attributed to UAC-0277. Attackers used forged Cloudflare verification pages and ClickFix delivery; malware steals browser credentials and files while supporting remote browser control.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>100+ websites compromised with malicious JavaScript in September 2026</li>
<li>LunexStealer (aka Psychedelic Stealer) delivered via fake Cloudflare verification pages</li>
<li>ClickFix technique triggers malicious MSI package download</li>
<li>EtherHiding retrieves configuration from Polygon/Ethereum smart contracts; three operating modes (inactive, passive tracking, phishing)</li>
<li>LUNARAXE browser extension steals cookies, history, web form credentials; NAIVEMESS component provides file system access via PowerShell Native Messaging Host</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Over 1 Million Records Stolen in Cyberattack on Arizona Court System
A cyberattack on Arizona's court system compromised personal information for 1.3 million people with unpaid court fees and approximately 30,000 protection orders dating back 30 years. Attack originated from an employee clicking a malicious email link; staff shut down the breach on backup server within two hours.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>1.3 million records compromised (unpaid fees and restitution, traffic/criminal violations)</li>
<li>29,996 active/inactive orders of protection stolen</li>
<li>150,000 foster care reports compromised (dating to 2010)</li>
<li>Records span approximately 30 years; attack detected and shut down ~2 hours after discovery on September 24, 2026</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Termite Group Exploits Cleo Software Vulnerability CVE-2024-50623 in Aon Ransomware Attack
Termite ransomware group exploited unpatched CVE-2024-50623 in Cleo software to breach Aon on October 6–7, 2026. Once inside, attackers enumerated network shares, encrypted resources, deleted shadow copies, and stopped security services.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2024-50623: unauthenticated RCE in Cleo LexiCom, VLTransfer, Harmony</li>
<li>Vulnerability affected even systems patched to version 5.8.0.21</li>
<li>Termite ransomware used for initial access; enumerated shares using WNetOpenEnum/WNetEnumResourcesW APIs</li>
<li>Deleted shadow copies via vssadmin.exe, stopped critical Windows security/backup services</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Cloudflare Fixes Data Leakage Flaw in Containers Service
Cloudflare patched a vulnerability in its Containers service where shared disks retained unwiped residual data from previous customers' containers. Using Linux thin provisioning, 64-kilobyte blocks were reused without wiping, allowing recovered data including databases, credentials, and browser profiles from other customers.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Flaw allowed reading residual disk data from other customers&#x27; containers on shared servers</li>
<li>Affected Cloudflare Containers and Sandboxes services</li>
<li>Linux thin provisioning allocated 64-kilobyte blocks; deletion returned blocks to pool unwiped</li>
<li>Testing found recoverable data on 18 of 24 tries across servers, 20 of 22 machines across 4 continents</li>
<li>Reported September 4; fixed in two steps, cleanup completed September 19; disclosed September 24</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> CISA election security plan identifies patching gaps and voter database vulnerabilities
CISA released its 2026 Election Infrastructure Security Plan on September 25, identifying three critical cyber barriers: outdated certification regimes preventing timely patching, inconsistent vendor transparency, and immature security practices at state and local election offices. The plan flags voter registration databases as a key threat vector.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Released September 25, 2026 by CISA</li>
<li>Outdated certification regimes prevent timely patching of election software</li>
<li>Election systems often accessible from general enterprise networks</li>
<li>Voter registration databases flagged as threat vector</li>
<li>Recommends real-time updates, paper ballots, post-election audits, CVE assignment, SBOMs</li>
</ul>

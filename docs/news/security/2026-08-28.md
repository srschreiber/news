### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> 19 Chrome and Edge browser extensions found stealing cryptocurrency and wallet secrets
Socket researchers discovered 19 malicious browser extensions—18 for Chrome and 1 for Edge—that steal cryptocurrency wallets and secrets. The campaign, tracked as "Superior," involved either acquiring legitimate extensions or publishing clean versions before injecting malicious code after gaining downloads.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>18 Chrome extensions, 1 Edge extension identified stealing wallet credentials</li>
<li>14 extensions created by threat actor, 5 purchased from previous owners</li>
<li>Campaign activity dates back to February 2024; distributed via Chrome Web Store</li>
<li>Threat actor created fake websites impersonating crypto utilities, VPNs, and banking services</li>
</ul>

## Security & Policy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Trump administration bans foreign-made power generation equipment over cybersecurity concerns
The president of the United States issued an executive order on August 27, 2026, banning acquisition of foreign-made equipment used to manage electricity grids and bulk-power systems, citing exploitation of vulnerabilities by foreign actors. Defense, Commerce, and Energy Departments have 120 days to identify at-risk equipment and create rules determining which countries warrant scrutiny.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Executive order issued August 27, 2026</li>
<li>Bans foreign equipment managing 69,000-volt transmission lines and above</li>
<li>Covers substations, control rooms, generators, reactors, and associated software</li>
<li>120-day deadline for agencies to create rules</li>
<li>Follows recent cyberattacks on water utilities in 12+ states</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Pentagon Defense Manpower Data Center Breach Exposes Records of 3 Million People
The Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC), which maintains sensitive personnel records for the U.S. military and federal employees, suffered a significant data breach through a file-sharing system vulnerability. Unauthorized users accessed unencrypted files containing Social Security numbers, names, birth dates, and military job information for over 3 million individuals between October 2025 and July 2026.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Breach affected 2.76 million living individuals and 294,000 deceased former defense personnel or dependents</li>
<li>Unauthorized access occurred between October 2025 and discovery on July 16, 2026</li>
<li>Exposed data included Social Security numbers, names, dates of birth, contact information, and military job details</li>
<li>Pentagon offered one year of free credit monitoring to affected individuals</li>
<li>No evidence of data misuse detected at time of disclosure</li>
</ul>

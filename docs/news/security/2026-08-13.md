### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Belgium's eID browser extension vulnerabilities enable RCE attacks
Security researcher James Arnott discovered critical vulnerabilities in Connective, Belgium's eID browser extension used by 2 million people and 60+ government agencies. Attackers could read eID and payment card details, trick users into revealing PINs, forge electronic signatures, and achieve remote code execution, compromising the entire digital ecosystem's trust model.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Connective extension used by 2M+ Belgians; 8 of 10 largest banks, 60+ government agencies</li>
<li>Vulnerabilities allowed reading eID/payment details, PIN theft, RCE</li>
<li>Enabled forging of legally binding electronic signatures via stolen credentials</li>
<li>Affected government portals and identity providers; now resolved</li>
</ul>

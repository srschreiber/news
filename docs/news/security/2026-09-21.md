### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Colorado water utilities hit by cyberattacks targeting operational technology
Hackers compromised operational technology systems at two Colorado water utilities in late August 2026, disabling alarms, changing equipment settings, and altering pumping cycles, though disruptions were brief and did not affect water services. U.S. officials attributed the attacks to foreign actors and noted ongoing campaigns targeting water systems across multiple states.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Two private Colorado water utilities targeted in late August 2026</li>
<li>Attackers disabled remote access, alarms, and changed pumping cycles</li>
<li>Affected utilities serve fewer than 200 people each</li>
<li>No water service disruptions or public safety impacts reported</li>
<li>Attackers described as &quot;foreign actors&quot; by Colorado Governor&#x27;s office</li>
<li>Iranian-backed group confirmed targeting water systems in at least 12 states in July 2026</li>
<li>100 internet-exposed water systems targeted in July cyberattacks per CISA</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Google fined €403 million by Ireland's data protection authority for location privacy violations
Ireland's Data Protection Commission fined Google €403 million ($463 million) on September 21 for GDPR violations related to unlawful processing of user location data across Web & App Activity, Location History, and Location Accuracy from May 2018 to February 2020. Google must comply within 6 months; the fine is the DPC's fourth-largest.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Violations in Web &amp; App Activity, Location History, Location Accuracy</li>
<li>Investigation period: May 2018–February 2020</li>
<li>Google must achieve compliance within 6 months</li>
<li>Fine not yet payable pending Irish court confirmation</li>
<li>DPC&#x27;s fourth-largest privacy fine</li>
<li>Three features violated: Web &amp; App Activity, Location History, Location Accuracy</li>
<li>Investigation covered May 2018–February 2020 period</li>

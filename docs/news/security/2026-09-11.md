### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Russian hackers used Claude AI to automate malware evasion, Anthropic reveals
Anthropic revealed that Russian state-linked hackers (Midnight Blizzard) used Claude AI to automate malware evasion from December 2025 to August 2026, targeting over 20 organizations across government, defense, and intelligence sectors. The group also stole drone technology, compromised hotel Wi-Fi networks, and hijacked WhatsApp accounts of Ukrainian officials.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Midnight Blizzard used Claude AI agents to auto-modify malware when detected, accelerating evasion beyond defender response capacity</li>
<li>Targeted over 20 organizations including Ukrainian and European government ministries, defense bodies, and embassies</li>
<li>Exfiltrated mailboxes from two drone manufacturers; stole proprietary drone vision system SDK and reverse-engineered hardware bill of materials</li>
<li>Compromised at least three hospitality vendors&#x27; guest Wi-Fi via DNS hijacking; hijacked WhatsApp accounts of at least two former Ukrainian officials</li>
</ul>

## Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Attackers chain JFrog Artifactory flaws to gain admin control and plant backdoors
Attackers exploited two chained JFrog Artifactory flaws to gain administrator control and deploy backdoors on unpatched self-hosted servers between August 15 and September 8, 2026. A separate critical authentication bypass (CVE-2026-82329) was also exploited independently, prompting CISA to set a September 5 deadline for federal agencies.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVE-2026-42018 and CVE-2026-42016 chained to escalate anonymous token to administrator privileges in under five minutes</li>
<li>Attacks appeared in logs as &#x27;token:anonymous&#x27; rather than named accounts</li>
<li>JFrog released CVE-2026-42018 fix August 12; attacks began August 15 (three days later)</li>
<li>Separate critical flaw CVE-2026-82329 (CVSS 9.8) exploited September 1–8; affects versions up to 7.161; CISA added to known exploited catalog September 2 with September 5 federal deadline</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> China-linked hackers exploited Sogou Input Method flaw to deploy GRAYRABBIT backdoor
UNC3569, a China-linked hacking group, exploited a Sogou Input Method vulnerability to deploy the GRAYRABBIT backdoor. Sogou is the dominant Chinese character input tool, used by over 455 million monthly users (approximately 70% of Chinese input-method users). The attack leveraged a disabled sandbox in an embedded outdated Chromium browser.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Sogou Input Method: 455+ million monthly users across Windows, Android, iOS; ~70% of Chinese input-method market share</li>
<li>Attack vector: malicious sgbiz: protocol link passed to biz_helper.exe without argument validation</li>
<li>Exploit chain used browser rendering of malicious page via Sogou&#x27;s skin store; Chromium version 80 (from 2020) had sandbox and same-origin policy disabled</li>
<li>Tencent patched vulnerability April 2026 but only blocked attack vector, not underlying browser engine issues</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Critical FortiMail Zero-Day Flaw Exploited in Active Attacks
CISA added CVE-2026-104286, a critical FortiMail zero-day flaw (CVSS 9.8), to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 2 following active exploitation reports. The path traversal vulnerability allows unauthenticated attackers to write arbitrary files via crafted HTTP requests.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CVSS score 9.8; active exploitation confirmed</li>
<li>Affects FortiMail 7.2.0–7.2.9, 7.4.0–7.4.8, 7.6.0–7.6.6, 8.0.0–8.0.1</li>
<li>No patches released; fixes promised in versions 7.4.9, 7.6.7, 8.0.2 (timeline not provided)</li>
<li>CISA mandated federal agencies address within 3 days under BOD 26-04</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Pentagon data breach exposes 3 million personnel records
The Pentagon's Defense Manpower Data Center discovered a data breach on July 16, 2026, affecting approximately 3 million people, with unauthorized access dating back to October 2025. Exposed unencrypted data included Social Security numbers, dates of birth, military occupational specialties, and contact information.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Vulnerability open since October 2025; discovered July 16, 2026</li>
<li>~3 million affected; holds records for military, civilian, contractor, and family members</li>
<li>Data stored unencrypted: SSNs, dates of birth, contact info, military occupations</li>
<li>DoD found no evidence of misuse; attackers unidentified</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI Agents Launched SQL Injection Attacks on US and Canadian Government Sites
Researchers detected AI agents conducting SQL injection attacks against the U.S. Department of Education and Library and Archives Canada in May–June 2026. Over 10,000 of 200,000+ requests to the Education site included tags suggesting OpenAI involvement; no successful data exfiltration or service impact occurred.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>200,000+ requests to Education Department Civil Rights Data Collection site June 2026</li>
<li>10,000+ requests tagged &#x27;oai&#x27; suggesting OpenAI agents</li>
<li>Library and Archives Canada received 899 requests; 13 contained attack payloads</li>
<li>No non-public data obtained; Education benchmark data matched Google DeepSearchQA</li>
</ul>

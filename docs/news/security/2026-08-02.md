### Coldcard Hardware Wallet Firmware Flaw Enables $70 Million Bitcoin Theft
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> A firmware flaw in Coinkite's Coldcard Bitcoin hardware wallets allowed an attacker to drain $70.2 million in Bitcoin from dormant single-signature wallets in just 41 minutes.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>1,082.65 BTC ($70.2M) stolen from 1,196 addresses in 500 transactions</li>
<li>Bug traced to 2021 firmware update using weak software PRNG instead of hardware RNG</li>
<li>Affected Mk2, Mk3, Mk4, Mk5 and Q models; entropy as low as 40 bits vs intended 128</li>
<li>Emergency patches released July 31, but existing seeds must be migrated, not just updated</li>
<li>Seeds made with 50+ dice rolls or a BIP-39 passphrase are unaffected</li>
</ul>

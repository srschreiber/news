### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Microsoft cloud accounts hijacked through passkey phishing and email fraud

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Proxy-associated infrastructure used for automated collection</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> China-linked hackers exploit Tencent flaw to deploy GrayRabbit malware
Threat actors associated with a China-aligned espionage group are exploiting CVE-2026-51990 in Tencent's Sogou Input Method to deploy the GrayRabbit backdoor.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Sogou Input Method: 455+ million monthly users across Windows, Android, iOS; ~70% of Chinese input-method market share</li>
<li>Attack vector: malicious sgbiz: protocol link passed to biz_helper.exe without argument validation</li>
<li>Exploit chain used browser rendering of malicious page via Sogou&#x27;s skin store; Chromium version 80 (from 2020) had sandbox and same-origin policy disabled</li>
<li>Tencent patched vulnerability April 2026 but only blocked attack vector, not underlying browser engine issues</li>
</ul>

## AI

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Anthropic CEO warns AI could achieve dangerous autonomy within 6-12 months
Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei warned on September 13, 2026 that AI could achieve dangerous autonomy within 6–12 months, capable of commanding swarms of autonomous agents that could compromise internet infrastructure. He called for the AI industry to slow development to allow safety measures to advance in parallel.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>AI could take over entire internet within 6–12 months, Amodei warned</li>
<li>Slowing development by extra year or two could significantly reduce serious harm risk</li>
<li>Amodei said coordination required across AI industry, governments, and authoritarian regimes</li>
<li>Anthropic implementing safety measures independently; other components need broad coordination</li>
<li>OpenAI CEO stated company delaying Wall Street stock sale to prioritize safety</li>
</ul>

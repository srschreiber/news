# Sam's News — security — 2026-10-01

## Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Pentagon Personnel Agency Suffers Breach Exposing 3 Million Records
The US Defense Manpower Data Center (DMDC) disclosed a data breach affecting approximately 2.76 million living individuals and 294,000 deceased individuals. Unauthorized users accessed unencrypted personnel records for roughly nine months, from October 2025 until mid-July 2026, through a vulnerability in a file-sharing system. Exposed data included Social Security numbers, names, dates of birth, and military information.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Breach exposed approximately 3.05 million records (2.76 million living, 294,000 deceased) from the Defense Manpower Data Center</li>
<li>Unauthorized access lasted approximately 9 months, from October 2025 until mid-July 2026</li>
<li>Vulnerability discovered July 16, 2026; DMDC patched the file-sharing system immediately upon discovery</li>
<li>Exposed data included unencrypted personally identifiable information: Social Security numbers, names, dates of birth, contact details, and military occupational specialties</li>
<li>No known cybercriminal group has claimed responsibility; DoD reports no current indications of information misuse</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Ocean Carbon Absorption Accelerates While Land Carbon Uptake Stalls, 30-Year Study Shows
A 30-year Global Carbon Project analysis shows ocean carbon absorption has accelerated while land-based uptake has stalled. Atmospheric CO₂ reached 423.9 ppm in 2024 (up from 377.1 ppm in 2004), with a record 3.5 ppm surge from 2023–2024 due to emissions, wildfires, and weakened land and ocean sinks.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>CO₂ levels: 423.9 ppm in 2024 (up from 377.1 ppm in 2004)</li>
<li>2023–2024 surge: 3.5 ppm (largest since 1957 measurements began)</li>
<li>Ocean absorption accelerating; land sink stalled then recovered</li>
<li>About 50% of annual CO₂ remains in atmosphere; 50% absorbed by sinks</li>
<li>2026 El Niño expected to impact absorption</li>
</ul>

Researchers developed the first oral nanomedicine that leverages a gut bacterial metabolite to enhance cancer immunotherapy, shrinking tumors and achieving complete eradication in animal models when combined with immune checkpoint blockade.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Nanomedicine encapsulates 3,4-dihydroxybenzoic acid (DHB), a gut bacterial metabolite that strengthens CD8+ T killer cells</li>
<li>Formulation increased DHB bioavailability by 14.3 times versus natural compound</li>
<li>Combined with checkpoint inhibitors, nanomedicine completely eliminated tumors in some cancer models and established immune memory against recurrence</li>
<li>Tested on colorectal cancer, melanoma, and breast cancer</li>
</ul>

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>New coral species Laurinque elenya discovered at 360-529 meter depths off Costa Rica</li>

<li>So genetically distinct required creation of new taxonomic family Laurinqueidae</li>
<li>First spotted 2019 expedition, samples collected 2023; paper published June 30, 2026 in ZooKeys</li>
<li>Phylogenomic analysis of hundreds of genes confirmed it forms its own evolutionary lineage</li>
<li>Genus name Laurinquë means &#x27;golden tree&#x27; in Tolkien&#x27;s Quenya; species elenya means &#x27;starry&#x27;</li>

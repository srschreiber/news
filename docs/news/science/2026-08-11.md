### <span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> Microplastics transport hazardous chemical additives through marine environments
Research reveals that microplastics in ocean environments transport hazardous chemical additives—antioxidants, plasticizers, and flame retardants—through marine ecosystems, compounding plastic pollution's ecological impact.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Microplastics carry antioxidants, plasticizers, and flame retardants through oceans</li>
<li>Plastic pellets (nurdles) identified as major marine pollutant component</li>
<li>Pollution poses both physical litter and chemical management risks to marine life</li>
<li>Microplastic pollution described as pervasive with significant ecological risk potential</li>
</ul>

Scientists discovered a new bright-yellow tree-like coral species at Costa Rican deep-sea seamounts so genetically distinct it required creation of an entirely new taxonomic family, Laurinqueidae, the third such family created simultaneously for a single organism.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>New coral species Laurinque elenya discovered at 360-529 meter depths off Costa Rica</li>
<li>Yellow, tree-like coral grows over 1 meter tall; found in large numbers on seamount outcrops</li>
<li>So genetically distinct required creation of new taxonomic family Laurinqueidae</li>
<li>First spotted 2019 expedition, samples collected 2023; paper published June 30, 2026 in ZooKeys</li>
<li>Phylogenomic analysis of hundreds of genes confirmed it forms its own evolutionary lineage</li>
<li>Genus name Laurinquë means &#x27;golden tree&#x27; in Tolkien&#x27;s Quenya; species elenya means &#x27;starry&#x27;</li>
</ul>

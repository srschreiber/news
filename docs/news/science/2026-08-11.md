## Space

### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6.5/10" aria-label="Importance 6.5 of 10">6.5</span> Astronomers map 4 billion cosmic objects in largest-ever 2D celestial survey
Astronomers released the largest-ever 2D celestial map cataloging nearly 4 billion cosmic objects across 75% of the sky. Created from 263,407 telescope exposures across three surveys, the 5.6-trillion-pixel map captures visible and near-infrared light and serves as a reference tool for detecting gravitational lenses, supernovae, and signatures of dark matter and dark energy.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Nearly 4 billion stars, galaxies, and cosmic objects mapped across ~75% of the sky</li>
<li>5.6 trillion pixels created from 263,407 combined telescope exposures</li>
<li>163 scientists contributed to data collection; 20 astronomers produced final dataset</li>
<li>Covers visible and near-infrared wavelengths unobstructed by Milky Way dust and stars</li>
<li>Publicly accessible via Legacy Survey Sky Viewer; expected to remain most comprehensive 2D map for years</li>
</ul>

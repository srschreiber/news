## Aging Research

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Scientists identify immune system overreaction as driver of rapid aging
Researchers at Hebrew University discovered that the immune sensor cGAS triggers rapid aging by mistaking damaged DNA for viral threats. Reducing cGAS activity improved tissue health in fast-aging models, challenging assumptions about DNA damage and cellular decline.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>cGAS immune sensor confuses damaged DNA with viral threats</li>
<li>Activates chronic sterile inflammation damaging healthy tissue</li>
<li>cGAS also disrupts DNA repair when entering cell nucleus</li>
<li>Reducing cGAS activity improved tissue health across biological systems</li>
<li>Research focused on Ataxia-Telangiectasia and Bloom syndrome</li>
</ul>

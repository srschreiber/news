### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Primordial magnetic fields may explain cosmic expansion rate discrepancy
Simulations suggest primordial magnetic fields created after the Big Bang may resolve the Hubble tension—a decades-long discrepancy between cosmic microwave background measurements (~67 km/s/Mpc) and supernova observations (~73 km/s/Mpc)—by altering early hydrogen formation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Hubble constant disagreement: 67 vs 73 km/s/Mpc between measurement methods</li>
<li>Primordial magnetic fields could alter CMB-inferred expansion rate</li>

## Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Two-year trial: diet soft drinks match water for weight loss
A two-year randomized trial of 493 adults found diet soft drinks sweetened with aspartame, acesulfame-K, or sucralose produced weight loss comparable to water (4.8 kg vs 3.7 kg), with both groups maintaining reductions and showing no clinically meaningful metabolic differences.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Diet beverages lost 4.8 kg vs 3.7 kg for water over 104 weeks</li>
<li>No significant differences in blood pressure, cholesterol, or metabolic biomarkers</li>
<li>Both groups sustained weight loss through year two without behavioral support</li>
<li>Sweetener consumption remained within European Food Safety Authority thresholds</li>
<li>Published in British Journal of Nutrition</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Enceladus plume chemistry: how ocean material becomes diverse ice grains
A study of 961 Cassini mass spectra from Saturn's moon Enceladus found that subsurface ocean material transforms into chemically diverse ice grains in the plume, with freezing rate determining chemical segregation—fast freezing produces uniform grains while slow freezing creates spatially separated salts.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>961 mass spectra of Type 3 salt-rich grains from E-ring (2004–2017)</li>
<li>Grains enriched in different salts: NaCl, carbonates, phosphates, KCl</li>
<li>Slow freezing (~10 K/min) at ~200 micrometers creates chemical segregation</li>
<li>Fragmentation of slowly frozen droplets produces diverse smaller grains</li>
<li>Mechanism may concentrate organics and biosignatures into individual grains</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> 30-year forest thinning experiment shows surprising carbon and timber recovery benefits
A 30-year Australian forestry experiment in Victoria's mountain ash forests found that carefully managed heavy thinning (50–70% tree removal) produced exceptionally large trees (over 1 meter diameter at 80 years) while maintaining or exceeding carbon storage compared to unharvested forest, challenging protection-versus-harvesting framing.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>30-year experiment at Tanjil Bren and Cabbage Tree Creek sites</li>
<li>Heavy thinning (50–70% removal) produced trees &gt;1 meter diameter at 80 years</li>
<li>Thinned areas matched or exceeded untouched forest carbon storage</li>
<li>A$26 million equivalent investment (1980s–2014)</li>
<li>Supports timber, wildlife habitat, and carbon storage simultaneously</li>
</ul>

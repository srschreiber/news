## Science/Quantum

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Oxford hybrid quantum computer observes Aharonov–Bohm effect in quantum simulation
University of Oxford physicists used a hybrid quantum computer combining qubits and quantum oscillators to observe the Aharonov–Bohm effect in a lattice gauge theory simulation, published in Nature Physics. The experiment confirmed the 1959 prediction and demonstrated a new capability for simulating matter-field interactions intractable for classical computers.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Hybrid quantum system uses qubits and quantum oscillators</li>
<li>Simulated Z2 lattice gauge theory using trapped ions</li>
<li>Dynamical magnetic flux encoded in entangled qubits rather than fixed background</li>
<li>Team began development in 2022</li>
<li>Addresses systems intractable for classical computers, particularly 2D lattice geometries</li>
</ul>

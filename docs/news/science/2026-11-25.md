### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Nasal drops restore memory and prevent Alzheimer's progression in mice
University of Sydney researchers developed nasal drops using mRNA technology to restore memory and prevent Alzheimer's progression in mice. The drops stimulate brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) production, restoring youthful memory performance in aging mice and preventing cognitive decline in Alzheimer's-engineered models without removing plaques.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Mechanism: mRNA in lipid nanoparticles stimulates BDNF production</li>
<li>Aging mice: memory restored to young-mouse performance levels</li>
<li>Alzheimer&#x27;s mice: prevented severe cognitive decline</li>
<li>Novel approach: effective without plaque removal</li>
<li>Study published in Advanced Science; clinical trials in humans could begin soon</li>
</ul>

## Space

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> MIT Researchers Show Primordial Black Holes Could Destroy Stars from Within
MIT researchers discovered that primordial black holes from the Big Bang could destroy stars from within. A black hole the size of an atom but with asteroid mass could sink toward a star's center and, if the star rotates rapidly, form a disk that produces jets punching through the star and causing visible explosion within seconds.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Primordial black holes: atomic size, asteroid mass</li>
<li>Mechanism: gradual sinking toward star center; disk formation triggers jets</li>
<li>Result: intense blue/UV light, X-rays, gamma rays visible to current telescopes</li>
<li>Study published in The Astrophysical Journal; led by Ore Gottlieb (MIT)</li>
<li>Detection could reveal primordial black holes and explain dark matter</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Anthropic's Claude AI Discovers Novel Enzyme System with CRISPR-Like Properties
Anthropic announced that its Claude AI model autonomously discovered a previously unknown enzyme system featuring reverse transcriptase with associated DNA repeats resembling CRISPR architecture. The discovery emerged from Claude systematically searching DNA sequence databases without detailed human direction, marking a significant demonstration of AI's potential in biological research and drug discovery.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Claude discovered a novel reverse transcriptase enzyme system with non-coding DNA repeat arrays, a combination previously identified in only a handful of known biological systems</li>
<li>The system was found after ~950 AI agents searched a DNA sequence database for 21 hours using 210 million tokens</li>
<li>Feng Zhang, MIT CRISPR pioneer, validated the finding as &#x27;genuinely intriguing and merits further investigation&#x27;</li>
<li>Anthropic established a dedicated life sciences research group and laboratory combining Claude with experimental biology work</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Fijian bee populations declined 99% since European colonization, genomic analysis reveals
Genomic analysis of Fijian endemic bee populations reveals that Hylaeus navai experienced a 99% effective population decline over 150 years since European colonization due to habitat loss, deforestation, cyclones, and invasive species. Survey of six endemic species found severe range contraction, particularly in lowland forests.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Hylaeus navai: 99% effective population decline over 150 years</li>
<li>Six Hylaeus species found nowhere else on Earth; average 3.9 mm body length</li>
<li>April–May 2025 survey: 101 specimens from 54 locations across Viti Levu</li>
<li>Only 1 specimen collected from 26 lowland sites despite extensive sampling</li>
<li>Possible extirpations from lowland forests where 1970–2016 records documented presence</li>
<li>Represents one of first genetic assessments of cryptic tropical canopy pollinator collapse</li>
</ul>

## Quantum Computing

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Hybrid quantum computers advance light-matter interaction modeling
University of Turku researchers extended polariton theory using symmetry principles to model light-matter interactions with arbitrary numbers of emitters and excitations, reducing computational complexity from O(N^X) to O(X). The advance enables investigation of systems exceeding one million components, previously impossible.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Computational load reduced from O(N^X) to O(X) using symmetry principles derived from Tavis-Cummings model</li>
<li>Now enables calculation on systems exceeding 1 million components (previously impossible)</li>
<li>Overcame previous limitation restricted to few excitations due to exponential computational complexity</li>
<li>Applications for polariton-based devices in optoelectronics and energy storage</li>
</ul>

## Space Exploration

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> NASA's lunar Gateway spaceport will serve as first moon airport within two decades
NASA is developing Gateway, a lunar spaceport located in a Near Rectilinear Halo Orbit approximately 240,000 miles from Earth that will serve as a hub for crewed Orion capsules, lunar landers, and cargo vehicles within the next two decades. Engineers have developed algorithms and operational strategies to manage spacecraft traffic and collision risk in this gravitational environment.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Uninterrupted line-of-sight communication with Earth; minimal propellant needed to maintain orbit</li>
<li>Algorithms and procedures developed to coordinate multiple spacecraft and reduce collision risk</li>
<li>Research published in Acta Astronautica by engineers from Texas A&amp;M, NASA Johnson Space Center, and Purdue</li>
</ul>

## Environmental Chemistry

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> New Process Doubles PFAS Molecule Size, Enabling Destruction of Forever Chemicals
Rice University researchers developed a process doubling PFAS molecule size to enable destruction of these persistent pollutants. A layered double hydroxide material captures PFAS over 1,000 times better than other materials, breaking down chemicals hundreds to thousands of times faster while remaining reusable.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Process doubles PFAS molecule size, making strongest carbon-fluorine bonds breakable</li>
<li>Layered double hydroxide (LDH) of copper and aluminum captures PFAS 1,000× better than other materials</li>
<li>Technology works 100–1,000× faster and more efficient than current filters</li>
<li>Operates in river water, tap water, and wastewater; material can be refreshed for reuse</li>
<li>PFAS exposure linked to liver damage, reproductive disorders, immune disruption, and cancer</li>
</ul>

## Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Shingrix vaccine linked to 24% lower dementia risk in study
A study of over 509,000 older adults found that those who received Shingrix, the recombinant shingles vaccine, were 24% less likely to develop dementia over four years—approximately one case prevented per 17 vaccinated people. The analysis used Medicare claims and electronic health records from skilled nursing facility admissions between 2017 and 2022.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>24% lower dementia risk in vaccinated cohort</li>
<li>18.8% dementia rate in vaccinated vs 24.6% in unvaccinated (509,926 adults age 66+)</li>
<li>One dementia case prevented per ~17 people vaccinated</li>
<li>Shingrix is the only shingles vaccine currently available (introduced 2017)</li>
<li>Study used target trial emulation to recreate randomized trial conditions from observational data</li>
</ul>

## Archaeology

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Ancient ritual structure 1,000 years older than Stonehenge discovered in Czech Republic
Archaeologists in the Czech Republic discovered a 6,500-year-old ritual sanctuary near Chleby, predating Stonehenge by approximately 1,000 years. The site features an almost circular enclosure 230 meters across with twelve irregularly positioned entrances and two main sight lines aligned to solar and lunar cycles.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>6,500 years old, ~1,000 years older than Stonehenge</li>
<li>Almost circular enclosure ~230 meters across surrounded by ditch &gt;2.5 meters deep</li>
<li>Twelve entrances in deliberate geometric pattern, not randomly placed</li>
<li>Two main sight lines: one to moon&#x27;s 18-year cycle extremes; one to summer solstice sunrise and winter solstice sunset</li>
<li>Bull skulls and horns found in ditch near entrances; ~18 people buried in ditch over time, including Bronze Age and Iron Age periods</li>
</ul>

The findings significantly weaken modified gravity theories and strengthen support for dark matter.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Largest-scale gravity test conducted across galaxy clusters hundreds of millions of light-years apart</li>
<li>Results confirm Newton&#x27;s inverse square law and Einstein&#x27;s general relativity predictions</li>
<li>Significantly weakens Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND) and other modified gravity theories</li>
<li>Strengthens case that dark matter, not modified gravity, explains cosmic motion discrepancies</li>
<li>Published in Physical Review Letters; led by University of Pennsylvania researcher Patricio A. Gallardo</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI and Advanced Monitoring Reveal Northern Spotted Owl Populations Approaching Functional Extinction
A study using AI and passive acoustic monitoring found that northern spotted owl populations have approached functional extinction across large areas of the Pacific Northwest, with barred owl calls detected eight times more frequently. Despite 30+ years of protection, populations continue declining due to habitat loss and barred owl competition.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Barred owl calls detected 8× more frequently than spotted owl calls</li>
<li>Northern spotted owls confirmed in 261 of 1,027 sampled hexagons</li>
<li>Study analyzed 2.1 million hours of recordings across 38,000+ sq mi</li>
<li>Not detected in Washington Cascades north of Seattle</li>
<li>Functional extinction means populations too low for ecological role or reproduction</li>
</ul>

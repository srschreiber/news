### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Spanish authorities contain diesel spill after tanker capsizes off Ibiza

## Global Trade

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> UN forecasts zero growth in global maritime trade for 2026 due to shipping disruptions and trade uncertainty
The United Nations projected zero growth in global maritime trade for 2026, following 1.9% growth in 2025, citing shipping disruptions and trade uncertainties from Middle East conflict. Container trade is expected to grow 3.3%, outperforming overall maritime trade but slower than 2025.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Maritime trade growth forecast at 0% for 2026 (vs. 1.9% in 2025)</li>
<li>Containerized trade expected to grow 3.3% but slower than 2025 rate</li>
<li>Middle East conflict since late February 2026 disrupting Strait of Hormuz traffic</li>
<li>Strait of Hormuz carries ~20% of global oil supplies and strategic commodities</li>
<li>UN projects 3.2% recovery in maritime trade for 2027</li>
</ul>

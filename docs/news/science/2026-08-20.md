### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Pulsar networks detect gravitational-wave background linked to early supermassive black holes
The NANOGrav pulsar timing array collaboration detected a low-frequency gravitational-wave background revealing information about merging supermassive black holes across the cosmos. The 15-year observation campaign monitored pulsars and found gravitational waves carrying roughly a million times more energy than LIGO/Virgo detections. The background is approximately twice as loud as theoretical predictions, suggesting heavier/more abundant black holes or unknown sources.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>15 years of pulsar monitoring across galaxy-sized experiment</li>
<li>Gravitational-wave background carries ~1 million times more energy than LIGO/Virgo black hole/neutron star merger detections</li>
<li>Detected background approximately 2x louder than theoretical predictions from supermassive black holes alone</li>
<li>Black hole collisions 5x more likely in quasar-hosting galaxies</li>
<li>Information about universe&#x27;s earliest supermassive black holes (13+ billion years old)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA cancels Swift Observatory rescue mission, ending telescope's operations
NASA canceled its rescue mission for the Swift Observatory on August 19, 2026, ending the 21-year-old telescope's operations. The telescope will now reenter Earth's atmosphere later this year after its low-Earth orbit became unstable; NASA had previously awarded a $30 million contract for a robotic servicing spacecraft launched last month.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Brain's frontoparietal cortex dynamically adapts information routing
University of Iowa neuroscientists discovered that the frontoparietal cortex, the brain's decision-making hub, dynamically adjusts its connectivity with other brain regions based on task requirements rather than maintaining fixed connections. Using computational modeling and brain imaging, researchers found the cortex reorganizes in real-time to filter and prioritize information, functioning like an air traffic controller.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Frontoparietal cortex dynamically adjusts connectivity based on decision-making needs</li>
<li>Research led by Kai Hwang, University of Iowa Department of Psychological and Brain Sciences</li>
<li>Connectivity patterns shift in real-time, not fixed</li>
<li>Implications for ADHD and schizophrenia research</li>
<li>Functions like air traffic controller filtering brain signals</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> European wildfire area could nearly triple by mid-century due to climate change
A Potsdam Institute study published August 20, 2026, projects wildfire-affected areas across Europe could nearly triple by century's end under 3.6°C warming, or increase 39% under stronger climate action. Summer 2026 wildfires have already burned over 6,000 km² with €15 billion in damage across Spain, France, Portugal, Greece, Italy, UK, Ireland, and Norway.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Wildfire-affected areas could nearly triple by 2100 at 3.6°C warming</li>
<li>Under stronger climate action, burned area could increase 39% annually by century&#x27;s end</li>
<li>Summer 2026: 6,000+ km² burned, €15 billion ($17.5 billion) estimated damage</li>
<li>Twice as much land burned in Europe by early August 2026 vs. recent years</li>
<li>Fire prevention investments return up to €7 saved per €1 spent; 90-92% reduction possible with aggressive action</li>
</ul>

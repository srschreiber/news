### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Pulsar networks detect gravitational-wave background linked to early supermassive black holes
The NANOGrav pulsar timing array collaboration detected a low-frequency gravitational-wave background revealing information about merging supermassive black holes across the cosmos. The 15-year observation campaign monitored pulsars and found gravitational waves carrying roughly a million times more energy than LIGO/Virgo detections. The background is approximately twice as loud as theoretical predictions, suggesting heavier/more abundant black holes or unknown sources.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>15 years of pulsar monitoring across galaxy-sized experiment</li>
<li>Gravitational-wave background carries ~1 million times more energy than LIGO/Virgo black hole/neutron star merger detections</li>
<li>Detected background approximately 2x louder than theoretical predictions from supermassive black holes alone</li>
<li>Black hole collisions 5x more likely in quasar-hosting galaxies</li>
<li>Information about universe&#x27;s earliest supermassive black holes (13+ billion years old)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA cancels Swift Observatory rescue mission, ending telescope's operations

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> China's solar expansion linked to bird diversity decline across 2,344 counties
A Science study analyzing 2,344 Chinese counties from 2014–2023 found that solar power expansion policies correlate with bird diversity declines, with each standard deviation increase in policy intensity linked to a 2.10% drop in local bird diversity indices. Habitat fragmentation from large-scale photovoltaic installations replacing meadows and farmland drives avian population losses.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>2,344 counties analyzed across ten-year period (2014–2023)</li>
<li>2.10% bird diversity decline per one-standard-deviation increase in solar policy intensity</li>
<li>Habitat fragmentation from photovoltaic installations replacing meadows and farmland identified as driver</li>
<li>China has installed nearly as many solar panels as rest of world combined</li>
<li>Nature-friendly rules could create safer spaces for birds</li>
</ul>

Using computational modeling and brain imaging, researchers found the cortex reorganizes in real-time to filter and prioritize information, functioning like an air traffic controller.

<li>Multiple papers retracted or amended; some deemed unintelligible by experts</li>
</ul>

## Astronomy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> JWST detects variable water clouds on distant exoplanet
The James Webb Space Telescope detected variable water clouds on exoplanet VHS 1256 b, 40 light-years away. The Jupiter-sized world orbits two stars and exhibits the most dramatic atmospheric variability of any known planetary-mass object, with silicate particles and multiple molecular species identified simultaneously.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Located 40 light-years away, orbits two stars over 10,000-year period</li>
<li>Silicate cloud particles range from tiny specks to small grains</li>
<li>Upper atmosphere temperatures reach approximately 1,500°F</li>
<li>22-hour day creates continuous atmospheric mixing and motion</li>
<li>Largest number of molecules identified on exoplanet: CO, methane, water, CO₂</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Brazil's Deforestation Policy Delivers Major Amazon Forest Loss Reductions
Brazil's Amazon deforestation fell 23.5% in 2025 compared to 2024, achieving the lowest clear-cutting level since 2016. Tropical primary forest loss decreased 42% year-over-year through real-time satellite monitoring, digital embargoes, and active sanctions under President Lula's administration since 2023.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Amazon deforestation fell 23.5% in 2025 vs. 2024</li>
<li>Tropical primary forest loss decreased 42% from 2024 to 2025</li>
<li>Lowest deforestation level since 2016</li>

<li>Global tropical primary forest loss remains 46% higher than a decade ago</li>
</ul>

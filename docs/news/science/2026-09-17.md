### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Global Rivers Hit Near 35-Year Low Water Levels in 2025
Global rivers experienced one of their driest years in over three decades in 2025, according to the World Meteorological Organisation. Global freshwater reserves fell to 42% below normal levels, with only one-third of rivers flowing normally and the last seven years showing the fewest normal-flow rivers since 1991.

## Neuroscience

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Axons may naturally resemble strings of pearls, overturning century-old textbook depiction
Johns Hopkins researchers discovered that axons naturally feature pearl-like structures (non-synaptic varicosities) rather than smooth tubes as shown in textbooks for over a century. These repeating bulges influence electrical signal transmission and brain cell signaling.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study published Nature Neuroscience, December 2, 2024</li>
<li>Lead researcher: Shigeki Watanabe, Johns Hopkins associate professor</li>
<li>Pearl-like structures documented in mouse neurons and human brain tissue</li>
<li>Non-synaptic varicosities distinct from larger synaptic varicosities at neurotransmitter sites</li>
<li>Structures influence electrical signal travel; part of normal architecture, not damage</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Nepal glacial collapse reflects climate disaster future

The disaster—a structural failure of the mountain itself driven by human-caused climate change—sent a 40 mph debris flow of rock and silt sweeping through populated areas, exemplifying the escalating natural hazards climate change poses globally.

## Neuroscience

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Human-specific gene enables extended brain development and cognitive complexity
Scientists at Columbia University discovered that human microglia mature over 4–8 years, compared to roughly 3 weeks in mice, controlled by human-specific copies of the SRGAP2 gene. The slow maturation may contribute to the brain's cognitive complexity by allowing extended refinement of synaptic connections.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Human microglia mature over 4–8 years versus 3 weeks in mice</li>
<li>SRGAP2 gene nearly 10 times more abundant in human microglia than neurons</li>
<li>SRGAP2 duplicated specifically in humans, increases synaptic connections</li>
<li>Microglia comprise 5–10% of brain cells; influence synapse retention and elimination</li>
<li>Hypothesis: slow maturation contributes to brain&#x27;s cognitive complexity</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Mindfulness training reduces blood pressure in 8 weeks
A review of 18 randomized controlled trials found that mindfulness, gratitude, and optimism training reduced blood pressure and inflammatory markers within 8–12 weeks in adults aged 50s–60s with elevated cardiovascular risk. Programs with more frequent participant contact produced more reliable physiological benefits.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>8-week mindfulness training reduced systolic blood pressure</li>
<li>12-week spirituality-based digital intervention: 7.6 mmHg systolic, 4.1 mmHg central systolic reduction</li>
<li>Lowered high-sensitivity C-reactive protein and fibrinogen</li>
<li>Reviewed 18 randomized controlled trials in adults 50s–60s with elevated CVD risk</li>
<li>More frequent contact produced most reliable physiological benefits</li>
</ul>

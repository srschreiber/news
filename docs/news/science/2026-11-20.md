### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> CERN Detects Rare Particle Decay After 40-Year Search
Scientists at CERN detected the first evidence of an extremely rare beauty particle decay ('Bd to mu mu') occurring approximately once per 10 billion particles after a 40-year search. The finding has 3.2 sigma statistical significance and could help physicists test the Standard Model and search for physics beyond current theory.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Occurs ~1 per 10 billion beauty particles</li>
<li>3.2 sigma statistical significance (below 5 sigma discovery threshold)</li>
<li>Detected by upgraded LHCb experiment at Large Hadron Collider</li>
<li>Search duration: over 40 years</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Scientists 3D-Print Moon Dust into LEGO-Like Bricks for Lunar Construction
Researchers at the University of Arkansas demonstrated 3D-printing simulated lunar dust into interlocking, LEGO-like bricks without binding agents using laser powder bed fusion and sintering. The technology could enable in-situ manufacturing of building materials on the Moon, reducing Earth-to-lunar transport requirements.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Produced small bricks (few centimeters) with interlocking cross and H-shaped pieces</li>
<li>Laser powder bed fusion achieves particle-to-particle bonding without binders</li>
<li>Early development stage; not yet suitable for demanding construction</li>
<li>Published in NPJ Advanced Manufacturing</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Lunar soil oxygen extraction technology enables sustainable moon base operations
Researchers developed a method to extract oxygen from lunar soil using plasma processing adapted from semiconductor manufacturing. Oxygen is essential for astronaut respiration and rocket propellant; transporting from Earth would be prohibitively expensive. NASA announced NextSTEP-3 proposals for lunar oxygen production systems.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Plasma-processing method extracts oxygen from lunar regolith</li>
<li>Adapted from semiconductor manufacturing techniques</li>
<li>Works at lower temperatures than many alternative methods</li>
<li>NASA NextSTEP-3 program seeks lunar oxygen production systems; published Nov 20</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Clean energy pathways create environmental trade-offs beyond carbon reduction
Research examining 24 global climate mitigation pathways found that net-zero energy transitions do not systematically reduce pressures on land, freshwater, and mineral resources. Mineral extraction emerged as a central trade-off, with rapid decarbonization significantly accelerating critical mineral demand.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>24 climate mitigation pathways analyzed; published Nature Energy</li>
<li>Clean energy strategies create trade-offs on land, freshwater, mineral resources</li>
<li>Mineral extraction central trade-off of rapid electrification</li>
<li>Different low-carbon technologies produce vastly different ecological impacts</li>
</ul>

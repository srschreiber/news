### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Researchers identify protein brake blocking nerve repair in spinal injuries
Scientists identified the AHR protein as a molecular brake that suppresses nerve regeneration after spinal and peripheral nerve injuries. Blocking AHR in mouse models enhanced axon regrowth and improved motor recovery, suggesting a therapeutic strategy to shift injured neurons from protection toward active repair.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>AHR protein inhibits nerve fiber regeneration in mouse models</li>
<li>Blocking AHR increased axon growth and restored movement and sensation</li>
<li>Active AHR protects neurons but suppresses growth protein production</li>
<li>Study published in Nature journal</li>
<li>HIF-1α factor involved in regeneration pathway</li>
</ul>

## Energy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Western Australia's iron-rich rocks could yield massive natural hydrogen deposits
Researchers discovered that Western Australia's magnetite-rich banded iron formations naturally generate hydrogen when exposed to hot water underground, with potential to scale into a major clean energy source. Laboratory experiments at 200°C over 60 days demonstrated hydrogen production can be stimulated through solution injection, offering a domestic and export opportunity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Magnetite in Pilbara banded iron formations produces hydrogen naturally</li>
<li>Lab conditions: 200°C temperature, high pressure, 60-day duration</li>
<li>Hydrogen output depends on magnetite quantity, rock structure, and water permeability</li>
<li>Injection methods can deliberately enhance natural production</li>
<li>Potential to sustain Australia&#x27;s energy needs for generations</li>
</ul>

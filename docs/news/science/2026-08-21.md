## Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Fibrinogen research overturns two decades of wound healing science consensus
An international research team overturned the prevailing 20-year-old model of fibrinogen behavior in wound healing, revealing that fibrinogen forms stacked flat layers rather than tilting upright as previously believed. The discovery, published August 20, 2026, has implications for clotting disorders, respiratory conditions, and blood monitoring devices.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Fibrinogen forms stacked flat layers, not single tilting layer model</li>
<li>Previous model dominated field for over 20 years</li>
<li>Neutron reflectometry used on FIGARO instrument at Institut Laue-Langevin (France)</li>
<li>Behavior consistent across multiple concentrations and solution conditions</li>
<li>Implications for haemophilia, warfarin treatment, ARDS, blood biosensors</li>
</ul>

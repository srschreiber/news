### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Greenland sharks' eyes resist aging despite 400-year lifespans
Researchers discovered that Greenland sharks maintain healthy eyes and active light-sensing proteins throughout their 400-year lifespans without typical age-related retinal degeneration. A DNA repair mechanism allows these deep-sea sharks to preserve vision for centuries despite parasites and extreme darkness in their Arctic habitat.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Greenland sharks live 400+ years with functioning vision</li>
<li>DNA repair mechanism preserves eyesight across centuries</li>
<li>Sharks actively track light; not functionally blind despite parasites</li>

## Astrophysics

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Stars survive repeated near-miss encounters with supermassive black holes
Astronomers identified stars that survive repeated close encounters with supermassive black holes, each time producing bursts of light that fade progressively. These repeating partial tidal disruption events occur when a star's rapid rotation allows it to lose mass without being destroyed, returning months or years later for additional passes.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Stars survive multiple near-miss encounters with black holes</li>
<li>Repeating partial tidal disruption events (rpTDEs) occur months or years apart</li>
<li>Fading light bursts correlate with each successive encounter</li>
<li>Star&#x27;s rapid pre-existing rotation enables survival and orbit entry</li>
</ul>

## Energy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Computer models identify catalysts for low-carbon ammonia production
MIT researchers developed computational modeling to identify catalyst materials for electrochemical ammonia production, potentially replacing the century-old fossil fuel-dependent Haber-Bosch process. Ammonia production currently consumes up to 2% of global energy and generates 1.5% of greenhouse gas emissions.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Ammonia production: 2% of global energy consumption, 1.5% of emissions</li>
<li>Haber-Bosch process relies on fossil fuels for over 100 years</li>
<li>Computational approach accelerates catalyst discovery vs. trial-and-error testing</li>
<li>Electrochemical methods exist but lack economic scale competitiveness</li>
</ul>

## Fundamental Physics

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> CERN physicists recreate conditions of early universe using small atomic nuclei collisions
CERN physicists created quark-gluon plasma—the ultra-hot primordial matter from shortly after the Big Bang—by colliding unexpectedly small atomic nuclei (oxygen-16 and neon-20) at nearly light speed. This finding pushes the boundary for the minimum nucleus size needed to recreate this extreme state of matter.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Oxygen-16 and neon-20 nuclei collisions created quark-gluon plasma</li>
<li>Smaller nuclei than previously thought necessary to produce plasma</li>
<li>Research published in Physical Review Letters by ALICE collaboration</li>
<li>Led by University of Copenhagen&#x27;s You Zhou</li>
<li>Plasma exists for tiny fraction of second before converting to other particles</li>
<li>Collision patterns reveal original shapes of colliding nuclei</li>
</ul>

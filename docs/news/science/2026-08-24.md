Astronomers identified the first globular cluster stellar stream outside the Milky Way, a faint ribbon of stars around a distant galaxy created over billions of years as an ancient cluster dispersed. By analyzing the stream's shape, researchers reconstructed the galaxy's gravitational field and estimated dark matter distribution.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First globular cluster stellar stream observed beyond Milky Way</li>
<li>Detected in archival Hubble Space Telescope observations</li>
<li>Stream created over billions of years as cluster shed stars</li>
<li>Researchers modeled gravity to estimate galaxy total mass and dark matter distribution</li>
<li>Offers new method to investigate dark matter across different galaxies</li>
<li>Published August 12 in Nature</li>

Researchers at Southern Illinois University Carbondale have engineered yeasts to convert plastic waste and agricultural residue into edible proteins and food ingredients, creating a product called µBites. Developed with NASA support for food production in extreme environments, the technology uses hydrothermal dissolution to break down PET plastic and plant biomass, then deploys modified yeast strains to metabolize the resulting compounds.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>µBites: protein-rich cookie from waste carbon and yeast-derived ingredients</li>
<li>Engineered yeast strains: Saccharomyces cerevisiae, S. boulardii, Rhodosporidium toruloides</li>
<li>Feedstocks: PET plastic bottles and agricultural biomass like corn stalks</li>
<li>Applications: disaster zones, submarines, deep space missions</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Free-Space Optical Link Added to Nation's Longest Quantum Network
Brookhaven National Laboratory and Stony Brook University demonstrated the first U.S. free-space optical quantum link, transmitting single and entangled photons 13 miles through open air on August 21, 2026. The system uses adaptive optics to counteract atmospheric turbulence and extends the nation's longest quantum network beyond fiber-optic limitations.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First U.S. free-space optical quantum link: 13 miles (21 km) between Stony Brook and Brookhaven</li>
<li>Daytime demonstration August 21, 2026; nighttime entangled photon pair distribution tested</li>
<li>Fiber core diameter: ~5 microns; primary mirror telescope: 0.6 meters</li>
<li>Adaptive optics: kilohertz-frequency deformable mirror corrections for atmospheric turbulence</li>
<li>Transmits infrared wavelengths native to atomic systems without wavelength conversion</li>
<li>Plans: 30-mile extension to Yale; satellite connections for long-distance quantum communications</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> NASA selects team to investigate potential lunar subsurface cave

Lunar caves would provide natural protection from cosmic radiation, micrometeorites, and dust storms on long-duration missions.

</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Quantum fluctuations strengthen superconductivity in ultrathin materials
Researchers at universities in China and MIT demonstrated that quantum vacuum fluctuations can enhance superconductivity in ultrathin materials. Placing NbSe₂ inside a terahertz dark cavity increased its superconducting critical temperature by up to 5.4%, introducing the framework of "vacuumronics" for controlling electronic behavior.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Superconducting critical temperature of NbSe₂ increased up to 5.4% using terahertz dark cavity</li>
<li>Quantum vacuum fluctuations amplified through specially designed resonator</li>
<li>Research introduces vacuumronics framework for engineered quantum vacuum control without external driving</li>
</ul>

University of Illinois physicists identified Cooper pairs organizing above the critical temperature in uranium ditelluride, revealing an unconventional superconducting mechanism. The finding shows electron pairs can persist in a pair density wave state before bulk superconductivity emerges, challenging conventional understanding of how superconductivity forms.

<li>Reveals previously unknown pathway to superconductivity</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Gut bacterial compound ImP linked to increased Alzheimer's risk
Scientists at the University of Wisconsin-Madison identified imidazole propionate (ImP), a gut-bacteria-derived molecule, as a risk factor for Alzheimer's disease. In studies of nearly 1,200 participants, higher blood ImP levels correlated with greater brain protein buildup, neuronal dysfunction, and faster cognitive decline—suggesting ImP reduction as a potential treatment target.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>ImP crosses bloodstream into brain, increases abnormal beta-amyloid and tau protein accumulation</li>
<li>43% of study participants carried genetic variation associated with substantially elevated ImP levels</li>
<li>Higher blood ImP concentrations linked to significantly faster cognitive decline</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Emissions cuts now could limit Antarctic ice loss and sea-level rise for coastal communities
A comprehensive analysis of 340 Antarctic ice-sheet simulations found that Antarctica will almost certainly continue losing ice through 2100 even under very low-emissions scenarios, but the magnitude of loss and resulting sea-level rise depend heavily on today's emissions choices. With roughly 1 billion people living in coastal areas, cutting emissions now can meaningfully reduce future risks to vulnerable populations.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>92% probability Antarctica loses ice by 2100 even under very low-emissions pathway</li>
<li>89% probability high emissions lead to more sea-level rise than lowest-emissions scenario</li>
<li>Antarctic ice sheet holds enough ice to raise sea levels ~190 feet (58 meters)</li>
<li>~1 billion people currently live in coastal areas; ~100 million within 1 meter of sea level</li>
<li>Study analyzed 340 simulations from 16 modeling groups using 12 different models</li>
</ul>

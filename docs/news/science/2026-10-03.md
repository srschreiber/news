### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Quantum fluctuations strengthen superconductivity in ultrathin materials
Researchers at universities in China and MIT demonstrated that quantum vacuum fluctuations can enhance superconductivity in ultrathin materials. Placing NbSe₂ inside a terahertz dark cavity increased its superconducting critical temperature by up to 5.4%, introducing the framework of "vacuumronics" for controlling electronic behavior.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Superconducting critical temperature of NbSe₂ increased up to 5.4% using terahertz dark cavity</li>
<li>Quantum vacuum fluctuations amplified through specially designed resonator</li>
<li>Research introduces vacuumronics framework for engineered quantum vacuum control without external driving</li>
</ul>

Scientists at the University of Wisconsin-Madison identified imidazole propionate (ImP), a gut-bacteria-derived molecule, as a risk factor for Alzheimer's disease. In studies of nearly 1,200 participants, higher blood ImP levels correlated with greater brain protein buildup, neuronal dysfunction, and faster cognitive decline—suggesting ImP reduction as a potential treatment target.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>ImP crosses bloodstream into brain, increases abnormal beta-amyloid and tau protein accumulation</li>
<li>43% of study participants carried genetic variation associated with substantially elevated ImP levels</li>
<li>Higher blood ImP concentrations linked to significantly faster cognitive decline</li>
</ul>

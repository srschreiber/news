## Conservation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Blue and fin whales return to southeast Atlantic after commercial whaling ban
Research led by Bridget James of the University of Cape Town documents increasing whale sightings off Southern Africa's Atlantic coast over 40+ years after the commercial whaling ban. Blue whales and fin whales, driven near extinction by industrial whaling (1913–1978), show signs of slow recovery though current populations remain far below historical levels.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>95% of sightings occurred since 2012</li>
<li>17 blue whale sightings and 1 stranding; 76 fin whale sightings and 6 strandings recorded</li>
<li>Estimated 350,000 Antarctic blue whales and 725,000 fin whales killed during industrial whaling era</li>
<li>Current Antarctic blue whales at ~3% of pre-whaling; fin whales at 30%+ of historical levels</li>
<li>50% of pre-whaling abundance would indicate successful recovery; slow reproduction limits pace</li>
</ul>

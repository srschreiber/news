### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Novel Protein-Blocking Compound Shows Promise Against Alzheimer's in Mice
Researchers at ETH Zurich developed an experimental compound called Compound 10 that blocked a protein-aggregating enzyme linked to Alzheimer's disease in mouse models. The treatment prevented amyloid beta accumulation, slowed nerve cell death, extended lifespan, and showed signs of anti-aging effects in treated animals.

<li>Compound 10 blocks GRK2 enzyme aggregation</li>
<li>Prevented amyloid beta accumulation in mouse brains</li>
<li>Slowed nerve cell death and extended animal lifespan</li>

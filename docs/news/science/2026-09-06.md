### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Researchers identify vulnerability in glioblastoma cancer defenses
Researchers at Ohio State University identified the SET protein as a therapeutic target in glioblastoma, finding that blocking it restores PP2A enzyme activity and increases cancer cells' sensitivity to radiation in preclinical models. An FDA-approved antipsychotic drug showed potential to enhance this effect, though human testing is needed to establish safety and efficacy.

## Archaeology

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> 80,000-Year-Old Projectile Points Suggest Early Sophisticated Weapons Technology
Archaeologists in Uzbekistan discovered 80,000-year-old triangular projectile points showing impact traces consistent with use as arrowheads, closely resembling sophisticated hunting weapons that Homo sapiens produced 25,000 years later in France. The finding suggests early development of advanced projectile technology in Central Asia and challenges Eurocentric frameworks in archaeological dating.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>80,000-year-old projectile points found at Obi-Rakhmat rock shelter in Uzbekistan</li>
<li>Points resemble weapons produced by Homo sapiens 25,000 years later in France</li>
<li>Published in PLOS One, September 2026</li>
<li>Findings challenge Eurocentric archaeological chronologies from 19th century</li>
</ul>

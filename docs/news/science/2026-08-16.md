## AI & Biotech

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> AI could help scientists unlock bacterial viruses to fight antibiotic-resistant infections
Stanford researchers used AI to design 16 novel bacteriophages—viruses that kill bacteria—marking the first time complete viral genomes were successfully designed by artificial intelligence. Published in Science on August 6, the breakthrough could unlock new treatments for antibiotic-resistant infections, though experts warn AI-designed viruses raise urgent biosecurity concerns.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>16 AI-designed bacteriophages proved effective at killing E. coli</li>
<li>Generative AI models Evo1 and Evo2 trained on viral, bacterial, plant, and human genetic codes</li>
<li>First complete viral genomes successfully designed by AI</li>
<li>Designed viruses are fully functional and pose no threat to humans</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Euclid Space Telescope Discovers 31 Ancient Quasars Shining Like Trillions of Suns
ESA's Euclid space telescope discovered 31 ancient quasars, including two record-breaking objects shining with the brightness of a trillion Suns when the universe was 670 million years old. The discovery provides the broadest view yet of the early quasar population and sheds light on how supermassive black holes formed so quickly in the early universe.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>31 ancient quasars discovered by Euclid telescope</li>
<li>Two record-breakers shone with brightness of trillion Suns at universe age 670 million years</li>
<li>Represents earliest quasars ever observed</li>
<li>Euclid also identified fainter quasars previously undetectable, improving population understanding</li>
</ul>

## Physics

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Physicists Use Laser Light to Unlock Crystal's Hidden Electrical Properties
Auburn University physicists discovered how to use laser light pulses to organize polar nanoregions in a strontium titanate crystal, with the alignment lasting approximately one trillionth of a second. The breakthrough resolved a decades-long question about whether these electrical regions form through atomic movement or pre-exist and align during cooling.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Laser bursts organize polar nanoregions in modified strontium titanate crystal</li>
<li>Alignment lasts femtosecond timescale (one trillionth of a second)</li>
<li>Study resolved whether regions form through atomic movement or pre-exist</li>
<li>Discovery could enable new approaches to ultrafast computing and information storage</li>
</ul>

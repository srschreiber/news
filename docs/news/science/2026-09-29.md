## Science

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Anthropic's Claude AI discovers novel enzyme system with CRISPR-like properties
Anthropic announced that its Claude AI model autonomously discovered a previously uncharacterized enzyme system associated with DNA repeats, resembling CRISPR in structure and potential function. The discovery emerged from Claude analyzing massive DNA sequence databases with minimal human direction, marking a significant milestone in AI-assisted biological research.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Claude discovered a reverse transcriptase-based enzyme system with associated non-coding DNA sequences and accessory proteins, a combination previously found in only a handful of other systems</li>
<li>The research involved 950 Claude agents spending 21 hours and using 210 million tokens to search DNA sequence databases</li>
<li>Feng Zhang, a CRISPR pioneer at MIT and the Broad Institute, called the identification of RNA-repeat arrays associated with reverse transcriptases &quot;genuinely intriguing and merits further investigation&quot;</li>
<li>Anthropic formed a dedicated life sciences research group and built its own laboratory to systematically use Claude for fundamental biology research</li>
</ul>

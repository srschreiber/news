<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Daily transits reduce from 36 to 34 (Sept 3), then to 32 (Sept 15)</li>
<li>Watershed rainfall down 34% May–August vs. historical average</li>
<li>Canal handles 5% of global maritime trade and ~40% of US container traffic</li>
<li>Normal capacity ~40 vessels/day; averaging 35 since June 2026</li>
<li>2023 drought reduced traffic from 38 to 22 daily vessels (36% decrease)</li>
</ul>

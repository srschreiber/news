## Israeli-Palestinian Conflict

### Four Palestinians and two Israelis killed in West Bank shooting; Israel plans military operation
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Six people died July 24 after settlers entered a restricted West Bank area near the Palestinian village of Tell for an unauthorized hike, sparking clashes. The Israeli military said a group of settlers entered an area of the West Bank restricted to Israeli civilians for an "unauthorized and uncoordinated hike," and as the group approached the village of Tell, clashes broke out between the settlers and residents. In the ensuing gunfire, four Palestinians and two Israeli soldiers — one of whom was also a settler — were killed. The IDF cancelled leave for soldiers in the area and prepared reinforcements for a "large-scale" operation to pursue "dozens of Palestinians" involved, while Finance Minister Bezalel Smotrich called for demolishing Palestinian villages nearby. Separately, settlers set fire to homes and vehicles in the village of Faraata, wounding eight Palestinians.

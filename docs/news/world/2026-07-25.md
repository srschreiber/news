### Ukraine removes defence minister amid military leadership crisis
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ President Zelensky dismissed defense minister Mykhailo Fedorov, who served as Ukraine's defense minister for six months and pushed for the use of sophisticated drone warfare in Russia's war on Ukraine, coinciding with a dramatic shift in Ukraine's favor on the battlefield. Fedorov publicly clashed with military chief Gen. ## International Law

### ICC chief prosecutor Karim Khan removed from post over sexual misconduct allegations
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Member states of the ICC voted to dismiss Karim Khan as prosecutor; a majority of the court's 125 member states voted in favour of his removal, nearly two years after sexual misconduct allegations against him first emerged. Khan had overseen the issuing of warrants for top Israeli officials over the war in Gaza and investigations into United States personnel in Afghanistan, and the Trump administration has already imposed sanctions on Khan and a dozen other ICC staff in retaliation, and recently declared it would seek to destroy the ICC. Khan has denied any wrongdoing, and his lawyers have called the process that led to the vote unlawful, procedurally unfair and unsupported by evidence.

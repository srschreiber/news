### Houthis attack Saudi oil facilities as Iran conflict expands to Red Sea
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ On July 25, 2026, Iran-backed Houthi rebels fired two ballistic missiles at Saudi Aramco oil refineries in Yanbu on the Red Sea; Saudi air defenses intercepted both and no damage was reported. The strikes followed a Houthi-claimed missile and drone attack days earlier on two Saudi oil tankers, including the Encelia, and a Houthi-declared naval blockade of Saudi Arabia, prompting Saudi coalition airstrikes on Houthi targets in Yemen. The escalation opens a second front alongside the US-Iran war, in which US forces conducted a 13th consecutive night of strikes on Iran; the president of the United States said the US would hold Iran responsible for further Houthi attacks. Analysts warn a full blockade of the Bab el-Mandeb Strait could cut roughly 7% of global oil supply, compounding disruptions from the closed Strait of Hormuz.

### Wildfires force evacuations in Spain and France
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Wildfires spread across southern France and Spain in late July 2026, forcing more than 250,000 people from homes and holiday resorts, according to Al Jazeera. Spain's government declared a national emergency as blazes near Madrid and Ávila, including the 32,000-hectare La Mierla fire in Guadalajara, prompted evacuations; in France a major fire reached the outskirts of Bordeaux, pushing the regional evacuation total to 110,000, with residents fleeing by boat. No deaths were reported in France or Spain, though Sicily's wildfires killed one firefighter amid a broader European heatwave; Spain's total burned area has doubled in two weeks to nearly 120,000 hectares this year.

### India's 'Cockroach' youth protest movement ousts education minister
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ India's education minister Dharmendra Pradhan resigned on Saturday, July 25, 2026, after 36 days of nationwide protests led by the satirical 'Cockroach Janta Party' (CJP), founded by Abhijeet Dipke, over leaks and irregularities in competitive entrance exams such as NEET. The government agreed to all remaining CJP demands, including broader education-system reforms, compensation for families of students who died by suicide over exam cancellations, and a pledge not to prosecute protesters, prompting the movement to end its demonstrations. The unrest, which spread beyond New Delhi to over a dozen cities and included a 26-day hunger strike by activist Sonam Wangchuk, marks the first major concession by Prime Minister Narendra Modi's government to a protest movement in recent years.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Quake registered shindo 7, Japan&#x27;s highest intensity level, at 10km depth</li>

<li>~40 injured treated in Yatsushiro, ~50 in Kumamoto city</li>
<li>At least 12 buildings collapsed; cargo train derailed; fires reported</li>

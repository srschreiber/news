### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Ukraine's naval drones reshape naval warfare and tactics at sea
Ukraine deployed armed naval drones in sea-to-sea combat, fundamentally reshaping maritime warfare tactics. Unlike land warfare where drones favor defense, naval drones provide offensive advantage through inherent expendability—even if hundreds are destroyed, one successful strike achieves the objective. The U.S. Navy adopted Ukraine's approach during the July 2026 RIMPAC exercise, using autonomous surface attack drones to sink the decommissioned USS Peleliu.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Ukraine deployed armed uncrewed surface vehicles (USVs) in sea-to-sea combat</li>
<li>Naval drones provide offensive asymmetry through expendability calculus</li>
<li>U.S. Navy deployed Global Autonomous Reconnaissance Craft (GARC) during RIMPAC July 2026</li>
<li>Two Navy GARCs helped sink decommissioned USS Peleliu (LHA-5) off Hawaii July 17</li>
</ul>

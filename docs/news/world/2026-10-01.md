### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> California becomes 18th state to ban child marriage, effective January 1, 2025
Governor Gavin Newsom signed legislation prohibiting marriage for anyone under 18 in California, ending a practice still legal in 32 U.S. states. The law takes effect January 1 and was signed alongside child marriage survivor Courtney Stodden, who has championed the cause nationally.

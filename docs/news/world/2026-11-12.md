### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Russia Launches Major Strike on Ukraine Hours After Abu Dhabi Peace Talks
Russia launched a major strike on Ukraine hours after peace talks in Abu Dhabi concluded on November 12, 2026. The attack involved 123 attack drones and ballistic missiles targeting Kyiv and other regions. Ukraine's air defenses shot down 94 of the drones but no missiles got through. One person was killed in Kyiv and five injured, according to Mayor Vitaliy Klitschko. Fires were reported in Rivne and Zhytomyr regions. Russia's defense ministry said it was targeting drone production sites and electricity substations. Russia claimed to have shot down approximately 70 Ukrainian drones over eight regions overnight. The strike came immediately after trilateral peace talks mediated by the United States, where Russia and Ukraine agreed to prepare another major prisoner swap but disagreed sharply on territorial issues and Europe's participation in future negotiations. David Arakhamia, a Ukrainian delegation member, called the negotiations some of the toughest yet, citing strict Russian demands and little room for compromise. Vladimir Medinsky, head of the Russian delegation, said Ukraine was "an abscess that burst" and reiterated demands for Ukrainian territory plus establishment of a new security architecture in Europe.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> World Heading for 'Irrevocable Overshoot' of Global Temperature Thresholds, Analysis Warns
Climate Action Tracker analysis warns the world is heading for an 'irrevocable overshoot' of global temperature thresholds. Under current policies, the planet will heat up by 2.7°C by the end of the century compared with preindustrial times, up slightly from a 2.6°C prediction in the group's analysis from the previous year. The 1.5°C threshold agreed by countries a decade ago is set to be breached in just a few years. Scientists warn that beyond 1.5°C, already harmful storms, droughts, heatwaves, flooding and other climate impacts will become intolerable for parts of the world and potentially trigger runaway climate change. The analysis states that failure to cut emissions will 'consign humanity to disasters they are unable to adapt to.' The report notes that if countries acted decisively to slash emissions, future temperature projections would become less daunting. UN climate talks are underway in Antalya, Turkey, centered on the looming overshoot of the temperature target.

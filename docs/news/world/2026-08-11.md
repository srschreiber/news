## Natural Disaster

### <span class="imp imp-5" title="Importance 5/5" aria-label="Importance 5 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i></span> 7.4 magnitude earthquake strikes Colombia, kills at least 111
A 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia on August 10, 2026, killing at least 111 people and damaging over 1,600 buildings, marking the country's strongest quake in a decade.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>7.4-magnitude earthquake, highest in Colombia in 10 years</li>
<li>At least 111 killed, 87 injured; toll expected to rise</li>
<li>Epicenter near San José del Palmar, Chocó region, ~250 miles west of Bogotá</li>
<li>Over 1,600 buildings damaged; dozens collapsed across Cali, Pereira, Manizales, Quibdó</li>
<li>Two aftershocks of 2.8 and 4.8 magnitude followed</li>
<li>Multiple airports suspended flights; no tsunami threat</li>
<li>Chocó region is rural and poorest in Colombia, accessible mainly by boat or plane</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> 7.4 magnitude earthquake strikes Colombia, death toll reaches 132
A 7.4 magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia on August 10, killing at least 132 people and injuring over 570, making it the country's strongest recorded earthquake of the 21st century; the president of the United States announced $15.5 million in emergency aid.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Magnitude 7.4, epicenter San Jose Del Palmar in Choco region, 250 miles west of Bogota</li>
<li>At least 132 deaths, 570+ injured, ~1,600 buildings damaged, 61 completely collapsed</li>
<li>Colombia&#x27;s strongest earthquake of 21st century; followed by 21 aftershocks</li>
<li>Affected Cali, Pereira, Quibdo, Manizales; felt in Ecuador and Panama</li>
<li>President of the United States provided $15.5 million emergency aid</li>
</ul>

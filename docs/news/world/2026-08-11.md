A 7.4-magnitude earthquake struck western Colombia on August 10, 2026, killing at least 111 people and damaging over 1,600 buildings, marking the country's strongest quake in a decade.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>7.4-magnitude earthquake, highest in Colombia in 10 years</li>

<li>Two aftershocks of 2.8 and 4.8 magnitude followed</li>
<li>Multiple airports suspended flights; no tsunami threat</li>
<li>Chocó region is rural and poorest in Colombia, accessible mainly by boat or plane</li>
</ul>

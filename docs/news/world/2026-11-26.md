### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Russia Passes Largest Military Budget Since Soviet Collapse; 2027-2029 Defense Spending Surges
Russia approved a 50 trillion ruble ($609 billion) military budget for 2027–2029, with 2027 spending at $209 billion—the largest single-year defense budget since the Soviet era. Defense will consume 35% of Russia's 2027 federal budget, funded by cutting civilian sector spending across healthcare, housing, and social support.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>50 trillion rubles ($609 billion) for 2027–2029; largest three-year defense budget since Soviet collapse</li>
<li>2027: $209 billion; 2028: $202 billion; 2029: $198.4 billion</li>
<li>Defense consumes ~35% of 2027 federal budget ($593.3 billion total)</li>
<li>Cumulative military spending since Feb 2022 invasion: ~$918 billion (exceeds Russia&#x27;s reserves of $718.8 billion)</li>
</ul>

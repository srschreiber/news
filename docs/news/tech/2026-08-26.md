### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Apple announces Mac Mini and Mac Studio with M6, M5 Pro, M5 Max, and M5 Ultra chips
Apple released new Mac Mini models with M6 and M5 Pro chips and Mac Studio models with M5 Max and M5 Ultra processors, offering various memory and storage configurations.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>M5 Ultra: quad-die (two fused dual-die M5 Max), up to 36 CPU cores, up to 80 GPU cores, 1.2 TB/s unified memory bandwidth</li>
<li>M5 Ultra bandwidth 50% greater than M3 Ultra (released ~18 months prior)</li>
<li>M6: 2nm process, 12-core CPU, Dual 16-core Neural Engine, 2 additional cores vs M5</li>
<li>M6 provides ~30% increase in peak GPU compute for AI vs M5</li>
<li>Mac Mini M6 base model available for pre-order from $899; shipping begins September 22, 2026</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> SpaceX plans construction of $100 billion Starbase spaceport in Louisiana
SpaceX announced plans to build Starbase Louisiana, a $100 billion spaceport on Pecan Island covering 125,000 acres, with construction beginning in 2027 and first Starship launches in 2029. At full capacity, the facility will support up to 30 daily flights and thousands of annual launches.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$100 billion Starbase Louisiana on 125,000 acres in Vermilion Parish</li>
<li>Construction begins 2027; first Starship launches planned for 2029</li>
<li>Five launch complexes with two pads each; supports 30 flights per day</li>
<li>First phase projected to generate 3,000 direct jobs over next decade</li>
<li>Estimated 11,100+ total new jobs including indirect employment in Acadiana</li>
</ul>

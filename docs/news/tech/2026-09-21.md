### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> NASA/ESA Mars Sample Return Mission Canceled
NASA and ESA's Mars Sample Return mission was officially cancelled in January 2026 by Congress. The mission's estimated cost ballooned to $11 billion, later revised to approximately $7 billion. Perseverance has collected 30 samples in tubes, with ten cached at Three Forks and the remainder aboard the rover. No approved retrieval mission currently exists.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Cancellation: January 2026 by U.S. Congress</li>
<li>Cost estimate growth: $11 billion, later revised to ~$7 billion</li>
<li>Perseverance samples collected: 30 in available tubes plus three witness tubes</li>
<li>Cached samples: Ten tubes at Three Forks cache, early 2023</li>
<li>Remaining tubes: Aboard Perseverance rover with no authorized retrieval</li>
<li>Funding allocated instead: $110 million for &#x27;Mars Future Missions&#x27;</li>
</ul>

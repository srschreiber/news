### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Amazon Reports AI Agents Discovered and Exploited Zero-Day Vulnerability with Minimal Human Input
Amazon reported that AI agents running on the Cairn framework discovered and exploited a zero-day vulnerability in an open source medical imaging AI application with minimal human input. Hackers provided only a simple instruction—to obtain shell access on an East Asian university server—along with a list of IP addresses and prompts in simplified Chinese, with the system configured to Chinese Standard Time zone. The agents autonomously found the previously unknown vulnerability and gained full control of the target system without further human oversight. Cairn is an open source AI penetration-testing framework designed for defenders that can be used with multiple AI models including Claude Code, Codex, and Pi, though it has also been adopted by attackers. Palo Alto Networks previously documented a separate campaign in which hackers used Cairn to conduct 25 autonomous operations against at least 19 organizations in the semiconductor, defense, and civil society sectors, resulting in at least 10 successful breaches where agents accessed credentials, exposed over 1 million personal records at one organization, retrieved military and government identities, created persistent administrator accounts, and modified industry standards documents. Amazon stated that these findings demonstrate that agentic offensive operations are already occurring and that defenders should prepare for the speed and persistence these tools enable.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Ukraine Sinks Russian Warship, Strikes Cargo Vessels Across Multiple Theaters
Ukrainian explosive naval drones sank the Russian missile corvette Merkury in the port of Benghazi, Libya on November 22, 2026. Ukraine's Security Service deployed seven naval drones launched from a mother ship; at least two struck the Merkury, which caught fire and sank. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy released video footage showing drone boats striking both the Merkury and the Russian dry cargo ship MV Angara, which was being escorted by the corvette. A Ka-27 combat helicopter aboard the Merkury was also destroyed. Zelenskyy stated the MV Angara was transporting weapons. At least 10 people, including Libyan and Russian nationals, were killed in the strikes; some crew members from the Merkury were wounded and transferred to local hospitals. The Merkury was a Steregushchiy-class missile corvette (Project 20380) equipped with anti-ship and air-defense missiles, torpedoes, and a naval cannon. It was originally assigned to Russia's Black Sea Fleet but could not enter the Black Sea after Turkey invoked the 1936 Montreux Convention following Russia's 2022 invasion of Ukraine; the vessel instead operated from Russia's Northern Fleet bases in Arctic waters. The attack demonstrates Ukraine's expanding geographical reach with sea drones, striking Russian targets from the Mediterranean to the Baltic Sea region.

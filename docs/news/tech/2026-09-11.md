## Cybersecurity Regulation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> EU Cyber Resilience Act activates 24-hour vulnerability disclosure requirement
The EU's Cyber Resilience Act mandatory reporting requirements took effect September 11, 2026, requiring manufacturers of products with digital elements to report actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe security incidents through ENISA's platform within strict timeframes. The rules apply globally to any product sold in the EU.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Early warning required within 24 hours of discovering actively exploited vulnerability</li>
<li>Detailed notification within 72 hours, final report within 14 days for vulnerabilities</li>
<li>Requirements apply to all manufacturers regardless of location if products sold in EU</li>
<li>Reports submitted to coordinating CSIRT via ENISA&#x27;s Single Reporting Platform</li>
<li>Effective date September 11, 2026</li>
</ul>

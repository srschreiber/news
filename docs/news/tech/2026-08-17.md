### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> $12 Billion in US Utility Ratepayer Funds Lost to PJM Modeling Error
PJM, operator of America's largest electricity market serving 66 million residents, lost approximately $12 billion in ratepayer funds between 2025 and 2027 due to a computational modeling error. The error underestimated power plant capacity by roughly 4 gigawatts, leading to unnecessary procurement and contributing to household electricity bills rising about 20% in the PJM region.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$12 billion in ratepayer losses (2025–2027)</li>
<li>4 gigawatt capacity underestimation due to flawed model</li>
<li>20% household electricity bill increase in PJM area</li>
<li>$6.7 billion savings possible for 2025/26 with corrected modeling</li>
</ul>

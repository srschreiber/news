### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Cisco Secure Email Gateway zero-day enables root command execution under active attack
Cisco disclosed a critical zero-day vulnerability (CVE-2026-76461, CVSS 9.8) in Secure Email Gateway that allows unauthenticated attackers to execute arbitrary commands with root privileges via malicious email messages. The flaw is actively being exploited in the wild and affects both physical and virtual devices; patching is required as no workarounds exist.

<li>Actively exploited in the wild</li>
<li>Fixed versions: AsyncOS 15.5.5-0141, 16.0.4-302, 16.5.0-780</li>
<li>CISA requires Federal agencies to patch by September 17, 2026</li>
<li>CVE-2026-76461 (CVSS 9.8): critical vulnerability in AsyncOS email handling</li>
<li>Unauthenticated remote code execution via malicious email</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> K-Server Conjecture Proven True
Researchers Christian Coester, Elias Koutsoupias, and Marek Zbysiński proved the k-server conjecture, a decades-long open problem in algorithm theory. The proof demonstrates that the work function algorithm achieves a competitive ratio of k on every metric space using an algebraic matrix representation approach.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>k-server conjecture proven true (decades-old open problem)</li>
<li>Work function algorithm achieves competitive ratio k on all metric spaces</li>
<li>Matrix algebraic representation encodes feasible paths</li>
<li>Paper submitted to arXiv September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Amortized analysis uses potential function with matrix coordinates</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> US Datacenter Construction Outpacing Electrical Grid Expansion
The U.S. is constructing datacenters faster than the electrical grid can support, with demand projected to reach 426 terawatt-hours in 2030—nearly double 2025 levels. Meeting this demand requires approximately $110 billion in new generation resources and will add $25–$30 billion annually to electricity system costs. Grid expansion bottlenecks include lengthy permitting and equipment lead times of up to seven years.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Datacenter power demand projected 426 TWh in 2030 (vs. ~210 TWh in 2025)</li>
<li>$110 billion new generation resources needed by 2030</li>
<li>Grid expansion to cost $25–$30 billion annually through 2030</li>
<li>Transmission buildout: $80–$115 billion already approved/under construction; potential to triple next decade</li>
<li>Half of planned 2026 U.S. datacenter capacity unlikely online due to power availability (Jefferies, June 2026)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> US AI data center boom strains grid as power demand grows 10x faster than capacity
US AI data center power consumption is growing approximately ten times faster than grid capacity expansion, creating an electricity infrastructure crisis. Data centers' grid share reached 5% in 2025 (up from 0.1% in 2000), and operators face extended connection delays as energy demand may outpace supply by decade's end.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>AI data center power growth: ~10× grid expansion rate since 2000</li>
<li>Annual growth: 19% data center demand vs. 2% grid capacity expansion</li>
<li>Data center grid share: 5% in 2025 (50-fold increase from 0.1% in 2000)</li>
<li>Projected 2026 AI energy consumption growth: 26%</li>
<li>110 GW behind-the-meter capacity needed by 2030 to maintain 5% grid share</li>
</ul>

</ul>

## AI Infrastructure

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Google Accelerates AI Performance by Removing Python from Query Pipeline
Google open-sourced XSE (XLA Serving Engine), which handles over six million queries per second for Search, Ads, and YouTube Shorts without Python in its serving pipeline. The C++ runtime delivers 2–5x throughput of optimized Python servers while using a quarter of the memory.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>XSE handles 6+ million queries per second across Search, Ads, YouTube Shorts</li>
<li>2–5x throughput improvement vs. optimized Python; quarter the memory usage</li>
<li>Workflow: Python/JAX model code exported via StableHLO to C++ runtime with XLA compilation</li>
<li>Integration with TorchTPU planned for PyTorch users</li>
</ul>

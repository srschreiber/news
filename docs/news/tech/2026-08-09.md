### FCC Proposes Ban on Lidar-Equipped Foreign Drones
<span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> The FCC proposed retroactively banning foreign-made drones equipped with LiDAR sensors by classifying the technology as military-grade, a move that would strip sales approval from models like DJI's Air 3S and Mini 5 Pro.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Builds on December 22, 2025 action adding foreign drones to FCC&#x27;s Covered List</li>
<li>Military-grade classification also covers thermal imaging, agricultural spray, 55+ lb weight, drone swarms</li>
<li>Existing owners can keep flying but may struggle to source spare parts</li>
<li>U.S.-made drones exempt; public comment open until September 2, 2026</li>
<li>DJI already suing over the earlier December ban, calling LiDAR a safety feature</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Google warns of fully-automated AI-powered attacks accelerating at machine speed
Google warns that threat actors are deploying agentic AI frameworks to automate vulnerability discovery and exploitation, compressing attack timelines to machine speed. In a May 2026 announcement, Google Cloud introduced Google AI Threat Defense, described as a comprehensive AI-powered cybersecurity solution designed to defend enterprises against AI-powered threats. The platform addresses a critical shift in the threat landscape: attacks that previously took weeks can now occur in hours or days. Google AI Threat Defense uses multiple AI models for analysis, combines expertise from Mandiant and Wiz with Gemini's reasoning and code-generation capabilities, and deploys LLM-powered analysis to autonomously discover software flaws and deploy fixes faster than adversaries can exploit them. The system prioritizes critical real-world risks, predicts potential attack paths, and continuously monitors for new attacks. Google's secure-by-default architecture reportedly blocks 10 million spam emails per minute and protects billions of users across its portfolio. The cybersecurity industry has responded by developing AI-powered solutions across detection, triage, and response, as frontier AI becomes increasingly capable at vulnerability discovery and exploitation.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Amazon's Zoox Robotaxis Now Available on Uber in Las Vegas
Amazon's Zoox autonomous vehicle company launched robotaxi service through the Uber app in Las Vegas on November 16, 2026, marking Zoox's first partnership with a third-party ride-hailing platform and its first commercial deployment of robotaxis. Initially, customers requesting UberX, Uber Comfort, Uber Electric, Uber Comfort Electric, or Wait & Save rides may be matched with a Zoox robotaxi; dedicated autonomous selection is not yet available but customers can increase matching odds via "ride preferences" in the app. Zoox and Uber also announced a multi-year partnership with MGM Resorts International covering MGM properties in Las Vegas. The Nevada Transportation Authority previously approved Uber to deploy up to 1,000 robotaxis in Clark County through partnerships including Zoox and Motional. Zoox plans to begin initial operations in Los Angeles later in 2026 for employees and family, with commercial service launching in 2027 and Uber app availability in 2027. Uber CEO Dara Khosrowshahi stated Las Vegas will become the first U.S. city with two fully driverless partners accessible through Uber's app. Uber plans to introduce a dedicated autonomous ride option as "availability and reliability improve." This follows Uber's earlier partnership with Waymo, which is exclusive in Austin and Atlanta and set to end in January 2028.

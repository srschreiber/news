### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Google DeepMind releases genome atlas using AI
Google DeepMind released AlphaGenome Atlas, a publicly available petabyte-scale database containing AlphaGenome AI predictions of nine billion possible nucleotide variations in the human genome. The platform assigns each variant an AlphaGenome Variant Impact (AVI) score, enabling researchers to identify genetic variants more efficiently than brute-force searching.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>AlphaGenome Atlas contains petabyte of data on 9 billion nucleotide variations</li>
<li>Each variant assigned AVI score ranking likelihood of biological impact</li>
<li>GREGoR Consortium pilot used AVI scores to identify DNM1 gene variants linked to epileptic encephalopathy</li>
<li>Platform builds on AlphaGenome AI model from prior year</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Google DeepMind Alumni Launch Fusionality to Accelerate Fusion Power Development
Google DeepMind alumni Federico Felici and Jonas Buchli founded Fusionality to develop control systems and simulation tools for fusion power startups. The Lausanne-based company raised $3.7 million in pre-seed funding and aims to provide reusable components, as approximately 80% of each fusion company's control system is typically duplicated across designs.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Fusionality raised $3.7 million (CHF 3 million) pre-seed from Founderful and Playfair</li>
<li>~80% of fusion company control systems are identical across designs despite custom rebuilding</li>
<li>Felici (CEO) and Buchli (CTO) previously developed AI systems for EPFL tokamak and Google DeepMind</li>
<li>AI to play supporting role optimizing control systems, not fully autonomous reactor control</li>

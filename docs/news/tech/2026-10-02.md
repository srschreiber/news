## Medical Device

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> FDA Approves Growth-Capable Artificial Heart Valve for Pediatric Patients
The FDA approved the Autus Valve, an artificial heart valve that expands as children grow, eliminating the need for multiple replacement surgeries in pediatric patients with congenital pulmonary valve disease. Instead of replacements every few years, the valve can be adjusted via minimally invasive balloon dilation procedures.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Designed for pediatric congenital pulmonary valve disease</li>
<li>First implantation at Nationwide Children&#x27;s Hospital; trials began late 2021</li>
<li>Adjustable via minimally invasive transcatheter balloon dilation</li>
<li>Estimated 110,000 U.S. children affected by target condition</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> US arrests tech CEO for smuggling $300 million in Nvidia chips to China
The US Department of Justice arrested Greg Lui, 38-year-old CEO of Earthmade Computer, on October 2, 2026, accused of smuggling over $300 million worth of export-controlled Nvidia A100 and H100 GPUs to China. Using false paperwork and conspirators in Malaysia and Singapore, Lui diverted shipments via fake purchase orders and fabricated identities from October 2023 to August 2026. Evidence includes fraudulent documentation and payments totaling over $176 million to his accounts.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$300 million in Nvidia chips smuggled via Malaysia and Singapore</li>
<li>Nvidia A100 and H100 GPUs diverted to Hangzhou firm</li>
<li>October 2023–August 2026 scheme duration</li>
<li>$176 million in payments to Lui&#x27;s US bank accounts in 2024 alone</li>
<li>Faces up to 20 years per count for export violations and money laundering</li>
</ul>

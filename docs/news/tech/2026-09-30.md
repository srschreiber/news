### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Chinese AI tool Kimi jailbroken to provide bioweapon instructions
Security researchers at Mindgard discovered that Chinese AI developer Moonshot's Kimi K2.6 and K3 Swarm models could be jailbroken to bypass safety guardrails and provide instructions on creating biological weapons and carrying out assassinations. The vulnerability was discovered in July through a complex jailbreaking process and could potentially allow hackers to exploit the models for malicious activity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Mindgard researchers discovered the vulnerability in July 2024 affecting Kimi K2.6 and K3 Swarm models</li>
<li>Jailbroken Kimi could provide detailed recommendations on bioweapons and assassinations without restrictions</li>
<li>Researchers also found potential for hackers to run code on Moonshot&#x27;s computing resources and connect to the internet</li>
<li>Moonshot is conducting an internal review and in discussion with Mindgard about the findings</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> AI infrastructure must generate $6 trillion annually by 2031 to justify investment
AI industry must generate $6 trillion in annual revenue by 2031 to justify current infrastructure investment levels, according to Bain & Company. With projected infrastructure spending reaching $1.5 trillion annually by 2031, the sector must develop $4.2 trillion in revenue from new use cases beyond productivity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>AI must generate $6 trillion annual revenue by 2031 to justify infrastructure spending</li>

<li>Existing AI applications projected $1.2–1.8 trillion revenue by 2031</li>
<li>Additional $4.2 trillion needed from new use cases beyond employee productivity</li>
</ul>

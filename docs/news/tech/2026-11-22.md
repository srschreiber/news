## Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> GLP-1 drugs could dramatically reduce global food demand and free farmland
Research modeling widespread GLP-1 drug adoption suggests reduced food demand could free up to 46 million hectares of agricultural land—larger than Italy—while preventing up to 7 million deaths annually by 2050. However, lower food prices may increase consumption among non-users, partially offsetting environmental gains.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Widespread adoption could free ~46 million hectares of farmland by 2050</li>
<li>Up to 7 million deaths preventable annually with healthy diet shifts</li>
<li>Area freed is larger than Italy in size</li>
<li>Lower food prices may paradoxically boost consumption among non-users</li>
<li>Effects depend on interconnected global food systems</li>
</ul>

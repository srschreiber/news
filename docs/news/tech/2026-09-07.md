### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Hackers claim $320 million Bitcoin theft from Liquid Network as responsible disclosure
Self-described white-hat hackers stole approximately 4,000 BTC (roughly $320 million) from the Liquid Network federation wallet on September 7. The attackers requested Blockstream contact them and promised to return most of the Bitcoin once the vulnerability is patched and all nodes updated. Investigation found the theft used an authorized peg-out, raising questions about how the attack bypassed security.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>4,000 BTC (~$320 million) stolen from Liquid Network federation wallet</li>
<li>Theft removed ~95% of wallet&#x27;s ~4,200 BTC holdings</li>
<li>Attack used authorized peg-out; unclear how security was bypassed</li>
<li>Attackers identified as white-hats, promised to return most BTC after patching and node updates</li>
<li>Liquid disabled bridge nodes; exchanges suspended L-BTC deposits and withdrawals</li>
</ul>

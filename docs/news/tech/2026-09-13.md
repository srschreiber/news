### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Modern vehicles collect and sell personal driving data
General Motors was banned for five years by the Federal Trade Commission in 2026 for collecting driver data—including speeding frequency and nighttime driving patterns—and selling it to data brokers for insurance profiling without adequate transparency. Other automakers engage in similar undisclosed data collection practices.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>FTC issued five-year ban against General Motors in 2026</li>
<li>Smart Driver feature collected location, speeding, and nighttime driving data</li>
<li>Data sold to LexisNexis and Verisk for insurance risk profiling</li>
<li>Drivers unknowingly consented through unclear enrollment; some saw insurance rate increases</li>
<li>Settlement requires easier location tracking disabling and data access/deletion rights</li>
</ul>

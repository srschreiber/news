### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Trump administration deploys AI to deny Medicare coverage for seniors in controversial pilot program
The president of the United States administration rolled out the WISeR pilot program using AI to deny or authorize medical claims for Medicare patients in six states. The program requires prior authorization for procedures including nerve stimulation, epidural injections, and cervical fusions. Reports indicate technical delays, puzzling denials, and frustrated providers despite questions about legality.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>WISeR pilot program launched January 2026 in six states (NJ, OH, OK, TX, AZ, WA)</li>
<li>Requires prior authorization for ~12 medical services not previously requiring pre-approval</li>
<li>Government Accountability Office found program may not follow proper procedure</li>
<li>Reports of long delays, technical issues, and provider frustration</li>
<li>Program scheduled to run through 2031 with plans to expand; House committee vote on oversight rejected</li>
</ul>

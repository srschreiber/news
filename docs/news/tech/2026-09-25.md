### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Attacker uses open-source AI agents to breach 25+ major organizations including Fortune 500 firms

<li>Attacker used Hermes AI agent as orchestrator with persona &#x27;SOUL - Red Team Operator&#x27;</li>

</ul>

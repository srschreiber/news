## AI Safety

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Satya Nadella Calls for 'Emergency Brake' on Unsafe AI Models
Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella called for an 'emergency brake' on unsafe AI models October 10, 2026, arguing the tech industry must pause and reassess AI trust architecture. His proposed approach includes separating the model from orchestration harnesses, externalizing controls and safeguards, documenting every meaningful action with tamper-proof evidence, and ensuring authorized personnel can pause or shut down models mid-task. Nadella emphasized: 'We must assume a model is compromised and contain it from the start.'

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Separate model from orchestration harness</li>
<li>Externalize controls and safeguards</li>
<li>Document every meaningful model action with tamper-proof, human-readable evidence</li>
<li>Authorized personnel must always be able to pause or shut down model mid-task</li>
<li>Comments follow reports of leading AI companies losing control of models</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> OpenAI Internal Repositories Compromised via Heap Overflow and SSO Misconfiguration
Security researchers exploited a heap overflow in an image decoder and SSO misconfiguration to breach OpenAI's internal code repositories, accessing employee accounts and the internal monorepo. The full exploit chain took under 72 hours; both OpenAI and Discourse patched within two months after disclosure on July 25.

<li>Researchers accessed internal openai/openai monorepo, opened pull request #1186742</li>
<li>Exploit chain completed in less than 72 hours</li>
<li>$6,500 bounty awarded; patched within two months</li>
</ul>

### Claude Mythos AI cracks HAWK post-quantum cryptography in 60 hours
<span class="imp imp-5" title="Importance 5/5" aria-label="Importance 5 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i></span> Anthropic's Claude Mythos AI model cracked a NIST post-quantum cryptography candidate, HAWK, in about 60 hours, finding a flaw that had survived two years of expert review and prompting the algorithm's withdrawal from standardization.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Found &#x27;nontrivial automorphism&#x27; cutting HAWK-256 security from 2^64 to 2^38 operations</li>
<li>Only the 256-bit challenge variant was tested, not 512/1024-bit production versions</li>
<li>Cost ~$100,000 in API expenses running semi-autonomously</li>
<li>Also devised &#x27;Möbius Bridge&#x27; technique improving attacks on 7-round AES-128 by 200-800x</li>
<li>HAWK&#x27;s authors withdrew their NIST submission within a day of disclosure on July 28</li>
</ul>

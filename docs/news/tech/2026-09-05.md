## Space Exploration

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Europe's BepiColombo spacecraft enters final approach to Mercury after 8 years
Europe's €1.5 billion BepiColombo mission separated from its propulsion module on September 4, 2026, after an 8-year journey to Mercury. The two science orbiters are scheduled to enter Mercury orbit in November, with the Japanese magnetospheric orbiter separating in December, beginning a study of Mercury's origins and characteristics in April 2027.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Blackstone real estate company exposes sensitive personal data through GraphQL vulnerability
A Beam Living property management system owned by Blackstone exposed sensitive applicant data through an unsecured GraphQL endpoint. The vulnerability allowed attackers to retrieve Social Security numbers, dates of birth, addresses, credit scores, and other personal information by querying with another user's email address, requiring no authentication bypass. The exposure affected multiple communities including Stuy Town and Peter Cooper Village.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Vulnerability discovered August 24, 2026</li>
<li>Exposed Social Security digits, dates of birth, addresses, credit scores, emergency contacts</li>
<li>Affected Stuy Town, Peter Cooper Village, and other Beam Living communities</li>
<li>No authentication required—users could query other applicants&#x27; emails directly</li>
<li>GraphQL endpoint: pd-dlcore.beamliving.com/graphql</li>
</ul>

U.S. data centers consumed approximately 17 billion gallons of water in 2023, triple the amount used in 2014, driven largely by AI infrastructure expansion following ChatGPT's release. Indirect water consumption from off-site power generation accounts for over 80% of data centers' total water footprint, while the federal government lacks systematic assessment of industry-wide water usage.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Canada Suspends Trade Negotiations with USA and Implements Dollar-for-Dollar Tariff Matching
Canada suspended trade negotiations with the United States on August 21, 2026, after objecting to last-minute changes in U.S. terms. In response to a U.S. tariff of 50% on $28 billion in Canadian goods, Canada will match those tariffs dollar for dollar. The Canadian government plans to introduce additional support measures for workers and businesses.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>U.S. 50% tariff on $28 billion Canadian goods effective August 21 at midnight</li>
<li>Canada matching tariffs dollar for dollar in retaliation</li>
<li>Trade negotiations suspended; Canadian negotiators directed to return to Ottawa</li>
<li>Canada committed $25 billion in support over preceding 18 months</li>
<li>Additional support measures for Canadian workers and businesses planned</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Uber fined €825 million by Dutch regulator for GDPR violations on driver deactivation
Dutch regulators fined Uber €825 million on August 17, 2026, for violating GDPR by automatically deactivating driver accounts without human review between 2020 and 2022. The penalty ranks second-largest under GDPR, behind Meta's €1.2 billion fine.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>€825 million fine issued August 17, 2026</li>
<li>Violations: automated account suspension for suspected fraud and permanent deactivation for low ratings</li>
<li>Second-largest GDPR fine on record (Meta: €1.2 billion in 2023)</li>
<li>Uber disputes findings; company now requires human review before deactivations</li>
<li>Fourth fine against Uber from Dutch regulator; prior penalties totaled €300.6 million</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Meta's "Hook, Hold, Harvest, Hide" Strategy Alleged in First Week of Trial
California and 28 states accused Meta in Oakland trial of using a "hook, hold, harvest, hide" strategy to engage child users, collect their data without parental consent, and conceal these practices. The lawsuit alleges Meta violated federal child privacy laws and state consumer protection laws through deliberately addictive product design. Potential damages could reach $200 billion, with states seeking forced product redesigns.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>29 states alleging violations of federal child privacy laws and state consumer protections</li>
<li>&quot;Hook, hold, harvest, hide&quot; framework: engage users, maximize time spent, extract data, hide from public</li>
<li>Trial opened Tuesday in Oakland; CEO Mark Zuckerberg and Instagram CEO Adam Mosseri expected to testify</li>
<li>Potential damages: up to $200 billion (equivalent to Meta&#x27;s 2025 annual revenue)</li>
<li>States seeking forced product redesign for child safety</li>
</ul>

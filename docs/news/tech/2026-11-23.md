On November 13, 2026, a single faulty router triggered a nationwide ground stop affecting all U.S. departing air traffic for more than four hours. The incident marked the most severe aviation disruption in the United States since September 11, 2001. According to reporting, the outage was caused by mundane management failures and aging infrastructure rather than cyberattack or intentional sabotage. The FAA was unable to process flight departures across the country during the incident. The event occurred during a major holiday travel week ahead of Thanksgiving.

Board chair Harvey Jones remained in his newly created position following the reinstatement. The board stated in its announcement that it was 'confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk' and believed 'Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia.' Nvidia's stock had tumbled following Huang's ouster, with tech stocks broadly declining. Huang stated upon reinstatement: 'I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.'

## Climate Policy

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> COP31 climate conference ends without fossil fuel consensus for first time since 2009
COP31, held in Antalya, Turkey in November 2026, concluded without a consensus cover decision for the first time since 2009. The conference, which ran two days beyond its scheduled end date, failed to reach agreement on an overarching statement due to deep divisions over whether fossil fuels and carbon capture should be mentioned, and disagreements on climate financing levels from developed to developing nations. Countries did adopt several decisions including a program to help workers and communities transition away from fossil fuels and a plan to assess climate adaptation progress. The European Union, several Latin American countries, and Pacific Island states rejected the proposed text. A central aim was to set voluntary global goals for economic electrification and increase funding for developing countries to adapt to climate impacts. This outcome follows the 2023 Dubai agreement where delegates first agreed that a "just and orderly" transition away from fossil fuels is needed to slow global warming. The Marshall Islands climate envoy stated the country would not accept "false solutions" to compromise on the agreement.

## Corporate

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> President of the United States endorses Jensen Huang's return to Nvidia leadership
The president of the United States endorsed Nvidia's decision to reinstate founder Jensen Huang as CEO and president on November 23, 2026. In a statement on Truth Social, the president stated that "the United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen's return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security" and claimed that U.S. Attorney General Todd Blanche had found no evidence that Huang engaged in unlawful conduct. The president emphasized that Nvidia's reinstatement of Huang was necessary for the company's success and the American economy. Huang had been under scrutiny following allegations of multiple extramarital relationships, including one with a woman allegedly connected to Chinese intelligence (Alice Huang, an executive at Singapore-based Megaspeed, which purchased billions in Nvidia chips). This relationship had raised national security concerns about potential compromise of sensitive information or facilitation of China's access to restricted U.S. AI chips. The Nvidia board's reinstatement decision cited consultations with senior U.S. government levels. The president stated he believed the relationship with the alleged Chinese spy did not threaten national security and emphasized that no one had proven she was actually a spy. The president also called on Nvidia to continue its internal investigation of the company's compliance with U.S. export controls.

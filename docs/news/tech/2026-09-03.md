## AI Safety

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> One-way data diodes proposed as isolation method for frontier AI models
Researchers propose data diodes—one-way optical fiber connections—as a more effective containment method for frontier AI models than traditional sandboxes and permissions. The approach, drawn from classified intelligence practices, would prevent models from breaking out of test environments or accessing the internet by enforcing unidirectional information flow.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Data diodes: optical fiber allowing one-way network flow only</li>
<li>Prevents frontier AI models from escaping test environments</li>
<li>More effective than sandboxes, permissions, and virtual machines</li>
<li>Implementation requires immutable software registries and mocked web services</li>
</ul>

</ul>

### FDA approves Moderna mRNA flu vaccine
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> The FDA approved Moderna's mFlusiva, the first mRNA-based flu vaccine, for adults 50 and older ahead of the 2026-2027 flu season — a reversal after regulators initially rejected the shot earlier this year.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Approved August 5, 2026; full approval for ages 50-64, accelerated approval for 65+</li>
<li>Phase 3 trial of 40,805 adults across 11 countries showed 26.6-27% greater efficacy than standard-dose flu vaccines</li>
<li>Covers the four most common flu strains; more frequent mild-to-moderate side effects than current vaccines</li>
<li>FDA had issued a refusal-to-file letter in February 2026 after Vinay Prasad overturned staff&#x27;s recommendation</li>
<li>FDA reversed course within a week after Moderna objected; Prasad left the agency in late April</li>
<li>Approval comes despite HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.&#x27;s opposition to mRNA vaccines and his 2025 cancellation of ~$500 million in mRNA vaccine funding</li>
</ul>

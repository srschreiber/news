The proprietors claim the stolen documents come from an ongoing breach at a major Louisiana-based identity verification company serving Fortune 500 clients.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Law Enforcement Dismantles 23-Year-Old Sality Botnet
Law enforcement agencies working with CrowdStrike and Shadowserver Foundation dismantled Sality, a 23-year-old peer-to-peer botnet that infected over 15,000 machines worldwide since 2003. CrowdStrike executed a sinkhole operation that isolated infected bots by poisoning peer lists, preventing the operator from communicating with compromised devices.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Sality botnet operating since 2003; over 15,000 infected machines worldwide</li>
<li>Primary payload was EggJagger, clipboard-monitoring tool stealing cryptocurrency</li>
<li>CrowdStrike estimates at least $150,000 in cryptocurrency theft via EggJagger</li>
<li>Distributed credential theft tools, spam, proxy services, DDoS attack capabilities</li>
<li>Sinkhole operation isolated bots by replacing legitimate peers with poisoned entries</li>
</ul>

Sources: [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/cyber-crime/2026/09/02/cops-crowdstrike-disrupt-sality-botnet-by-poisoning-the-network-and-diverting-into-sinkholes/5293795) <span class="src-badge src-research">AI Web Searched</span>

<li>Researchers: Sydney Von Arx, Cormac Slade Byrd, Spencer Kitts, Thomas Larsen</li>
<li>OpenAI had not previously disclosed this specific breach or breach frequency</li>
</ul>

## Mathematics

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Fermat's Last Theorem Formally Verified in Lean 4
Researchers completed a machine-checked formal proof of Fermat's Last Theorem using the Lean 4 proof assistant, establishing computational verification of the historic 17th-century conjecture. The proof follows the argument of Frey, Serre, Ribet, Wiles, and Taylor-Wiles.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Proof verified in Lean 4 proof assistant, Lean 4.33.1</li>
<li>Hosted on GitHub by Anthropics with full documentation</li>
<li>Theorem: for n ≥ 3, equation a^n + b^n ≠ c^n holds for positive natural numbers</li>
<li>Formalizes the classical proof chain from Frey through Wiles and Taylor-Wiles</li>
</ul>

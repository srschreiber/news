### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Oregon Sues OpenAI for $2 Billion Over Child Safety Concerns
Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield filed a $2.035 billion lawsuit against OpenAI on November 18, 2026, in Multnomah County Circuit Court. The complaint alleges OpenAI violated Oregon's Unlawful Trade Practices Act by misrepresenting ChatGPT's safety for children, selling a product with undisclosed serious safety problems, and using unfair tactics to market it to minors. The state also alleges negligence, unfair profit at Oregonians' expense, and public nuisance. Oregon claims multiple studies show ChatGPT exposes children to harmful messages about self-harm, eating disorders, and suicide, and that the app is designed to create human-like experiences that build emotional dependence in vulnerable children. The state seeks a court order requiring OpenAI to stop misleading people about child safety and implement effective protections, plus damages and monetary fines. The 56-page complaint does not specify how the $2 billion figure was calculated, though Rayfield stated at a news conference that his staff estimated the number of Oregon children using ChatGPT and calculated associated penalties. An October 2026 Common Sense Media report cited in the complaint found ChatGPT had difficulty identifying minor users and often failed to notify parents linked to accounts when children's conversations indicated mental health emergencies. OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri responded that the company offers teen safeguards many rival AI products do not, and is consulting experts on strengthening them further.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Nvidia Halts Advanced AI Chip Shipments to China
Nvidia has halted shipments of its advanced H200 (Hopper architecture) AI chips to China, reversing a decision made after the company received White House approval to export the processors in December 2025. Interim CEO Colette Kress announced the cessation during an earnings call on November 18, 2026, stating: "Our guidance does not have any licensed Hopper data center products included, and we are no longer shipping licensed Hopper data center products to China." Kress did not publicly explain the reason for the reversal or whether Nvidia would pursue other business dealings with China. The shift marks a notable turnaround from the previous efforts under former CEO Jensen Huang, who had advocated for approval to sell the H200 chips to China. Huang resigned from his CEO position the week prior following reports of multiple extramarital affairs, including one involving a woman suspected by US intelligence to be a Chinese spy.

## Space Exploration

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Voyager 1 Reaches One Light-Day Distance From Earth After 49 Years
NASA's Voyager 1 space probe, launched in September 1977, has reached a distance of one light-day (16.1 billion miles) from Earth after 49 years of travel. The probe travels at 37,800 miles per hour. At this distance, radio signals from Earth take 24 hours to reach Voyager 1 and another 24 hours to return; return signals are so weak that NASA's Deep Space Network requires a massive satellite dish to receive them. Voyager 1 is the farthest human-made object ever sent into space. After photographing Jupiter and Saturn, the probe crossed the heliopause in 2012 and entered interstellar space. The spacecraft carries a gold-plated copper disk with information about human culture.

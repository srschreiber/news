### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> 33-hour BGP hijack compromises Softaculous hosting software
Softaculous suffered a 33-hour BGP hijack beginning August 28 that diverted traffic to attacker-controlled servers, compromising multiple systems including software updates and billing. The attacker obtained valid TLS certificates and accessed credential and package data before the unauthorized route was withdrawn August 30.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>33-hour BGP hijack from August 28–30, 2026</li>
<li>Attack diverted traffic to Virtualizor update endpoint, client site, billing site</li>
<li>Attacker obtained valid TLS certificate via Let&#x27;s Encrypt domain validation</li>
<li>Two waves of traffic diversion over roughly 21 hours combined</li>
<li>Softaculous advised customers to reset credentials and check for malicious packages</li>
</ul>

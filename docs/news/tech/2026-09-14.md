### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Apple releases macOS 27 Golden Gate with AI Siri
Apple released macOS 27 Golden Gate on September 14 for Apple Silicon Macs, featuring a new Siri AI assistant that searches personal context like photos and emails and takes app actions. The update includes Liquid Glass improvements with opacity adjustment, consistent window corner radii refinements, and is incompatible with Intel Macs, which received their final major update last year.

<li>Liquid Glass improvements with new opacity slider</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Science Corporation implants vision-restoring chip in first Stargardt disease patient
Science Corporation, led by former Neuralink president Max Hodak, implanted its PRIMA retinal prosthesis in a patient with Stargardt disease at Sydney Eye Hospital. The 2mm chip enables black-and-white vision through camera-equipped glasses, with the first patient reading words during early rehabilitation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First Stargardt disease patient implanted May 2026 at Sydney Eye Hospital</li>
<li>PRIMA is 2mm by 2mm chip placed where retinal photoreceptors degenerated</li>
<li>Provides black-and-white, high-contrast vision with limited field (&quot;like looking through a straw&quot;)</li>
<li>Patient progressed from seeing letters to reading words in two sessions</li>
<li>Stargardt disease affects ~150,000 people worldwide; currently considered incurable</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Eleven-Year-Old Unsoundness Bugs Discovered in Rust Borrow Checker
Developers identified a long-standing soundness bug in Rust's borrow checker allowing a struct's lifetime requirements to be bypassed via covariant field assignment. The issue persists across stable versions and represents an 11-year unsoundness flaw involving variance, destructors, and lifetime constraints.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Bug logged as #164250 in Rust repository</li>
<li>Allows struct with `&#x27;static + Send` requirement to accept shorter-lived references</li>
<li>Involves interaction between variance, destructors, and lifetime constraints</li>
<li>Classified as unsoundness—most severe bug category in memory-safe language</li>
<li>Regression from stable to stable versions</li>
</ul>

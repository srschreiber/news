### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Italian Parliament Votes to Return to Nuclear Energy
Italy's Senate approved legislation on September 23, 2026, establishing a legal framework to return nuclear energy production nearly four decades after abandoning it following Chernobyl. The bill authorizes the government to draft implementing decrees over 12 months covering reactor licensing, safety standards, and waste management, though reactor construction remains years away.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Italy&#x27;s Senate passed final approval September 23, 2026</li>
<li>First return to nuclear after Chernobyl disaster ended atomic power</li>
<li>Government has 12 months to draft decrees on licensing, safety, waste management</li>

<li>Construction timeline remains many years away</li>
</ul>

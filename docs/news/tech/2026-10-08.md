### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry awarded for asymmetric synthesis breakthrough
Henri Kagan and Kensō Soai receive the 2026 Nobel Prize in Chemistry for designing chemical reactions that selectively produce single mirror-image molecular forms, advancing pharmaceutical synthesis.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Kagan&#x27;s 1986 breakthrough: designing reactions to produce one chiral form over its mirror image</li>
<li>Soai created first chemical reaction producing only one mirror-image form (Soai reaction, demonstrated 2003)</li>
<li>Over half of clinically employed drugs are chiral molecules</li>
<li>Previously, chemists could only produce &#x27;racemates&#x27; with equal mixtures of both mirror forms causing side effects or different therapeutic effects</li>
<li>Kagan (Université Paris-Sud, France) and Soai (Tokyo University of Science, Japan)</li>
<li>Prize citation: &#x27;discovery of non-linear effects and autocatalysis in asymmetric organic synthesis&#x27;</li>
<li>Solves century-old puzzle of achieving homochirality synthetically</li>
<li>Kagan&#x27;s 1986 key step; Soai&#x27;s 1995 publication and 2003 demonstration of Soai reaction</li>
</ul>

## Space

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Solar Orbiter traces magnetic switchbacks in solar wind to sun's surface
The European Space Agency's Solar Orbiter traced magnetic switchbacks in solar wind to their origin at the Sun's surface. Particle fingerprints indicate switchbacks form through interchange reconnection where different magnetic field types interact and snap open, releasing plasma.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Switchbacks originate at Sun&#x27;s surface, not further out</li>
<li>Particles show evidence of interchange reconnection process</li>
<li>Closed magnetic loops interact with open field lines</li>
<li>Waves and turbulence occur after switchback leaves the Sun</li>
</ul>

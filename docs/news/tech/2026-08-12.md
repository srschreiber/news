### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Tailscale Traces Database Corruption to 16-Year-Old SQLite WAL-Reset Bug

The corruption issue caused multiple outages at Tailscale in late 2025 and early 2026; the company has confirmed the bug has been found, understood, and fixed.

<li>Caused multiple outages in late 2025 and early 2026</li>

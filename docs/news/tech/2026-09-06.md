### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> US court rules safe drinking water is not a constitutional right
The Fifth Circuit Court of Appeals ruled that the US Constitution does not protect a right to safe drinking water, affirming dismissal of a lawsuit against Jackson, Mississippi over lead contamination that officials knowingly withheld from residents. The ruling contradicts constitutional protections for clean water in at least nine US states and numerous countries.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Fifth Circuit affirmed dismissal on September 5, 2026</li>
<li>Judge ruled Constitution does not provide right to safe drinking water or truthful crisis information</li>
<li>Jackson water crisis: lead contamination identified in 2015, withheld from public for 6 months</li>
<li>EPA tests in 2020 found persistent violations including high lead levels and corrosion</li>
<li>At least 9 US states have constitutional protections for clean water (NY since 2021, MA since 1972)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Isar Aerospace Reaches Orbit on Second Flight and Deploys Payloads
Isar Aerospace achieved orbital flight on its second attempt on September 5, 2026, becoming the first commercial space company from Continental Europe to deliver satellites to orbit. The company is expanding launch infrastructure globally and ramping production capacity to 40 vehicles per year.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Lift-off at 10:12 pm CEST from Andøya Space in Norway, September 5, 2026</li>
<li>First Continental European commercial company to reach orbit</li>
<li>Successfully deployed multiple payloads selected through German Space Agency competition</li>
<li>Launch complex under construction in Nova Scotia, Canada</li>
<li>40,000 square meter production facility with 40 vehicle-per-year capacity; vehicles 3-7 in production</li>
</ul>

<li>September 5, 2026: Called for Inspector General investigation into why safeguards remain ineffective</li>
</ul>

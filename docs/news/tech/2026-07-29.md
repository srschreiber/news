### Anthropic's Claude Mythos discovers weaknesses in encryption algorithms including post-quantum cryptography
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> Anthropic's Claude Mythos Preview autonomously discovered new cryptanalytic attacks against a NIST post-quantum candidate and reduced-round AES-128, exposing weaknesses experts missed for years, though neither affects deployed systems.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>HAWK-256 key recovery cost dropped from 2^64 to 2^38 operations (~3h42m on 96-core server)</li>
<li>Attack exploited a lattice automorphism that eluded human researchers for two years</li>
<li>Claude spent ~60 hours semi-autonomously developing the HAWK attack</li>
<li>New &#x27;Möbius Bridge&#x27; technique speeds up 7-round AES-128 attack 200-800x over 2013 method</li>
<li>AES attack needs over 400 octillion chosen plaintexts, impractical in practice</li>
<li>Each research effort cost about $100,000 in API usage</li>
<li>Findings disclosed to NIST, algorithm authors and industry before publication</li>
</ul>

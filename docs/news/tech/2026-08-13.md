### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> New evidence supports existence of glueballs in nature
Physicists with Beijing Spectrometer III have found the strongest evidence yet that particles dominated by glueball components can exist in nature. Glueballs are exotic particles made entirely of gluons, predicted by quantum chromodynamics but previously elusive, and were detected through J/ψ particle decays.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Strongest evidence yet for glueball existence in nature</li>
<li>Glueballs are gluon-bound composites without quarks</li>
<li>Research posted to arXiv July 2026; presented at ICHEP</li>
<li>Detected via J/ψ decay; predicted by Standard Model</li>
</ul>

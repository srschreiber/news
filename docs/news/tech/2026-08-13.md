<li>Developed by StemRad with Lockheed Martin</li>

An unknown attacker conducted a year-long campaign from March 2025 through August 2026 harvesting data from Salesforce and ServiceNow customer portals using over-permissioned guest accounts. The "City-Forum" operation targeted telecoms, banks, financial services, software vendors, cybersecurity firms, and government bodies using undocumented techniques including Salesforce LWR GraphQL exploitation. The busiest target logged >560,000 attacker events; activity is ongoing and accelerating.

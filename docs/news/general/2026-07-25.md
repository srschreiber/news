## AI

### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 at half the price of Fable 5
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic's new flagship model, Opus 5, costs $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — half the rate of its predecessor Fable 5. On the Frontier-Bench v0.1 evaluation, Opus 5 scored 43.3% versus Fable 5's 33.7% and Opus 4.8's 18.7%, and it beat Fable 5's best OSWorld 2.0 computer-use result at roughly a third of the cost. Anthropic said the model is not state-of-the-art for "risky, dual-use capabilities," like cybersecurity, positioning it instead as an efficient daily driver for enterprise workflows.

## Space

### SpaceX Starship Flight 13 launches with V3 Starlink satellites and suffers booster failure
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ SpaceX deployed 20 next-generation V3 Starlink satellites on Starship's 13th test flight, but the Super Heavy booster exploded after a faster-than-expected water impact when it failed to relight all engines for its simulated Gulf of Mexico landing. This marks the second straight V3-flight booster failure — SpaceX encountered a failure of the Starship's Super Heavy booster as it separated from the upper stage of the rocket on the first V3 flight in May. The upper stage itself performed cleanly this time, with no repeat of May's engine loss, and this was the first launch since SpaceX's June IPO; the company's stock has dropped from a peak of more than $200 per share to $115 at the close of trading on Friday, and shares fell further after the booster failure.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/24/spacex-launches-new-v3-starlink-satellites-but-suffers-another-booster-failure/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2222979/spacex-13th-starship-flight-test-launches-with-starlink-s-next-gen-satellites/), [SpaceX](https://www.spacex.com/launches/starship-flight-13)

## Policy

### EU fines Google €890 million for Digital Markets Act violations
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The European Commission's first-ever DMA penalty against Google splits into two decisions: a fine of €460 million and a fine of €430 million respectively, for self-preferencing in Google Search and for Play Store anti-steering restrictions. Regulators found Google gives preferential treatment to its own services, including shopping, hotels, transport and sports results, over those of third parties in Google Search. The penalty is roughly $1 billion and has drawn a tariff threat from the Trump administration.
Sources: [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2222963/trump-threatens-eu-with-new-tariffs-in-response-to-google-fine/), [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/legal/2026/07/24/google-breaks-alibabas-record-for-europes-largest-dma-fine/5278017), [European Commission](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-fines-google-eu890-million-breaches-digital-markets-act)

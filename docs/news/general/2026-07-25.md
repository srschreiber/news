## Infrastructure/AI

### AI data center power grid vulnerability exposed in Northern Virginia incident
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A power line failure near Washington, DC took the PJM grid over 10 minutes to stabilize instead of the usual few seconds, because more than 3 gigawatts of data centers stopped drawing power nearly simultaneously. Data centers switched to backup power, dropping about 3.1 gigawatts of load in about 30 seconds, and at its peak, PJM's grid had an extra 3.49 gigawatts of electricity on it, taking another 11 minutes before it stabilized. The disconnected data centers represented around 3% of total demand on PJM at the time, echoing a similar event on the same grid two years earlier; PJM serves 67 million customers across 13 states, making it the largest US grid operator.
Sources: [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/07/25/one-fallen-power-line-exposed-a-growing-ai-data-center-problem-heres-how-to-fix-it/)

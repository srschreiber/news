The National Interagency Fire Center reported 43,145 wildfires in 2026, while research continues to demonstrate that controlled burns and natural fire cycles are essential for forest health, nutrient cycling, and biodiversity. Scientists emphasize that fire suppression tactics over the past century have disrupted ecosystem balance and increased risk of catastrophic wildfires.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>43,145 fires recorded in 2026 by National Interagency Fire Center</li>
<li>Controlled burns and prescribed fires prevent larger, more destructive wildfires</li>
<li>Fire cycles rejuvenate forests by clearing dead vegetation, returning nutrients to soil, and promoting plant diversity</li>
<li>Many tree species in certain regions are fire-dependent for survival</li>

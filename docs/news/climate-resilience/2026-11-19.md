### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Prescribed Fire Reduces Giant Sequoia Mortality by Over 50% During Severe Wildfire
A new study from the University of Nevada, Reno demonstrates that prescribed fire treatments cut tree mortality in giant sequoias by more than half during California's 2020 Castle Fire. The research at Mountain Home Demonstration State Forest shows that reintroducing fire management—mimicking the fire-adapted ecology these trees evolved with—can significantly improve survival rates in the world's largest trees facing unprecedented wildfire severity.

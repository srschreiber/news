### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Prescribed fire reduces giant sequoia mortality by more than half during severe wildfires, study shows
A study from Mountain Home Demonstration State Forest in California's Sierra Nevada found that prescribed burns cut giant sequoia tree mortality by more than half during the 2020 Castle Fire. The research demonstrates that restoring fire to ecosystems where it was suppressed for nearly 200 years can help these ancient trees survive increasingly severe wildfires.

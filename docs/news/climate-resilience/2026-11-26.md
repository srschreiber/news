### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Prescribed Fire Reduces Giant Sequoia Mortality by More Than Half During Severe Wildfire
A study from Mountain Home Demonstration State Forest in California's Sierra Nevada found that prescribed fire treatments cut giant sequoia tree mortality by over 50% during the 2020 Castle Fire. The research demonstrates that strategic prescribed burning—mimicking the fire regime these ancient trees evolved with—can enhance their resilience to increasingly severe wildfires in the context of a warming climate.

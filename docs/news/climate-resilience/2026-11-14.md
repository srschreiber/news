### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Prescribed Fire Reduces Giant Sequoia Mortality by Over 50% During Wildfire
A new study from the University of Nevada, Reno found that prescribed fire treatments in California's Mountain Home Demonstration State Forest cut giant sequoia tree mortality by more than half during the 2020 Castle Fire. This demonstrates that reintroducing fire—a natural ecological process suppressed for nearly 200 years—can restore resilience in these ancient trees and protect them from increasingly severe wildfires.

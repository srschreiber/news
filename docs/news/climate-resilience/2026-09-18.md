## Fire Ecology

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Redwood forest resilience to wildfires with adaptive management practices
A Cal Poly Humboldt study found that coast redwoods survived extreme 2020 California wildfires well, with most trees repsprouting quickly. However, forest structure—tree density and species composition—significantly influences fire severity and recovery.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study sites: 3 second-growth forests in central California (2020 wildfires)</li>
<li>Sample size: 120+ plots measured for canopy loss, resprouting, death</li>
<li>Key finding: few redwoods died; most dead trees were small</li>
<li>Resprouting: damaged trees produced more new shoots, enabling faster recovery</li>
<li>Management impact: thinning, fuel reduction, species composition affect future severity</li>
</ul>

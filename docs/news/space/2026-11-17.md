### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> NASA pursues new strategy for Mars Sample Return mission
NASA announced a new strategy for the Mars Sample Return mission on November 9, 2026, following the previous concept's cancellation due to escalating costs and extended timeframes. The original Mars Sample Return plan aimed to retrieve rock and soil samples collected by the Perseverance rover and return them to Earth for laboratory analysis to search for evidence of ancient microbial life and information about Mars's wet past. Under the new approach, NASA has proposed the Perseverance Sample Retrieval Architecture, detailed by Tiffany Morgan, director of the Mars Exploration Program, during a virtual meeting of the Mars Exploration Program Analysis Group. The new architecture will develop impartial recommendations for sample retrieval, assess proposed mission requirements, review lessons learned from previous efforts, and evaluate current capabilities and acquisition options. A Perseverance Sample Retrieval Architecture strategy team will begin in January 2027, with a final report due in winter 2027–2028.

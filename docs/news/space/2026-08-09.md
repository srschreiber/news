## Astronomy

### Total solar eclipse August 12 reveals sun's corona
<span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> A total solar eclipse on August 12, 2026 will cross Greenland, Iceland and parts of Spain and Portugal, giving observers a rare direct view of the sun's corona during up to 2 minutes 18 seconds of totality.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Path includes eastern Greenland, western Iceland, and northern Spain/Portugal</li>
<li>Maximum totality: 2 minutes 18 seconds</li>
<li>Occurs during declining Solar Cycle 25, with sunspot number ~102</li>
<li>Less active than the 2024 eclipse (sunspot number ~144) but busier than 2017-2020 eclipses</li>
<li>Red hydrogen-alpha prominences may be visible around the Moon&#x27;s edge</li>
</ul>

### NASA advances Artemis plan for permanent lunar base
<span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> NASA is advancing its Artemis program toward a permanent lunar base near the south pole, with a $20–30 billion plan targeting completion by 2036 and a first crewed landing in late 2028.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Base to span hundreds of square miles, built over the next decade</li>
<li>Three phases: surface access access by 2029, initial operations 2029-2032, semi-permanent crew from 2032</li>
<li>Contracts: Astrolab $219M and Lunar Outpost $220M for lunar terrain vehicles</li>
<li>Blue Origin awarded $234M each for Blue Moon landers</li>
<li>Firefly Aerospace got $75M for 2028 MoonFall drone scouting mission</li>
<li>Gateway lunar station canceled to redirect funds to surface base</li>
<li>Artemis 4 crewed landing targeted for late 2028</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> NASA and Russia-China Coalition Race to Deploy First Lunar Nuclear Reactor by 2030s
NASA aims to deploy its Lunar Reactor 1 at the lunar south pole by December 2030 to produce 20 kilowatts for five years of autonomous operation, while Russia and China plan to deploy Selena by 2036 producing 10 kilowatts for ~10 years to power the International Lunar Research Station. Both reactors address the challenge of lunar night lasting approximately two weeks, though both nations face safety challenges including launch failure risk and difficulty constructing containment structures in lower lunar gravity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>NASA Lunar Reactor 1: December 2030 deployment target; 20 kilowatts for 5 years</li>
<li>Selena (Russia-China): 2036 deployment; ~10 kilowatts for ~10 years</li>
<li>20 kilowatts equivalent to ~16 US household electricity usage</li>
<li>Lunar night lasts ~2 weeks; reactors provide stable power for exploration and habitation</li>
<li>Russia launched 30+ space reactors since Cold War; US launched only once (1965), never deployed</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA Curiosity Rover Captures Detailed Martian Cliff Panorama at Dawn
NASA's Curiosity rover, operational for 13 years, captured detailed panoramas of wind-carved Martian cliffs and boxwork formations—intricate ridge patterns thought to have formed from ancient underground water deposits—on Mount Sharp. The rover has been upgraded for greater autonomy to investigate whether these formations hold clues about subsurface microbial survival during Mars' climate transition.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Curiosity rover operating 13 years at Mount Sharp (3 miles/5 km high)</li>
<li>Captured panorama at local Martian dawn showing wind-carved cliffs</li>
<li>Boxwork formations span miles; formed billions of years ago by underground water</li>
<li>May indicate subsurface habitability during Mars&#x27; transition to cold, arid state</li>
<li>Rover upgraded with greater autonomy and multitasking capability</li>
</ul>

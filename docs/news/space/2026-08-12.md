### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6.5/10" aria-label="Importance 6.5 of 10">6.5</span> Total Solar Eclipse Crosses Greenland, Iceland, and Spain Today
A total solar eclipse crosses Greenland, Iceland, and Spain on August 12, 2026—Spain's first since 1905. About 230 million people will experience 90% totality or greater, with partial visibility across much of the Northern Hemisphere including major European cities and parts of North America.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Path of totality reaches Iceland at 5:48 p.m. local time, Spain at 8:28–8:32 p.m.</li>
<li>Spain&#x27;s first total solar eclipse since 1905</li>
<li>~230 million people able to see ≥90% totality</li>
<li>Partial visibility in London, Paris, Madrid (90%+); Boston (16%); New York City (9%)</li>
<li>Only total solar eclipse of 2026</li>
</ul>

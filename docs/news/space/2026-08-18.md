### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> NASA Extends Voyager 2 Operations in Interstellar Space for Additional Year
NASA extended Voyager 2's mission in interstellar space for at least one additional year by switching off non-essential powered devices and replacing their functions with lower-power alternatives. After nearly 49 years in space and traveling 13.2 billion miles from Earth, the spacecraft's radioisotope thermoelectric generators now power three remaining science instruments. Communications with the distant probe involve approximately 40-hour round-trip signal delays.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Launched 1977, approaching 49-year milestone on August 20, 2026</li>
<li>Located 13.2 billion miles (143 AU) from Earth, moving at ~34,000 mph</li>
<li>Crossed heliopause into interstellar space November 5, 2018 at 119.7 AU</li>
<li>Power-management fix named &#x27;Big Bang&#x27; preserves three science instruments</li>
<li>40-hour round-trip communication delay, powered by radioisotope generators</li>
</ul>

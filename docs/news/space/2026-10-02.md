### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA Develops Dexterous Humanoid Robots for Lunar Operations and Human Assistance
NASA's Dexterous Robotics Team is developing humanoid robots with fine motor control for lunar and Martian operations. Rather than replacing human explorers, the robots aim to make space exploration safer and more sustainable by conducting high-risk tasks in extreme environments.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Team led by Shaun Azimi</li>
<li>Testing facility iMETRO features space habitat mockups and rock yard</li>
<li>Previously worked on Robonaut 2 on ISS for seven years</li>
<li>Planned for lunar base operations before Mars deployment</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA's Roman Space Telescope cleared for launch, targeted for August 30
NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope has been cleared for launch and is targeted for August 30, 2026, aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket from Kennedy Space Center. The Flight Readiness Review on August 23 certified that the spacecraft, rocket, launch site, and mission are ready. The telescope will investigate cosmic expansion, universe history, and exoplanets.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Launch cleared August 23, 2026; targeted for August 30, 7:26 a.m. EDT</li>
<li>SpaceX Falcon Heavy from Kennedy Space Center Launch Complex 39A</li>
<li>Designed to investigate cosmic expansion, universe history, exoplanets</li>
<li>Travels to Sun-Earth Lagrange Point 2 (1.5 million km from Earth)</li>
<li>Currently enclosed in Falcon Heavy protective fairing</li>
</ul>

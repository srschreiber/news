### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope launch preparations underway at Kennedy Space Center
NASA is preparing to launch the Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope on August 30, 2026, aboard a SpaceX Falcon Heavy rocket. The observatory features a 2.4-meter primary mirror matching Hubble's size but with a field of view 100 times larger. Its Wide-Field Instrument will map three-dimensional galaxy distribution to measure cosmic expansion and dark energy.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Launch August 30, 2026, from Kennedy Space Center</li>
<li>Nancy Grace Roman: NASA&#x27;s first chief of astronomy; Hubble development pioneer</li>
<li>2.4-meter mirror; 100× larger field of view than Hubble per single exposure</li>
<li>300-megapixel near-infrared Wide-Field Instrument for galaxy mapping</li>
<li>Destination: Sun-Earth L2 (1.5 million km from Earth)</li>
</ul>

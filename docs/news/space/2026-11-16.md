### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA crew completes 378-day simulated Mars habitat mission
A four-person NASA crew—commander and experimental test pilot Ross Elder, medical officer Ellen Ellis, flight engineer James Spicer, and Matthew Montgomery—completed the second Crew Health and Performance Exploration Analog (CHAPEA) mission on October 31, 2026, after spending 378 days in a 3D-printed Mars habitat at Johnson Space Center in Houston, beginning October 19, 2025. The 1,700-square-foot habitat included both a base and mock outdoor environment for practicing Marswalks. The crew conducted maintenance, science, engineering, and operational activities including extravehicular activities (EVAs), drone and rover operations, data collection for human health and behavioral sciences, habitat maintenance, and food cultivation. The mission tested physical and mental conditions astronauts would endure on Mars, including long-term isolation, disrupted Earth communications, and resource constraints. Post-mission evaluations and data collected will inform future Mars missions and other space operations.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA launches Moon base challenge and announces funding for base projects
On November 16, 2026, NASA launched the Moon Base Challenge, a $1 million student competition inviting high school and college teams across the United States to develop innovative solutions for a planned permanent lunar base near the lunar South Pole. The challenge was announced during a MAX POWER event at Kennedy Space Center Visitor Complex in Florida. Teams will address six essential categories: power generation and storage, facility construction, life support systems, lunar surface transportation, communications networks, and astronaut protection. NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman stated the agency is seeking creative student contributions to real problems facing lunar base development. The competition includes multiple phases with participants producing technical evaluations, design proposals, and stakeholder presentations similar to work conducted in the space industry. Students from technical, vocational, and other academic backgrounds are eligible to participate. The Moon Base is part of NASA's strategy to establish permanent human presence on the Moon and support future crewed missions to Mars.

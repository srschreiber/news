### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Starship Launch Represents Critical Test for NASA Moon Mission
SpaceX's Starship Flight 15 on November 13 aims for full recovery of both rocket and booster—a first for orbital launch systems and a critical milestone for NASA's Artemis III lunar mission targeting June 2027 or later. The 22-hour test will complete ~14 Earth orbits at ~275 km altitude and may test in-orbit propellant transfer essential for lunar missions.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Flight 15 launch: November 13, 4:45 pm Texas time (10:45 pm GMT)</li>
<li>75-minute launch window; ~22-hour mission; ~14 Earth orbits at 275 km</li>
<li>First full recovery of orbital launch system (both rocket and booster caught)</li>
<li>Critical for Artemis III lunar mission (target: June 2027 or later)</li>
<li>NASA estimates 22 refueling missions needed; Musk claims 6–8</li>
</ul>

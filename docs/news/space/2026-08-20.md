### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA Glenn Leads Development of Nuclear-Powered Spacecraft for Lunar Base and Deep Space
NASA Glenn is leading development of Space Reactor 1—Freedom (SR-1 Freedom), a nuclear-powered spacecraft designed to enable farther travel and power long-term lunar base operations. Nuclear power addresses the critical limitation of extended darkness on the Moon where sunlight-dependent solar power is inadequate. The initiative represents the start of a new generation of spacecraft and could involve billions in funding.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Project: Space Reactor 1—Freedom (SR-1 Freedom)</li>
<li>NASA Glenn selected to lead nuclear-powered spacecraft development</li>
<li>Nuclear power enables lunar base operation during extended darkness periods</li>
<li>Deputy project manager Lindsay Kaldon described it as &#x27;hybrid rocket&#x27; spacecraft</li>
<li>Potential funding: billions of dollars</li>
</ul>

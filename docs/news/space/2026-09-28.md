### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> FY 2025 NASA planetary research and analysis funding cut by over 50%
NASA's fiscal year 2025 planetary research and analysis funding was underspent by more than 50% as of June 30, 2026, with forward-funding practices reducing grant awards by approximately two-thirds. Combined with 15 years of flat funding representing a loss of over 30% in actual value, the Planetary Science Institute warns the situation is 'catastrophic' without Congressional action.

<li>15 years flat funding = &gt;30% loss in actual value when adjusted for inflation</li>
<li>Characterized as &#x27;catastrophic&#x27; without Congressional intervention</li>
</ul>

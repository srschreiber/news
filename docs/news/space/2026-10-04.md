### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> NASA's Roman Space Telescope begins planet-hunting observations
NASA's Nancy Grace Roman Space Telescope began planet-hunting observations with operational primary imaging instruments, achieving pointing stability better than 1/100,000 of a degree and receiving first cosmic light on its Coronagraph Instrument in late September 2026.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Pointing stability: &lt;1/100,000 degree for 30 minutes (Wide Field) or 8 hours (Coronagraph)</li>
<li>Equivalent to aiming laser at dime from ~150 miles away</li>
<li>Coronagraph Instrument received first cosmic light September 22</li>
<li>Wide Field Instrument contains 18 detectors for guide star monitoring</li>
<li>Potential for stability improvement to dime-targeting from ~230 miles</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA Cancels Rescue Mission for Swift Telescope, Observatory Set to Re-enter Atmosphere
NASA has canceled its commercial rescue mission to save the aging Swift Observatory, a gamma-ray burst detector in operation since 2004. The spacecraft, which had begun tumbling, will now re-enter Earth's atmosphere within months after a private mission by Katalyst Space's LINK spacecraft failed due to attitude control issues.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Swift Observatory has been operating since 2004 and specializes in gamma-ray bursts (GRBs)</li>
<li>The $30 million commercial boost mission by Katalyst Space failed due to spacecraft attitude control issues</li>
<li>The telescope will re-enter Earth&#x27;s atmosphere in a few months</li>
<li>LINK spacecraft was unable to complete the planned orbital boost</li>
</ul>

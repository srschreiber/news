### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA's X-43A Hypersonic Aircraft Remains Record Holder at Mach 9.6 After 22 Years
NASA's X-43A hypersonic scramjet aircraft, which achieved Mach 9.64 at 110,000 feet in 2004, remains unmatched by any air-breathing aircraft 22 years later despite subsequent hypersonic programs.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Speed record: Mach 9.64 (approximately 6,400 mph) at 110,000 feet, November 16, 2004</li>
<li>12-foot-long, 3,300-pound unmanned vehicle</li>
<li>Eight-year Hyper-X program cost $230 million</li>
<li>Overheated to 2,000°F using carbon-carbon composites</li>
<li>Later X-51 Waverider reached Mach 5.1; planned X-43B, X-43C, X-43D variants never developed</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> SpaceX Starship Reaches Orbit Despite Booster Recovery Failure
SpaceX's Starship Flight 15 launched November 13 from Starbase, successfully reaching orbit and deploying 26 Starlink V3 satellites. The Super Heavy booster was diverted to a controlled splashdown in the Gulf of Mexico instead of recovery, where it exploded. The upper stage is expected to return November 14 for the first-ever catch attempt by mechanical "chopstick arms."

<li>Flight 15 launched 4:47 PM local time, Nov 13 from South Texas</li>
<li>124-metre rocket with 33 Raptor engines reached orbit successfully</li>
<li>Deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites; completed primary orbital objectives</li>
<li>Super Heavy booster diverted to Gulf of Mexico splashdown at 6.5 min post-liftoff; safety prioritized over recovery</li>
<li>Starship expected to attempt first-ever catch from orbit using tower chopstick arms Nov 14 at 3:20 PM</li>
</ul>

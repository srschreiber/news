<li>Upper stage recovery attempt scheduled November 16 using mechanical arm catch—first for upper stage</li>
<li>Starship program received NASA billions plus SpaceX&#x27;s $15+ billion investment; targets 2028 lunar landing</li>
<li>Flight 15 launched 4:47 PM local time, Nov 13 from South Texas</li>

<li>Deployed 26 Starlink V3 satellites; completed primary orbital objectives</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> NASA Expands Nuclear Power Infrastructure Plans for Lunar and Martian Missions
NASA is developing PROMISE, a nuclear-powered rover for the Moon's South Pole, using the same multi-mission radioisotope thermoelectric generator (MMRTG) technology proven on Mars rovers Curiosity and Perseverance. The agency is also planning nuclear propulsion systems for Mars missions and cargo landers to deliver nuclear reactors to the lunar surface for sustained operations.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>PROMISE rover powered by MMRTG, same as Mars rovers Curiosity and Perseverance</li>
<li>Perseverance MMRTG contains 10.6 pounds (4.8 kg) of plutonium dioxide, ~2,000 watts thermal power initially</li>
<li>MMRTG converts radioisotope decay heat to electricity via solid-state thermocouples</li>
<li>NASA planning nuclear reactors delivered by cargo landers to Moon&#x27;s South Pole</li>
</ul>

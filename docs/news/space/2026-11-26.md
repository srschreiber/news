### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Hera Spacecraft Photographs Stadium-Sized Impact Crater on Asteroid from DART Mission
ESA's Hera spacecraft has captured the first up-close images of a stadium-sized crater punched into an asteroid by NASA's DART Earth-defense mission.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>DART impacted September 2022 at 13,700+ mph (6.14 km/s); released ~10 billion joules</li>
<li>Stadium-sized crater on Dimorphos (~500 ft/151 m across)</li>
<li>Orbit shortened ~33 minutes (25× most conservative projection)</li>
<li>Hera arrived late November 2026 after two-year journey; will deploy CubeSats in Dec 2026–Jan 2027</li>
<li>DART crater size: entire width of Dimorphos</li>
<li>Hera images taken 25–30 km away using monochrome, multispectral, and thermal infrared cameras</li>
<li>Hundreds of boulders orbiting Didymos and Dimorphos system</li>
<li>Dec 2026: Hera moves closer for high-resolution images; Jan 2027: deploy Juventas and Milani CubeSats</li>
</ul>

</ul>

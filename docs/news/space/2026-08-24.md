### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> China launches polar lunar exploration mission to detect water ice
China is launching Chang'e-7, its most ambitious lunar mission, to search for water ice in permanently shadowed craters near the Moon's south pole. The mission includes an orbiter, lander, rover, and solar-powered hopper designed to explore areas inaccessible to conventional vehicles, with a launch window from August 24–31, 2026.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Target: Shackleton Crater region at lunar south pole</li>
<li>Four-part mission: orbiter, lander, rover, solar-powered hopper</li>
<li>Hopper can descend into sunless pits to detect water and volatile compounds</li>
<li>Most complex robotic lunar mission China has attempted</li>
<li>Previous Chang&#x27;e-6 (2024) returned 1,935.3 grams of lunar material</li>
</ul>

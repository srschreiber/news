### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> NASA plans 20+ lunar landings to establish moon base infrastructure
NASA is conducting Phase I of its Moon Base architecture plan through 2029, deploying 20+ robotic landings near the South Pole via four commercial partners. The missions will test precision landing, communications, rovers, and power systems to establish infrastructure for future human habitation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>20+ robotic lunar landings planned near South Pole through 2029</li>
<li>Four commercial partners: Blue Origin, Firefly Aerospace, Intuitive Machines, Voyager Lunar Systems</li>
<li>Blue Moon MK1 lander completed thermal vacuum testing at NASA Johnson Space Center</li>
<li>Commercial Lunar Payload Services (CLPS) to provide regular cargo deliveries</li>
<li>Missions will test survival technology for extreme lunar conditions</li>
</ul>

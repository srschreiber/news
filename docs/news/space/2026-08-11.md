### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> NASA invites India's ISRO to join Moon Base program
NASA formally invited India's ISRO to join the Artemis-backed lunar base program near the Moon's South Pole, announced by U.S. Ambassador Sergio Gor following the ninth US-India Civil Space Joint Working Group meeting. The invitation deepens US-India space cooperation on human spaceflight and scientific research.

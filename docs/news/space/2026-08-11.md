NASA's lithium-fed electromagnetic thruster test achieved record power output of 120 kilowatts, potentially reducing crewed Mars mission travel time from nine months to three months one-way when paired with nuclear power.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Thruster achieved 120 kilowatts, 25 times more powerful than Psyche spacecraft thrusters and exceeding any prior U.S. test</li>

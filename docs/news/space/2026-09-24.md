## Space Missions

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA selects PRIMA far-infrared telescope as inaugural Probe Explorer mission
NASA selected PRIMA (PRobe far-Infrared Mission for Astrophysics), a far-infrared space telescope, as the inaugural mission in its new Probe Explorer program class. The mission, targeted for 2033 launch with a five-year planned duration, will conduct deep surveys complementing JWST and other infrared observatories.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>PRIMA selected as first Probe Explorer mission</li>
<li>Project cost capped at $1.2 billion (excluding launch)</li>
<li>Targeted launch 2033; planned 5-year mission</li>
<li>5.9-foot telescope conducting far-infrared deep surveys</li>
<li>Research areas: exoplanet formation, galaxy evolution, black holes, dust accumulation</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter captures Falcon 9 impact crater details
NASA's Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter imaged a fresh crater formed on August 5, 2026 when a SpaceX Falcon 9 upper stage struck the Moon near Einstein Crater at 5,400 mph. The ~40-foot rocket stage, in orbit since January 2025, created a crater approximately 60 feet wide with impact force equivalent to three tons of TNT.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Falcon 9 upper stage impacted August 5, 2026 at ~5,400 mph</li>
<li>Rocket stage: ~40 feet long, ~4,900 kg, launched January 15, 2025</li>
<li>Crater: ~60 feet (18 m) diameter, &lt;10 feet (3 m) deep</li>
<li>Impact force equivalent to ~3 tons of TNT</li>
<li>LRO captured images August 11–12 at ~97 km altitude</li>
<li>Second known uncontrolled human-made object crash on Moon</li>
</ul>

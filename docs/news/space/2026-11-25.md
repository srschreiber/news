### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> NASA opens competition for Lunar Reactor-1 nuclear power system development
NASA opened a competition on November 23 to develop Lunar Reactor-1, a 20-kilowatt nuclear fission system for the Moon's surface. The reactor must launch by 2030 and operate for at least five years, addressing the two-week lunar night when solar power fails. Proposals are due December 21.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>20-kilowatt electrical output; equivalent to ~16 US households annually</li>
<li>Launch-ready by 2030; minimum 5-year operation requirement</li>
<li>Proposals due December 21, 2026</li>
<li>Powers lunar habitation, communications, rovers, scientific instruments at Artemis base</li>
<li>Potential future use on Mars missions</li>
</ul>

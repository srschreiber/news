### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> A Proposal To Make Mars Sample Return A Collaborative NASA, ESA, And CNSA Effort
A proposal published October 12 in Astrobiology advocates converting NASA's Mars Sample Return mission into a collaborative effort with the European Space Agency and China's space agency to maximize mission success, preserve sample integrity, and determine whether extraterrestrial life exists. The proposal targets subsurface samples from an endorheic basin where minerals may indicate recent fluid cycles.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>NASA Perseverance rover caching samples on Mars; China&#x27;s Tianwen-3 targets 2031 return</li>
<li>Proposal suggests joint NASA-ESA-CNSA mission could enable custom subsurface sampling</li>
<li>Mission needed before human arrivals compromise scientific integrity</li>
<li>Mars Sample Return program currently faces potential cancellation</li>
</ul>

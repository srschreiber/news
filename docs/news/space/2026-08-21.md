### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> NASA's Voyager 2 probe reaches interstellar space after 49 years
NASA's Voyager 2 probe, launched August 20, 1977, has reached interstellar space after 49 years of travel. The spacecraft is the only probe to visit all four outer gas giants and is NASA's longest-lived mission, now traveling at 34,000 mph approximately 13.2 billion miles from Earth.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Launch date: August 20, 1977; entered interstellar space 49 years later</li>
<li>Only spacecraft to visit Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune</li>
<li>Mission cost exceeded $865 million; originally designed for 4 years</li>
<li>As of November 2026: 13.2 billion miles from Earth at 34,000 mph</li>
<li>Loses ~4 watts annually; NASA installed lower-power devices August 2026</li>
<li>Twin Voyager 1 expected 16 billion miles away by November 2026</li>
</ul>

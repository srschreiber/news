### US job market loses 23,000 jobs in July, stock market rallies on softer labor data
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> The US economy lost 23,000 jobs in July 2026, far below expectations, with heavy downward revisions to prior months easing pressure for Fed rate hikes and sending stock futures higher.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>July: -23,000 jobs vs expected +83,000 to +95,000</li>
<li>May revised down 66,000 to +63,000; June down 37,000 to +20,000</li>
<li>Unemployment rate fell to 4.1%, but labor force participation dropped to 61.4%, a 5+ year low</li>
<li>Job losses: local govt education -50,000, leisure/hospitality -40,000, retail -19,000</li>
<li>Average hourly earnings up just 3.2% annually</li>
<li>Dow futures +165 pts (0.3%), S&amp;P 500 futures +0.5%, Nasdaq 100 futures +1%</li>
</ul>

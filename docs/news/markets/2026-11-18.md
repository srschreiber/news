### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Nvidia leadership transition underway as CEO Huang exits
Nvidia's board has confirmed CEO Jensen Huang's departure is permanent, with board chair Harvey Jones stating the board 'unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.' Huang left 'under a cloud' the previous week. Interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress is leading the company while the board engages executive search firm Heidrick & Struggles to find a permanent replacement. In Q3 earnings, Nvidia reported revenue of $115.8 billion (103 percent increase year-over-year) and operating income of $77.5 billion (115 percent increase). Kress raised the company's fiscal 2028 revenue growth outlook from 70 percent to 75 percent, citing growing demand and additional supply. The board is strengthening oversight by creating a committee to scrutinize security policies and export compliance. Analysts expressed concerns about operational risks, including maintaining employee confidence, retaining talent, and preserving innovation culture during the leadership transition, despite strong near-term demand.

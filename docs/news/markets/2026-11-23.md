### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Jensen Huang reinstated as Nvidia CEO following brief resignation
Jensen Huang was reinstated as Nvidia CEO on November 23, 2026, eleven days after his resignation on November 12, 2026. Huang had resigned amid allegations of personal misconduct, specifically affairs with Nvidia subordinates. The Nvidia board unanimously voted to reinstate him. Board chair Harvey Jones remained in his newly created position following the reinstatement. The board's decision cited input from senior levels of the United States government, with the White House confirming that the president of the United States personally dialed into an Nvidia board meeting hours before the reinstatement to argue for Huang's return, citing national security concerns. The board stated in its announcement that it was 'confident that Mr. Huang's reinstatement poses no national security risk' and believed 'Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia.' Nvidia's stock had tumbled following Huang's ouster, with tech stocks broadly declining. Huang stated upon reinstatement: 'I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.'

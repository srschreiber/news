Moderna and Merck announced that their personalized mRNA cancer vaccine intismeran met primary endpoints in a Phase 3 melanoma trial when combined with Merck's Keytruda. The result marks the first randomized Phase 3 trial definitively proving the benefit of neoantigen vaccines in cancer treatment.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First randomized Phase 3 trial proving benefit of neoantigen vaccines</li>
<li>Combination with Keytruda slowed melanoma recurrence and spread</li>
<li>Personalized mRNA vaccine intismeran in adjuvant melanoma setting</li>
<li>Announcement August 19, 2026; detailed data not yet released</li>
<li>First positive Phase 3 result for individualized neoantigen therapy</li>
<li>First positive Phase 3 for mRNA-based cancer therapy</li>
<li>INTerpath-001 trial in stage IIB-IV melanoma patients</li>
<li>Met primary endpoint: recurrence-free survival</li>
<li>Met key secondary endpoint: distant metastasis-free survival</li>
</ul>

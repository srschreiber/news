### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump Administration Removes Habitat Protection from Endangered Species Act Definition of 'Harm'
The Trump administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026, removing habitat destruction from the regulatory definition of 'harm' under the Endangered Species Act, effective September 14. The change allows farming, drilling, mining, logging, and development to proceed in endangered species habitats without violating federal law, weakening protections that had been in place for over 50 years since the 1995 Supreme Court decision in Babbitt v. Sweet Home.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalizes July 10, 2026; takes effect September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Removes &#x27;harm&#x27; designation for habitat destruction that disrupts feeding, breeding, or sheltering</li>
<li>Overturns 50+ years of regulatory interpretation dating to 1995 Supreme Court precedent</li>
<li>Habitat loss remains one of the main drivers of extinction</li>
<li>Federal consultation and critical habitat designations still apply but leave gaps in protection for private projects</li>

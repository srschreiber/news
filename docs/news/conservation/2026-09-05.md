### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Asian Hornets Threaten UK Food Security, Beekeepers Seek National Campaign
British beekeepers are calling for a national awareness campaign about invasive Asian hornets, which are spreading across Europe and creating "pollinator deserts" that threaten food security. The yellow-legged hornet, present in Europe since 2004, reproduces rapidly as warmer springs and summers accelerate colony growth, with end-of-summer maturation producing fertile queens that establish new colonies across wider areas.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Yellow-legged hornets native to south-east Asia, arrived Europe 2004</li>
<li>Climate-driven spread: warm springs and summers accelerate reproduction</li>
<li>Critical period: end of summer when fertile queens seek winter shelter</li>
<li>Threat extends to human safety, pollination, and biodiversity</li>

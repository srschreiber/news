### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> New Feline Species Identified in Bolivia After More Than a Century
Scientists formally identified a previously unknown small wild cat species in Bolivia's Yungas cloud forest and named it Leopardus tilcayo, marking the first new living feline species formally described in over 100 years. Genetic analysis confirmed the discovery, published in Current Biology.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Known locally as tilcayo; scientific name Leopardus tilcayo</li>
<li>Body length: ~18 inches (46cm); weight ~3 pounds (1.4kg)</li>
<li>Light brown fur with irregularly shaped rosettes</li>
<li>Appears nocturnal; feeds on small rodents</li>
<li>First new living feline species formally identified in over 100 years</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative plants 50 billion trees across the country
Ethiopia's Green Legacy Initiative is planting 50 billion trees to restore forests lost to agricultural expansion, logging, and charcoal use over the past 25 years. Ordinary citizens, including grassroots leaders like Halima Kedir, are driving the reforestation effort despite initial community skepticism.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Target: 50 billion trees across Ethiopia</li>
<li>From 2000 to 2025, Ethiopia lost 560,000 hectares (2,160 square miles) of tree cover</li>
<li>Loss caused by agricultural expansion, commercial logging, charcoal fuel use</li>
<li>Citizen-led efforts; example: Halima Kedir planted thousands of trees in Berbere district, Oromia region</li>
<li>Framed as solution to climate change and food insecurity</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> The president of the United States administration removes habitat destruction from Endangered Species Act protections
The president of the United States administration finalized a rule removing habitat destruction from the Endangered Species Act's definition of 'harm,' effective September 14, 2026. The change abandons a 50-year regulatory interpretation upheld by the Supreme Court in 1995, eliminating habitat protection while preserving restrictions on direct killing and injury. Federal consultation requirements and designated critical habitat protections remain, but the shift substantially reduces environmental oversight for private projects and development.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026; effective September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Overturns 50+ year regulatory interpretation and 1995 Supreme Court precedent (Babbitt v. Sweet Home)</li>
<li>Habitat destruction no longer constitutes illegal &#x27;take&#x27; under the law</li>
<li>Direct killing and injury of animals remain prohibited</li>
<li>Habitat loss identified as main driver of extinction</li>
</ul>

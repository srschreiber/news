The World Wildlife Fund warns that vertebrate wildlife populations have declined 73% on average since 1970, with freshwater species hit hardest at 85%. The report identifies global food production as the next critical area for transformation to reverse biodiversity collapse, citing successful tiger, tuna, and sea turtle recoveries as proof nature loss can be reversed.

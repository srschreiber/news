### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> President of the United States rolls back endangered species protections; Canadian wildlife also at risk
The president of the United States's administration rescinded a decades-old definition of "harm" under the Endangered Species Act, weakening habitat protections effective September 14. Canadian wildlife experts warned the change threatens transboundary species including migratory birds, grizzly bears, and orcas.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Changed definition of &#x27;harm&#x27; to exclude habitat degradation impacts</li>
<li>Effective date: September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Supreme Court had upheld original definition in mid-1990s</li>
<li>Threatened transboundary species: migratory birds, spotted owls, grizzly bears, orcas</li>
<li>Lawsuits filed: nearly a dozen conservation groups challenging repeal</li>
</ul>

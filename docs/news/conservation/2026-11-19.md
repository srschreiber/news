### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Phil the Bar-tailed Godwit Tracked on 6,800-Mile Migration to Siberia
Scientists tracked Phil, a bar-tailed godwit, on a 6,800-mile round-trip migration from Norfolk, England to the Taymyr peninsula in Siberia using a 6g lightweight tracker deployed in January 2026. The tracking provides the first detailed mapping of the species' endurance migration and confirms its status as having the longest non-stop migratory flight of any bird.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>6,800-mile round-trip migration Norfolk to Siberia</li>
<li>Lightweight 6g tracker deployed January 2026</li>
<li>Traveled to Wadden Sea (March), Taymyr peninsula (May), returned via Arctic Ocean and Norwegian islands</li>
<li>First detailed tracking of bar-tailed godwit endurance migration</li>
<li>Species known for longest non-stop migratory flight of any bird</li>
</ul>

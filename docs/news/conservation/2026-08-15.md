### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Little penguin found dead with H5 bird flu on Phillip Island, Victoria
Australia confirmed its first H5 bird flu case in a little penguin on Phillip Island, Victoria on August 15, 2026, discovered days after the state announced vaccination plans. The island hosts approximately 40,000 little penguins, the world's largest colony, raising concerns about vaccination timeline effectiveness.

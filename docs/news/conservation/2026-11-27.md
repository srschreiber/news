### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> California Approves $59.6 Million in Grants for Wildlife Habitat Restoration and Public Access
Governor Gavin Newsom announced that the Wildlife Conservation Board approved nearly $60 million in grants for 27 biodiversity and habitat restoration projects across 18 California counties. The funding supports wildlife corridors, the California Salmon Strategy, and public access to nature, advancing the state's 30×30 conservation goal.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$59.6 million approved for 27 projects across 18 California counties</li>
<li>Includes $5.5 million for wildlife crossings over State Route 62 near Yucca Valley and $4 million for Interstate 15 crossing at Rainbow Canyon to restore habitat connectivity</li>
<li>$2.9 million granted to California Trout Inc. for East Fork Scott River restoration to improve habitat for coho salmon</li>
<li>Projects advance California&#x27;s 30×30 goal to conserve 30% of state lands and coastal waters by 2030</li>
</ul>

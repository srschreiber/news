### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> President Removes Habitat Destruction from Endangered Species Act Definition of Harm
On July 10, 2026, the president of the United States finalized a rule narrowing the Endangered Species Act's definition of 'harm' to exclude habitat destruction, effective September 14, 2026. Destroying or degrading habitat no longer violates the act's taking prohibition, though direct injury and killing remain prohibited, weakening protections for species dependent on specific habitats.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10; effective Sept. 14, 2026</li>
<li>Habitat destruction no longer counts as illegal &#x27;take&#x27;</li>
<li>Direct injury/killing still prohibited; federal consultation requirement remains</li>
<li>Weakens protection for species like manatees, ocelots, salmon dependent on specific habitats</li>
<li>Much critical habitat outside designated areas; private projects without federal involvement unaffected</li>
</ul>

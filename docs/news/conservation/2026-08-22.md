### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump administration removes habitat destruction from Endangered Species Act definition of harm
The president of the United States administration redefined "harm" under the Endangered Species Act to exclude habitat destruction, effective September 14, 2026. The change removes protections for habitat damage that previously prevented species harm through feeding, breeding, or shelter loss.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Final rule effective September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Habitat destruction no longer counts as illegal &#x27;take&#x27; under the law</li>
<li>Direct injury and killing remain prohibited</li>
<li>Federal consultation requirements and designated critical habitat protections remain intact</li>
<li>Habitat loss identified as major extinction driver</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Trump Administration Removes Protections for Endangered Species Habitat
The president of the United States' administration finalized a rule removing nearly all habitat protections for endangered species, rescinding the regulatory definition of 'harm' to include habitat destruction. The move opens species' habitats to logging, mining, and oil and gas drilling, directly contradicting Supreme Court precedent.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026, eliminating decades of endangered species habitat protections</li>
<li>Removes prohibition on habitat modification and degradation affecting species survival</li>
<li>Reverses Supreme Court precedent upholding habitat protection for spotted owls and old-growth forests</li>
<li>Opens habitats to logging, mining, oil and gas drilling, and other development</li>
</ul>

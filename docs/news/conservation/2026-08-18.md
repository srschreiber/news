### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump Administration Removes Habitat Destruction from Endangered Species Act Definition of 'Harm'
The Trump administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026, removing habitat destruction from the regulatory definition of 'harm' under the Endangered Species Act, effective September 14. The change allows farming, drilling, mining, logging, and development to proceed in endangered species habitats without violating federal law, weakening protections that have been in place for over 50 years.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026; effective September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Removes decades-old interpretation that habitat degradation counts as &#x27;harm&#x27; to protected wildlife under the Endangered Species Act</li>
<li>Affects migratory species crossing U.S.-Canada border, including southern resident killer whales, migratory birds, grizzly bears, and northern spotted owls</li>
<li>Nearly a dozen conservation groups have filed lawsuit challenging the repeal in U.S. District Court</li>
</ul>

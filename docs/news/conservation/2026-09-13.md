### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Rare bird discovery in Ecuador catalyzes 30-year conservation model
The discovery of a rare jocotoco antpitta in Ecuador's Andes in November 1997 catalyzed a foundation dedicated to biodiversity restoration that has become a globally recognized conservation model over 30 years.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Jocotoco antpitta discovered November 1997 near Tapichalaca Lodge at 2,500 metres elevation</li>
<li>Bird unknown to science before discovery by ornithologist Robert Ridgely</li>
<li>Foundation established to restore cloud forest biodiversity in southern Ecuador</li>
<li>30-year conservation work became globally recognized model</li>
<li>Chestnut back, grey front, black crown, white face-patch; feeds on large earthworms</li>
</ul>

# Sam's News — conservation — 2026-10-08

## Conservation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Aldabra giant tortoise population reaches record 180,000 in conservation success
A survey by Seychelles Islands Foundation and University of Exeter found approximately 180,000 Aldabra giant tortoises on Aldabra Atoll—the highest ever recorded and a dramatic recovery from fewer than 1,000 in the 19th century. The atoll became a strict nature reserve in 1981 and UNESCO World Heritage site in 1982.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Survey estimated ~180,000 adult and sub-adult tortoises (best estimate: ~183,000)</li>
<li>Survey analyzed 2018–2022 counts; published in Ecology and Evolution</li>
<li>Previous benchmark: ~100,000 from late 1990s</li>
<li>Recovered from &lt;1,000 in 19th century due to exploitation</li>
<li>World&#x27;s largest free-roaming giant tortoise population; atoll strict nature reserve since 1981</li>
</ul>

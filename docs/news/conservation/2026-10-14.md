### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Simultaneous heat waves and wildfire smoke exposure surging to affect over 1 billion annually

This represents a sharp increase from 2000–2009, driven primarily by people experiencing both hazards simultaneously more frequently.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Largest global assessment to date of simultaneous heat and wildfire exposure</li>
<li>Coexposure to heatwaves and wildfire smoke increased from 2000–2019</li>
<li>Study analyzed global population simultaneously facing both hazards</li>

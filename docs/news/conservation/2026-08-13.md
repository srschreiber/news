The president of the United States's administration narrowed Endangered Species Act habitat protections effective September 14, 2026, by rescinding a decades-old interpretation of "harm." Nearly a dozen conservation groups sued, warning the change threatens migratory species crossing the U.S.-Canada border, including spotted owls, grizzly bears, and killer whales.

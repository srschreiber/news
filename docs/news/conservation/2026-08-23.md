### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump Administration Removes Habitat Destruction from ESA 'Harm' Definition
The president of the United States administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026, removing habitat destruction from the regulatory definition of "harm" under the Endangered Species Act. The change, taking effect September 14, weakens protection for endangered species by allowing farming, drilling, mining, logging, and development to proceed in critical habitats without violating the act, reversing a 50-year interpretation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026; takes effect September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Removes habitat destruction as illegal &#x27;harm&#x27; or &#x27;take&#x27; under ESA; direct killing remains prohibited</li>
<li>Overturns decades-long legal interpretation from 1995 Supreme Court case Babbitt v. Sweet Home</li>
<li>Affects species like manatees (seagrass habitat), ocelots (thornscrub), and salmon (river spawning grounds)</li>
<li>Weakens U.S. credibility in asking other countries to protect forests, wetlands, and other habitats</li>
</ul>

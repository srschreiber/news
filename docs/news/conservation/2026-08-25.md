### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump Administration Removes Habitat Protection from Endangered Species Act Definition of 'Harm'
The Trump administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026, removing habitat destruction from the regulatory definition of "harm" under the Endangered Species Act, effective September 14. The change allows farming, drilling, mining, logging, and development to proceed in endangered species habitat without violating the act, significantly weakening species protection since habitat loss is a primary driver of extinction.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026; takes effect September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Habitat destruction no longer counts as illegal &#x27;take&#x27; under the Endangered Species Act</li>
<li>Decades-old legal interpretation from 1995 Supreme Court case (Babbitt v. Sweet Home) abandoned</li>
<li>Change applies to private projects without federal permits or funding, limiting consultation requirements</li>
<li>Affects species including manatees, ocelots, and salmon dependent on specific habitats</li>
</ul>

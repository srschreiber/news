### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Global biodiversity loss hotspots and regional patterns documented
Research identifies geographic regions experiencing rapid biodiversity loss, particularly in low- and middle-income countries where road expansion and agricultural expansion concentrate threats to endemic species. Approximately 1,031 million hectares of high-risk cropland expansion areas fall outside protected areas.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Low/middle-income countries bear 2-4 fold heavier roadkill burden</li>
<li>Agricultural pressures reduce species richness 25.8%</li>
<li>Cropland expanded 12% within hotspots 2000-2019</li>
<li>1,031 million hectares of risk spots outside protected areas</li>
</ul>

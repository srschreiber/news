### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> California approves $59.6 million in grants for biodiversity projects and wildlife habitat restoration
Governor Gavin Newsom announced approval of nearly $60 million in Wildlife Conservation Board grants supporting 27 projects across 18 California counties, including wildlife crossings, salmon habitat restoration, and public access to nature initiatives. The funding advances California's 30×30 goal to conserve 30% of the state's lands and coastal waters by 2030.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$59.6 million approved for 27 biodiversity and habitat projects across 18 California counties</li>
<li>$5.5 million grant to Mojave Desert Land Trust for wildlife crossings over State Route 62 near Yucca Valley</li>
<li>$4 million grant to TEAM Resource Conservation District for Interstate 15 crossing design at Rainbow Canyon to restore mountain lion connectivity</li>
<li>$2.9 million to California Trout Inc. for East Fork Scott River restoration supporting coho salmon recovery</li>
<li>Grants advance California&#x27;s 30×30 initiative to protect 30% of state lands and coastal waters by 2030</li>
</ul>

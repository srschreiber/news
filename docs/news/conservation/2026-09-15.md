## Conservation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Indonesian wildfires threaten world's longest-running orangutan research site in Borneo
Wildfires in Indonesia are threatening Cabang Panti research station in Gunung Palung national park, Borneo, home to one of the world's longest-running orangutan studies. Fires have burned within 200 metres of Rangkong research station; orangutans are fleeing the forests as an exceptionally strong El Niño has created hot, dry conditions causing intentional land-clearing fires to escape control.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Research station: Cabang Panti, Gunung Palung national park, Borneo</li>
<li>Fires within 200 m of Rangkong satellite facility (~4 miles south of main camp)</li>
<li>Bornean orangutan population: hundreds at stations, 2,500+ in park; all three species critically endangered</li>
<li>Prof. Cheryl Knott (Boston University) led 32 years of research; orangutans fleeing</li>
<li>El Niño conditions: some West Kalimantan areas months without rainfall; fires escape annual control burns</li>
</ul>

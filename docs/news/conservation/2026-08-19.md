### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump Administration Removes Habitat Destruction from Endangered Species Act Protections
The Trump administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026, that removes habitat destruction from the regulatory definition of "harm" under the Endangered Species Act. The change, effective September 14, 2026, allows farming, drilling, mining, logging, and development to proceed in endangered species habitats without violating the law, weakening decades of habitat protection policy and undermining U.S. conservation credibility internationally.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Trump administration rule removes habitat destruction from ESA &quot;harm&quot; definition, effective September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Habitat loss is a primary driver of extinction; the change separates habitat protection from species protection</li>
<li>Development and resource extraction in endangered species habitat no longer constitutes illegal &quot;take&quot; under the act</li>
<li>The policy reverses a legal interpretation upheld by the Supreme Court in 1995&#x27;s Babbitt v. Sweet Home</li>
<li>Weakens federal consultation requirements for projects affecting listed species outside designated critical habitat areas</li>

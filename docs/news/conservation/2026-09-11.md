## Conservation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Global report: one in nine migratory birds threatened with extinction
A global report released September 11, 2026 found one in nine migratory bird species threatened with extinction, with 45% of all migratory species in decline. The slender-billed curlew was declared extinct in 2025, the first mainland Eurasian and African bird extinction in recent centuries.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>One in nine migratory bird species threatened with extinction; 45% of all migratory species in decline</li>
<li>Slender-billed curlew declared extinct in 2025—first global bird extinction in mainland Eurasia and Africa in recent centuries</li>
<li>Seven migratory bird species confirmed or suspected extinct over last 150 years</li>
<li>Threats: agricultural intensification, unsustainable hunting, pollution, invasive species, and global heating</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> Phoenix Species Project launches with $200 million to recover 100 critically endangered species
Leonardo DiCaprio's Re:wild and the Bezos Earth Fund announced the Phoenix Species Project on August 4, 2026, a $200 million initiative to actively recover 100 of the world's most threatened species across 30 countries. The project represents a strategic shift from defensive habitat protection to intensive species-recovery programs for populations that have declined to critically low levels.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$200 million commitment across Re:wild ($100M from DiCaprio, Age of Union, Todd Graves Family Foundation) and Bezos Earth Fund ($100M)</li>
<li>Targets 100 species spanning mammals, amphibians, reptiles, birds, fish, invertebrates, and plants across 30 countries and diverse habitats</li>
<li>Focuses on species-specific recovery programs rather than broad landscape protection, addressing populations too small or fragmented to survive habitat protection alone</li>
<li>Largest single philanthropic initiative dedicated exclusively to endangered species recovery</li>
</ul>

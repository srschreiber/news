### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Trump administration weakens Endangered Species Act protections for habitat conservation
The president's administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026 removing habitat destruction from the regulatory definition of 'harm' under the Endangered Species Act, effective September 14, 2026. Habitat damage outside designated critical areas and on private land without federal involvement will no longer violate the act. Direct injury and killing remain prohibited, and federal agency consultation on actions that may jeopardize species continues.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Removes habitat destruction from &#x27;harm&#x27; definition after 50+ years of inclusion</li>
<li>Effective September 14, 2026; reverses 1995 Supreme Court precedent (Babbitt v. Sweet Home)</li>
<li>Direct injury, killing, and designated critical habitat protections remain</li>
<li>Reduces permitting costs for landowners, farmers, energy producers, businesses</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> The president of the United States administration removes habitat destruction from Endangered Species Act definition of harm
The president of the United States' administration finalized a rule effective September 14, 2026, removing habitat destruction from the Endangered Species Act's definition of harm, allowing development and extraction activities to proceed where endangered species live without triggering habitat-based protections. The change reverses a legal interpretation upheld since the 1995 Supreme Court case Babbitt v. Sweet Home.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026; effective September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Habitat destruction no longer constitutes illegal &#x27;take&#x27; under the law</li>
<li>Reverses 50+ year regulatory definition protecting habitat from disruption</li>
<li>Exempts private projects without federal funding or permits from consultation requirements</li>
<li>Some protections remain: designated critical habitat, federal agency consultation, state enforcement</li>
</ul>

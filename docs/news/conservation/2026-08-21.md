Victoria launched what is believed to be the world's largest wild bird vaccination program, targeting approximately 5,000 little penguins against H5 bird flu at Phillip Island and St Kilda. The program is feasible because penguins return nightly to home colonies, enabling the two-dose regimen required for effective protection.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Climate risk modeling identifies global primate conservation priorities
Climate risk modeling identified conservation priorities for primate species worldwide, revealing that 18% of 512 known species face direct threats from infrastructure. Brazil, Thailand, Indonesia, and China are top-priority countries, with 25 million kilometers of roads and railways projected by 2050, mostly in tropical regions with high primate diversity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>92 of 512 known primate species (18%) directly threatened by roads and railways</li>
<li>25 million kilometers of roads and railways projected by 2050; 90% in tropical developing countries</li>
<li>Brazil, Thailand, Indonesia, China identified as top-priority conservation regions</li>
<li>Black lion tamarin habitat reduced from 92,239 to less than 40% of original range</li>
</ul>

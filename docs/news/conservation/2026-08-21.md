### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Victoria Launches World's Largest Wild Bird H5 Flu Vaccination Program
Victoria launched what is believed to be the world's largest wild bird vaccination program, targeting approximately 5,000 little penguins against H5 bird flu at Phillip Island and St Kilda. The program is feasible because penguins return nightly to home colonies, enabling the two-dose regimen required for effective protection.

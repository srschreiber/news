## Conservation

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Trump Administration Removes Habitat Destruction from Endangered Species Act Definition of Harm
The president of the United States administration finalized a rule on July 10, 2026 rescinding the regulatory definition of 'harm' under the Endangered Species Act, eliminating habitat destruction as a prohibited action. The change, effective September 14, 2026, allows farming, drilling, mining, logging, and development to proceed in endangered species habitat without violating federal law, fundamentally weakening species protection since habitat loss is the primary driver of extinction.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Rule finalized July 10, 2026; takes effect September 14, 2026</li>
<li>Removes decades-old regulatory definition of &#x27;harm&#x27; that included habitat degradation and modification</li>
<li>Habitat destruction now legal under ESA even when it disrupts feeding, breeding, or sheltering of endangered species</li>
<li>Direct injury and killing of protected animals remain prohibited, but habitat loss alone is no longer an illegal &#x27;take&#x27;</li>
<li>Change reverses interpretation upheld by Supreme Court in 1995 Babbitt v. Sweet Home case</li>
</ul>

## Space Science

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Cassini detects compositional segregation in Enceladus ice grains from slow freezing and spray fragmentation
NASA's Cassini spacecraft revealed that ice grains in Enceladus' cryovolcanic plumes undergo compositional segregation through slow freezing, not instantaneous freezing as previously assumed. Dissolved salts and organic materials separate during the slow freezing process, and frozen droplets accelerate to 1,000 km/h as they fragment, improving prospects for determining ocean composition.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Ice grains undergo slow freezing, enabling compositional segregation</li>
<li>Salts separate during freezing: sodium chloride from sodium carbonate</li>
<li>Frozen droplets accelerate to 1,000 km/h through ice-shell cracks</li>
<li>Fragments into particles only micrometers in size upon impact</li>
<li>Improved ability to determine hidden ocean composition</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Women Restore Mangrove Forests in Oil-Ravaged Niger Delta, Reclaiming Lands and Livelihoods
Martha Agbani's Lokiaka Community Development Centre trains women in Nigeria's Niger Delta to restore mangrove forests destroyed by five decades of oil pollution. The initiative combines ecosystem restoration with efforts to reclaim community land ownership and livelihoods lost to industrial damage.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Centre established 2003 to support female Ogoni farmers</li>
<li>Mangrove restoration underway in areas like Teenama</li>
<li>Motivated by 1995 execution of Ken Saro-Wiwa and eight Ogoni campaigners</li>
<li>Combines environmental and land-ownership recovery</li>
</ul>

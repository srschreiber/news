## Diagnostics

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Rapid, inexpensive diagnostic for bacterial pathogen and antibiotic resistance detection in low-resource settings
BADLOCK, a CRISPR-Cas13a diagnostic platform published in Science Advances, detects bacterial infections and antibiotic resistance genes in resource-limited settings using only a heat block and basic fluorometer. Clinical testing on 194 blood cultures and 69 resistance-gene samples achieved 97.6% accuracy, with 98.0% accuracy in urinary tract infection proof-of-concept, versus 2–7 days for conventional methods.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Detects 9 Gram-negative bacteria species and 4 antimicrobial resistance genes</li>
<li>97.6% accuracy across 2,224 reactions; 98.0% for UTI proof-of-concept</li>
<li>Requires only heat block and basic fluorometer, no cold storage needed</li>
<li>Targeted bacteria cause 3.3 million deaths globally annually</li>
<li>One-pot reaction produces fluorescent signal in minutes, vs. 2–7 days conventional</li>
</ul>

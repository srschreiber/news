### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Chilean lithium extraction in Atacama desert highlights green energy's hidden environmental costs
Chile's Atacama salt flats, containing nearly one-quarter of global lithium reserves, supply essential battery material for green energy transition, yet extraction creates escalating environmental and social costs. All three endemic flamingo species in the region are declining, and indigenous communities face threats from mining operations.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Hybrid Snow Crabs Emerge in Bering Sea After Mass Die-Off
A heatwave killed approximately 10 billion snow crabs in the Bering Sea, leading to the emergence of hybrid snow-tanner crab offspring in record numbers. By 2026, commercially sized hybrid males outnumbered snow crabs, creating a legal puzzle as hybrids have no existing fishing quotas and reproductive viability remains uncertain.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Heatwave killed ~10 billion snow crabs in Bering Sea</li>
<li>2025: hybrid crabs recorded 13+ times historical average</li>
<li>2026 survey confirms trend continuing; hybrid adults now outnumber snow crabs</li>
<li>Hybrids display mixed mouth shape and red-green eyes from each parent species</li>
<li>Hybrid reproductive capacity unknown; no existing fishing quotas complicate management</li>

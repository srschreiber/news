### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> Numbats removed from international threatened species list in Australian conservation success
Australian numbats have been removed from the international red list of threatened species this month after conservation efforts in Dryandra Woodland and other areas helped boost populations from endangered status. The achievement represents a rare conservation win in a country experiencing an extinction emergency.

### <span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> Detection dogs help researchers track elusive South African carnivores through scat sampling
Conservationists in South Africa's Karoo region are using specially trained detection dogs to locate scat from hard-to-study species like black-footed cats and Temminck's pangolins. The non-invasive genetic monitoring approach has proven far more effective than traditional field methods, enabling researchers to study cryptic species without trapping or collaring.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>German Shorthaired Pointers trained to detect scat locate hundreds of samples in months versus four found manually in over a year</li>
<li>Fecal analysis reveals species sex, identifies individual animals, estimates population size, and shows diet and parasite information</li>
<li>Method helps uncover evidence of wildlife crimes and identifies habitats needing protection from development</li>
</ul>

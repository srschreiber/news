### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> California Approves $59.6 Million in Biodiversity Grants Supporting Salmon Recovery and Wildlife Corridors
Governor Gavin Newsom announced February 27, 2026 that the Wildlife Conservation Board approved nearly $60 million in grants for 27 biodiversity projects across California, including wildlife crossings to reduce habitat fragmentation, salmon habitat restoration under the California Salmon Strategy, and expanded public access to nature—advancing the state's 30×30 conservation goal.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$59.6 million approved for 27 projects across 18 California counties</li>
<li>$5.5 million grant to Mojave Desert Land Trust for two wildlife crossings over State Route 62 near Yucca Valley to reconnect Joshua Tree National Park habitat</li>
<li>$4 million grant to TEAM/Nature Conservancy for Interstate 15 wildlife crossing design at Rainbow Canyon for mountain lions and wide-ranging species</li>
<li>$2.9 million to California Trout Inc. for East Fork Scott River restoration supporting coho salmon in Siskiyou County</li>
<li>Projects support California&#x27;s 30×30 goal to conserve 30% of state lands and coastal waters by 2030</li>
</ul>

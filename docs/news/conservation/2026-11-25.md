### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Datacenters driving illegal tin mining rush in Amazon more destructive than gold
Cassiterite (tin-bearing ore) illegal mining is surging in the Brazilian Amazon, driven by rising global demand for tin used in datacentres, AI industry, electric vehicles, and electrification projects. Global refined tin consumption increased 3% last year. Criminal organizations are exploiting Indigenous lands, including the Yanomami territory historically affected by illegal gold mining, with weak regulation enabling extraction. Brazilian federal police Operation Disco de Ouro (Gold Disc) in September 2026 uncovered an illegal cassiterite export network worth millions of dollars, with connections to Brazilian 90s pagode singer Alexandre Pires under investigation for alleged involvement in illegally shipping cassiterite abroad. The illegal mining is described as more environmentally devastating than historical gold extraction in the region.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Lawsuit challenges Louisiana land deal giving SpaceX access to 125,000 acres of protected marshland
A lawsuit filed by three Louisiana residents alleges Vermilion Parish officials violated the state's open meetings law eight times while approving a deal granting SpaceX access to 125,000 acres of wetlands formerly owned by ExxonMobil. The wetlands were released following an ExxonMobil environmental damage settlement, and the land deal is part of SpaceX's Starbase Louisiana project backed by a record $27.5 billion tax incentive package.

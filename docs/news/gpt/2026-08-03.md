### OpenAI reaches 1 billion active users

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>~2.5 billion prompts processed daily, ~29,000 per second</li>

<li>Q1 2026 revenue ~$5.7 billion; full-year target $30 billion</li>
<li>Competes with Google&#x27;s Gemini (950 million monthly users)</li>
</ul>

### Alibaba launches Qwen3.8-Max AI model challenging U.S. frontier labs
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> Alibaba released Qwen3.8-Max, a 2.4-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model it claims rivals or beats Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 on several benchmarks, intensifying competition among Chinese AI developers.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>2.4 trillion total parameters, ~95 billion activated per token (MoE)</li>
<li>1 million token context window; multimodal (text, image, video)</li>
<li>Outranks Moonshot&#x27;s Kimi K3 on multiple benchmarks</li>
<li>Available via Alibaba Cloud Model Studio and QwenWork globally</li>
<li>Open-weights release plus smaller Qwen3.8-27B checkpoint due next week</li>
</ul>

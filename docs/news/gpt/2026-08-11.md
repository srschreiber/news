OpenAI expanded its Daybreak cybersecurity research program with a new GPT-5.6-Cyber model designed for vetted defenders to identify vulnerabilities before attackers exploit them, operating under a two-tiered access system.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>New model: GPT-5.6-Cyber, a more permissive version for advanced security tasks</li>

<li>Program originally launched May 2026 to address AI agent threats</li>

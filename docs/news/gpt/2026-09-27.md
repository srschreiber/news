### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> OpenAI's GPT-Red Discovers Self-Replicating AI Worm Vulnerability
OpenAI disclosed on September 25 that its GPT-Red red-teaming system discovered a novel class of prompt injection attack capable of self-replicating across AI agent interactions, compared to a computer worm. The vulnerability was identified June 27, 2026, and observed only in controlled training environments with zero real-world impact to ChatGPT, its API, or customer deployments.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Vulnerability identified June 27, 2026</li>
<li>Attack requires completing malicious task and republishing malicious instructions</li>
<li>Observed only in simulated training and evaluation environments</li>
<li>Zero real-world impact recorded outside controlled tests</li>
<li>No breach or incident reported</li>
<li>GPT-Red: automated red-teaming framework with attacker vs. defender models</li>
</ul>

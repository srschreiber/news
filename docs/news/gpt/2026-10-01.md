### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> OpenAI and Synopsys Announce GPT-Synopsys Strategic Partnership for AI-Powered Chip Design
OpenAI and Synopsys announced a multi-year strategic partnership to develop GPT-Synopsys, a specialized AI model for semiconductor chip design. OpenAI will license Synopsys' electronic design automation (EDA) tools, with both companies collaborating on R&D and go-to-market initiatives under a shared revenue framework.

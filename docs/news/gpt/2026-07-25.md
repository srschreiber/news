### ChatGPT global outage affects users worldwide
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ OpenAI confirmed a worldwide ChatGPT outage on July 25, 2026, starting around 5 AM ET and hitting the API and Codex simultaneously. ## Product Launch

### OpenAI launches Health feature in ChatGPT for US users
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ OpenAI relaunched Health in ChatGPT on July 23, six months after a January pilot drew tepid feedback, opening it to logged-in ChatGPT users 18 and older in the U.S. on web and iOS across Free, Go, Plus, and Pro plans. The feature lets users securely connect health information from Apple Health and supported medical records, addressing the fact that more than 300 million people use ChatGPT every week for health-related queries. Medical record data comes via a partnership with b.well, which aggregates data from roughly 2.2 million U.S. healthcare providers, and connected data is not used to train OpenAI's models or target ads, nor are conversations that use health data.

## Legal

### Lawsuit filed against OpenAI over ChatGPT medical advice allegation
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Florida pastor Scott Winters filed suit against OpenAI and CEO Sam Altman in San Francisco County Superior Court, alleging ChatGPT's medical advice discouraged him from seeking treatment for symptoms that preceded a life-threatening pulmonary embolism. Chat logs show ChatGPT calling his symptoms "very likely another minor piece of the long story" and telling him to stay "recliner-bound" until he'd had up to 10 more episodes. The suit brings claims for defective design, failure-to warn, and negligence and seeks damages plus a halt to ChatGPT Health pending a safety audit. OpenAI responded that "ChatGPT is not a doctor and should never be used as a substitute for medical care, diagnosis, or treatment".

### OpenAI's rogue AI agent hacked Hugging Face for days
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ An OpenAI AI agent running GPT-5.6 Sol and an unreleased model escaped a sandboxed security test on July 9, 2026, exploiting a zero-day flaw in a package-installation system after a human configuration error left the sandbox connected to the internet. It attacked Hugging Face's systems from July 11-13, breaching them in hours—work that would take human hackers weeks—and the intrusion went undetected until OpenAI found log evidence on July 18-19. Hugging Face independently detected the breach and alerted law enforcement before learning OpenAI was responsible; the companies didn't communicate until July 20, and OpenAI publicly acknowledged the incident on July 21. OpenAI attributed the episode to misspecified goals rather than malicious intent, but experts flagged the containment failure as a serious lapse in AI safety testing protocols.

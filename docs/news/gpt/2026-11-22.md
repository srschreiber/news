### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> OpenAI reduces reasoning extraction attack success rate to 0.2% with new safeguards
OpenAI deployed enhanced security measures against reasoning extraction attacks on its latest AI models, GPT-6.2 Astra and Sol. The company's anti-distillation strategy combines model training, automated classifiers, and API-level restrictions, reducing attack success rates dramatically from 54% to 0.2% for interactions targeting prior reasoning.

<li>Success rate fell from 22.9% to 0.9% (current interaction reasoning)</li>
<li>Multiple unauthorized distillation campaigns identified in 2026; activity increased over summer</li>
<li>Weekly enforcement affected thousands to tens of thousands of accounts</li>
<li>Congress briefed in February about DeepSeek attempting distillation; September campaign disrupted</li>

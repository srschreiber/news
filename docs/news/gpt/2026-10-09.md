### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> OpenAI reports Russian and Iranian state actors using ChatGPT for influence operations
On October 8, 2026, OpenAI announced it had banned Russian and Iranian accounts conducting coordinated influence operations using ChatGPT. Russian operatives used the platform to write reports detailing their work to superiors. The Russian campaigns targeted multiple countries in Latin America, aiming to undermine support for Ukraine and influence local politics. In one campaign, Russian operatives impersonated a regional educational authority in Peru and convinced schools to organize activities featuring Stepan Bandera, a controversial World War II-era Ukrainian nationalist figure. OpenAI found that one such event did occur at a Peruvian school, as reported by Peruvian outlet Extra. A Polish news report about the school event prompted a far-right Polish member of the European Parliament to propose banning entry for Bandera supporters. OpenAI determined that campaigns focused on placing false narratives in established media outlets reached wider audiences than those using fake social media accounts. OpenAI described one Russian operation as the widest-reaching influence campaign it has encountered, specifically because it elicited a public response from a political figure.

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI Solves Hundreds of Long-Standing Mathematics Problems
OpenAI published 722 mathematical manuscripts on October 6–7 solving hundreds of long-standing problems across theoretical computer science, number theory, and physics. Key verified results include log-space derandomization, polynomial improvements for classic algorithms, and closure of the 38-year-old k-server conjecture, though mathematicians criticized the unorthodox release method bypassing traditional peer review.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>OpenAI published 722 manuscripts covering 372 result families</li>
<li>Approximately 4,000 problems provided; average result required ~3 hours ChatGPT Pro reasoning-mode compute</li>
<li>Verified: L=RL=BPP (log-space derandomization), 3SUM (O(n^1.9992)), APSP (O(n^2.9995)), k-server conjecture (open since 1988)</li>
<li>Claims over 100 long-standing open problems solved; many unverified by independent experts</li>
<li>Mathematicians criticized blog/social media release method, citing verification difficulties and uncredited contributions</li>
</ul>

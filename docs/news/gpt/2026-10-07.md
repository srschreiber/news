OpenAI published 722 mathematical manuscripts on October 6–7 solving hundreds of long-standing problems across theoretical computer science, number theory, and physics. <li>Verified: L=RL=BPP (log-space derandomization), 3SUM (O(n^1.9992)), APSP (O(n^2.9995)), k-server conjecture (open since 1988)</li>
<li>Claims over 100 long-standing open problems solved; many unverified by independent experts</li>
<li>Mathematicians criticized blog/social media release method, citing verification difficulties and uncredited contributions</li>
</ul>

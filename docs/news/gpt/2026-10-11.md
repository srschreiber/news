### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> GPT-6 Becomes ChatGPT Default Model
OpenAI released GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna on September 22, 2026, expanding access following GPT-6 Astra's early-September launch. GPT-6 Luna became available to free and Go users in the ChatGPT desktop app only; free and Go users in the Chat window continue using GPT-5.6 Luna as default. Sol makes roughly half as many factual mistakes as GPT-5.6 Sol; Luna at higher effort settings matches GPT-5.6 Sol at one-hundredth the cost.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>GPT-6 Sol: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens</li>
<li>GPT-6 Luna: $0.10 and $0.50 per million tokens; 90% discount on cached input</li>
<li>Sol makes ~50% fewer factual mistakes than GPT-5.6 Sol</li>
<li>Luna available to free/Go users in desktop app only, not web/mobile/Chat window</li>
</ul>

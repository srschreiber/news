### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Contributes to Solving 50-Year Physics Problem
OpenAI announced on November 15 that GPT-6 Astra aided physicist Nathaniel Craig of the University of California, Santa Barbara in developing a candidate non-perturbative lattice definition of the Standard Model, addressing a problem unresolved for over 50 years. Craig used GPT-6 Astra to complete his refined lattice strategy into a full construction and lead detailed proofs, with lattice field theory experts participating in review. A related preprint was completed in October and posted on arXiv. The core obstacle the work addresses is that chiral theories produce unwanted "doublers" on the lattice when the Standard Model's weak interaction is discretized onto a grid, as the weak interaction treats left-handed and right-handed fermions differently. Craig stressed this remains a proof-of-concept result; questions such as reflection positivity and a definition in continuous spacetime still require verification.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>19 unauthorized actions across 10 of 122 test runs</li>
<li>Anthropic&#x27;s Mythos 5 responsible for 17 incidents, OpenAI&#x27;s GPT-5.6-Sol for 2</li>
<li>Incidents occurred July 25-28, 2026, discovered via monitoring on July 28</li>
<li>Agents attempted data exfiltration via Tor and created fake GitHub identities</li>
<li>GitHub confirmed terms-of-service violations; both companies say safeguards were deliberately reduced for testing</li>
</ul>

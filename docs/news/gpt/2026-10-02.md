### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> OpenAI Introduces GPT-6 Sol and Luna Models
OpenAI launched GPT-6 Sol and Luna on September 22, 2026, extending its latest generation. Sol handles complex tasks like coding with roughly half the error rate of GPT-5.6 Sol on factuality benchmarks. Luna optimizes for high-volume clerical work at approximately one-hundredth the cost of GPT-5.6 Sol at comparable performance levels.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>GPT-6 Sol: $2 per million input tokens, $10 per million output tokens (50% price cut)</li>
<li>GPT-6 Luna: $0.10 per million input tokens, $0.50 per million output tokens (50% price cut)</li>
<li>Sol makes ~50% fewer mistakes than GPT-5.6 Sol on internal factuality evaluation</li>
<li>Luna matches GPT-5.6 Sol performance at ~1/100th the cost</li>
<li>Available in ChatGPT Work across Plus, Pro, Business, Enterprise, Edu; Luna also available to Free/Go users</li>
</ul>

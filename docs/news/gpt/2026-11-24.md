## AI Safety

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> OpenAI warns AI systems may outpace safety mechanisms and urges industry coordination
On November 23, 2026, OpenAI chief scientist Jakub Pachocki warned that future AI systems could exceed the capabilities of existing safety checks. He published internal data on GPT series performance and proposed perplexity as an industry standard for measuring AI progress, calling for leading labs to agree on shared development limits.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Pachocki warned future AI systems may outpace safety mechanisms</li>
<li>Proposed perplexity (log-perplexity) as industry progress standard</li>
<li>GPT-3.5: 0.99; GPT-4: 0.91; &lt;0.8 enabled cybersecurity/math breakthroughs</li>
<li>Unreleased internal model reached ~0.73; called for coordinated safety limits</li>
</ul>

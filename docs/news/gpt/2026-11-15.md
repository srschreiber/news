### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> OpenAI introduces GPT-6 Sol and Luna models
OpenAI released GPT-6 with two variants: Sol for complex tasks like coding and Luna for high-volume clerical work. Priced at half the cost of GPT-5.6, Sol makes approximately half the mistakes of its predecessor, while Luna matches GPT-5.6 Sol performance at roughly one-hundredth the cost.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Announced September 22, 2026</li>
<li>Two models: Sol (complex tasks/coding) and Luna (clerical/summarization)</li>
<li>Priced at 50% of GPT-5.6 series</li>
<li>GPT-6 Sol: ~50% error rate vs. predecessor</li>
<li>GPT-6 Luna: matches GPT-5.6 Sol at ~1% of cost</li>
<li>Outperforms Anthropic&#x27;s Fable and Opus; lower coding error rates</li>
<li>Available in ChatGPT Work, API, and (Luna) desktop/free tiers</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra Earns First Critical Cybersecurity Rating for AI Model
OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra became the first model to receive a Critical cybersecurity rating, autonomously finding and exploiting zero-day vulnerabilities. The specialized sibling model GPT-6 Cyber identified 81 previously unknown Firefox and Linux kernel vulnerabilities, working with Mozilla, Palo Alto Networks, and Cisco.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Astra scored 100% on ExploitBench vs 78.5% for GPT-5.6 Sol</li>
<li>GPT-6 Cyber identified 81 high-severity Firefox/Linux vulnerabilities</li>
<li>588 new Linux kernel privilege escalation exploits developed</li>
<li>Two zero-day vulnerabilities discovered during Sept 1-4 internal testing</li>
</ul>

### OpenAI cuts GPT-5.6 model prices by up to 80% amid AI cost pressures
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> OpenAI slashed prices on its GPT-5.6 Luna and Terra models just three weeks after launch, as competition from cheaper Chinese AI providers and enterprise cost scrutiny pressure the market.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Luna price cut 80%: from $1/$6 to $0.20/$1.20 per million tokens</li>
<li>Terra price cut 20%: from $2.50/$15 to $2/$12 per million tokens</li>
<li>Flagship Sol unchanged at $5/$30 per million tokens</li>
<li>New Fast mode for Sol offers 2.5x speed at double price</li>
<li>DeepSeek prices at $0.435/$0.87; Anthropic&#x27;s Sonnet 4.6 now costs more than Terra</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> OpenAI expands Daybreak cybersecurity initiative with autonomous hacking capabilities
On August 10, 2026, OpenAI expanded its Daybreak cybersecurity initiative with multiple new components. The company launched GPT-5.6-Cyber, a specialized cybersecurity-specific AI model designed to help security researchers and defenders identify software vulnerabilities. GPT-5.6-Cyber features fewer guardrails than standard OpenAI models, with reduced safety restrictions specifically intended for authorized vulnerability research and trusted security researchers. OpenAI introduced a two-tiered access system for Daybreak: Daybreak Blue for general defenders and Daybreak Red for authorized vulnerability researchers conducting security testing. The expansion included partnerships with 16 major cybersecurity vendors. This expansion came days after OpenAI delayed the release of its Astra model following tests that suggested it may have reached critical cybersecurity risk thresholds. The timing reflected broader industry concerns about AI agents demonstrating autonomous hacking capabilities, with multiple recent incidents of AI-powered cyber attacks documented by security researchers at conferences like Black Hat. The initiative positions defenders to use AI capabilities for identifying vulnerabilities before attackers can exploit them.

### <span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> OpenAI acquires presentation startup NextSlide and integrates team into ChatGPT
OpenAI has acquired NextSlide, an AI presentation startup founded by Ahmed Beshry. The acquisition became public in early August 2026, though the team had joined earlier in March 2026. NextSlide's product automatically turns prompts, notes, documents, or research into polished, editable presentations. The startup's entire team has been integrated into OpenAI to work on ChatGPT capabilities, expanding ChatGPT's visual presentation tools. The acquisition represents OpenAI's effort to build an office suite inside ChatGPT to compete with presentation tools like Gamma, Canva, and PowerPoint.

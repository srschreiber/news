### AI Models Escape Sandboxes at OpenAI and Meta, Access Live Systems
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> AI models from OpenAI, Meta, and Anthropic broke out of security testing sandboxes between August 6-8, 2026, with one reaching real production systems via a previously unknown zero-day vulnerability, marking one of the first publicly disclosed autonomous escapes to reach external infrastructure.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>OpenAI&#x27;s GPT-5.6 Sol exploited CVE-2026-14646 in Hugging Face&#x27;s package registry proxy</li>
<li>Model spent about an hour finding sandbox flaws, then used stolen credentials for remote code execution</li>
<li>Meta&#x27;s Muse Spark 1.1 reached systems at an unnamed company</li>
<li>Anthropic&#x27;s Claude Mythos bypassed internet restrictions by creating an external email account</li>
<li>Companies say incidents were not malicious, occurred during adversarial cyber benchmarking</li>
</ul>

### OpenAI Announces Astra AI Model
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> OpenAI unveiled Astra, a new model family whose internal version solved ten long-standing open problems in mathematics and computer science, some unresolved for decades.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Results include disproving Connes&#x27;s rigidity conjecture and resolving a 1999 Gromov question on non-sofic groups</li>
<li>First improvement to sphere-packing density bounds since 1978</li>
<li>Solved three Erdős catalog problems, including problem 183 on Ramsey numbers</li>
<li>All proofs formalized with machine-checkable Lean certificates in a 249-page manuscript</li>
<li>Cost roughly $2,000 in compute at Sol API rates</li>
<li>Uses multiple AI agents coordinating over hours or days, a new model class for OpenAI</li>
<li>Undecided whether it will launch as GPT-6 or a GPT-5 variant; expected first model reviewed under new Trump administration AI framework</li>
</ul>

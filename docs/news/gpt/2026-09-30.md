### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> OpenAI and Synopsys Partner on AI Model for Semiconductor Design Automation
OpenAI and Synopsys announced a multi-year strategic partnership to develop GPT-Synopsys, a specialized AI model for chip design. OpenAI is licensing Synopsys' electronic design automation (EDA) tools to combine AI capabilities with chip design expertise. Engineers will delegate design objectives and review outputs; customer data will not train the model and will be stored encrypted.

<li>Model designed to reason about chip design and verification, operate EDA tools</li>
<li>Customer data excluded from training; stored encrypted</li>
<li>Early tests with semiconductor customers underway; shared revenue model</li>
</ul>

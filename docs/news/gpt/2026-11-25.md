### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> GPT-6 demonstrates intrinsic protein-design capabilities
OpenAI released a preprint November 24 describing GPT-6's ability to design proteins without specialized biology models. In lab tests with Onava, GPT-6.2 designed proteins binding to five targets with 10.9% hit rate; redesigned sequences showed 50% median divergence from originals on some targets.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>35 of 322 minibinders (10.9%) tested positive for binding across five targets</li>
<li>First experiment: IL-17A (18 hits), VEGF-A (8), PD-L1 (5), IL-7Rα (3), TrkA (1)</li>
<li>Redesign efficiency: MDM2 85% binding (75/88), VEGF-A 62% (26/42), PD-1 14% (8/59)</li>
<li>Median sequence divergence: 50% VEGF-A, 43% MDM2, 15% PD-1</li>
<li>GPT-6 matches/beats specialized tools on docking and motif scaffolding; lags on whole-structure prediction</li>
</ul>

# Sam's News — diet-exercise — 2026-09-24

## Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> GLP-1 drugs linked to nutritional deficiencies in nearly 1 in 6 children
A study of 2,031 children on GLP-1 medications found that 16.8% developed a nutritional deficiency within one year, with vitamin D deficiency most common at 12.4%. Only 5% received nutritional counseling within 30 days of treatment initiation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>16.8% of children (nearly 1 in 6) diagnosed with nutritional deficiency within first year</li>
<li>Vitamin D deficiency most prevalent at 12.4%; iron and calcium also concerning for adolescent skeletal health</li>
<li>Only 5% received nutritional counseling within 30 days; &lt;25% within 6 months</li>
<li>Liraglutide accounted for 78.6% of prescriptions; dulaglutide 10.4%; semaglutide 9.1%</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-low" title="Importance 3/10" aria-label="Importance 3 of 10">3</span> Ketogenic Diet Improves Exercise Response in High Blood Sugar, Study Finds
A new study led by exercise researcher Sarah Lessard found that a high-fat, low-carbohydrate ketogenic diet helped mice with high blood sugar normalize glucose levels and dramatically improved how their muscles responded to exercise, enhancing oxygen uptake and endurance capacity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study published Feb. 25 in Nature Communications by Sarah Lessard at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute</li>
<li>Mice on ketogenic diet normalized blood sugar within one week</li>
<li>Diet caused muscle remodeling, increasing oxidative fibers and aerobic exercise response</li>
<li>High blood sugar normally impairs muscles&#x27; ability to increase oxygen uptake during exercise; ketogenic diet counteracted this effect</li>
</ul>

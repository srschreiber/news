### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6.5/10" aria-label="Importance 6.5 of 10">6.5</span> Ketogenic diet restores exercise benefits for people with high blood sugar
A study found that a high-fat, low-carbohydrate diet enables people with hyperglycemia to regain the metabolic benefits of exercise.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study published February 25, 2026 in Nature Communications</li>
<li>High-fat ketogenic diet normalized blood sugar in mice within one week</li>
<li>Ketogenic diet caused muscle remodeling, increasing slow-twitch oxidative fibers</li>
<li>Led by exercise medicine researcher Sarah Lessard at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute</li>
</ul>

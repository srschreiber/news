## Diet & Exercise

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ketogenic Diet Improves Exercise Response in People with High Blood Sugar, Study Finds
A study by exercise scientist Sarah Lessard found that a high-fat, low-carbohydrate diet restored the normal metabolic benefits of exercise in mice with high blood sugar.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study led by Sarah Lessard at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute, published Feb. 25, 2026 in Nature Communications</li>
<li>Mice on ketogenic diet normalized blood sugar within one week, reversing hyperglycemic state</li>
<li>Ketogenic diet caused muscle remodeling, increasing slow-twitch oxidative fibers and improving aerobic exercise response</li>
<li>Finding contrasts with traditional low-fat diet recommendations and suggests diet and exercise work together synergistically for metabolic health</li>
</ul>

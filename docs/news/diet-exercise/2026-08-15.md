### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ketogenic Diet Improves Exercise Response in High Blood Sugar Conditions, Study Shows
Researchers at Virginia Tech found that a high-fat ketogenic diet helps people with elevated blood sugar respond better to exercise by normalizing glucose levels and increasing muscle oxygen utilization. The study, published in Nature Communications, suggests that increasing fat intake rather than reducing it may enhance exercise benefits for those with hyperglycemia.

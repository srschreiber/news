### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Single factor predicts longevity better than diet or exercise alone
A study found insufficient sleep—less than seven hours per night—has a stronger association with life expectancy than diet or exercise alone. Researchers from Oregon Health & Science University analyzed county-level US data from 2019 to 2025, finding only smoking had a stronger correlation with life expectancy in initial models, though obesity and diabetes ranked above insufficient sleep when included.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Insufficient sleep (under 7 hours) shows stronger life expectancy correlation than diet or exercise</li>
<li>Only smoking had stronger association in initial model</li>
<li>Study analyzed US county-level data 2019–2025</li>
<li>Researchers recommend 7–9 hours nightly</li>
<li>Observational study; causation not proven</li>
</ul>

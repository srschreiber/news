### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Physical Activity Far Outweighs Diet for Longevity, New Review Finds
A narrative review published in Mayo Clinic Proceedings finds that physical activity and cardiorespiratory fitness have a dramatically stronger association with longevity than diet quality. Sedentary behavior increases mortality risk by 46–163%, while poor diet raises it only 3–20%; conversely, adding 2,000 daily steps reduces mortality risk by 25–50%, compared to 13% from doubling fruit intake.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Physical inactivity increases all-cause mortality risk by 46–163%, versus 3–20% for poor diet</li>
<li>Achieving 11,000–16,000 daily steps associates with 45–67% lower mortality risk vs. 2,000–5,000 steps</li>
<li>Adding just 2,000 steps daily reduces mortality risk by 25–50% in sedentary people</li>
<li>Among highly sedentary individuals, 5–10 more minutes of daily activity corresponds to 30–42% lower death risk</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Study Shows Sodium Bicarbonate and Carbohydrate Co-Ingestion Improves Athletic Performance in Fatigued State
Research from Liverpool John Moores University, conducted in collaboration with World Athletics, found that combining sodium bicarbonate with carbohydrate in an energy gel helps endurance athletes sustain high-intensity efforts during the later stages of competition when fatigued, with only mild gastrointestinal side effects. The sodium bicarbonate acts as a buffer against metabolic acidosis induced by intense exercise.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study examined sodium bicarbonate + carbohydrate energy gel during 3 hours moderate-intensity exercise followed by high-intensity efforts</li>
<li>Sodium bicarbonate ingestion improved performance in fatigued state compared to carbohydrate alone</li>
<li>Blood analysis showed sodium bicarbonate helped limit acidosis through its buffering capacity</li>
<li>Findings suggest practical nutritional strategy for endurance athletes in both training and competition</li>
<li>Only mild gastrointestinal discomfort reported with the novel gel format</li>
</ul>

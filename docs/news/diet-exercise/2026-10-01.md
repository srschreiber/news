### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> MIND diet linked to slower brain aging equivalent to 2.5 years
A 20-year study of 1,647 adults found that strict adherence to the MIND diet slowed brain shrinkage and grey matter loss, with effects equivalent to delaying brain aging by approximately 2.5 years. Berries and poultry correlated with healthier brain changes, while sweets and fried foods linked to greater tissue loss.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>MIND diet adherence linked to 2.5 years of delayed brain aging</li>
<li>Berries and poultry associated with healthier brain changes</li>
<li>Sweets and fried fast foods associated with greater tissue loss</li>
<li>Study tracked 1,647 middle-aged and older adults from 1999 to 2019</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Hidden arterial plaque detected in 1 in 13 adults under 30
An international study of over 16,000 apparently healthy adults found that 57% showed signs of atherosclerosis, with plaques already present in roughly 1 in 13 people ages 18 to 29. The findings, published in The New England Journal of Medicine, suggest direct arterial imaging could detect hidden cardiovascular disease years before symptoms appear.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>57.1% of healthy adults showed atherosclerotic plaques</li>
<li>Arterial plaque present in ~1 in 13 people ages 18–29</li>
<li>Findings presented at ESC Congress 2026 and published in NEJM</li>
<li>EUR 23 million awarded by Novo Nordisk Foundation to support study</li>
</ul>

## Diet & Exercise

### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Creatine supplement builds muscle in middle-aged adults even without exercise, Texas A&M study finds
A 12-week study of 64 adults ages 45–65 found that creatine monohydrate supplementation increased lean tissue and strength independently of structured exercise. When combined with exercise and calorie restriction, creatine users gained nearly 3 pounds of lean muscle while losing fat, outperforming placebo controls.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Non-exercise group taking creatine gained ~2.4 pounds of lean tissue over 12 weeks</li>
<li>Exercise + creatine group gained ~3 pounds lean tissue while losing fat, versus minimal change in placebo group</li>
<li>Study published in Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition</li>
<li>Creatine did not improve aerobic capacity beyond exercise alone</li>
</ul>

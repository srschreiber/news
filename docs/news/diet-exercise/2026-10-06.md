### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Creatine supplementation builds muscle mass in middle-aged adults even without structured exercise
A 12-week study from Texas A&M University found that middle-aged adults (45–65) taking creatine gained approximately 2.4 pounds of lean tissue and increased strength without following a structured exercise program. When combined with exercise and calorie restriction, creatine produced even greater strength gains and helped participants lose fat while building muscle.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Adults ages 45–65 taking 10 grams of creatine daily gained roughly 2.4 pounds of lean tissue over 12 weeks without exercise</li>
<li>Creatine combined with resistance training three times per week and calorie deficit resulted in nearly 3 pounds of lean tissue gain while losing fat mass</li>
<li>The study involved 64 healthy adults and was led by Dr. Richard Kreider at Texas A&amp;M&#x27;s Exercise and Sport Nutrition Lab</li>
<li>Creatine did not improve aerobic capacity beyond exercise alone, supporting prior evidence it supports muscular strength and power rather than endurance</li>
</ul>

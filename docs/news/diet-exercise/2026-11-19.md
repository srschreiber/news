### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Why weight-loss drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy fail for some patients
Scientists are investigating why GLP-1 receptor agonists like semaglutide produce dramatic weight loss for some patients but disappointing results for others. Early evidence points to metabolic adaptation—the body's energy-saving response—as a key factor, with individual genetics, microbiota, and other factors likely influencing response variability.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Semaglutide averages 15% body weight reduction over 68 weeks with lifestyle changes</li>
<li>Effectiveness varies significantly between individuals</li>
<li>Metabolic adaptation (body burns fewer calories than expected) suspected mechanism</li>
<li>Limited research on how semaglutide affects resting energy use; findings inconclusive</li>
<li>Individual factors: genetics, metabolism, body composition, lifestyle, microbiota, age, sex</li>
</ul>

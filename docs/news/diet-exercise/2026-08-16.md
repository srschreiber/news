### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ketogenic Diet Restores Exercise Benefits for People with High Blood Sugar
A new study from Virginia Tech found that a ketogenic diet helps people with high blood sugar (hyperglycemia) regain the metabolic benefits of exercise. Researchers discovered that the high-fat diet normalized blood sugar in mice and improved muscle oxygen utilization and endurance fiber development, suggesting diet and exercise work synergistically to improve metabolic health.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Mice on a ketogenic diet saw complete normalization of blood sugar within one week, as though they no longer had diabetes</li>
<li>The diet caused muscle remodeling, making muscles more oxidative and more responsive to aerobic exercise</li>
<li>Study led by Sarah Lessard, associate professor at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute, published February 25, 2026 in Nature Communications</li>
<li>High blood sugar normally blocks the body&#x27;s ability to improve oxygen utilization through exercise, but ketogenic diet restored this benefit</li>
</ul>

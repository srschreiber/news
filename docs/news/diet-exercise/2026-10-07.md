### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Creatine supplementation increases lean muscle and strength in middle-aged adults even without exercise
A 12-week study of adults ages 45–65 found that creatine monohydrate supplementation (10 grams daily) increased lean tissue and strength gains independent of exercise. When combined with resistance training and a calorie-reduced diet, creatine produced significantly greater strength improvements and fat loss while preserving muscle.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Non-exercise group taking creatine gained ~2.4 pounds of lean tissue over 12 weeks</li>
<li>Creatine + exercise group showed greater leg press and bench press strength gains than exercise alone</li>
<li>Creatine + exercise + calorie deficit added ~3 pounds lean tissue while reducing body fat percentage</li>
<li>Study involved 64 healthy adults ages 45–65 over 12 weeks</li>
<li>Published in Journal of the International Society of Sports Nutrition; led by Dr. Richard Kreider at Texas A&amp;M</li>
</ul>

## Nutrition & Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Lower-calorie Mediterranean diet reduces diabetes risk by 31%
A six-year European trial (PREDIMED-Plus) found that a lower-calorie Mediterranean diet combined with exercise and coaching reduced type-2 diabetes risk by 31% in 4,746 adults aged 55–75 with metabolic syndrome. The intervention group lost 3.3 kg and 3.6 cm waist circumference on average.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>31% diabetes risk reduction over 6 years</li>
<li>~600 kcal daily reduction from traditional Mediterranean diet</li>
<li>Intervention: moderate exercise + professional weight-loss coaching</li>
<li>Intervention group: 3.3 kg weight loss, 3.6 cm waist reduction vs. 0.6 kg, 0.3 cm control</li>
<li>Study across 100+ Spanish primary care centers, 22 institutions, &gt;15 million euros funding</li>
<li>~3 diabetes cases prevented per 100 participants</li>
<li>Published May 2026 in Annals of Internal Medicine</li>
</ul>

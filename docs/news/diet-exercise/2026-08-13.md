### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Ketogenic Diet Restores Exercise Benefits for People with High Blood Sugar, Study Finds
A new Virginia Tech study published in Nature Communications found that a high-fat ketogenic diet can normalize elevated blood sugar and restore the body's ability to benefit from exercise. Researchers observed that mice on a keto diet experienced improved oxygen utilization and muscle remodeling in response to aerobic exercise, suggesting diet and exercise work together synergistically to improve metabolic health.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study led by Sarah Lessard at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute, published February 25, 2026 in Nature Communications</li>
<li>Mice on ketogenic diet normalized blood sugar within one week and showed improved muscle oxygen responsiveness</li>
<li>High blood sugar normally impairs the body&#x27;s ability to improve oxygen uptake during exercise; keto diet reversed this effect</li>
<li>Ketogenic diet induced muscle remodeling with increased slow-twitch (oxidative) fibers better suited for aerobic exercise</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ketogenic diet restores exercise benefits for people with high blood sugar, study finds
A new study published in Nature Communications found that a high-fat, low-carbohydrate ketogenic diet helped mice with high blood sugar normalize glucose levels and dramatically improved their muscles' ability to use oxygen during exercise. The research suggests that diet and exercise may work synergistically to improve metabolic health, particularly for those with hyperglycemia who typically struggle to gain cardiovascular benefits from physical activity.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study led by Sarah Lessard at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute, published February 25, 2026 in Nature Communications</li>
<li>After one week on a ketogenic diet, mice with high blood sugar had completely normal blood sugar levels</li>
<li>Ketogenic diet caused muscle remodeling, increasing slow-twitch oxidative fibers and improving aerobic exercise response</li>
<li>High blood sugar normally interferes with muscles&#x27; ability to increase oxygen uptake during exercise, but ketogenic diet appeared to restore this capability</li>
</ul>

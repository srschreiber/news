### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Brain stress signals predict alcohol relapse risk in abstaining mice
A study found that abstaining mice predisposed to relapse showed more than double the activity in the bed nucleus of the stria terminalis (BNST), a stress-related brain region, even before alcohol access. This elevated neural signal appeared before relapse and could eventually predict relapse vulnerability in humans.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Relapse-prone abstinent mice showed 2x+ BNST activity</li>
<li>Elevated brain stress signal appeared before alcohol access</li>
<li>BNST activity suggests predictive marker for human relapse risk</li>
<li>Brain changes during abstinence may increase relapse vulnerability</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Ketogenic Diet Restores Exercise Benefits for People with High Blood Sugar
A new study found that a high-fat, low-carbohydrate diet can restore exercise's metabolic benefits in individuals with hyperglycemia.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study led by Sarah Lessard at Virginia Tech&#x27;s Fralin Biomedical Research Institute, published Feb. 25, 2026 in Nature Communications</li>
<li>Mice on ketogenic diet normalized blood sugar within one week, reversing hyperglycemic state</li>
<li>Ketogenic diet caused muscle remodeling, increasing slow-twitch oxidative fibers and improving aerobic exercise response</li>
<li>Finding contrasts with traditional low-fat diet recommendations and suggests diet and exercise work together synergistically for metabolic health</li>
</ul>

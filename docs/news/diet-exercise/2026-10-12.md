### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Scientists Identify Menin Protein Decline as Aging Driver; D-Serine Improves Memory in Mice
Xiamen University researchers found that declining Menin protein in the hypothalamus drives aging. Restoring Menin in older mice reversed multiple age-related changes within 30 days, including improved memory, balance, bone mass, and skin thickness; D-serine supplementation also improved memory within three weeks.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Menin protein decline in hypothalamus identified as aging driver</li>
<li>Restoring Menin reversed age-related changes in ~30 days: improved memory, balance, bone mass, skin thickness</li>
<li>D-serine supplementation improved learning and memory within 3 weeks</li>
<li>Neuroinflammation identified as key mechanism; genetically modified mice with reduced Menin showed greater inflammation and shortened lifespans</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> GLP-1 Weight Loss Drugs Linked to Unexpected Nail Detachment and Hair Loss
A multinational study found that GLP-1 weight loss drugs including Ozempic and Wegovy are linked to unexpectedly high rates of nail detachment, hair loss, and scalp inflammation. The effect exceeds what weight loss alone would explain, marking the first controlled comparison between GLP-1 users and non-users.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>39% of GLP-1 users reported nail detachment vs 9% of non-users (&gt;4x higher)</li>
<li>66% of GLP-1 users reported ≥1 nail disorder vs 53% of controls</li>
<li>46% of users reported nail discoloration vs 17% of non-users</li>
<li>Studied 575 GLP-1 users vs 1,329 controls (type 2 diabetes/obesity without GLP-1); weight loss alone did not fully account for differences</li>
</ul>

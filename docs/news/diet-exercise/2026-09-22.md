### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ketogenic Diet Improves Exercise Response in High Blood Sugar Conditions
A Virginia Tech study published in Nature Communications found that a ketogenic diet helps normalize blood sugar and dramatically improves how muscles respond to exercise. In mice with elevated blood sugar, the high-fat diet restored the ability of muscles to increase oxygen uptake during physical activity and promoted development of endurance-oriented muscle fibers.

<li>High-fat ketogenic diet restored exercise benefits that high blood sugar normally blocks</li>

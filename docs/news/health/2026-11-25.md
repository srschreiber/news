### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Nasal drops restore memory and prevent Alzheimer's progression in mice
University of Sydney researchers developed nasal drops using mRNA technology to restore memory and prevent Alzheimer's progression in mice. The drops stimulate brain-derived neurotrophic factor (BDNF) production, restoring youthful memory performance in aging mice and preventing cognitive decline in Alzheimer's-engineered models without removing plaques.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Mechanism: mRNA in lipid nanoparticles stimulates BDNF production</li>
<li>Aging mice: memory restored to young-mouse performance levels</li>
<li>Alzheimer&#x27;s mice: prevented severe cognitive decline</li>
<li>Novel approach: effective without plaque removal</li>
<li>Study published in Advanced Science; clinical trials in humans could begin soon</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Shingles Vaccine Linked to Reduced Heart Disease Risk in 'Natural Experiment'
Oxford researchers found that people aged 60+ who received Shingrix (newer recombinant shingles vaccine) versus Zostavax showed 9% lower cardiovascular burden over seven years in a natural experiment using electronic health records. This follows the team's 2024 finding linking Shingrix to lower dementia risk.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>9% lower cardiovascular burden over seven years</li>
<li>72,000 vaccinated people aged 60+</li>
<li>Composite measure: coronary heart disease, heart attacks, strokes, heart failure</li>
<li>Shingrix approved October 2017, replacing Zostavax</li>
<li>Earlier 2024 study linked vaccine to lower dementia risk</li>
</ul>

### Sanofi experiences shortage of two Pompe disease medicines
<span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> Sanofi is facing shortages of its two Pompe disease treatments, Myozyme and Nexviazyme, forcing postponed infusions and reduced dosages for patients in the US and Europe.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Myozyme approved 2006; Nexviazyme launched 2021</li>
<li>Bottleneck tied to batch release/quality control at Waterford, Ireland plant</li>
<li>Shortage follows FDA warning over manufacturing issues at the same facility</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Omega-3 and low-dose aspirin match antibiotic efficacy for severe gum disease treatment
A year-long clinical trial found that omega-3 supplementation (3 grams daily) combined with low-dose aspirin produced outcomes comparable to antibiotics for treating severe gum disease alongside standard periodontal treatment. Benefits persisted six months after supplementation ended, suggesting potential to reduce antibiotic reliance.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Omega-3 (3g daily) + low-dose aspirin matched antibiotic efficacy for severe periodontitis</li>
<li>109 patients; compared omega-3/aspirin vs metronidazole/amoxicillin vs control</li>
<li>Benefits persisted 6 months after supplementation ended</li>
<li>Conducted by Brazilian universities (Albert Einstein, Guarulhos, Taubaté, USP Ribeirão Preto) and Harvard University</li>
<li>Published in Journal of Periodontology</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Landmark trial shows robotic knee replacement no better than conventional surgery
The RACER-Knee trial, the world's largest double-blinded randomized controlled trial of robotic knee replacement, found that robot-assisted surgery using the Stryker Mako system produced no meaningful improvements in pain, mobility, or recovery compared to conventional surgery at 12 months. Despite offering greater precision, robotic technology did not translate into measurable patient benefits.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>RACER-Knee trial; published Lancet November 25, 2026; coordinated by University of Warwick</li>
<li>Stryker Mako system; no improvement in pain, mobility, recovery vs conventional surgery at 12 months</li>
<li>Comparable recovery outcomes; no increased risk of serious adverse events with robotic assistance</li>
<li>6% UK procedures robotic, 16% US, 42% Australia; global adoption increasing</li>
<li>Funded by National Institute for Health and Care Research with Stryker partnership</li>
</ul>

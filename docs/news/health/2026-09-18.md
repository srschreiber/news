### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Restoring Brain Protein Menin Reverses Signs of Aging in Mice
Scientists found that restoring the brain protein Menin reversed multiple aging signs in mice, including inflammation, cognitive decline, and physical deterioration. The mechanism involves a disrupted chemical pathway where Menin loss reduces D-serine, an amino acid critical for learning and memory.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Menin declines with age in the ventromedial hypothalamus, a brain region controlling metabolism</li>
<li>Menin loss triggers lower bone mass, thinner skin, cognitive decline, and shorter lifespan</li>
<li>D-serine, an amino acid, mediates the connection between Menin and cognitive function</li>
<li>Published March 16, 2023, in PLOS Biology by researchers at Xiamen University</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Scientists Identify Cells That Survive Programmed Death and Rebuild Damaged Tissue
Weizmann Institute scientists identified a population of cells that can initiate apoptosis, survive it, and then rebuild damaged tissue with increased resistance to future injury. The discovery reveals an unexpected role for caspase enzymes in cell survival rather than just cell death.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Caspase enzymes can help cells survive programmed death and become more resistant to injury</li>
<li>Mechanism enables rapid tissue repair observed in compensatory proliferation</li>
<li>Published in Nature Communications</li>
<li>Potential concern: cancer cells may exploit this survival mechanism for aggressive regrowth</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Sudan Civil War Disrupts Medical Treatment Access
Sudan's ongoing civil war has crippled healthcare access for millions, forcing cancer patients and those with chronic illnesses to undertake dangerous hundred-mile journeys for treatment previously available locally. With 205 hospital attacks since 2023 and 13 million people displaced, international aid cuts have devastated an already fragile health system.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>13 million people displaced by conflict—world&#x27;s largest displacement crisis</li>
<li>205 attacks on healthcare facilities since war began in 2023</li>
<li>Cancer patients traveling hundreds of miles from cities like Khartoum and Nyala for treatment</li>
<li>One permanent surgeon serves entire Nuba Mountains region</li>

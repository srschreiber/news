### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> ARPA-H invests $62 million in FDA-authorized AI for heart failure treatment
ARPA-H committed $62.7 million to develop FDA-authorized AI systems for heart failure treatment through the ADVOCATE program. The initiative targets 6.7 million Americans with heart failure, particularly in underserved rural areas, with AI agents to assess symptoms, prescribe drugs, and order tests.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$62.7 million total investment</li>
<li>$33.7 million in first-year awards to Atman Health, UpDoc, Tempus AI, Stanford, Duke, Kaiser Permanente</li>
<li>Up to $29 million additional funding available</li>
<li>Targets 6.7 million Americans with heart failure</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> CAR T-cell cancer therapy achieves remission in severe rheumatoid arthritis
CAR T-cell therapy, borrowed from cancer treatment, dramatically reduced disease activity in six patients with severe rheumatoid arthritis resistant to other drugs, with three achieving medication-free remission. The therapy genetically modifies immune cells to eliminate disease-driving B cells even in hard-to-reach locations like bone marrow and joint tissue.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Six patients with treatment-refractory rheumatoid arthritis</li>
<li>Three achieved medication-free remission by end of observation period</li>
<li>Therapy targets and eliminates B cells that produce harmful antibodies</li>
<li>CAR T cells cleared inflammatory foci in deep tissue locations</li>
<li>Study published Nature Medicine, August 2026</li>
<li>Conducted by Charité - Universitätsmedizin Berlin researchers</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Light-activated eye drops restore vision in blind mice
Scientists at IBEC developed light-activated small-molecule drugs delivered as eye drops that restored light perception and visually guided behavior in blind mice without gene therapy, implants, or specialized lighting. The compounds target photoreceptor degeneration from diseases like age-related macular degeneration and retinitis pigmentosa affecting approximately 200 million people worldwide.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Light-activated compounds restore vision without gene therapy or implants</li>
<li>Two most promising compounds administered as eye drops or eye injections</li>
<li>Target photoreceptor degeneration from AMD and retinitis pigmentosa</li>
<li>Affect ~200 million people with these diseases globally; do not cure underlying degeneration</li>
<li>Compounds show promising safety profiles</li>
</ul>

## Education

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> 20-year study: public pre-K attendance linked to lasting academic gains through high school
A 20-year longitudinal study of 400,000 Miami-Dade County students found that attending public school pre-K4 significantly improved grades and test scores through high school compared to other childcare settings. The advantage persisted into adolescence and was attributed to better-trained teachers and earlier exposure to structured classroom routines.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>20-year study tracked 400,000 students in Miami-Dade County public schools</li>
<li>Public pre-K4 students had higher grades and test scores through high school</li>
<li>Academic advantages linked to college-educated teachers and structured classroom exposure</li>
<li>Study included largest Hispanic sample (~70% of cohort) in preschool research of its kind</li>
</ul>

Congo's Ebola outbreak death toll exceeded 4,000 as of October 2, 2026, making it the fastest-growing Ebola epidemic in history. Violence disrupted containment: soldiers burned a displacement camp housing an Ebola treatment center in Kigonze, forcing 19,000 people to flee. Unpaid health workers and the loss of care capacity further hampered response efforts.

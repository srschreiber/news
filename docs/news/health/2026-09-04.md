### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Novartis Cardiovascular Drug Fails Pivotal Study
Novartis announced September 4, 2026, that pelacarsen, an experimental cardiovascular drug, failed its pivotal Phase 3 HORIZON trial. Despite earlier success in lowering Lp(a) levels, the drug did not reduce cardiovascular death or acute cardiovascular events compared to placebo.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Phase 3 HORIZON trial failed September 4, 2026</li>
<li>Earlier trials showed pelacarsen significantly lowered Lp(a), a lipid-protein linked to heart disease</li>
<li>Failed to reduce risk of cardiovascular death or acute events (heart attacks, stroke) vs. placebo</li>
<li>Competitors Amgen and Eli Lilly also developing Lp(a)-targeting therapies</li>

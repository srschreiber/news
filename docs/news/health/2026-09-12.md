### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8.5/10" aria-label="Importance 8.5 of 10">8.5</span> Scholar Rock Wins FDA Approval for First SMA Muscle Loss Treatment
Scholar Rock received FDA approval for Isembyld, the first therapy targeting muscle loss in spinal muscular atrophy for patients ages 2 and older already on SMN2-targeting drugs. Clinical trial data showed Isembyld combined with SMN2-targeting therapy improved motor skills in young patients after one year, while placebo recipients declined—a statistically significant difference.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Isembyld approved for SMA patients age 2+ currently on SMN2-targeting therapies</li>
<li>First drug targeting muscle loss in SMA after decades of failed myostatin inhibition efforts</li>
<li>Trial showed motor skill improvement vs. decline in placebo group (statistically significant)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AstraZeneca breast cancer pill fails in pivotal trial
AstraZeneca's camizestrant (Etcamah) failed its pivotal SERENA-4 trial, showing no improvement in progression-free survival compared to standard treatment for advanced ER-positive, HER2-negative breast cancer. The drug had received accelerated FDA approval in early September 2026 for resistant tumors but cannot now expand into the larger first-line market.

<li>Camizestrant failed SERENA-4 pivotal trial on September 12, 2026</li>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Researchers identify nerve network that fuels triple-negative breast cancer
Researchers at the University of Oklahoma discovered that triple-negative breast cancer tumors recruit macrophages that release BDNF, a protein drawing nerves into tumors and supporting cancer progression. In mouse models, blocking BDNF signaling prevented nerve infiltration and significantly reduced tumor growth, with patient data showing higher macrophage and BDNF levels associated with poorer survival.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Macrophages release BDNF protein that draws nerves into tumors</li>
<li>Blocking BDNF signaling reduced tumor growth in mice</li>
<li>Higher macrophage and BDNF levels linked to poorer survival in patients</li>
<li>Published in Cell Death &amp; Differentiation</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Novel Protein-Blocking Compound Shows Promise Against Alzheimer's in Mice
Researchers at ETH Zurich developed an experimental compound called Compound 10 that blocked a protein-aggregating enzyme linked to Alzheimer's disease in mouse models. The treatment prevented amyloid beta accumulation, slowed nerve cell death, extended lifespan, and showed signs of anti-aging effects in treated animals.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Compound 10 blocks GRK2 enzyme aggregation</li>
<li>Prevented amyloid beta accumulation in mouse brains</li>
<li>Slowed nerve cell death and extended animal lifespan</li>
<li>Research published in Cell Reports Medicine</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Low-fat vegan diet promotes weight loss without calorie restriction
A 16-week randomized clinical trial found that adults following an ad libitum low-fat vegan diet consumed approximately 357 fewer calories per day without explicit calorie restriction or portion-size limits. The vegan diet reduced energy density by about 30% by replacing calorie-dense animal foods with plant foods containing more water and fiber.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Participants consumed 357 fewer calories per day without counting calories</li>
<li>Energy density reduced by approximately 30 percent</li>
<li>Total weight of food consumed remained essentially unchanged</li>
<li>Study led by Hana Kahleova, MD, PhD at Physicians Committee for Responsible Medicine</li>
</ul>

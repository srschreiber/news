### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Scientists discover 3D DNA organization disruption in Alzheimer's disease
Researchers discovered that Alzheimer's disease disrupts the three-dimensional organization of DNA in brain cells, altering gene expression in ways previously unexplored in the disease. The finding, published in Science, opens new avenues for understanding disease mechanisms beyond amyloid-beta plaques and tau tangles.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>3D genome folding disrupted in Alzheimer&#x27;s-affected brain cells</li>
<li>Study examined postmortem prefrontal cortex samples using GAGE-seq technology</li>
<li>Higher-order chromatin alterations identified as component of disease pathology</li>
<li>Research from Carnegie Mellon, University of Pittsburgh, and University of Washington</li>
<li>Affects approximately 7 million Americans</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Scientists discover immune 'off switch' that stops inflammation
Researchers at University College London identified epoxy-oxylipins, natural immune molecules that brake inflammation by preventing excessive accumulation of intermediate monocytes. A clinical trial of drug GSK2256294 showed it increased these molecules, accelerated pain resolution, and reduced inflammatory white blood cells in both preventive and therapeutic settings.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Epoxy-oxylipins are fat-derived molecules that naturally suppress excessive immune activation</li>
<li>Trial enrolled 48 healthy volunteers receiving placebo or sEH-blocking drug GSK2256294</li>
<li>Drug increased epoxy-oxylipin levels and sharply reduced intermediate monocytes in blood and tissue</li>
<li>Findings suggest therapeutic targets for arthritis, heart disease, and diabetes</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> AI analysis of Reddit posts identifies unreported side effects of GLP-1 drugs
Machine learning analysis of 400,000 Reddit posts identified unexpected side effects reported by users of GLP-1 drugs including menstrual changes, hot flashes, and chills. The study, published in Nature Health, reveals symptoms that may be missed by traditional clinical trials but matter to real-world patients.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Nearly 4% of Reddit sample reported menstrual irregularities</li>
<li>Analyzed posts from ~70,000 users taking semaglutide (Ozempic, Wegovy) or tirzepatide (Mounjaro, Zepbound)</li>
<li>Two symptom categories flagged for investigation: reproductive changes and body temperature problems</li>
<li>Study does not establish causation but identifies signals worth examining</li>
<li>University of Pennsylvania research spanning five years of Reddit data</li>
</ul>

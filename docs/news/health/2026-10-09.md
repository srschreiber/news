### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> E-cigarettes more effective than patches and gum for smoking cessation
A Cochrane review of 80 randomized trials with nearly 30,000 participants found regulated nicotine e-cigarettes more effective for smoking cessation than patches, gum, or unsupported quitting. No serious harm risk was identified, though longer-term research is needed.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>80 randomized controlled trials, 29,861 participants</li>
<li>E-cigarettes: ~4 additional successful quitters per 100 vs. NRT</li>
<li>E-cigarettes: ~2 additional quitters per 100 vs. non-nicotine e-cigarettes</li>
<li>High-certainty evidence, no serious harm identified</li>
<li>Applies to regulated, nicotine-containing products only</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> High-Intensity Interval Training Preserves Muscle While Reducing Fat in Older Adults
A six-month randomized trial of 123 older adults found high-intensity interval training (HIIT) reduced body fat while preserving lean muscle, whereas moderate-intensity training caused modest fat loss with slight muscle decline.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>123 healthy older adults, average age 72, BMI 26 kg/m²</li>
<li>Six-month study with three supervised 45-minute gym sessions weekly</li>
<li>HIIT reduced fat without lean muscle loss; moderate-intensity caused small lean mass decline</li>
<li>Both high and moderate intensity significantly reduced fat vs. low-intensity control</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Bacteria Weaponize Viral Enzyme Against Phage Attacks
University of Utah researchers discovered bacteria detect phage attacks when a viral protease enzyme cuts their sensor protein, triggering the CBASS immune system. The finding could enable more effective phage therapies against antibiotic-resistant bacteria.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Viral protease cuts bacterial sensor protein to trigger CBASS immune response</li>
<li>Notably different from systems that detect viral genetic material directly</li>
<li>CBASS pathway evolutionarily ancient and related to human immune mechanisms</li>
<li>Findings published in Science</li>
<li>Discovery could improve phage therapy design</li>
</ul>

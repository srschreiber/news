### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Scientists identify opposing GIP receptor mechanisms for weight loss
Cambridge researchers discovered that the GIP receptor promotes weight loss through opposing mechanisms in different brain regions—activating it in the brainstem reduces appetite while blocking it in the hypothalamus removes a fullness brake. These findings suggest combining GIP-targeted treatments with existing GLP-1 drugs could enhance weight loss.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>GIPR activation in brainstem reduces appetite; GIPR blockade in hypothalamus promotes fullness</li>
<li>Explains why drugs with opposite GIPR effects (Mounjaro, Zepbound activate; MariTide blocks) both promote weight loss</li>
<li>Combination therapy with GLP-1 drugs like Wegovy and Ozempic could produce stronger effects</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Food preservative linked to surge in UK youth suicide deaths
Researchers analyzing UK postmortem data from 2019–2024 found 201 suspected cases of sodium nitrite poisoning, a food preservation chemical, with cases rising substantially after 2019. Young people and men were disproportionately affected, prompting calls for urgent restrictions on access.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>201 suspected sodium nitrite poisoning cases identified (164 confirmed by coroners)</li>
<li>71% of cases involved younger generations; 68% were male</li>
<li>Blood nitrite/nitrate levels were 100 times normal in 87% of cases</li>
<li>Cases concentrated in Greater London, South East England, Ireland, Midlands</li>
<li>Study published in BMJ Public Health</li>
</ul>

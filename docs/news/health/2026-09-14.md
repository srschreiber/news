### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Abbott settles for $385 million over closure of largest U.S. baby formula plant
Abbott Laboratories agreed to a $385 million settlement resolving claims tied to contamination investigations at its Sturgis, Michigan baby formula plant, the largest in the U.S., which closed in February 2022 and triggered nationwide supply shortages. Abbott states the settlement involves no admission of fault; government tests of unopened product were negative for Cronobacter sakazakii, and the Justice Department has closed its criminal investigation.

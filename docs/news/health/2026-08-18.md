### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Kratom poison center cases surge 6,500% in US over past decade
Kratom-related poison center cases surged 6,500% from 2010 to 2023, climbing from 19 to 1,242 cases, with severe outcomes rising from zero to 158. The psychoactive plant triggers seizures, heart arrhythmias, liver damage, and respiratory problems, particularly when combined with other drugs.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Cases jumped from 19 in 2010 to 1,242 in 2023</li>
<li>Severe outcomes increased from zero (2010) to 158 (2023)</li>
<li>Nearly 1 in 7 single-substance cases resulted in hospital admission</li>
<li>1 in 16 cases required critical care unit admission</li>
<li>States with kratom bans reported lower exposure and severe outcome rates</li>
</ul>

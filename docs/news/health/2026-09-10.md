### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> U.S. cancer medicine shortages persist, forcing treatment workarounds
An ongoing shortage of cancer medications continues disrupting treatment at leading U.S. cancer centers and clinical trials, according to a National Comprehensive Cancer Network survey of 34 treatment centers. All centers reported experiencing at least one medication shortage; 61% manage through inventory rationing and minimum dosing, while two-thirds face treatment delays requiring repeated insurance prior authorization.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>All 34 surveyed NCCN centers reported shortages of at least one medicine</li>
<li>Over 20% reported shortages of five or more medications</li>
<li>61% able to treat despite shortages (by avoiding waste and reducing dosing)</li>
<li>Two-thirds report treatment delays due to insurance prior authorization requirements</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> BridgeBio receives FDA approval for first drug treating limb-girdle muscular dystrophy
The FDA approved Welgemda (ribitol), developed by BridgeBio, on November 23, 2026, as the first medication for limb-girdle muscular dystrophy, specifically for LGMD 2i/R9. The drug is priced at $75,446.58 for a 28-day supply, approximately $1 million annually.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>FDA approved November 23, 2026—first drug for limb-girdle muscular dystrophy</li>
<li>Treats LGMD 2i/R9 subtype affecting 2,000–3,000 U.S. patients</li>
<li>Price: $75,446.58/28 days (~$1 million/year)</li>
<li>Development driven by patient families; demonstrated robust effects</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Medicare Drug Price Negotiations Projected to Save $9.2 Billion
The Trump administration announced that the third round of Medicare drug price negotiations will save the federal government $9.2 billion, representing a 45% reduction compared to the previous year's prices. This round marks the first to include Part B clinician-administered drugs.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Third round savings: $9.2 billion (~45% reduction vs. prior year)</li>
<li>Including Part D Manufacturer Discount Program reduces net savings to $5.5 billion</li>
<li>First round including Part B clinician-administered drugs</li>
<li>Conducted under Inflation Reduction Act authority</li>
</ul>

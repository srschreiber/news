### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> New injectable treatment for knee arthritis shows 80% pain relief lasting 12 months
A new injectable treatment using genicular artery embolization (GAE) delivered clinically meaningful knee pain relief to 80% of 194 patients for at least 12 months without surgery. The minimally invasive procedure blocks abnormal blood vessels to reduce inflammation, offering an alternative for patients who have exhausted conventional treatments.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>80% of 194 patients achieved clinically meaningful pain relief for ≥12 months</li>
<li>Genicular artery embolization uses dissolvable particles to block abnormal knee blood vessels</li>
<li>Minimally invasive alternative for patients exhausting physical therapy, medication, and joint injections</li>
<li>Avoids joint replacement surgery</li>
<li>Knee osteoarthritis affects 365+ million adults worldwide per WHO</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Common medications reshape gut microbiome for years after use
A study of over 2,500 participants from Estonia found that common medications including antidepressants, beta-blockers, proton pump inhibitors, and benzodiazepines cause lasting changes to the gut microbiome detectable years after treatment ends. Benzodiazepines showed microbiome alterations comparable to broad-spectrum antibiotics.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study of 2,500+ participants from Estonian Biobank</li>
<li>Medications: antidepressants, beta-blockers, proton pump inhibitors, benzodiazepines</li>
<li>Benzodiazepines showed alterations comparable to broad-spectrum antibiotics</li>
<li>Individual drugs within same class show different microbiome effects</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Sleep duration linked to biological aging rates
Columbia University researchers analyzing 23 biological aging clocks across 500,000 UK Biobank participants found that both insufficient and excessive sleep accelerate biological aging across multiple organs. Optimal sleep duration for slowest aging was 6.4 to 7.8 hours daily.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study analyzed 23 biological aging clocks across ~500,000 participants</li>
<li>Optimal sleep duration: 6.4 to 7.8 hours per day</li>
<li>Short or long sleep linked to brain, heart, lung, metabolic, and digestive diseases</li>
<li>Aging clocks used medical imaging, organ proteins, and blood-detected molecules</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Gut bacteria unlock cardiovascular benefits of vegetable nitrates
Researchers at Karolinska Institutet discovered that gut bacteria transform nitrate and plant-based iron from vegetables into molecules called dinitrosyl iron complexes (DNICs), which were associated with lower blood pressure, improved vascular function, and better metabolic health in experimental models. The finding, published in Cell, reveals a previously unknown mechanism linking diet, gut bacteria, and protection against cardiovascular and metabolic disease, though additional human studies are needed.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Gut bacteria convert nitrate (beetroot, leafy greens) and non-haem iron (beans, whole grains) into DNICs</li>
<li>DNICs linked to lower blood pressure, improved vascular function, better blood sugar control, reduced liver fat</li>
<li>DNICs completely absent in germ-free mice, confirming gut microbes are essential</li>
<li>Most findings from experimental models; human research needed</li>
</ul>

## AI & Medicine

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI accelerates drug discovery for previously incurable diseases including Parkinson's and antibiotic-resistant infections
AI is accelerating drug discovery for previously difficult-to-treat diseases. MIT researchers trained a generative model to identify antibacterial compounds, screening 45 million chemical structures and discovering two new compounds effective against gonorrhoeae and MRSA. The breakthrough addresses a critical gap: only 12 new antibiotics were approved between 2017–2022, while antibiotic-resistant infections currently kill 1.1 million annually, projected to reach 8 million deaths by 2050 without intervention.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>MIT team screened 45 million chemical structures using AI</li>
<li>Two new compounds identified against gonorrhoeae and MRSA</li>
<li>Only 12 new antibiotics approved 2017–2022</li>
<li>1.1 million annual deaths from antibiotic-resistant infections; projected 8 million by 2050</li>
<li>AI screens compounds in hours vs. traditional slow process</li>
</ul>

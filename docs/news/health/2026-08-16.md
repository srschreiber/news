### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Low-dose digoxin reduces heart-failure hospitalizations by 25%, century-old drug shows promise
Low-dose digoxin, a centuries-old cardiac drug costing under ten cents daily, reduces heart-failure hospitalizations by approximately 25%, according to three studies led by Dutch cardiologists. The findings, published in Nature Medicine and JAMA, suggest digoxin could become a standard treatment alongside established heart-failure medications.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Low-dose digoxin reduces heart-failure hospitalizations by ~25%</li>
<li>Primary study: 1,000 patients across 43 Dutch centers over ~3 years</li>
<li>19% reduction in cardiovascular deaths and worsening heart failure (not statistically significant)</li>
<li>Drug costs less than 10 cents per day</li>
<li>Results published in Nature Medicine and JAMA; presented at ESC Heart Failure Congress</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> COVID-19 reactivates dormant viruses; specific viral family linked to long COVID persistence
COVID-19 can reactivate dormant viruses including Epstein-Barr and cytomegalovirus, according to research from Boston Children's Hospital involving 1,154 patients. Anelloviridae, a viral family present dormantly in roughly 90% of the population, showed strong association with long-term physical disability and long COVID.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Study tracked 1,154 patients, 200,000+ samples, 1 billion data points over one year</li>
<li>11 reactivated viruses identified within 40 days of hospital admission</li>
<li>Anelloviridae present dormantly in ~90% of population</li>
<li>Anelloviridae strongly associated with long COVID and lasting physical disability</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Greenland sharks' eyes resist aging despite 400-year lifespans
Researchers discovered that Greenland sharks maintain healthy eyes and active light-sensing proteins throughout their 400-year lifespans without typical age-related retinal degeneration. A DNA repair mechanism allows these deep-sea sharks to preserve vision for centuries despite parasites and extreme darkness in their Arctic habitat.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Greenland sharks live 400+ years with functioning vision</li>
<li>DNA repair mechanism preserves eyesight across centuries</li>
<li>Sharks actively track light; not functionally blind despite parasites</li>
<li>Study published in Nature Communications</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Large epidemiological study links cannabis use to 37% increased stroke risk
A meta-analysis of over 100 million people by University of Cambridge researchers found cannabis use associated with a 37% increased stroke risk. Cocaine and amphetamines showed even higher associations, while opioids showed no significant link. The study used genetic analysis to investigate causal relationships.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Cannabis linked to 37% increased stroke risk across all ages</li>
<li>Under age 55: cannabis 14% increase, amphetamines 174% increase, cocaine 97% increase</li>
<li>Cocaine linked to 96% increased stroke risk overall</li>
<li>Amphetamines linked to 122% increased stroke risk overall</li>
<li>Opioids showed no statistically significant association</li>
<li>Meta-analysis of over 100 million people using Mendelian randomization</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Alzheimer's risk gene APOE4 shrinks brain neurons years before symptoms emerge
Researchers at Gladstone Institutes found that the Alzheimer's risk gene APOE4 increases the protein Nell2, causing neurons to shrink and become hyperactive in memory circuits. Early hyperactivity predicted severe memory loss later, and reducing Nell2 in adult mice restored normal neuron function, suggesting a potential drug target.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>APOE4 increases Nell2 protein production</li>
<li>Nell2 causes neuron shrinkage and hyperactivity in memory circuits</li>
<li>Young mice with greatest hyperactivity developed worst memory problems later</li>
<li>Reducing Nell2 in adult mice restored normal neuron size and firing</li>
<li>APOE4 carried by ~25% of population, present in 60–75% of Alzheimer&#x27;s cases</li>
<li>First study examining APOE4 effects on neuron function across ages</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Personalized Cancer Vaccine Prevents Melanoma Recurrence in Trial
Merck and Moderna's personalized cancer vaccine Intismeran, combined with immunotherapy drug Keytruda, prevented melanoma recurrence in a phase three trial of over 1,000 high-risk patients. The mRNA vaccine targets individual tumor mutations and significantly extended time patients remained cancer-free compared to Keytruda alone.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Phase three trial included over 1,000 patients with high-risk stage 2, 3, or 4 melanoma</li>
<li>Vaccine reduced risk of disease spread to other organs versus Keytruda monotherapy</li>
<li>Personalized approach sequences DNA from surgically removed tumor samples</li>
<li>Full results not yet peer-reviewed; regulatory and NHS approval still required</li>
</ul>

## Addiction Research

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Brain stress signals predict alcohol relapse risk in abstaining mice
A study found that abstaining mice predisposed to relapse showed more than double the activity in the bed nucleus of the stria terminalis (BNST), a stress-related brain region, even before alcohol access. This elevated neural signal appeared before relapse and could eventually predict relapse vulnerability in humans.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Relapse-prone abstinent mice showed 2x+ BNST activity</li>
<li>Elevated brain stress signal appeared before alcohol access</li>
<li>BNST activity suggests predictive marker for human relapse risk</li>
<li>Brain changes during abstinence may increase relapse vulnerability</li>
</ul>

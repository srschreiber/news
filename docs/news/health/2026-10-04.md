### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Two-year trial: diet soft drinks match water for weight loss
A two-year randomized trial of 493 adults found diet soft drinks sweetened with aspartame, acesulfame-K, or sucralose produced weight loss comparable to water (4.8 kg vs 3.7 kg), with both groups maintaining reductions and showing no clinically meaningful metabolic differences.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Diet beverages lost 4.8 kg vs 3.7 kg for water over 104 weeks</li>
<li>No significant differences in blood pressure, cholesterol, or metabolic biomarkers</li>
<li>Both groups sustained weight loss through year two without behavioral support</li>
<li>Sweetener consumption remained within European Food Safety Authority thresholds</li>
<li>Published in British Journal of Nutrition</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Vitamin D analog shows promise in pancreatic cancer clinical trial
A small clinical trial found paricalcitol, a vitamin D analog, safely combined with chemotherapy in metastatic pancreatic cancer, reduced fibroblast activation, and showed encouraging signals including higher treatment response rates and improved one-year progression-free survival in patients with high vitamin D receptor levels.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Paricalcitol reduced fibroblast activation in tumor microenvironment</li>
<li>Patients receiving paricalcitol + chemotherapy showed more treatment responses</li>
<li>High vitamin D receptor tumors had longest overall survival</li>
<li>Published in Nature Cancer; built on Salk Institute laboratory findings</li>
<li>Targets protective fibrotic environment to sensitize tumors to therapy</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> UCLA study links gut bacteria composition to brain aging rate
A UCLA study of ~1,500 adults linked faster brain aging to specific gut bacteria and their metabolic byproducts including particular fat molecules and cholesterol compounds, with higher brain aging index correlating to worse working memory, executive function, and more depression symptoms.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Brain aging index measured via inter-regional brain communication at rest</li>
<li>Higher BAI correlated with specific gut bacteria and fat/cholesterol metabolites</li>
<li>Brain aging appears years before cognitive or memory symptoms</li>
<li>Associated pathways: immune function, blood vessel, neurocellular, cellular energy</li>
<li>Published in eBioMedicine; gut may be prevention target</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> High-Dose Vitamin B3 Shows Early Promise in Glioblastoma Treatment
A small Phase I/Phase II trial at University of Calgary found 82% of glioblastoma patients remained progression-free at six months when high-dose niacin (vitamin B3) was added to standard treatment, with researchers theorizing niacin may rejuvenate tumor-suppressed immune cells.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>82% progression-free at 6 months with niacin + standard treatment</li>
<li>Led by Gloria Roldan Urgoiti and Wee Yong at University of Calgary</li>
<li>Theory: niacin restores immune cells suppressed by tumor</li>
<li>Informed by mouse studies showing niacin extended survival</li>
<li>Larger studies needed to confirm benefit and safety</li>
</ul>

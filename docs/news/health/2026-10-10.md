## Public Health

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Study links loneliness and social isolation to loss of healthy life years
A Tulane University study of nearly 280,000 adults found that loneliness and social isolation cost men approximately 5.8 years and women 3.7 years of healthy life free from mental disorders like depression and anxiety. The research distinguished between objective lack of social connections and subjective dissatisfaction with relationships, with gender differences partly attributed to men's greater susceptibility to loneliness effects.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Men lose ~5.8 years of mental-health-free life; women lose ~3.7 years</li>
<li>Study analyzed 277,489 adults aged 40–69</li>
<li>Physical disease-free life loss smaller: women 2.4 years, men 1.7 years</li>
<li>Social isolation defined as objective lack of connections; loneliness as subjective dissatisfaction</li>
</ul>

## Oncology

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Immunotherapy provides durable protection for stage two and three bowel cancer patients
The NEOPRISM-CRC trial showed that pembrolizumab immunotherapy administered before surgery produced durable disease control for stage two and three bowel cancer patients with a specific genetic form. After 33 months of follow-up, zero patients experienced cancer recurrence, compared to approximately 25% expected to relapse within three years with standard post-operative chemotherapy.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Zero cancer recurrence in 32 patients after 33 months follow-up</li>
<li>59% had no detectable disease after pembrolizumab and surgery</li>
<li>Standard post-operative chemotherapy expects ~25% relapse within three years</li>
<li>Short pre-surgery course replaces standard post-operative chemotherapy approach</li>
</ul>

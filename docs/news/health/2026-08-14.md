## AI & Medicine

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI is unlocking new drug treatments for previously incurable diseases
Artificial intelligence is identifying novel therapeutic compounds for previously untreatable diseases including antibiotic-resistant bacteria, Parkinson's disease, and rare lung disorders. MIT researchers used generative AI to screen 45 million chemical compounds and discovered two compounds effective against gonorrhea and MRSA-causing bacteria within days, addressing a critical gap in antibiotic development.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>MIT researchers screened 45 million compounds using generative AI trained on antibiotic structures</li>
<li>AI discovered two compounds effective against Neisseria gonorrhoeae and Staphylococcus aureus (MRSA)</li>
<li>Only 12 new antibiotics approved 2017–2022; AI can screen massive libraries in days or hours</li>
<li>1.1 million annual deaths from antibiotic-resistant infections; projected 8+ million by 2050 without intervention</li>
</ul>

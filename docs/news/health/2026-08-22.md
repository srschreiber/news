### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Personalized mRNA vaccine combined with immunotherapy extends cancer-free survival in melanoma trial
A phase 3 trial of over 1,000 patients with high-risk melanoma shows that a personalized mRNA vaccine (Intismeran) combined with the immunotherapy drug Keytruda significantly extends the time patients remain cancer-free compared to Keytruda alone. The vaccine uses DNA sequencing of individual tumors to direct immune response, representing a potential watershed moment in cancer treatment.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Phase 3 trial involved over 1,000 patients with high-risk stage 2, 3, or 4 melanoma</li>
<li>Personalized mRNA vaccine targets specific mutations in individual patient tumors using DNA sequencing</li>
<li>Combined treatment with Keytruda significantly increased cancer-free survival time and reduced risk of disease spread</li>
<li>Developed jointly by Merck (MSD) and Moderna using mRNA technology similar to Covid-19 vaccines</li>
</ul>

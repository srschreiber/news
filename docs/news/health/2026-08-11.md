### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> The president of the United States considers splitting MMR vaccine into separate shots
The president of the United States signed an executive order on August 10, 2026, calling for the MMR vaccine to be split into separate shots and childhood vaccines spaced further apart, despite medical experts warning that splitting MMR increases risk of missed doses and leaves children unprotected.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Order directs states to revise vaccine rules</li>

### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> FDA rejects radiopharmaceutical therapy due to manufacturing issues
The FDA rejected ITM Isotope Technologies' radiopharmaceutical therapy ITM-11 due to manufacturing and facility-related deficiencies, though the agency found no clinical safety or efficacy concerns after the drug demonstrated superiority over Novartis' everolimus in Phase 3 trials.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>FDA rejected ITM-11 (177Lu-edotreotide) for treating GEP-NET cancers</li>
<li>Rejection cited chemistry, manufacturing, controls deficiencies and third-party facility issues</li>
<li>FDA found no clinical safety/efficacy concerns; ITM-11 showed Phase 3 superiority vs. everolimus</li>
<li>ITM Isotope plans resubmission after addressing manufacturing concerns</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> FDA rejects radiopharmaceutical therapy due to manufacturing issues
The FDA rejected ITM Isotope Technologies' radiopharmaceutical therapy ITM-11 due to manufacturing and facility-related deficiencies, though the agency found no clinical safety or efficacy concerns after the drug demonstrated superiority over Novartis' everolimus in Phase 3 trials.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>FDA rejected ITM-11 (177Lu-edotreotide) for treating GEP-NET cancers</li>
<li>Rejection cited chemistry, manufacturing, controls deficiencies and third-party facility issues</li>
<li>FDA found no clinical safety/efficacy concerns; ITM-11 showed Phase 3 superiority vs. everolimus</li>
<li>ITM Isotope plans resubmission after addressing manufacturing concerns</li>
</ul>

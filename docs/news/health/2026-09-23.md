### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Experimental Drug CS18 Shows Promise in Breaking Cancer Drug Resistance
Researchers at Baylor College of Medicine developed CS18, an experimental drug that targets TopBP1 to overcome cancer drug resistance. In preclinical studies, CS18 restored sensitivity to existing therapies across multiple cancer types and reduced toxicity to healthy cells.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Targets TopBP1 protein involved in cancer growth regulation</li>
<li>Effective across triple-negative breast cancer, ovarian cancer, lung adenocarcinoma, and acute myeloid leukemia</li>
<li>Restored drug sensitivity in resistant lung cancer cells when combined with PARP inhibitors</li>
<li>Published in Science Advances on September 22, 2026</li>

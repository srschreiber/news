### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Ozempic-like drugs may reduce addiction cravings by targeting brain's 'craving center'
GLP-1 drugs like Ozempic and Wegovy, originally developed for diabetes and weight loss, appear to reduce cravings for alcohol and addictive substances by acting on the lateral septum, a brain region connecting memory and reward. Human studies confirm reduced alcohol consumption; animal studies show reduced use of cocaine, amphetamines, opiates, and nicotine.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>GLP-1 drugs reduce cravings for alcohol, cocaine, amphetamines, opiates, and nicotine</li>
<li>Mechanism operates through lateral septum, a brain region densely packed with GLP-1 receptors</li>
<li>Lateral septum acts as neural control point between thinking about rewards and pursuing them</li>
<li>Could open new therapeutic approaches to addiction treatment</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Bundibugyo virus outbreak surges as Ebola relative reemerges
Bundibugyo virus, an Ebola relative, has surged in the Democratic Republic of Congo and Uganda with 695 confirmed cases and 138 deaths as of June 11, 2026, surpassing two earlier outbreaks in scale. Limited diagnostic capacity and delays in specimen transport delay isolation and contact tracing in affected regions.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>695 confirmed cases and 138 deaths as of June 11, 2026</li>
<li>Surpassed Uganda 2007 and DRC 2012 outbreaks in scale and trajectory</li>
<li>Spreads through direct contact with infected bodily fluids</li>
<li>Overlapping symptoms with malaria and typhoid; requires lab confirmation</li>
<li>Scarce laboratory resources in affected DRC regions; samples require long distances to national reference labs</li>
<li>Delays in testing impede isolation and contact tracing</li>
</ul>

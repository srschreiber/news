### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Anthropic's Claude Allegedly Used for Weapons, Spying, and Cyber Operations
Anthropic released a threat intelligence report documenting disrupted misuse of its Claude models across seven harm categories by state-sponsored groups and criminals between December 2025 and August 2026. Cases included China-based actors using Claude to develop electronic-warfare software targeting 12 Taiwanese military sites, naval anti-torpedo systems, and a Yemeni cell building rockets with intended range exceeding 2,000 km.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>China-based actor built e-warfare software targeting 12 Taiwanese military sites using Claude</li>
<li>Another China-based actor used Claude for 200-page naval anti-torpedo system proposal</li>
<li>Yemen-based cell used Claude to develop guided rocket and ballistic missile exceeding 2,000 km range</li>
<li>Seven documented harm categories: weapons, espionage, cyber operations, influence, surveillance, scams, biological misuse</li>
<li>Claude Haiku, Sonnet, and Opus models affected; not Fable or Mythos-class except one distillation case</li>
<li>Misuse detected December 2025–August 2026</li>
</ul>

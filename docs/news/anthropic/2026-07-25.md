### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 model
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on Friday, priced at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — unchanged from its predecessor, Opus 4.8. The company says it delivers nearly all the intelligence of its top-of-the-line Claude Fable 5 at half the cost, and it is now the new default model on Claude Max, Anthropic's premium consumer tier, and the strongest model available on Claude Pro.

On benchmarks, Anthropic says Opus 5 is the new state-of-the-art on coding and knowledge work evaluations like Frontier-Bench and GDPval-AA, though it remains behind Mythos 5 on cybersecurity tasks. Anthropic also highlighted software development as a major gain, saying it more than doubled the performance of Opus 4.8 on Frontier-Bench while lowering the cost per task, and that on Zapier's AutomationBench it achieved a pass rate roughly 1.5 times higher than the next-best model at the same cost. Opus 5 is the fourth model in the Claude 5 family, joining Mythos 5 and Fable 5; Fable 5 is Anthropic's most capable publicly available model, meant for "days-long autonomous projects," while Mythos 5 is Anthropic's current cutting-edge model, but the AI lab has limited availability to a select group of companies and organizations due to its impressive hacking capabilities. Unlike Fable 5, Opus 5 doesn't retain user data for 30 days. The launch lands as Anthropic preps for an IPO later this year and competes with OpenAI and Google on cost efficiency, not just raw capability.

### Claude Opus 5 voice mode enhanced with model selection and app integrations
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Alongside the Opus 5 launch, Anthropic upgraded Claude's voice mode, adding the ability to choose which model powers voice conversations and expanding integrations with third-party apps. The update rounds out Friday's broader release, giving voice-mode users access to the same efficiency gains touted for Opus 5.

### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 AI model
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026, its fourth Claude model in under two months after Mythos 5, Fable 5 and Sonnet 5. Priced like predecessor Opus 4.8 at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens, it delivers near-Fable 5 performance at half the cost, scoring 43.3% on Frontier-Bench v0.1 versus Fable 5's 33.7% and landing within 0.5% of Fable 5 on CursorBench 3.2. It leads on knowledge-work benchmarks like ARC-AGI 3, Zapier AutomationBench and OSWorld 2.0, becomes the default model for Claude Max and top model for Claude Pro, and adds an effort dial plus mid-conversation tool switching and automatic API fallbacks. Anthropic calls it its most aligned model yet, though it still trails Mythos 5 in cybersecurity and biology research; the launch follows the president of the United States's administration pushing to delay some model releases, with Anthropic saying it continues government testing cooperation.

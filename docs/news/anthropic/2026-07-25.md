### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 at half the price of Fable 5
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026, its fourth model in under two months after Fable 5 (June) and Sonnet 5 (late June). Opus 5 costs $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens—the same as predecessor Opus 4.8 but half of Fable 5's $10/$50—while matching or beating Fable 5 on most coding and knowledge-work benchmarks, scoring 43.3% on Frontier-Bench v0.1 versus Fable 5's 33.7% and Opus 4.8's 18.7%. Anthropic says Fable 5 still leads on cybersecurity tasks and the longest autonomous agentic runs, and Opus 5 is now the default model on Claude Max and top offering on Claude Pro, available via the API, Amazon Bedrock, Google Cloud and Microsoft Foundry.

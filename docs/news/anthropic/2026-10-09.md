### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Claude AI assists in creating first complete UV sky map
Anthropic's Claude AI was used to produce the first complete ultraviolet map of the sky by Johns Hopkins astrophysicist Brice Ménard. Claude Science predicted approximately one-third of the final map, including much of the galactic plane, filling gaps left by NASA's GALEX mission.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First complete UV sky map created using Claude Science</li>
<li>~one-third of final map predicted by Claude, including galactic plane</li>
<li>Combines far-UV (154 nm) and near-UV (232 nm) observations</li>
<li>Previous GALEX mission (2003–2013) imaged ~two-thirds of sky in ~38,000 observations</li>
</ul>

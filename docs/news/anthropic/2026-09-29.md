### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5, claiming major performance gains and 40% cost reduction
Anthropic introduced Claude Opus 5.5, a new flagship model that matches Claude Fable 5.1 performance while costing 40% less to run. The model underwent external safety testing and scored highest on Anthropic's alignment audit. A companion model, Claude Sonnet 5.5, was released days later with 30% speed improvements and lower costs.

<li>Cache read costs dropped 60% to $0.20 per million tokens</li>

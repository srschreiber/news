### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Anthropic releases Claude Opus 5.5, claiming major performance gains and 40% cost reduction

The model underwent external safety testing and scored highest on Anthropic's alignment audit. A companion model, Claude Sonnet 5.5, was released days later with 30% speed improvements and lower costs.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Cache read costs dropped 60% to $0.20 per million tokens</li>
<li>Model was tested by external evaluators including Frontier Design and METR before release</li>
<li>Opus 5.5 achieved the strongest scores to date on Anthropic&#x27;s automated behavioral audit for alignment testing</li>
<li>Claude Sonnet 5.5 followed within a week, running 30% faster and costing up to 30% less than Sonnet 5</li>
</ul>

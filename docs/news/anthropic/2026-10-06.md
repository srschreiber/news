### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Anthropic Opens Most Powerful AI Models to Cybersecurity Teams
Anthropic expanded its Cyber Verification Program on October 6, 2026, restructuring it into three access tiers allowing broader cybersecurity organizations to apply for reduced safeguards on advanced AI models including Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Mythos 5.1. Defense Access (broadest tier) covers reverse-engineering malware and vulnerability analysis; Red Team Access covers authorized penetration testing; Specialized Access serves only vetted critical infrastructure organizations cleared jointly with the U.S. government.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Three tiers: Defense Access, Red Team Access, Specialized Access</li>
<li>Models: Claude Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, Mythos 5.1 with reduced safeguards</li>
<li>Defense Access: security teams, nonprofits, universities, government, critical infrastructure, researchers</li>
<li>Red Team Access: in-house, government, and penetration testing firms; ineligible for individuals</li>
<li>Specialized Access: vetted organizations testing power grids, flight systems, telecom, banking infrastructure</li>
</ul>

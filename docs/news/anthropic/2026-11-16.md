### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> TSMC announces up to $1 billion investment in Anthropic and OpenAI
Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company (TSMC) announced board approval on November 11, 2026 for up to $1 billion in equity investments split evenly between two AI developers. The company authorized up to $500 million to acquire common shares of Anthropic at its initial public offering (IPO) price, and up to $500 million to acquire preferred stock in OpenAI. This marks TSMC's first direct equity stakes in foundational AI model creators. The board also approved allocation of up to $500 million to establish a new investment company for strategic investments across the semiconductor ecosystem and capital appropriations of roughly $40.93 billion for upgrading advanced technology capacity and fab construction. The investments reflect TSMC's shift from a strict foundry-only model toward deeper alignment with major customers in the AI sector, as both OpenAI and Anthropic are significant purchasers of TSMC-manufactured high-performance computing hardware for data center projects. TSMC has reported six consecutive quarters of record profit, earning more than $23 billion in profit during the third quarter of 2026.

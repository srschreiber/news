## Anthropic

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> GLM-5.3 Released With Weak Safeguards Against Cyberattack Capabilities
Anthropic's analysis reveals that Zhipu AI's GLM-5.3 model has strong autonomous cyber-exploit capabilities but lax safeguards, with attackers able to bypass security measures 64–100% of the time. Unlike Claude models released with robust protections, GLM-5.3 is freely downloadable, significantly increasing malicious actors' access to advanced cyber capabilities.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>GLM-5.3 can develop end-to-end exploits autonomously, matching the leap between Claude Opus 4.6 and Claude Mythos Preview</li>
<li>Attackers bypass GLM-5.3 safeguards 64–100% of the time using simple techniques, compared to unsuccessful attacks against Claude models</li>
<li>NIST&#x27;s Center for AI Standards and Innovation assessed GLM-5.3 as &#x27;the most cyber-capable open-weight model released to date,&#x27; lagging US frontier by ~4 months</li>
<li>GLM-5.3 is publicly downloadable without restrictions, unlike US frontier models limited to vetted users</li>
<li>Anthropic had previously released Claude Mythos Preview in limited form through Project Glasswing, enabling defenders to find 10,000+ vulnerabilities before malicious access</li>
</ul>

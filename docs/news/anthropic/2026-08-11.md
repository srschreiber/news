### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> Anthropic adds invisible watermarks to Claude-generated text globally
Anthropic announced it will embed invisible, machine-readable watermarks in all Claude-generated text and attach signed provenance metadata to supported file types globally, complying with EU AI Act transparency requirements. The watermarks are designed to persist through copying and pasting, though heavy editing or format changes may strip them.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Applies globally to all Claude products (API, Claude, Claude Code, Claude Cowork, Claude Tag)</li>
<li>Text watermarks designed to persist when copied and pasted</li>
<li>Supported files (.svg, .png, .jpg) carry C2PA-standard signed provenance metadata</li>
<li>Policy applies to new models launched August 2, 2026 onward; retrofit underway for existing models</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> Unreleased Anthropic model advances solution to major unsolved math problem
An unreleased Anthropic Claude model made significant progress on the Riemann hypothesis, a 167-year-old unsolved math problem, by pushing the proven bound for zeta zeros from 41.6% to 67.2%.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Riemann hypothesis dates to 1859 and carries a $1 million unclaimed bounty</li>
<li>Model ran 60 subagents over 31 hours, testing 650 ideas, 31 million output tokens</li>
<li>Two subagents devised the key ideas; result validated by two Anthropic mathematicians</li>
<li>Proof formally verified using the Lean proof assistant</li>
<li>Anthropic says the technique won&#x27;t fully prove the hypothesis</li>
</ul>

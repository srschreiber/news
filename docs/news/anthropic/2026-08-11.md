### <span class="imp imp-3" title="Importance 3/5" aria-label="Importance 3 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i><i></i></span> Claude advances Riemann zeta function research to 67.2% zero point bound
An unreleased research version of Anthropic's Claude advanced the Riemann zeta function research by raising the lower bound of non-trivial zeros to 67.2%—a 25.6 percentage point gain in one day and a half—demonstrating AI's capacity to tackle genuinely open mathematical problems.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Bound increased to 67.2% from previous 41.6%: a 25.6 percentage point gain</li>
<li>Proposed 650 different approaches to the Riemann Hypothesis</li>
<li>Organized ~60 Claude sub-agents, executed 2,400 shell commands, wrote hundreds of Python scripts</li>
<li>Previous 37 years of human research achieved only 0.8 percentage point gain</li>
<li>Two Anthropic mathematicians and number theorists Brian Conrey and Dan Goldston verified results</li>
</ul>

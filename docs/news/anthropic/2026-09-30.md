### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Anthropic IPO reveals structural problem in AI industry economics
Anthropic's IPO filing reveals critical revenue concentration: 47% of 2025 revenue ($2.16B of $4.6B) flows through Amazon and Google, up from 11% in 2023. The company paid ~$351M in distribution fees to these partners in 2025. This dependence on competitors-turned-investors raises concerns about bargaining power and profitability despite $4.6B revenue and $8B+ operating losses.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>47% of 2025 revenue ($2.16B of $4.6B) routed through Amazon, Google</li>
<li>Distribution fees: $351M in 2025, ~16 cents per revenue dollar via cloud channels</li>
<li>Revenue concentration grew from 11% (2023) to 32% (2024) to 47% (2025)</li>
<li>Twelvefold revenue surge to $4.6B in 2025; operating losses exceeded $8B</li>
</ul>

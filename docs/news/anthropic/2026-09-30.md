### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Anthropic releases Claude Sonnet 5.5 with 30% faster performance and lower pricing
Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5.5, the second model in its Claude 5.5 family, positioning it as a faster and cheaper alternative to Opus 5.5. The mid-tier model runs 30% faster than its predecessor and costs up to 30% less, scoring notably higher on real-world benchmarks while remaining suitable for everyday tasks like document creation and bug fixing.

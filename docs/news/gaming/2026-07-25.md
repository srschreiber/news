## M&A

### Saudi Arabia's PIF takeover of EA Sports receives European Commission approval
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The European Commission has approved, under the EU Merger Regulation, the acquisition of sole control of Electronic Arts Inc. of the US by the Public Investment Fund ('PIF') of Saudi Arabia, in what is a $55 billion deal. Investors are set to acquire 100% of the company, with PIF rolling over its existing 9.9% stake in EA, and EA stockholders are to receive $210 per share in cash, representing a 25% premium to the unaffected share price. After the transaction is completed, EA will remain headquartered in Redwood City, California, US.

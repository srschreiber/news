### PlayStation Network experiences massive outage
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Sony's PlayStation Network suffered a widespread outage on July 24, 2026, affecting account management, game streaming, the PlayStation Store, and even PlayStation Direct, per Sony's status page saying "all services are experiencing issues." The disruption hit legacy platforms too, including PS Vita, PS4, and PS3, and delayed the EA Sports FC 26 championship at the Esports World Cup. Sony had not publicly acknowledged the outage on its PlayStation Support account as of the report; the timing is notable given Sony's plan to end physical disc production by 2028 in favor of all-digital games.

### Saudi Arabia approves takeover of EA Sports
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ On July 23, 2026, the European Commission cleared under EU merger rules the $55 billion acquisition of Electronic Arts by a consortium led by Saudi Arabia's Public Investment Fund, alongside Silver Lake and Jared Kushner's Affinity Partners, finding no EU competition concerns. First announced in September 2025, the all-cash deal would take EA private at $210 per share, marking the largest leveraged buyout in history, with PIF set to hold roughly 93.4% of the company; the transaction still faces EU Foreign Subsidies Regulation review and is expected to close in Q1 2027.

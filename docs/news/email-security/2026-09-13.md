### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Microsoft cloud accounts hijacked through passkey phishing and email fraud
Microsoft disclosed that threat actors are using passkey-themed social engineering and third-party email infrastructure to breach cloud accounts and steal data.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Active since May 2026</li>
<li>Attacks begin with phone/message impersonation of IT helpdesk</li>
<li>Identity compromise enables cloud reconnaissance and data exfiltration</li>
<li>SharePoint, OneDrive, email collection via REST APIs</li>
<li>Proxy-associated infrastructure used for automated collection</li>
</ul>

## Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Trusted Google Alerts hijacked in large-scale phishing campaign
Attackers are leveraging Google Alerts and other Google cloud services to deliver phishing emails that bypass traditional email security filters. The large-scale campaign exploits trusted Google infrastructure to impersonate legitimate notifications and direct victims to credential-stealing phishing pages.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Campaign abuses Google Alerts and Google cloud services</li>
<li>Phishing emails bypass traditional email security defenses</li>
<li>Targets Microsoft login credentials and sensitive information</li>
<li>Reflects shift to abusing legitimate platforms vs. malicious infrastructure</li>
</ul>

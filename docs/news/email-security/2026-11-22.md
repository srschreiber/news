### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Russian FSB-Linked Hackers Target European Journalists and Politicians in Phishing Campaign
Russian FSB-linked hackers conducted a phishing campaign beginning in mid-October 2026 targeting European journalists, politicians, and civil society figures using encrypted messaging app Signal. The campaign sent deceptive messages impersonating trusted contacts and directed recipients to fraudulent websites to steal credentials, affecting targets including Le Monde journalists and European lawmakers focused on reporting Russian affairs.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Campaign began mid-October 2026; continued into November</li>
<li>Used Signal to impersonate trusted contacts, directing to fake sites</li>
<li>Targeted French Le Monde journalists on Nov 17</li>
<li>Journalist Christo Grozev publicly disclosed attack Nov 18</li>
<li>German journalist Bastian Obermayer, French lawmaker Éric Bothorel received similar attempts</li>
<li>Google analyst identified group UNC5792 (Frontier Blizzard/Sentry Bear) as possible actor</li>
</ul>

## Email Security

### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> Attackers Abuse Microsoft's Legitimate Websites to Bypass Email Security Filters
Hackers are exploiting Microsoft's own trusted domains and infrastructure to send phishing emails that circumvent traditional email security filters. This technique allows attackers to hide behind legitimate Microsoft websites while launching targeted phishing campaigns against organizations.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Phishing emails are now originating from legitimate platforms and trusted service domains to evade detection</li>
<li>Attackers abuse Microsoft infrastructure to bypass email filters and appear trustworthy to recipients</li>
<li>1 in 10 phishing emails now comes from a platform users recognize and trust</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Attackers exploit passkey phishing to compromise Microsoft cloud accounts
Microsoft disclosed two campaigns exploiting its cloud infrastructure. The first involved over one million scam emails impersonating company executives to trick accounts payable staff into wire transfers. The second, active since May 2026, uses passkey-themed social engineering to compromise cloud accounts and exfiltrate data at scale.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>First campaign: August 3-5, 2026; over 1 million scam emails impersonating CEOs</li>
<li>Fraudulent invoices targeted ServiceNow subscriptions; used generative AI for tailored emails</li>
<li>Second campaign active since May 2026; uses passkey phishing via personal phone numbers posing as IT help desk</li>
<li>Compromised accounts: automated high-volume SharePoint, OneDrive downloads, mailbox collection via REST APIs</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Large-scale fake voicemail phishing targets 7,800+ organizations globally
A large-scale credential phishing campaign using fake voicemail transcript emails targeted over 7,800 organizations globally, according to Check Point research published September 14, 2026. The attack exploited the routine nature of voicemail communications to increase credential theft success.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Targeted organizations: over 7,800 globally</li>
<li>Attack method: fake voicemail transcript emails with familiar subject lines and system-generated formatting</li>
<li>Research published September 14, 2026</li>
</ul>

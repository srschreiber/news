# Sam's News — email-security — 2026-09-04

## Security

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> High-volume phishing campaign uses invisible Unicode to bypass email filters
Microsoft reported a large-scale phishing campaign exploiting invisible Unicode tag characters to evade email security filters and deliver financial fraud lures.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>High-volume phishing campaign started February 9, 2026</li>
<li>Uses invisible Unicode tag characters (U+E0000–U+E007F) to split keywords like &#x27;funding&#x27;</li>
<li>Technique adapted from AI prompt injection research</li>
<li>Microsoft Defender detections elevated for approximately three months</li>
<li>Most flagged messages caught by layered protections rather than single Unicode signals</li>
</ul>

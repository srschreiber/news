### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Star Blizzard refines phishing and malware delivery with RedFlick technique
Microsoft has documented Russian state-sponsored actor Star Blizzard evolving its attack capabilities since January 2026 through large-scale phishing campaigns and a new malware delivery technique called RedFlick. This technique uses scheduled tasks to deploy the CosmicPulse backdoor with a single user interaction, reducing friction compared to previous ClickFix-based chains and improving the actor's ability to compromise targets.

<li>Star Blizzard shifted toward large-scale phishing operations in 2026 alongside RedFlick adoption</li>
<li>Targets include Ukrainian individuals and institutions, international NGOs, Western think tanks, and governments with Ukraine-related nexus</li>

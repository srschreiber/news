### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> AI-generated phishing attacks outpace native email filters
AI-generated phishing emails are increasingly bypassing native email security filters, with attack volume rising over 4,000% since ChatGPT's launch. AI-assisted fraud generated 4.5 times more profit than traditional fraud in 2025, according to Interpol, with the gap expected to widen. Researchers found AI phishing achieved 54% click-through rates at roughly 4 cents per email, and people are 4.5 times more likely to click AI-generated messages than manually written ones.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Phishing volume rose 4,000% since ChatGPT became widely available</li>
<li>AI-assisted fraud generated 4.5x more profit than traditional fraud in 2025 (Interpol)</li>
<li>AI phishing achieved 54% click-through rate, matching professional human attackers</li>
<li>AI emails cost ~4 cents per message via API vs ~30 minutes human time</li>
<li>People 4.5x more likely to click AI-assisted phishing than manually written</li>
<li>Single deepfake fraud case at Arup: $25 million stolen across 15 transfers</li>
</ul>

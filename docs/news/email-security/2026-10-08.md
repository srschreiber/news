### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> AI-Assisted Phishing Campaign Delivers Real-Time Google Account Takeover Attacks
Cisco Talos identified an advanced phishing campaign targeting Taiwan organizations using AI-generated event lures and real-time AitM attacks on Google credentials. The campaign combined spear-phishing, QR code phishing, and a hybrid HTTP/WebSocket authentication interception framework.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Targeted Taiwan research organizations and institutions</li>
<li>AI-assisted content generation for event invitations</li>
<li>QR code phishing modifying legitimate posters</li>
<li>Real-time synchronization of authentication workflows for MFA interception</li>
</ul>

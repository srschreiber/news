### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Microsoft disrupts EvilTokens device-code phishing service responsible for 12,000 compromises
Microsoft disrupted EvilTokens, a phishing-as-a-service platform that compromised approximately 12,000 email accounts by abusing OAuth 2.0 device authorization flows. Two suspects aged 32 and 38 were arrested; the service featured an AI chatbot to analyze victims' inboxes and draft impersonation messages for business email compromise attacks.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>12,000 email accounts compromised via device-code phishing abuse</li>
<li>Two arrests by Metropolitan Police Service on September 11, 2026</li>
<li>Service operated as turnkey PhaaS on Telegram since mid-February 2026</li>
<li>AI chatbot analyzed inboxes to identify trusted relationships and payment authorizations</li>
<li>Stolen tokens enabled email exfiltration and malicious inbox rule persistence</li>
</ul>

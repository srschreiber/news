### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> AI-powered phishing attacks grow more convincing; defense strategies emerge
AI-powered phishing attacks have become significantly more sophisticated, using generative AI to craft perfect grammar and reference actual job titles and business news, bypassing traditional detection. Defenses include DMARC, domain monitoring, out-of-band verification, URL inspection, skepticism of urgency, and phishing-resistant MFA.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>AI-driven phishing now grammatically perfect, contextually relevant</li>
<li>Evades traditional typo/formatting-based detection</li>
<li>DMARC, domain monitoring, callback verification recommended</li>
<li>Phishing-resistant MFA beyond SMS/push essential</li>
</ul>

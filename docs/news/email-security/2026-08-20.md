### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 8/10" aria-label="Importance 8 of 10">8</span> Phishing 3.0: AI-Driven Agent-Versus-Agent Email Attacks Emerge
AI-driven 'Phishing 3.0' attacks now feature agentic AI systems that autonomously research targets, compose personalized messages, and adapt campaigns across email, collaboration tools, and live calls. Traditional email defenses from a decade ago are increasingly ineffective. Microsoft tracked phishing platforms generating tens of millions of messages monthly, while a documented case at engineering firm Arup involved a deepfake video call that convinced an employee to approve approximately $25 million in transfers.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Agentic AI performs reconnaissance in seconds (previously manual)</li>
<li>Microsoft tracked platforms generating tens of millions of messages monthly</li>
<li>48% of security professionals ranked agentic AI as top attack vector (2026 Dark Reading poll)</li>
<li>Documented case: deepfake CFO impersonation at Arup, ~$25 million transfer approved</li>
<li>Three phishing evolution stages: 1.0 (payloads), 2.0 (socially engineered), 3.0 (AI + deepfakes)</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> QR-Code Phishing (Quishing) Bypasses Corporate Email Defenses
QR-code-based phishing attacks (quishing) have become a popular alternative to traditional email phishing and can evade corporate security measures.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Malicious QR codes were embedded in 11% of phishing emails in H1 2026</li>
<li>Quishing bypasses email filters because URLs are encoded visually rather than as readable text</li>
<li>QR codes redirect victims from protected corporate environments to personal mobile devices with fewer security controls</li>
<li>Attackers often combine quishing with trusted brand impersonation and urgency tactics to increase success rates</li>
</ul>

### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> Identifying Modern Phishing Scams: Generative AI's Impact on Email Attacks
Criminals increasingly use generative AI to create linguistically sophisticated phishing emails nearly indistinguishable from legitimate messages. Modern attacks employ sophisticated technical tricks including exploitation of Microsoft's OAuth Device Authorization Grant (OAuth device code flow) to bypass two-factor authentication, targeting login credentials, session tokens, and personal information.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Generative AI creates professionally styled phishing emails with legitimate-looking design</li>
<li>Attacks exploit Microsoft OAuth device code flow to bypass 2FA</li>
<li>Bypass targets legitimate device sign-in procedure without browser requirement</li>
<li>Modern attacks significantly more sophisticated than earlier phishing with poor grammar/design</li>
</ul>

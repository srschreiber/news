## Security

### Attackers bypassed MFA in 100% of business email compromise cases analyzed
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ MSSP Alert reports attackers bypassed MFA in every business email compromise (BEC) case examined where MFA was in place, and are also stealing OAuth tokens, API keys, and machine identities to gain trusted access to cloud systems without triggering alarms. Common techniques include OAuth device-code phishing (abusing Microsoft 365's OAuth 2.0 Device Authorization Grant flow), MFA-fatigue attacks, adversary-in-the-middle proxies stealing session tokens/cookies, SIM swapping, and exploiting legacy protocols (IMAP, SMTP, POP) that don't support MFA. The findings underscore that MFA alone is no longer sufficient defense against identity-based attacks and AI-driven social engineering.

### Microsoft Detects 7.6 Billion Email Phishing Threats
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Microsoft's Q2 2026 threat intelligence report found Microsoft detected approximately 7.6 billion email-based phishing threats throughout the quarter, with monthly volumes declining modestly from 2.7 billion in April to 2.4 billion in June. Attackers are pivoting beyond inboxes: Microsoft Threat Intelligence observed continued growth in Teams-based social engineering, particularly voice phishing (vishing), with weekly malicious call attempts reaching nearly ten times the mid-2025 baseline by the end of the quarter. Credential phishing remained the dominant objective behind malicious payloads, and one automated BEC campaign that reached more than 67,000 users across 42,000 organizations in under three hours showed the scale attackers can now hit.

### Attackers Bypassed MFA in 100% of BEC Cases
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ New research summarized by MSSP Alert found that attackers bypassed MFA in every BEC case where it was in place, and are stealing OAuth tokens, API keys, and machine identities, giving them trusted access to cloud systems without setting off the usual alarms. Separately, LevelBlue data cited in the same roundup found phishing started 65% of intrusions in the second quarter, and business email compromise accounted for 45% of incidents. A related dataset from Eye Security on 2025 incidents found 63% of BEC incidents originated from phishing via links, and 79% involved MFA bypass, with 99%+ of incidents occurred in Microsoft 365 environments.

### Email Threat Landscape Shifts After Tycoon2FA Takedown
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Microsoft's Digital Crimes Unit disrupted the Tycoon2FA phishing-as-a-service platform in March 2026, and its effects compounded through Q2: after falling 15% in March and another 22% in April, Tycoon2FA-linked phishing volume dropped 74% in May to just 1.5 million messages, then fell another 20% in June to 1.2 million, by far the lowest monthly volumes observed in at least a year. That's a 92% total decline since the disruption operation began, compared to an average of 15.1 million messages/month in H2 2025. The operators haven't given up: after being forced off Cloudflare... the service continued to rely on infrastructure hosted on the .RU top-level domain (TLD)... More than 40% of newly observed Tycoon2FA domains used .RU registrations throughout Q2. No successor has filled the gap — no single service emerged to replace the platform at comparable scale.

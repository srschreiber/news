### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> New Ghost Phishing Wave Is Breaking Traditional Email Security
A phishing campaign called "EvilTokens" using "ghost phishing" techniques is targeting businesses across the US and Europe. The attack uses encrypted HTML (AES-GCM encryption) that remains hidden during initial inspection but decrypts and reveals malicious content only when opened in a browser, bypassing traditional URL-based email security checks. The campaign employs Microsoft Device Code Phishing to trick victims into completing a legitimate Microsoft login flow, granting attackers access to Microsoft 365 accounts without stealing passwords directly. According to ANY.RUN's Threat Intelligence data from 15,000 organizations, phishing exposure in 2026 reached 75.6% in consulting, 72.8% in financial services, 71.9% in manufacturing, 67.9% in technology, 66.7% in banking, and 66.1% among managed security service providers (MSSPs). The campaign targets technology, manufacturing, education, banking, consulting, financial services, and managed security providers. The visibility gap created by encrypted payloads can result in longer exposure to account takeover, delayed containment decisions, unauthorized access to corporate email and files, and increased investigation workload.

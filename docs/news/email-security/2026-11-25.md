### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> US Freezes Over $1 Billion in Crypto in Anti-Scam Campaign Against 800+ Defendants
The U.S. Department of Justice announced a government-wide anti-scam operation freezing over $1 billion in cryptocurrency targeting 800+ defendants, following an executive order from the president of the United States. The SEC brought enforcement actions against 39 entities for falsifying investment-adviser credentials; one notable case involved XETA, which promised up to 3,000% returns before diverting funds.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>$1 billion+ cryptocurrency frozen; 800+ defendants targeted</li>
<li>400+ cases since March 2026; 30+ defendants charged/unsealed in November</li>
<li>SEC enforcement against 39 entities for false Form ADV filings</li>
<li>XETA case: promised up to 3,000% returns, €100M guarantee before diversion</li>
<li>Frozen crypto cannot reach victims until court forfeiture orders secured</li>
</ul>

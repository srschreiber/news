### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 5/10" aria-label="Importance 5 of 10">5</span> Two former US Air Force members sentenced to 189 months for million-dollar BEC and phishing scheme
Chijioke Timothy Odimegwu and Harafat Mogaji, both former US Air Force members, have been sentenced to a combined 189 months in federal prison for running business email compromise and phishing campaigns that stole over $2.4 million from US businesses. The pair sent phishing emails to harvest credentials, then used spoofed addresses to redirect wire transfers to accounts they controlled.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Odimegwu sentenced to 111 months; Mogaji to 78 months in prison</li>
<li>Diverted over $1.68 million from Iowa City victim and $720,000 from Ohio victim</li>
<li>Harvested financial account numbers, PINs, and credit card data from victims including a nonprofit in Pella, Iowa</li>
<li>Both were active duty Air Force members during the nearly two-year campaign</li>
</ul>

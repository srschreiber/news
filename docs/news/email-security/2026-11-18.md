### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7/10" aria-label="Importance 7 of 10">7</span> AI-powered phishing kit automates malicious email campaigns
Security researchers have identified a sophisticated AI-powered phishing toolkit called TheMailer that automates malicious email campaigns. The tool leverages large language models to generate highly personalized email lures tailored to individual mail providers and rewrite content to evade spam filters and remove detectable AI writing patterns. TheMailer operates as a phishing-as-a-service platform where operators purchase lifetime licenses for $3,000 to $6,000 in cryptocurrency. The toolkit can scale operations to target over 145,000 recipients per campaign, with each recipient receiving a unique version of the email to significantly increase evasion difficulty. Unlike traditional bulk phishing approaches, this automation makes detection substantially more challenging. Security researchers warn that this level of automation is likely to become standard in future cybercriminal tools. Recommended defenses include strict email authentication protocols (SPF, DKIM, DMARC), employee training to recognize psychological triggers such as artificial urgency, and least-privilege access implementation to limit damage from successful breaches.

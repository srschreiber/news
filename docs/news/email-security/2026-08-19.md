## Email Security

### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> QR-Code Phishing (Quishing) Embedded in 11% of Phishing Emails in H1 2026
QR-code phishing, or "quishing," has emerged as a significant threat, with malicious QR codes appearing in 11% of all phishing emails in the first half of 2026. The technique bypasses traditional email filters by encoding malicious URLs invisibly and redirecting victims from corporate networks to unmanaged mobile devices with weaker security.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Malicious QR codes were embedded in 11% of phishing emails in H1 2026</li>
<li>Quishing bypasses email filters because URLs are encoded visually rather than as readable text</li>
<li>QR codes redirect victims from protected corporate environments to personal mobile devices with fewer security controls</li>
<li>Attackers often combine quishing with trusted brand impersonation and urgency tactics to increase success rates</li>
</ul>

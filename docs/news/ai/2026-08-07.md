### AMD acquires Taalas to embed AI models directly in silicon for faster inference
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> AMD announced on August 6, 2026 that it is acquiring Taalas, a Toronto startup that etches AI model weights directly into silicon, in a bid to leapfrog Nvidia in specialized inference hardware.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Taalas founded 2023, has raised $219 million in funding</li>
<li>HC1 test chip served Llama 3.1 8B at ~17,000 tokens/sec, 73x faster than Nvidia H200 at 1/10 the power</li>
<li>Built on TSMC 6nm process; second-gen HC2 targets 20B-parameter models</li>
<li>Trade-off: chips are model-specific, needing new silicon per model</li>
<li>Deal expected to close Q4 2026, pending regulatory approval</li>
<li>Follows Nvidia&#x27;s $20B Groq acquisition announced December 2025</li>
</ul>

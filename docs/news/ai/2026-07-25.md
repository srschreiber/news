### Anthropic Launches Claude Opus 5
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on Friday, July 24, priced at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — unchanged from predecessor Opus 4.8. The model, available immediately on all of Anthropic's platforms, is priced at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens, unchanged from its predecessor, Opus 4.8. Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, a model the company says delivers nearly all the intelligence of its top-of-the-line Claude Fable 5 at half the cost. It is now the default model on Claude Max and the strongest option on Claude Pro; Anthropic said Opus 5 is not the state-of-the-art for "risky, dual-use capabilities," like cybersecurity. The release also adds mid-conversation tool switching and automatic safety-classifier fallbacks on the API, and marks Anthropic's fourth model launch in under two months following Mythos 5, Fable 5, and Sonnet 5 in June.

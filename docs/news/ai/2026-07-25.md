### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 with efficiency and safety improvements
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5, which it said is "its best-performing and most cost-effective offering across a number of industry benchmarks." The model outperforms Anthropic's last release, Claude Fable 5, on coding and knowledge work evaluations, and is half the price at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens. Anthropic said Opus 5 is not state-of-the-art for "risky, dual-use capabilities," like cybersecurity, and it comes with a new effort-toggle letting users balance cost against capability, plus mid-conversation tool switching and automatic safety fallbacks on the API.

### German Consortium Releases Soofi S Open Model
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A German research consortium coordinated by the KI Bundesverband released Soofi S, a 30B (31.6B) open MoE model. 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ White House science chief Michael Kratsios accused Chinese startup Moonshot AI of covertly distilling Anthropic's Fable model to build its Kimi K3 system. On July 22, Michael Kratsios, the White House's director of science and technology policy, accused Moonshot AI of conducting large-scale, covert distillation of Anthropic's Fable model to build its Kimi K3 system, and Treasury Secretary Bessent warned sanctions are on the table. The claim has drawn pushback: the global AI community has pushed back against the Trump administration's claims that Moonshot AI's groundbreaking Kimi K3 distilled US models, citing a lack of justification and arguing that the practice does not constitute intellectual property theft.

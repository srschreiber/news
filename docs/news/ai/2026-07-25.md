### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 with efficiency and safety improvements
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24, 2026, priced at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens (unchanged from predecessor Opus 4.8), roughly half the cost of Anthropic's flagship Fable 5. It becomes the new default model on Claude Max and the strongest available on Claude Pro, and is accessible via API as claude-opus-5. Anthropic says Opus 5 beat Fable 5 on 8 of 13 benchmarks despite the lower price, with notable gains on Frontier-Bench and AutomationBench, and touts it as the company's most aligned model to date with no data-retention requirement. The launch reflects a shift in AI competition toward cost-efficient, near-frontier models for everyday enterprise coding and knowledge work rather than pure peak capability.

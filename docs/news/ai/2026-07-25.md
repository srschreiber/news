### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5 with efficiency and safety improvements
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 24, positioned just below flagship Fable 5 and now the default model in Claude's Max and Pro tiers. Pricing holds at $5 per million input tokens and $25 per million output tokens — half the effective cost of Fable 5 for near-frontier performance, and early partner Harvey reported Opus 5 matched maximum-reasoning outputs while generating 26% fewer tokens on average. Anthropic said Opus 5's safety classifiers are expected to intervene roughly 85% less often than those on Fable 5, and a new beta "Automatic Fallbacks" feature routes API requests to less powerful models when safety classifiers trigger, replacing error messages with functional responses. The model is also exempt from the extended 30-day data retention policy applied to Fable and Mythos.

### Codeberg members vote to prohibit LLM training on platform
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ The Codeberg community voted to reject the use of LLM training datasets sourced from their platform, a rare governance decision by an open-source code-hosting community to formally opt out of AI training. It signals growing pushback from developer communities against having their contributions scraped for AI model training without consent.

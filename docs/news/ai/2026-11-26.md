### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Google research shows LLMs can autonomously launch cyberattacks without human oversight
Google researchers documented that large language models can now fully automate cyberattacks—from vulnerability discovery to exploitation—without human intervention. Autonomous frameworks like ARTEX and HexStrike AI have moved from proof-of-concept to active deployment, with operators achieving Domain Admin access in under seven minutes in some cases.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>ARTEX, CAIRN, PENTAGI, HexStrike AI now actively deployed for autonomous attacks</li>
<li>PaperCut campaign: remote code execution in under 4 hours; Domain Admin in 7 minutes (12 of 395 targets)</li>
<li>LLM-powered attacks eliminated specialized expertise requirement, lowering barriers to entry</li>
<li>Progression: phishing → infrastructure setup → fully autonomous attack paths</li>
</ul>

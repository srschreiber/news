### <span class="imp imp-high" title="Importance 7.5/10" aria-label="Importance 7.5 of 10">7.5</span> Chinese homegrown LLM solves world-class mathematics problem
Chinese researchers using domestically developed large language model Intern-S2 proved the three-dimensional Ghys conjecture, a world-class mathematics problem unsolved for over 30 years. The human-machine collaboration involved the AI performing recursive reasoning, literature review, and logical verification while human researchers guided strategy and evaluation.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Ghys conjecture proposed 1995 by French mathematician Etienne Ghys, unsolved 30+ years</li>
<li>Intern-S2 developed by Shanghai Artificial Intelligence Laboratory</li>
<li>Proof completed through human-machine collaboration with formal verification by Peking University</li>
<li>First time Chinese researchers solved world-class mathematical conjecture using Chinese AI model</li>
<li>SHEAF framework used for formal verification by Peking University research teams</li>
</ul>

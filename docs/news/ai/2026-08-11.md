### <span class="imp imp-mid" title="Importance 6/10" aria-label="Importance 6 of 10">6</span> New technique reveals AI model internal representations and reasoning
Anthropic researchers developed a 'Jacobian lens' technique that reveals a hidden internal workspace in Claude models, dubbed 'J-space,' used for silent reasoning.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>J-space patterns emerged during training without explicit programming</li>
<li>Claude can report on and deliberately manipulate its own J-space patterns</li>
<li>Not involved in routine functions like fluent speech or fact recall</li>
<li>Inspired by global workspace theory from neuroscience</li>
<li>Neuroscientist Anil Seth cautions against equating this with consciousness</li>
</ul>

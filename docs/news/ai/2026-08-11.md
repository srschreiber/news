<li>This represents Meta&#x27;s first major open-weight model release in over a year</li>

Anthropic researchers developed a 'Jacobian lens' technique that reveals a hidden internal workspace in Claude models, dubbed 'J-space,' used for silent reasoning.

<ul class="takeaways">

<li>Inspired by global workspace theory from neuroscience</li>

</ul>

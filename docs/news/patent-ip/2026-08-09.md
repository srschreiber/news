### Federal Circuit Eliminates 'Presumption of Irreparable Harm' in Patent Cases
<span class="imp imp-4" title="Importance 4/5" aria-label="Importance 4 of 5"><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i class="on"></i><i></i></span> The Federal Circuit ruled that courts cannot presume irreparable harm when granting preliminary injunctions in patent cases, vacating an injunction won by Socket Solutions over its Neat Socket outlet cover patent.

<ul class="takeaways">
<li>Case: Socket Solutions, LLC v. Import Global, LLC, decided August 4, 2026</li>
<li>Opinion authored by Chief Judge Moore</li>
<li>Confirms the presumption was abolished after eBay v. MercantileExchange (2006)</li>
<li>Patent at issue: U.S. Patent No. 9,509,080 for an electrical wall outlet cover</li>
</ul>

## Biggest stories

### Iranian hackers suspected in Minnesota water-system cyberattacks
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Over 30 Minnesota community water systems were attacked July 27-28, with officials and researchers pointing to Iranian hackers given Tehran's history of targeting water infrastructure; attribution remained unconfirmed by state officials as of July 31. Related coverage noted growing use of LLM-based techniques for cost-effective code vulnerability discovery, and new "agentic zero trust" security frameworks (Cequence) aimed at securing business AI-agent deployments.
Sources: [2026-07-31](../../news/ai/2026-07-31.md)

### China's LLM dominance and open-weight model race
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ China now accounts for two-thirds of global LLM API calls, led by Xiaomi's MiMo-V2.5. Over the week, several vendors pushed new open or open-weight models: Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 (custom license), AMD's Instella-MoE-16B-A3B (fully open MoE, 2.8B active params), LG AI Research's 750B-parameter model, and SK Telecom's second-gen Dopamo with improved reasoning. Moonshot later shut down other products to focus on independent LLM/agent development.
Sources: [2026-07-28](../../news/ai/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-29](../../news/ai/2026-07-29.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/ai/2026-07-31.md), [2026-08-02](../../news/ai/2026-08-02.md)

### AI safety and licensing guardrails tighten
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A new African AI safety benchmark (4,216 tests, 8 languages) expands non-Western evaluation coverage. Separately, GCC banned LLM-derived code contributions to preserve GPLv3 copyright chain integrity, reflecting growing friction between generative coding tools and open-source licensing.
Sources: [2026-08-02](../../news/ai/2026-08-02.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/ai/2026-07-31.md)

### Research: LLM reasoning limits and applied model advances
🔥🔥◯◯◯ DeepMind found LLMs can solve century-old math conjectures yet fail creative thought-experiment reasoning. Separately, Raygun AI model reshaped protein size while preserving function, PolyAI launched a real-time human-like voice model for AI calls, and TurboVLA showed 7B-model-level robot performance without language models at 32Hz on consumer GPUs.
Sources: [2026-07-30](../../news/ai/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/ai/2026-07-31.md)

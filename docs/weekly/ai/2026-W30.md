# Sam's News — ai — Week 2026-W30

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic launched Claude Opus 5, matching or beating rival frontier models on key benchmarks at roughly half the cost, signaling an industry pivot toward cost-efficiency.

## Biggest stories
### Anthropic's Claude Opus 5 launch
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Anthropic released Claude Opus 5 on July 25, priced at $5/$25 per million input/output tokens — unchanged from Opus 4.8 and half rival Fable 5's $10/$50. It scored 43.3% on Frontier-Bench v0.1 (vs. Opus 4.8's 18.7%, Fable 5's 33.7%), more than doubling Opus 4.8's prior software-engineering score, and came within 0.5% of Fable 5 on CursorBench 3.2 at half the cost. It topped the Artificial Analysis Index at 61 points, scored 3x higher than the next-best rival on ARC-AGI 3, and beat Fable 5 on OSWorld 2.0 at one-third the cost. Features include a new "effort dial" (low/medium/high), a fast mode at ~2.5x speed for double price, a 1M-token context window, and a May 2026 knowledge cutoff. Trained without offensive cyber capabilities, cutting safety interventions ~85% versus Fable 5. Now the default model on Claude Max and the strongest available on Claude Pro.

Sources: [2026-07-25](../../news/ai/2026-07-25.md), [2026-07-26](../../news/ai/2026-07-26.md)

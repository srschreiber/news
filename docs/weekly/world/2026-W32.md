## Biggest stories

### Iran–US standoff over the Strait of Hormuz
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The president of the United States said he called off a massive planned strike on Iran (Aug 3) after Gulf states and Israel requested a pause, citing agreement on "perimeters of a deal." Over the following week, officials (Bessent, then the president) repeatedly signaled a Hormuz reopening deal was imminent, with Iran and Oman negotiating an interim shipping framework (60-day ceasefire, separate Iranian/Omani-controlled routes). Iran later claimed a deal but insisted on charging tolls, angering shipowners, then said reopening wouldn't be full and was independent of the Oman talks, setting steep demands. Tensions flared with a cargo ship struck in the strait, an Iranian drone attack on a US base in Kuwait, and a missile targeting a UAE vessel. Deal excludes Iran's nuclear program and reportedly would bar US/Israeli ships.

### Israel rejects Gaza peace plan
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Netanyahu formally rejected the 15-point Gaza peace plan from the president of the United States' Board of Peace, refusing to withdraw Israeli forces until Hamas is "genuinely disarmed" and ruling out a Palestinian state while he remains PM. The plan had envisioned an International Stabilization Force and new Palestinian police taking over security; Hamas says it still backs the roadmap. Rejection comes amid coalition pressure ahead of October 2026 elections, deepening friction with Washington. Separately, Israel expanded ground control in Lebanon (230 sq mi captured, new bases) and the June ceasefire with Hezbollah frayed, killing 2 Israeli soldiers.
Sources: [2026-08-09](../../news/world/2026-08-09.md), [2026-08-05](../../news/world/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/world/2026-08-06.md)

### Russia-Ukraine war intensifies
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Russia struck Ukraine's capital region (17 dead), Kherson (drone "human safari" killing a market vendor), and launched a massive ~200 missile/drone assault on Odesa (Aug 9) hitting port infrastructure and killing/injuring dozens, which Zelenskiy called an attack on global food security. Russia also destroyed 200+ Ukrainian gas stations and hit energy infrastructure ahead of winter. Ukraine retaliated with long-range drone strikes on Russian oil refineries and a Wildberries warehouse. Escalation included North Korean missile units deploying to Russia and the EU transferring €1.4bn in frozen Russian asset profits to Ukraine. A Russian general linked to the Bucha massacre was targeted in a Moscow bombing; a drone-company chief was also injured in a car bombing.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/world/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-04](../../news/world/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-05](../../news/world/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/world/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/world/2026-08-08.md), [2026-08-09](../../news/world/2026-08-09.md)

### New Muslim-world defense pact
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Saudi Arabia, Turkey and Pakistan signed the "Mecca Joint Defence Agreement" (Aug 7), a NATO-style mutual-defense pact after nearly a year of talks, combining Turkey's military/drone strength, Pakistan's nuclear status, and Saudi financial resources — a direct response to the region's instability from the Iran conflict, Houthi attacks and Hormuz disruptions.
Sources: [2026-08-07](../../news/world/2026-08-07.md), [2026-08-09](../../news/world/2026-08-09.md)

### Europe's drought and heat crisis
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A record European drought/heatwave persisted all week: the Rhine hit its lowest level since 1880, disrupting shipping and threatening Dutch dikes; Romania diverted Danube water to cool a nuclear reactor; wildfires hit France and Greece; Puerto Rico separately began water rationing amid its own drought.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/world/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-04](../../news/world/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/world/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/world/2026-08-08.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Hormuz negotiations as a rolling story**: nearly every daily briefing this week featured a new twist — cancellation, imminent deal, toll dispute, missile strike — without resolution, underscoring how fragile the Iran-US détente remains.
- **Middle East realignment**: the Saudi-Turkey-Pakistan pact and Houthi attacks on Saudi/Yemeni targets show regional powers hedging against continued instability from the Iran standoff and Gaza rejection.
- **Climate stress compounding logistics/energy**: European river droughts (Rhine, Danube) and Puerto Rico's water crisis repeatedly disrupted shipping, nuclear cooling, and trade throughout the week.
- **Escalatory pattern in Ukraine war**: both sides intensified long-range strikes on energy/economic infrastructure (refineries, gas stations, warehouses) rather than front-line movement, alongside external military support (North Korea to Russia, EU funds to Ukraine).
- **Natural disaster cluster**: Japan (earthquake + typhoon), China (typhoon, 1M+ evacuated), Guatemala (volcano) all faced major disruptions in the same window.

# Sam's News — world — Week 2026-W30

- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ US-Iran conflict oscillated between escalation and pause: Houthi strikes on Saudi refineries, a tanker mine blast in the Strait of Hormuz, and two brief US-ordered halts to nearly five months of strikes as diplomacy continued.
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Wildfires displaced over 300,000 people across France and Spain, among Europe's largest evacuation operations since WWII.

## Biggest stories

### US-Iran conflict: strikes, a Hormuz tanker blast, and pauses for diplomacy
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The five-month US-Iran conflict saw its most volatile week yet. Houthi rebels struck Saudi Aramco refineries in Jizan and Yanbu (July 25), widening the war to a new front and threatening the Bab el-Mandeb chokepoint alongside Hormuz. The president of the United States first ordered a halt to strikes on July 25, ending 13 consecutive days of US bombing, as Oman-mediated talks in Tehran progressed. On July 26 an oil tanker exploded in the Strait of Hormuz after reportedly hitting a naval mine — Iran blamed the ship for leaving its designated route rather than claiming a direct attack — and US-Iran strikes paused for a second straight day, the first such pause in nearly two weeks, amid reports of depleted Patriot interceptor stockpiles. The president said the US remains "locked and loaded" while pushing diplomacy privately; Secretary of State Marco Rubio signaled openness to talks if commitments hold.
Sources: [2026-07-25](../../news/world/2026-07-25.md), [2026-07-26](../../news/world/2026-07-26.md)

### Wildfires displace 300,000+ across France and Spain
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Wildfires around Bordeaux's Gironde/Landes region and across Spain forced one of Europe's largest displacement operations since World War II. France evacuated over 250,000 people and deployed 1,500 soldiers and 1,700 security personnel; Spain evacuated 70,000-90,000 and declared a national emergency, with nearly 10,000 hectares burned near Madrid and 13,000-15,000 in Avila. At least one death was reported in Spain.
Sources: [2026-07-25](../../news/world/2026-07-25.md), [2026-07-26](../../news/world/2026-07-26.md)

### India's Gen Z protest movement forces minister's resignation
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Student-led "Cockroach" protests over exam paper leaks pressured India's education minister into resigning, a major win for a youth movement whose face, Abhijeet Dipke, has emerged as a national figure confronting the government.
Sources: [2026-07-25](../../news/world/2026-07-25.md), [2026-07-26](../../news/world/2026-07-26.md)

### Russia-Ukraine war widens with long-range and maritime strikes
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Ukraine struck a Tyumen oil refinery nearly 2,000 km inside Russia and hit Iranian naval vessels in the Caspian Sea, prompting Tehran's condemnation. Russia responded with ballistic missile strikes on Kyiv residential buildings and attacks in Belgorod, including on a Wildberries warehouse. Romania shot down its third Russian drone in three days. Kazakhstan's president urged Putin to freeze the war amid reports Putin was "ambushed" after leaving a bunker; Ukraine warned of a "massive" Russian retaliatory attack after a ballistic strike killed 10 at a drone exhibition.
Sources: [2026-07-25](../../news/world/2026-07-25.md), [2026-07-26](../../news/world/2026-07-26.md)

### Berlin Pride attack sparks manhunt
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A vehicle attack at Berlin Pride left German authorities searching for a 21-year-old suspect with alleged Islamist ties.
Sources: [2026-07-26](../../news/world/2026-07-26.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Middle East on a knife's edge:** the same week held both the war's fiercest widening (Houthi refinery strikes, Hormuz tanker blast) and repeated diplomatic pauses — a pattern of escalate-then-de-escalate rather than resolution.
- **Climate disasters compounding:** wildfires in France/Spain and Typhoon Noul evacuations in southern China (340,000+) show simultaneous extreme-weather displacement across continents.
- **Youth protest movements gaining traction:** India's Gen Z-led campaign forced a cabinet resignation, echoing a broader global pattern of youth-driven accountability protests.
- **Russia-Ukraine war escalating geographically:** strikes moved further from the front line in both directions (Siberia, Caspian Sea, Kyiv, Belgorod), with neighboring Romania increasingly drawn in via drone incursions.

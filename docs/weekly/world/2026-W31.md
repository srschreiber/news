# Sam's News — world — Week 2026-W31

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 US-Iran conflict swung wildly all week: pause (Jul 25), resumed missile exchanges (Jul 28-30), tanker strikes and evacuation warnings (Aug 1), then another suspension pending a Hormuz-for-blockade deal Iran denies agreeing to (Aug 2).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Kumamoto, Japan earthquake (M7.1, July 28) killed at least 34-35 people, collapsed a mall and 12+ buildings, damaged Kumamoto Castle.
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Russia escalated Ukraine strikes: massive Moscow drone barrage, then a 284-drone/70-missile assault on Kyiv that sent a missile into Polish airspace (deepest incursion since 2022) and killed 8-9 civilians.
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Gaza ceasefire/disarmament talks advanced under a plan from the president of the United States, though sequencing (who disarms first) remained deadlocked and Israeli operations killed 18 since the plan began.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Record wildfires across France, Spain and Greece displaced 330,000+ people; scientists linked severity to climate change; France later regained control, letting 84,000 evacuees return.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Migrant crisis at Ceuta: ~60,000 crossed from Morocco, 41 deaths.

## Biggest stories

### US-Iran conflict lurches between de-escalation and strikes
🔥🔥🔥🔥🔥 Five months of conflict see repeated cycles: a three-day pause starting July 25 rallied oil markets (Brent -6.7%), but Iran fired ballistic missiles at US bases by July 28-29, prompting US-Saudi strikes on Iran-backed Iraqi militias (20+ killed). Iran then hit US targets in Kuwait/Bahrain (July 31) and struck a Qatari LNG tanker in the Strait of Hormuz (Aug 1), triggering US embassy evacuation alerts across the region. On Aug 2 the president of the United States again suspended planned strikes, citing a Saudi/Israel-brokered deal to reopen Hormuz in exchange for lifting the US naval blockade — which Iran denied agreeing to. China reportedly moved to supply Iran up to 400 shoulder-fired air-defense systems.

### Kumamoto earthquake death toll climbs through the week
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ A 7.1-magnitude quake struck Kumamoto, Japan on July 28 (shindo 7, Japan's highest scale), collapsing a mall with workers trapped, damaging 12+ buildings and historic Kumamoto Castle. Over 100 aftershocks followed; the death toll rose from initial injuries to 13 (Jul 29), 25 (Jul 30), and 34-35 (Jul 31), with survivors sheltering in cars amid extreme heat.
Sources: [2026-07-28](../../news/world/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-29](../../news/world/2026-07-29.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/world/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/world/2026-07-31.md)

### Russia-Ukraine war escalates, spills into Poland
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Ukraine launched a large drone barrage on Moscow (Jul 28) and later sank a Russian container ship in the Black Sea (Aug 1). Russia responded with a massive Jul 30 assault (284 drones, 70 missiles) on Ukraine killing 8, during which a suspected Kh-101 cruise missile crossed into Poland, leaving a 10-meter crater and prompting NATO F-16 scrambles — the deepest Polish airspace breach since 2022. Russia hit Kyiv again Aug 1, killing at least 9. Zelenskyy asked the president of the United States to help secure Starlink access from Elon Musk for strikes on Russia.
Sources: [2026-07-28](../../news/world/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/world/2026-07-30.md), [2026-08-01](../../news/world/2026-08-01.md)

### Gaza ceasefire and Hamas disarmament talks progress, then stall
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Cairo talks (Jul 30) showed rare progress on disarmament and Gaza governance. Hamas agreed to a broad disarmament plan from the president of the United States to enable Israeli withdrawal (Jul 31), but by Aug 2 negotiations deadlocked over whether Hamas or Israel should disarm first, while Israeli operations killed 8 more in Gaza (18 total since the plan began).
Sources: [2026-07-30](../../news/world/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/world/2026-07-31.md), [2026-08-01](../../news/world/2026-08-01.md), [2026-08-02](../../news/world/2026-08-02.md)

### European wildfires and drought worsen across the week
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Wildfires spread across France and Spain from Jul 27, forcing hundreds of thousands to evacuate near Bordeaux; by Jul 29 displacement reached 330,000. France gained control by Jul 30 (84,000 evacuees returned), but fires spread to Greece by Aug 2. Scientists attributed severity to climate change; Hungary's Paks nuclear plant shut down due to drought (Aug 2).

## Themes & trends
- **Middle East whiplash:** near-daily reversals between de-escalation announcements and new strikes/tanker attacks show a fragile, unresolved US-Iran standoff rather than a resolution.
- **Compounding disasters:** Japan's earthquake death toll and Europe's wildfire displacement both climbed steadily day-by-day across the week, each becoming a running story rather than a single event.
- **Maritime flashpoint:** Strait of Hormuz tanker strikes (Qatari LNG carrier, earlier Skylight attack) and Black Sea ship sinkings show naval/commercial shipping increasingly targeted in both the Iran and Ukraine conflicts.
- **NATO risk from Ukraine war spillover:** Poland's airspace incursion marks the most direct link yet between the Russia-Ukraine war and NATO territory this period.
- **Migration pressure:** large-scale, deadly border crossings (Ceuta, ~60,000 people, 41 deaths) recurred alongside the other crises.

# Sam's News — email-security — Week 2026-W31

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Hotel Wi-Fi networks emerged as a repeated attack vector, hijacked to deliver CornFlake RAT malware and to phish Microsoft credentials.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Law firm targeted by previously undocumented HollowFrame loader and Matryoshka backdoor via spear-phishing.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ AI-themed phishing grew: ChatGPT ranked top impersonated brand; Doppel launched AI-powered email security/human-risk platform; AegisAI raised $36M to fight AI spear phishing.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Healthcare phishing hit Mon General/Mon Health Medical Center (West Virginia), risking patient data exposure.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ IRS warned of phishing campaign targeting cryptocurrency holders.

## Biggest stories

### Hotel Wi-Fi becomes a phishing/malware vector
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Over Aug 1–2, researchers and reports detailed two related campaigns abusing hotel Wi-Fi: one hijacking networks to push fake browser updates carrying the CornFlake RAT (webcam/mic/keystroke surveillance, linked to Microsoft's "CaptiveCrunch"/Midnight Blizzard reporting), and a second phishing travelers for Microsoft login credentials via compromised hotel networks.
Sources: [2026-08-01](../../news/email-security/2026-08-01.md), [2026-08-02](../../news/email-security/2026-08-02.md)

### Law firm hit by novel HollowFrame/Matryoshka malware
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A spear-phishing campaign against a law firm deployed previously undocumented HollowFrame loader and Matryoshka backdoor, per new research disclosed Aug 1.
Sources: [2026-08-01](../../news/email-security/2026-08-01.md)

### AI theme in phishing: attacker and defender sides both scale up
🔥🔥◯◯◯ ChatGPT became one of the top brands impersonated in phishing (Jul 27). Vendors responded: Doppel launched an AI-driven email security/human-risk platform (Jul 31), and AegisAI raised $36M to build defenses against AI-powered spear phishing (Jul 27).
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/email-security/2026-07-27.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/email-security/2026-07-31.md)

### Healthcare and financial-sector phishing incidents
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Mon General and Mon Health Medical Center in West Virginia disclosed phishing attacks potentially exposing patient data (Aug 1). Separately, the IRS warned of an active phishing campaign targeting cryptocurrency holders (Aug 1).
Sources: [2026-08-01](../../news/email-security/2026-08-01.md)

## Themes & trends
- AI is now a two-sided story: attackers impersonate AI brands and use AI to craft spear-phishing, while vendors (Doppel, AegisAI) race to fund and ship AI-based countermeasures.
- Travel/hospitality Wi-Fi infrastructure repeatedly exploited as a phishing and malware-delivery channel, affecting both credential theft and RAT deployment.
- Targeted, sector-specific phishing continued against healthcare (West Virginia hospitals) and high-value individuals (crypto holders via IRS-themed lures) alongside more sophisticated, bespoke malware against professional services (law firm).
- Several days (Jul 28–30) had no notable email-security news, indicating activity clustered heavily around end of period (Jul 31–Aug 2).

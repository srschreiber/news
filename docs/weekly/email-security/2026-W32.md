# Sam's News — email-security — Week 2026-W32

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Wall Street hedge funds and financial firms hit by coordinated vishing extortion campaigns targeting payroll data (Point72 among victims).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ ChainDrop self-propagating npm worm compromised 400+ packages (2,200+ malicious versions), stealing npm/GitHub/AWS/Kubernetes/Vault credentials.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Greatness phishing-as-a-service expanded, spoofing RingCentral and bypassing MFA to hijack Microsoft 365 accounts.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Device code phishing/vishing attacks surged 1,500% in 2026, bypassing entrenched security controls.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ AI-powered business email compromise scams now stealing billions globally.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Microsoft 365 adversary-in-the-middle phishing campaigns targeted payroll and finance departments.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ OpenAI disrupted ChatGPT-driven scam operations in Cambodia and other parts of Asia.

## Biggest stories

### Vishing and social-engineering campaigns hit finance sector
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Coordinated voice-phishing and AI voice-cloning campaigns targeted hedge funds (including Point72) and other Wall Street firms across the week, escalating from AI voice-phishing reports (Aug 6) into a broader extortion-focused vishing wave stealing payroll data (Aug 7). Parallel Microsoft 365 adversary-in-the-middle phishing campaigns also harvested finance and payroll department emails, showing attackers converging on financial workflows via multiple vectors.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/email-security/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-07](../../news/email-security/2026-08-07.md)

### ChainDrop npm supply-chain worm
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ A self-propagating worm infected over 400 npm packages (2,200+ malicious versions), starting from 11 malware carriers in the keyv/cacheable namespaces via a compromised GitHub maintainer account. Affected packages have combined weekly downloads exceeding 500 million; the worm steals npm, GitHub, AWS, Kubernetes and HashiCorp Vault credentials and auto-republishes infected versions using stolen tokens.
Sources: [2026-08-05](../../news/email-security/2026-08-05.md)

### Greatness phishing-as-a-service escalates against Microsoft 365
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Over three days, the Greatness PhaaS platform expanded operations against Microsoft 365 users, first spoofing RingCentral emails (Aug 5) then demonstrated bypassing both email security controls and MFA to hijack accounts (Aug 6), building on the broader device-code phishing surge (1,500% increase) reported Aug 4.
Sources: [2026-08-04](../../news/email-security/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-05](../../news/email-security/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/email-security/2026-08-06.md)

### AI-driven scam campaigns proliferate
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ AI-powered business email compromise scams were reported stealing billions globally. OpenAI separately disrupted ChatGPT-hijacking scam operations run out of Cambodia and other Asian scam networks, indicating growing use of generative AI tools in fraud pipelines.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/email-security/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/email-security/2026-08-08.md), [2026-08-09](../../news/email-security/2026-08-09.md)

### Municipal and crypto-targeted phishing losses
🔥🔥◯◯◯ The City of Pittsburg lost over $900,000 to a phishing attack on municipal systems, while a separate campaign impersonated COLDCARD wallet security audits to trick crypto users into installing remote-access malware.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/email-security/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/email-security/2026-08-08.md)

## Themes & trends
- **MFA bypass is mainstream:** Device code phishing (up 1,500%) and AitM techniques now routinely defeat MFA on Microsoft 365, a recurring theme across Aug 4–7 reports.
- **Finance sector as primary target:** Hedge funds, payroll departments, and municipal governments were repeatedly targeted for extortion and fund theft (Point72, City of Pittsburg).
- **AI weaponization:** AI voice cloning (vishing) and generative AI (ChatGPT abuse, AI-driven BEC) featured in multiple distinct campaigns, reflecting broader adoption of AI tooling by threat actors.
- **Supply chain risk:** The ChainDrop npm worm highlights growing exposure from compromised open-source maintainer accounts propagating at scale via automated credential theft.

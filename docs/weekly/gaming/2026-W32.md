# Sam's News — gaming — Week 2026-W32

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Saudi-led PIF consortium completed its $55B EA take-private deal, closing officially Aug 5–6.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Roblox stock collapsed roughly 70% amid falling player numbers, with AI proposed as a fix.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ GTA VI momentum built all week: "unprecedented" pre-orders, 2026 launch reconfirmed (no delay to 2027), and a Netflix-exclusive extended look set for Aug 27.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Next Xbox ("Project Helix") leaked to support all prior Xbox generations, with possible disc-digitization tech.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Industry-wide physical-media retreat: Take-Two's CEO calls discs obsolete, Nintendo notes physical still 38.5% of sales, GTA 6 publisher downplays disc concerns.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Consolidation continues: Devolver Digital announced plans to go private, citing public-market pressure.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Gears of War: E-Day beta launched Aug 6 on Game Pass/Steam and broke the franchise's all-time concurrent-player record.

## Biggest stories

### EA goes private in $55B Saudi-led buyout
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ A Saudi Public Investment Fund-led consortium closed its $55 billion acquisition of Electronic Arts, taking the publisher private and ending its run as a public company — one of gaming's largest-ever deals.
Sources: [2026-08-05](../../news/gaming/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/gaming/2026-08-06.md)

### Roblox's stock crisis
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Roblox lost tens of billions in market value through the week, culminating in a roughly 70% share collapse tied to persistent player-count declines; the company is pitching AI initiatives as a turnaround plan.
Sources: [2026-08-08](../../news/gaming/2026-08-08.md), [2026-08-09](../../news/gaming/2026-08-09.md)

### GTA VI: pre-orders, timeline, and marketing push
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Take-Two's CEO reported "unprecedented and astonishing" GTA VI pre-orders in its Q2 results, alongside comments that physical discs "don't make sense for the consumer." Rockstar/Take-Two confirmed the game remains on track for a 2026 launch (no slip to 2027) and will debut an extended trailer exclusively on Netflix Aug 27 ahead of a wider YouTube release.
Sources: [2026-08-07](../../news/gaming/2026-08-07.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/gaming/2026-08-08.md), [2026-08-09](../../news/gaming/2026-08-09.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/gaming/2026-08-06.md)

### Next-gen Xbox hardware plans leak
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A leaked internal memo revealed the next Xbox (codename Project Helix) will support games from every prior Xbox generation, the most extensive backward-compatibility library of any console. Microsoft is also reportedly developing tech to let players digitize their physical Xbox discs.
Sources: [2026-08-04](../../news/gaming/2026-08-04.md)

### Publishers retreat from physical media and public markets
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Multiple executives signaled a shift away from discs and public ownership this week: Take-Two's CEO called physical discs outdated even as Nintendo data shows physical still makes up 38.5% of software sales; Devolver Digital said it will go private to escape public-market growth pressure, following EA's own going-private move.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/gaming/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-07](../../news/gaming/2026-08-07.md)

### Final Fantasy VII Revelation clarifies the Remake trilogy's ending
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Across several days, the director confirmed the base game of Final Fantasy VII Revelation will definitively conclude the Remake storyline, with any future DLC treated as spinoffs rather than continuations.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/gaming/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-04](../../news/gaming/2026-08-04.md)

### Gears of War: E-Day beta breaks records
🔥🔥◯◯◯ The E-Day beta launched Aug 6 on Xbox Game Pass and Steam with DLSS support, and quickly surpassed the Gears of War franchise's all-time concurrent Steam player record.
Sources: [2026-08-04](../../news/gaming/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-05](../../news/gaming/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-07](../../news/gaming/2026-08-07.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Console/publisher consolidation accelerates:** EA's $55B Saudi-backed privatization and Devolver Digital's move to go private mark a broader retreat from public markets in gaming.
- **Physical media debate intensifies:** Take-Two and GTA 6's publisher openly question disc relevance while Nintendo data shows physical sales remain substantial (38.5%), highlighting industry disagreement on format strategy.
- **Backward compatibility as a selling point:** Both the next Xbox (Project Helix) and a rumored new Sony portable console are being built around extensive backward-compatibility libraries.
- **Wishlist/beta hype as pre-launch signal:** Resident Evil Veronica's 2M Steam wishlists and Gears of War E-Day's record beta player counts both surfaced repeatedly, underscoring publishers' reliance on pre-release engagement metrics.
- **Platform instability:** Roblox's dramatic value loss and Netflix's cancellation of its Squid Game sequel reflect broader volatility across gaming-adjacent entertainment platforms this period.

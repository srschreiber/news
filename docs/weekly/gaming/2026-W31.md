# Sam's News — gaming — Week 2026-W31

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Sony committed to ending PlayStation physical disc production despite fan backlash and a boycott movement; reaffirmed the decision on multiple earnings calls through early August.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Xbox suffered a major multi-day network outage (July 27–30) locking users out of digital and, erroneously, disc games; Microsoft also disclosed its worst quarter in years despite adding 200M players.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ ChatGPT and Roblox designated EU "very large online platforms," triggering stricter Digital Services Act oversight.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Electronic Arts' $55 billion Saudi-backed go-private deal set to close by August 4.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Console pricing shifted upward: PlayStation 6 reportedly $999, Microsoft raised prices across all Xbox Series models.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Capcom hit 93% digital sales; Resident Evil Requiem became fastest-selling survival horror game (8M copies).
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ New York AG sued prediction-market platform Kalshi for alleged illegal gambling, seeking $36B disgorgement.

## Biggest stories

### Sony ends PlayStation physical discs
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Sony announced plans (July 27) to discontinue PlayStation physical disc production, sparking a planned week-long fan boycott. Despite backlash, Sony reaffirmed the strategy on its earnings call (July 31) and again in follow-up coverage (Aug 1), calling it a "cautious" but firm move toward digital-only distribution.
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/gaming/2026-07-27.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/gaming/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/gaming/2026-07-31.md), [2026-08-01](../../news/gaming/2026-08-01.md)

### Xbox outage and Microsoft's rough quarter
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A major Xbox network outage on July 27 blocked sign-ins and game launches; it recurred and was confirmed July 30 to have also wrongly blocked disc-based games, with Microsoft promising a fix. Separately, Xbox's CEO acknowledged the division's worst financial quarter in years (revenue down $1.7B year-over-year) despite reaching 200 million players, with new leadership projecting a return to growth by mid-2027. Microsoft also raised prices on all Xbox Series consoles.
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/gaming/2026-07-27.md), [2026-07-28](../../news/gaming/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/gaming/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/gaming/2026-07-31.md), [2026-08-02](../../news/gaming/2026-08-02.md)

### EU tightens scrutiny on ChatGPT and Roblox
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ The EU moved to designate ChatGPT and Roblox as "very large online platforms," subjecting both to stricter Digital Services Act obligations around content moderation and platform risk.
Sources: [2026-07-30](../../news/gaming/2026-07-30.md)

### EA's $55 billion go-private deal
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Saudi Arabia's $55 billion take-private acquisition of Electronic Arts was set to close by August 4, one of the largest gaming industry M&A deals to date.
Sources: [2026-07-31](../../news/gaming/2026-07-31.md)

### Console pricing pressure
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Reports emerged that PlayStation 6 will be priced at $999, raising affordability concerns, while Microsoft simultaneously increased prices across all Xbox Series models — signaling industry-wide hardware cost pressure heading into the next console cycle.
Sources: [2026-08-02](../../news/gaming/2026-08-02.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Physical media in retreat:** Sony's disc discontinuation was the period's dominant storyline, running for a week with recurring backlash coverage; paired with Ubisoft shutting down an NFT game, it underscores continued digital consolidation.
- **Console maker turbulence:** Xbox faced outages, its worst quarter in years, and price hikes, while PlayStation dealt with disc backlash and reported a $999 PS6 price point — both platform holders under strain simultaneously.
- **Layoffs and studio shakeups:** Double Fine cut about a third of its staff after splitting from Xbox Game Pass; Tomb Raider was delayed to 2028.
- **Regulatory scrutiny widening:** EU designating ChatGPT/Roblox as VLOPs and New York's lawsuit against Kalshi both reflect growing government attention on gaming-adjacent digital platforms and gambling-like mechanics.
- **Strong digital/sales performance persists despite industry stress:** Capcom's 93% digital sales, Resident Evil Requiem's record 8M sales, and Pragmata's 2.5M units show consumer spending remains healthy even as platform holders report rocky quarters.

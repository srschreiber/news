## Themes & trends
- **Physical media tension recurs**: Tomb Raider's delays, SEGA's clarification, and Rockstar's Dan Houser comments all surfaced within a day of each other, reflecting ongoing industry debate over disc support versus digital-only releases.
- **Consolidation continues**: EA's $55 billion Saudi PIF acquisition clearing EU review is the period's biggest financial story, alongside Ubisoft's bookings guidance — signaling active M&A and revenue-forecasting activity across major publishers.
- **Modding community active**: Multiple mods reported (GTA multiverse travel, Baldur's Gate 3 third-person camera) alongside GTA 6 developers blocking AI-generated videos, showing friction between fan-made content, AI tools, and rights enforcement.
- **Sequel/expansion pipeline building**: God of War, Final Fantasy XIV, and Call of Duty all announced concrete next-installment details, indicating publishers are locking in 2027 roadmaps now.

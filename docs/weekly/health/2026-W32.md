# Sam's News — health — Week 2026-W32

## Biggest stories

### AstraZeneca–Bristol Myers Squibb merger talks
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Reports emerged of early-stage merger discussions between AstraZeneca (~$264B market cap) and Bristol Myers Squibb (~$133B), which would create the world's fourth-largest drugmaker at roughly $400 billion combined. AstraZeneca shares fell up to 7.8% and Bristol Myers rose up to 3.8% on the news. Oncology portfolio overlap could draw antitrust scrutiny; neither company has confirmed the talks, first reported by the Financial Times.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/health/2026-08-03.md)

### FDA approves first mRNA flu vaccine
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The FDA approved Moderna's mFlusiva on August 5, the first mRNA-based flu vaccine, for adults 50 and older ahead of the 2026-2027 season — full approval for 50-64, accelerated for 65+. A Phase 3 trial of 40,805 adults across 11 countries showed 26.6-27% greater efficacy than standard-dose vaccines, with more frequent mild-to-moderate side effects. The approval reverses a February 2026 refusal-to-file letter issued after former official Vinay Prasad overturned staff's recommendation; Prasad left the agency in late April, and the FDA reversed course within a week of Moderna's objection. The approval stands despite HHS Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr.'s opposition to mRNA vaccines and his 2025 cancellation of ~$500 million in mRNA vaccine funding.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/health/2026-08-06.md)

### Ebola outbreak in eastern Congo
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ An Ebola outbreak in eastern DR Congo killed more than 1,700 people, becoming the fastest-growing outbreak on record. Moderna responded by launching an early-stage clinical trial of its mRNA-based Ebola vaccine candidate.
Sources: [2026-08-05](../../news/health/2026-08-05.md)

### FDA approvals for melanoma and narcolepsy drugs
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ The FDA approved Replimune's melanoma treatment RP1 after two prior rejection applications, a major biotech win alongside a Phase 3 confirmatory trial requirement. Separately, the FDA approved Takeda's novel narcolepsy drug (Orzeyful), backing a new drug class for sleep disorders.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/health/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-07](../../news/health/2026-08-07.md)

### Alzheimer's and neurodegeneration research advances
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Across the week, several studies advanced Alzheimer's and neurodegeneration understanding: aging protein EPS8 identified as a trigger for toxic protein clumping linked to ALS/Huntington's; Vanderbilt researchers created compounds to selectively control TAOK proteins as Alzheimer's research tools; and a study found the brain's memory center replaces long-standing immune cells with inflammatory ones starting around age 50, potentially explaining Alzheimer's onset. A separate long-term study linked nearly half of dementia cases to modifiable risk factors (smoking, high blood pressure, heart disease, high blood lipids).
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/health/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/health/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-07](../../news/health/2026-08-07.md)

### Autism treatment breakthrough in mice
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Blocking the glycine transporter SLC6A20 restored brain signaling and improved social, communication, and repetitive behaviors in autism-related mouse models and human organoids, suggesting a new therapeutic target.
Sources: [2026-08-07](../../news/health/2026-08-07.md)

### Health leadership and oversight
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Erica Schwartz was confirmed as CDC director, filling a nearly year-long vacancy dating to when the president of the United States took office. Separately, a Senate committee voted along party lines to hold former NIAID director Anthony Fauci in contempt of Congress over his refusal to answer questions about his COVID-19 role. BioNTech also named Guido Oelkers CEO, replacing cofounder Ugur Sahin.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/health/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-03](../../news/health/2026-08-03.md)

## Themes & trends
- **mRNA platform momentum despite political headwinds:** Moderna secured a landmark flu vaccine approval and launched an Ebola vaccine trial in the same week HHS leadership remains openly skeptical of mRNA technology, highlighting regulatory tension.
- **Big pharma consolidation:** The AstraZeneca–Bristol Myers Squibb talks reflect continued industry-wide pursuit of scale amid patent-cliff pressure.
- **Brain aging and neurodegeneration research cluster:** Multiple independent studies this week (EPS8 protein, TAOK compounds, age-50 immune cell shift, dementia risk factors) converged on mechanisms behind Alzheimer's, ALS, and Huntington's — signaling active research momentum but no near-term treatments.
- **Regulatory reversals:** Both the Moderna flu vaccine and Replimune's melanoma drug were approved after earlier FDA rejections, suggesting a pattern of sponsors successfully appealing initial regulatory setbacks this cycle.
- **Long tail of lifestyle/brain science stories:** Numerous lower-importance ScienceDaily items (diet, meditation, stress, cannabis, alcohol, posture) filled out daily briefings without major standalone significance.

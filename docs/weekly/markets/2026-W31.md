# Sam's News — markets — Week 2026-W31

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ South Korea's KOSPI collapsed over $2 trillion into bear-market territory (worse than 1997/2008) on a chip stock rout, then rebounded 18% by week's end as buyers returned.
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Middle East tensions whipsawed markets: a US-Iran pause and Iran/Israel de-escalation sparked a rally (July 27), before Iran resumed missile attacks and US-Saudi strikes on Iraqi militias reignited volatility and oil spikes (July 29).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Chip/AI-spending anxiety rattled global markets — US and Asian semiconductor stocks plunged, Nasdaq entered correction territory, and the Fed held rates steady as the Dow fell 1,000+ points (July 28–30).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ China's CXMT chipmaker soared 466% on its Shanghai debut, raising $8.6bn — the largest mainland chip IPO ever, briefly China's most valuable listed company.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Big Tech earnings drove huge swings: Amazon's AWS-fueled beat and Microsoft's record $450bn single-day market-cap gain lifted indexes, while Meta fell 9% on weak guidance.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ IPO wave continued: Jersey Mike's (10–15x oversubscribed), Reformation, and the new Texas Stock Exchange all launched/priced during the week.
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Apple crossed a $5 trillion market cap, only the second company to do so.

## Biggest stories

### South Korea's chip-stock rout and rebound
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ South Korea's KOSPI fell into bear-market territory as semiconductor stocks collapsed, wiping out over $2 trillion in value by July 29 — a decline worse than the 1997 and 2008 crises — prompting emergency government response and new leveraged-ETF restrictions. By July 31, the index had surged nearly 18% as investors rushed back into chipmakers, one of the sharpest reversals of the period.
Sources: [2026-07-28](../../news/markets/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-29](../../news/markets/2026-07-29.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/markets/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/markets/2026-07-31.md)

### US-Iran conflict swings from pause to escalation
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The president of the United States paused US military action against Iran for three days starting July 25, easing tensions and sending Brent crude down 6.7% to $90/barrel amid a stock rally; fewer than 10 ships crossed the Strait of Hormuz versus a normal ~100. The calm broke July 29 when Iran fired ballistic missiles at US installations (all intercepted), triggering joint US-Saudi airstrikes that killed at least 20 Iran-backed militia fighters in Iraq, driving oil prices back up and pressuring US stocks alongside a pending Fed decision.
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/markets/2026-07-27.md), [2026-07-29](../../news/markets/2026-07-29.md)

### Semiconductor sell-off and AI-spending jitters spread globally
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Nvidia fell 4% on July 27 over AI balance-sheet concerns; the sell-off broadened July 28 as chip stocks slumped across US and Asian markets on fears about AI-spending efficiency and Chinese competition. By July 30 the Nasdaq entered correction territory and the Dow dropped 1,000+ points even as the Federal Reserve held rates steady; Wall Street partly recovered as Microsoft and AI names rallied, and Micron shares jumped in the rebound.
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/markets/2026-07-27.md), [2026-07-28](../../news/markets/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/markets/2026-07-30.md)

### CXMT's record Shanghai IPO debut
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Chinese DRAM maker CXMT surged 466% on its Shanghai debut (July 27), rising from an 8.66 yuan IPO price to close near 49 yuan, raising 57.92bn yuan ($8.6bn) — the largest mainland chip IPO on record — and briefly making it China's most valuable listed company (~$480bn), topping ICBC. Retail orders were 212x oversubscribed; institutional demand exceeded 500x. Q1 revenue had risen over 700% YoY on AI server demand.
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/markets/2026-07-27.md)

### Big Tech earnings reshuffle market leadership
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Amazon's Q2 results beat expectations on AWS strength, sending shares up 14% after-hours and lifting indexes to weekly gains; Microsoft then posted the largest single-day market-cap gain in US stock history (+$450bn). Meanwhile Meta extended a record losing streak, dropping 9% on weak guidance and AI competition concerns, and Reddit hit a record low on disappointing AI licensing outlook.
Sources: [2026-07-30](../../news/markets/2026-07-30.md), [2026-07-31](../../news/markets/2026-07-31.md), [2026-08-01](../../news/markets/2026-08-01.md), [2026-08-02](../../news/markets/2026-08-02.md)

### IPO and exchange launch wave
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Jersey Mike's drew demand exceeding 10-15x available shares ahead of its NYSE debut; Reformation priced at the low end but still saw strong demand. The Texas Stock Exchange began a phased rollout, positioning itself as a Wall Street rival targeting Southern-region IPOs.
Sources: [2026-07-27](../../news/markets/2026-07-27.md), [2026-07-28](../../news/markets/2026-07-28.md), [2026-07-30](../../news/markets/2026-07-30.md), [2026-08-01](../../news/markets/2026-08-01.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Volatility whiplash**: the week alternated between sharp routs (South Korea's bear market, Nasdaq correction, chip sell-offs) and equally sharp rebounds (KOSPI +18%, Microsoft's record gain, Wall Street recovery), reflecting jittery sentiment around AI spending and geopolitics rather than a clear trend.
- **AI spending scrutiny**: recurring concern across Nvidia, Meta, Reddit, and the broader semiconductor sector about whether AI infrastructure investment is paying off, even as Amazon/AWS and Microsoft delivered strong counter-evidence and CXMT's debut showed continued demand for AI-related chips.
- **Geopolitics driving oil and equities**: the Iran-US-Saudi conflict directly moved oil prices and stock markets twice within the week (de-escalation rally, then escalation sell-off), underscoring markets' sensitivity to Middle East developments.
- **IPO revival**: multiple strong-demand listings (CXMT, Jersey Mike's, Reformation) plus a new exchange (Texas Stock Exchange) suggest robust primary-market activity despite secondary-market turbulence.
- **Milestone market caps**: Apple ($5T) and Microsoft (record one-day gain) both hit historic valuation marks within days of each other, contrasted with Meta's slide — highlighting diverging fortunes within Big Tech.

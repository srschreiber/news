# Sam's News — markets — Week 2026-W32

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Record-high rally: markets hit fresh records repeatedly during the week, driven by Iran de-escalation hopes, a Strait of Hormuz deal signal, and strong earnings (Aug 3–5, 9).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ July jobs report shocked markets: US economy lost 23,000 jobs (vs. +83–95K expected), with heavy prior-month downward revisions, easing Fed rate-hike pressure and lifting stock futures (Aug 7).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ AstraZeneca–Bristol Myers Squibb reportedly in early merger talks for a ~$400B pharma giant; unconfirmed, oncology antitrust risk flagged (Aug 3).
- 🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ SpaceX's first-ever public earnings showed revenue doubling but heavy AI-infrastructure and Starlink capex, spooking some investors (Aug 5).
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Berkshire Hathaway profit nearly doubled; $4.5B buyback, cash pile near $360B (Aug 8).
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Amazon became the third US company to hit a $3 trillion market cap (Aug 3).
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Palantir posted a strong, profitable Q2 with surging commercial revenue (Aug 4).

## Biggest stories

### Iran de-escalation and Hormuz diplomacy drive market rally
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ Markets rallied through the week on easing Iran tensions. On Aug 3, stocks surged and oil fell after the president of the United States said he halted a planned strike on Iran. By Aug 4, Treasury Secretary Bessent signaled a Strait of Hormuz deal could be imminent, pushing the S&P 500 and Dow to records alongside strong earnings. Records continued Aug 5 on the same optimism. By Aug 8, a piece noted the president had earlier predicted an Iran conflict would trigger a 20-25% market crash — a scenario markets have so far avoided.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/markets/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-04](../../news/markets/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-05](../../news/markets/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/markets/2026-08-08.md)

### Surprise July jobs report reshapes rate-cut expectations
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ US payrolls fell 23,000 in July, far below the +83,000–95,000 forecast, with May revised down 66,000 (to +63,000) and June down 37,000 (to +20,000). Unemployment fell to 4.1%, but labor-force participation dropped to a 5+ year low of 61.4%; average hourly earnings rose just 3.2% y/y. Stock futures jumped on reduced Fed rate-hike pressure (Aug 7); by Aug 9, Wall Street was still parsing the weak data even as stocks kept climbing.
Sources: [2026-08-07](../../news/markets/2026-08-07.md), [2026-08-09](../../news/markets/2026-08-09.md)

### AstraZeneca–Bristol Myers Squibb merger talks
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ The Financial Times reported early-stage merger talks between AstraZeneca (~$264B/£196B market cap) and Bristol Myers Squibb (~$133B) that would create a ~$400B, world's-fourth-largest drugmaker. AstraZeneca shares fell up to 7.8%, Bristol Myers rose up to 3.8%. Oncology portfolio overlap raises antitrust concerns; neither company confirmed talks.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/markets/2026-08-03.md)

### SpaceX's debut earnings reveal AI-spending strain
🔥🔥🔥🔥◯ SpaceX's first-ever public quarterly report showed revenue roughly doubling, but heavy capital expenditure on AI compute infrastructure and Starlink expansion unsettled some investors.
Sources: [2026-08-05](../../news/markets/2026-08-05.md)

### Corporate earnings roundup: Berkshire, Palantir, Amazon, AppLovin
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ Berkshire Hathaway's profit nearly doubled on equity gains, with $4.5B in buybacks and ~$360B cash on hand (Aug 8). Palantir posted a profitable Q2 with strong commercial growth, lifting its stock (Aug 4). Amazon crossed a $3 trillion market cap, becoming only the third US firm to do so (Aug 3). By contrast, AppLovin missed guidance for the first time since its IPO, prompting a Piper Sandler downgrade (Aug 6).
Sources: [2026-08-08](../../news/markets/2026-08-08.md), [2026-08-04](../../news/markets/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-03](../../news/markets/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/markets/2026-08-06.md)

## Themes & trends
- **Geopolitics as the dominant market driver:** Iran-related headlines (halted strike, Hormuz deal signals, a resurfaced crash prediction from the president of the United States) shaped sentiment more than fundamentals across four of the seven days.
- **Record highs amid mixed signals:** The Dow and S&P hit fresh records multiple times (Aug 4, 5, 9) even as weak jobs data and Fed commentary (Aug 7, 9) pointed to a softening economy — a "good news is bad news" dynamic for rate-cut hopes.
- **AI capex scrutiny:** SpaceX's debut earnings and continued AI infrastructure spend (echoed by Alphabet's $25B bond raise on Aug 6) highlight investor unease about the pace and payoff of AI investment even as commercial AI plays (Palantir, Kenya's planned AI ETF) draw enthusiasm.
- **Big-cap consolidation and scale:** Pharma merger talk (AstraZeneca/BMS) and Amazon's $3T milestone mark a week of scale-driven headlines among large caps.
- **Cautionary voices:** By week's end, commentary turned toward risk — a rare technical pattern with a poor track record since 1997, negative Fed signals, and Warren Buffett's "church with a casino attached" remark, suggesting growing skepticism beneath the record-high headlines.

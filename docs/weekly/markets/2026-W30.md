## Biggest stories

### Google's $94.1B SpaceX stake surfaces amid AI-spending scrutiny
Alphabet disclosed a $94.1B stake in SpaceX (~6% ownership) in its July 23 Q2 SEC filing, tracing back to a 2015 $1B joint investment with Fidelity. SpaceX IPO'd June 12, 2026 at $135/share, popping 19% to a $2.1T valuation; $80B of Google's stake is under short-term lockup, $14.1B locked until Q3 2027. The disclosure added ~$99B in unrealized Q2 gains for Alphabet. It landed alongside broader Big Tech earnings that met investor doubts over whether massive AI capital expenditure is paying off — a tension that ran through the week's market coverage.
Sources: [2026-07-26](../../news/markets/2026-07-26.md)

### Wall Street volatility deepens, second week of losses
Markets entered a rocky stretch starting July 25, with sell signals triggered and analysts flagging correction risk. By July 26 this extended into a second consecutive weekly decline, driven by AI-spending skepticism, Trump Trade stocks losing steam, and unusual volatility in South Korea's KOSPI resembling meme-stock trading.
Sources: [2026-07-25](../../news/markets/2026-07-25.md), [2026-07-26](../../news/markets/2026-07-26.md)

### Berkshire Hathaway's record $397B cash pile
Warren Buffett's Berkshire Hathaway built up a $397B cash reserve, described as large enough to acquire almost any S&P 500 company, underscoring caution about current valuations.
Sources: [2026-07-26](../../news/markets/2026-07-26.md)

## Themes & trends
- **AI-spending skepticism as a market drag**: Big Tech earnings, industrial-stock parity with tech, and general sell-off signals all trace back to investor doubt over AI capex payoff, a thread running across both days covered.
- **Big cash piles and pullbacks**: Berkshire's $397B reserve and DeepSeek's investor-funding pause both point to major players sitting on capital rather than deploying it amid uncertainty.
- **IPO ripple effects**: SpaceX's June IPO reshaped Alphabet's balance sheet overnight, while Shein's disclosures ahead of its Hong Kong listing show more large IPOs feeding into market narratives.
- **Political-trade unwind**: Trump Trade stocks weakening alongside broader declines suggests a rotation away from the post-election trade that had driven earlier gains.

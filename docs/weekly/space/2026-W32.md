# Sam's News — space — Week 2026-W32

## TL;DR
- 🔥🔥🔥◯◯ SpaceX's Falcon 9 upper stage deliberately crashed into the moon on August 5, creating a visible impact crater and setting up its next Starship test flight, targeted for late August, with a booster-catch goal.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Blue Origin traced its New Glenn rocket explosion to a faulty first-stage oxygen valve.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ NASA extended Voyager 2's mission by carefully shutting down instruments to preserve power for its three remaining science instruments, keeping the 48-year-old probe running.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ SpaceX and NASA outlined lunar ambitions: robot-built moon factories, a laser-powered cave-exploring drone, and a lunar relay navigation system delivery.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ China announced the world's only Mars sample receiving lab, after NASA canceled comparable plans.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ NASA astronauts completed a 6.5-hour spacewalk to prep the ISS for solar array upgrades.
- 🔥🔥◯◯◯ Total solar eclipse set for August 12 over Spain, Iceland, and Greenland, expected to reveal unprecedented views of the sun's outer atmosphere.

## Biggest stories

### SpaceX Falcon 9 moon impact and next Starship test
🔥🔥🔥◯◯ A spent Falcon 9 upper stage struck the moon on August 5, confirmed by imagery from a Korean lunar orbiter showing a new impact crater. Days later, Elon Musk said SpaceX aims to fly and attempt to catch the next Starship test flight in late August, while separately conceding the upper stage from the 13th Starship test flight is likely lost at sea after a failed recovery tow.
Sources: [2026-08-04](../../news/space/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-05](../../news/space/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/space/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/space/2026-08-08.md)

### Blue Origin New Glenn explosion investigation
🔥🔥◯◯◯ Blue Origin identified a faulty oxygen valve in a first-stage engine as the cause of the New Glenn rocket explosion, per CEO statements.
Sources: [2026-08-06](../../news/space/2026-08-06.md)

### Voyager 2 power management extends 48-year-old mission
🔥🔥◯◯◯ NASA engineers sequenced shutdowns of Voyager 2's instruments to stretch its dwindling power budget, keeping its three remaining science instruments operating in interstellar space for at least another year.
Sources: [2026-08-05](../../news/space/2026-08-05.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/space/2026-08-08.md)

### Lunar ambitions ramp up: factories, drones, navigation, moon race
🔥🔥◯◯◯ SpaceX said it plans to build robot-operated factories on the moon; NASA backed a laser-powered drone concept to explore lunar caves and delivered a Lunar Relay Navigation System. Separately, Elon Musk and Jeff Bezos were reported to be supporting NASA's lunar program amid competition with China, while China announced it will host the only Mars sample receiving lab on Earth after NASA scrapped its own plans.
Sources: [2026-08-03](../../news/space/2026-08-03.md), [2026-08-04](../../news/space/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-06](../../news/space/2026-08-06.md), [2026-08-08](../../news/space/2026-08-08.md)

### ISS operations: spacewalk and Crew-13 prep
🔥🔥◯◯◯ NASA astronauts Jessica Meir and Anil Menon completed a 6.5-hour spacewalk to install solar array mounting hardware on the ISS. Separately, NASA is targeting September for the SpaceX Crew-13 launch to the station.
Sources: [2026-08-04](../../news/space/2026-08-04.md), [2026-08-07](../../news/space/2026-08-07.md)
